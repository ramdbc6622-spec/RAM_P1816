@section 13.1 | Rise of Babur
- Mughals were **Chaghtai Turks** {2010}
- Babur lost at **Sar-i-Pul** to **Shaibani Khan** {2015}
- Took the title **Padshah** at **Kabul** {2015}
- Invited to India by **Daulat Khan** Lodi {2021}

@section 13.2 | Battles
- **First Panipat**: **Babur vs Ibrahim Lodi**, **1526** {1996, 2012, 2016}
- Babur's victory: **skilled generalship** (key; many guides say artillery) {1990}
- First use of artillery among options: **First Panipat** (Bajaur 1519 was earlier) {1996}
- **Jihad** declared at **Khanwa** {2009}
- **Rana Sanga** fought Babur at **Khanwa** (1527) {2004, 2010}
- Wrong pair: **Chanderi-1530** (it was **1528**) {2015}

@section 13.3 | Baburnama and Babri Mosque
- Babur wrote in **Turki**, but the court language was **Persian** (A true, R false) {2025}
- Hindu state named in the Baburnama: **Mewar** (Vijayanagara also named) {1995}
- **Babri mosque** (Ayodhya) built by **Mir Baqi** {1992}

@trap
- Mughals = **Chaghtai Turks**, not Mongols or Persians.
- Jihad was declared at **Khanwa**, not Panipat.
- Chanderi = **1528**; Ghaghra = **1529**.
- Babur died at Agra but lies at **Kabul**.
@end
