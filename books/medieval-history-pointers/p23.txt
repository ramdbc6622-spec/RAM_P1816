@section 23.1 | The Gurus
- Guru Nanak's birthplace: **Nankana** (Talwandi) {2007}
- Founded Sikhism under **Sikandar Lodi** {2008}
- Nanak's successor: **Lehna** (Guru Angad) {2021}
- **Gurmukhi** started by **Guru Angad** {2017}
- **Amritsar** founded by **Guru Ram Das** {1996}
- Put to death: **Arjan Dev and Tegh Bahadur** (2 and 4) {2002}
- Helped rebel prince **Khusrau**: **Guru Arjan Dev** {2001}
- **Khalsa** founded by **Guru Gobind Singh** {2006}
- **Nanded**: samadhi of **Guru Gobind Singh** {2002}
- Banda Bahadur's original name: **Lachhman Dev** {2014}

@table
Guru | Known for
Nanak | Founder; Kartarpur
Angad | Gurmukhi script
Amar Das | Langar, manjis
Ram Das | Founded Amritsar
Arjan Dev | Adi Granth (1604); executed under Jahangir
Hargobind | Akal Takht; militarised the Sikhs
Tegh Bahadur | Executed under Aurangzeb (1675)
Gobind Singh | Khalsa (1699); died at Nanded
@end

@trap
- Amritsar = **Ram Das**; Harmandir and Adi Granth = **Arjan Dev**.
- Hargobind was imprisoned at Gwalior but **not** executed.
- Gobind Singh: born at **Patna**, Khalsa at **Anandpur**, died at **Nanded**.
@end

@up UP link: Guru Nanak visited **Varanasi, Ayodhya** and **Prayag**; Guru Tegh Bahadur stayed at **Prayagraj** (Pakki Sangat) and Agra, where he was arrested before his execution in Delhi (1675).
