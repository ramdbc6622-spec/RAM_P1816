@section 1.3 | Muhammad Ghori
- Conquered North India in 1192: **Shihabuddin Muhammad Ghori** {2004}
- Battle that founded Muslim rule: **Second Battle of Tarain** {1995}
- First defeated Ghori: **Mularaja II** (Kasahrada, 1178) {1990}
- **Chandawar (1194)**: Ghori beat **Jayachandra** {2008, 2016}
- Bihar and Bengal conquered by **Bakhtiyar Khalji** {1991, 2018}
- Order: **Mahmud, Ghori, Genghis Khan, Timur** {2008}
- Turkish success: North India lacked **political unity** (A and R true, R explains A) {2018, 2024}

@trap
- Hijra = **622**; Prophet's birth = **570**.
- Ghori's first defeat was by **Mularaja II**, not Prithviraj.
- Chandawar (1194) is not the Battle of Kannauj (1540, Sher Shah vs Humayun).
- Shahnama = **Firdausi**, not Firishta.
@end
