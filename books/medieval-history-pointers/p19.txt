@section 19.1 | Officers and Units
- The Mughal state was first a **military state** (A and R true, R explains A) {2021}
- Army head: **Mir Bakhshi**; also oversaw officials {1992, 2004}
- Wazirs: **Akbar-Muzaffar Khan, Jahangir-Itimad-ud-Daula, Shah Jahan-Sadullah Khan, Aurangzeb-Asad Khan** {2021}
- **Diwan-i-Tan**: jagirs and salaries (with other officers' duties) {2020}
- District: **sarkar** {2009}
- **Chakla**: unit **between subah and pargana**, not the same as a sarkar {2018}

@flow Mughal administrative units
Subah | Province (subahdar, diwan)
Sarkar | District (faujdar, amalguzar)
Pargana | Group of villages (shiqdar, amil, qanungo)
Gram | Village (muqaddam, patwari)
@end

@section 19.2 | Mansabdari and Revenue
- Mansabdars were the official nobility started by **Akbar**; **not hereditary** (only 1) {2019}
- Not true: **sawar rank could exceed zat** {2009}
- Zat and mansab granted; **three classes** (1 and 3) {2010, 2010}
- Main income: **land revenue** {1995}
- **Mal**: **land revenue** {2009}
- Akbar's Deccan revenue: by the **number of ploughs** {2017}

@end
