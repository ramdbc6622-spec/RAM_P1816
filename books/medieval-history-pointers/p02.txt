@section 2.1 | Aibak and Iltutmish
- Founder of the Slave dynasty: **Qutbuddin Aibak** {1990}
- Aibak's capital: **Lahore** {1990}
- Made Delhi the capital: **Iltutmish** {2004, 2012, 2014, 2016, 2016}
- First Muslim ruler of Delhi (key): **Iltutmish** (many books say Aibak) {2002}
- "**Slave of a slave**": **Iltutmish** {2016}
- Genghis Khan reached the Indus under **Iltutmish** {1993, 2007, 2010}
- Genghis Khan's original name: **Temujin** {2015}

@section 2.2 | Razia and Balban
- First woman ruler: **Razia Sultan** {2004, 2010}
- Razia was overthrown by the **Turkish** nobles (the Forty) {2004}
- "**Blood and iron**": **Balban** {2009}
- Balban centralised power; the reason given does not explain it (A and R true, R does not explain A) {2013, 2024}
- Called himself "helper of the Caliph" at **Garhmukteshwar**: **Balban** {2014}

@trap
- Aibak's capital was **Lahore**; Delhi became the capital under **Iltutmish**.
- Genghis Khan came under **Iltutmish** (1221), not Alauddin.
- Razia was removed by **Turkish**, not Afghan, nobles.
@end

@up UP link: **Garhmukteshwar** (Hapur, on the Ganga) has Balban's inscription calling himself helper of the Caliph; **Badaun** was Iltutmish's iqta before he became Sultan.
