@section 9.1 | Amir Khusrau and Amir Hasan
- Khusrau born at **Patiali** (Etah in 2002-03; now Kasganj) (*) {2002, 2003, ?}
- "**Parrot of India**": **Amir Khusrau** {2013}
- Court poet of **Alauddin Khalji** {1995}
- Saw seven Sultans: **Amir Khusrau** and Nizamuddin both fit (*) {2005}
- Father of Hindi **Khari Boli**; master of Hindi and Persian {1990, 2002, 2008, 2012}
- First Persian poet to depict India: **Amir Khusrau** {2017}
- **Tabla** introduced by **Amir Khusrau** (tradition) {2009}
- "Sadi of India": **Amir Hasan** {2013}
- **Fawaid-ul-Fuad**: compiled by **Amir Hasan Sijzi** {2021}

@section 9.2 | Translations and Chronicles
- Shukasaptati into Persian as **Tutinama**: **Ziya-uddin Nakhshabi** {2022}
- Translated Firuz's 300 Nagarkot volumes: **Azizuddin Khan** (per key) {2023}
- **Futuh-us-Salatin-Isami, Futuhat-i-Firozshahi-Firuz, Tarikh-i-Firozshahi-Barani, Khazain-ul-Futuh-Khusrau** {2023}
- Sultan who wrote his memoirs: **Firuz Tughluq** {2008}
- **Barani-Tarikh-i-Firozshahi, Hasan Nizami-Taj-ul-Maasir, Minhaj-Tabaqat-i-Nasiri, Yahya-Tarikh-i-Mubarakshahi** {1997}
- **Taj-ul-Maasir-Hasan Nizami, Khazain-ul-Futuh-Khusrau, Tarikh-i-Mubarakshahi-Yahya, Fatawa-i-Jahandari-Barani** {2019}
- **Tarikh-i-Hind-Al-Biruni, Tarikh-i-Delhi-Khusrau, Rihla-Ibn Battuta, Tabaqat-i-Nasiri-Minhaj** {2009}
- Order: **Kitab-ul-Hind, Prithviraj Raso, Tabaqat-i-Nasiri, Fatawa-i-Jahandari** {2020}

@section 9.3 | Regional Histories and Hindavi
- **Mirat-i-Sikandari** (Gujarat), **Burhan-i-Maasir** (Bahmanis), **Riyaz-us-Salatin** (Bengal), **Riyaz-ul-Insha** (Mahmud Gawan's letters) {2023}
- Mirat-i-Sikandari describes Mahmud Begada's prosperity and security (both) {2023}
- **Mulla Daud-Chandayan, Damodar-Padmavati Katha, Somnath-Raga Vibodh, Khusrau-Ashiqa** {2019}
- Jain scholar among options: **Hemachandra Suri** {2009}

@trap
- Futuh-us-Salatin (**Isami**) vs Futuhat-i-Firozshahi (**Firuz**).
- Fatawa-i-Jahandari is **Barani's**, not Firuz's.
- Amir Hasan Sijzi (Sufi poet) is not Hasan Nizami (Aibak's historian).
- Patiali was in Etah until 2008; it is now **Kasganj**.
@end

@up UP link: **Amir Khusrau** was born at **Patiali** (now Kasganj district, UP) in 1253; **Mulla Daud's Chandayan** (1379) was written at **Dalmau** (Rae Bareli), the first Sufi masnavi in Awadhi.
