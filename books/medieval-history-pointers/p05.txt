@section 5.1 | Sayyids and Lodis
- Sayyid order: **Khizr Khan, Mubarak Shah, Muhammad Shah, Alauddin Alam Shah** {2023}
- Afghan origin: **Lodis** {1993, 2008, 2011, 2016}
- Agra founded by **Sikandar Lodi** {1993, 2004, 2005, 2008, 2010, 2011, 2011}
- Pen-name "**Gulrukhi**": **Sikandar Lodi** {2015}
- Abolished the tax on grain: **Sikandar Lodi** {2010}
- Order: **Slave, Khalji, Sayyid, Lodi** (4, 1, 3, 2) {2012}
