@section 25.1 | Later Mughals

- Order: **Jahandar Shah, Muhammad Shah, Ahmad Shah, Alamgir II** {2018}
- Duty-free trade to the English in Bengal: **Farrukhsiyar** {2010}
- Kingmakers: **Hussain Ali and Abdullah** (Sayyid brothers) {2017}
- Nadir Shah's attack: under **Muhammad Shah** {2004}
- Eunuchs and ladies ruled under **Muhammad Shah** {2004}
- **Tappa** music refined at **Muhammad Shah's** court {2023}
- Bahadur Shah Zafar's father: **Akbar Shah II** {2005}
- Not correct of Zafar: pension **under one lakh** {2006}

@section 25.2 | Successor States and Jai Singh
- First Nawab of Awadh: **Saadat Khan** (Burhan-ul-Mulk) {2008}
- Saadat Khan founded Awadh and joined the plot against the Sayyids (both) {2023}
- Founder of Hyderabad: **Chin Qilich Khan** (Nizam-ul-Mulk) {2011}
- **Jantar Mantar**, Delhi: **Jai Singh II** {2005}
- Jai Singh's observatories: **Delhi, Jaipur, Ujjain, Varanasi** (and Mathura) {2007}

@trap
