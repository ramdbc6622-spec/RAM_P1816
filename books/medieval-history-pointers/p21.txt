@trap
- Only **Babur** and **Jahangir** wrote their own memoirs.
- Two Humayun-namas: **Gulbadan** (memoir), **Khwandamir** (Qanun-i-Humayuni).
- Tughlaqnama = **Amir Khusrau**, not Ibn Battuta (Rihla).
- Mrigavati is older than Padmavat.
@end

@up UP link: **Malik Muhammad Jayasi** wrote the **Padmavat** (1540) at **Jais** (Amethi); **Qutban's Mrigavati** came from the Jaunpur region, and **Tulsidas** and **Surdas** wrote in Awadhi and Braj in UP.
