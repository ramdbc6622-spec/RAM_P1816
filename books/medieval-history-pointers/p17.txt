@section 17.1 | Court and Buildings
- Capital moved from Agra to **Delhi**: **Shah Jahan** {2008}
- Abolished **sijda**: **Shah Jahan** {2010, 2010}
- **Lal Qila** (Delhi): **Shah Jahan** {2002, 2010}
- **Jama Masjid**, Delhi: **Shah Jahan** {2004}
- **Kohinoor** given by **Mir Jumla** {2015}
- Poet laureate: **Kalim** {2015}
- Patronised **Kavindracharya Saraswati** of Banaras: **Shah Jahan** {2022}
- Led Banaras pandits to end the pilgrim tax: **Kavindracharya** {2000}
- Deccan governor for most of the reign: **Aurangzeb** {2009}

@section 17.2 | Dara Shikoh
- Upanishads into Persian under **Shah Jahan** {1992, 2006, 2009}
- Title: **Sirr-i-Akbar**, by **Dara Shikoh** {2000, 2002, 2004}
- "**Shah Buland Iqbal**": **Dara Shikoh** {2014}
- First Muslim to study Hindu scriptures (key): **Dara Shikoh** {2003}
- Dara is buried in **Delhi** (Humayun's tomb) {2017}
