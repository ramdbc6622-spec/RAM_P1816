@section 8.1 | Monuments
- First Indo-Islamic tomb (key): **Balban's tomb** (first true arch) {2004}
- Did not build the Qutb Minar: **Alauddin Khalji** {2002, 2003, 2013}
- Fifth storey: **Firuz Shah Tughluq** {2004}
- **Sultan Ghari-Iltutmish, Red Palace-Balban, Jamaat Khana Masjid-Alauddin Khalji, Adhai Din ka Jhonpra-Aibak** {2022}
- Wrong pair: **Ajmer-Quwwat-ul-Islam** (that is Delhi; Ajmer has the Adhai Din ka Jhonpra) {2011}

@trap
- Alauddin built the **Alai Darwaza** and began the unfinished Alai Minar, not the Qutb Minar.
- Delhi = Quwwat-ul-Islam; Ajmer = Adhai Din ka Jhonpra.
- Balban's tomb has the first **true arch**.
@end

@up UP link: the Sharqi sultans built the **Atala, Jhanjhri and Jama mosques** in **Jaunpur**, the finest regional Sultanate style in UP.
