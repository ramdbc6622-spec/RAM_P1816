@trap
- Chauth = **one-fourth** (protection); sardeshmukhi = **one-tenth**.
- Shahu ruled **after** Rajaram and Shivaji II (he was a Mughal prisoner).
- Balaji Vishwanath (first Peshwa) is not Balaji Baji Rao (Panipat).
@end

@up UP link: the Marathas raided the **Doab** and **Rohilkhand**; **Najib Khan** of the Rohillas (Najibabad, Bijnor) and **Shuja-ud-Daula** of **Awadh** sided with Abdali at Third Panipat; Shivaji escaped from **Agra** in 1666.
