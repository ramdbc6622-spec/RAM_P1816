@chapter 14 | Humayun and Sher Shah
@meta Built from 15 UPPSC questions (1993–2023). Focus: Humayun's family, Chausa and Kannauj, Sher Shah's rise, death at Kalinjar and tomb at Sasaram, the Purana Qila, revenue and the rupiya.

@hot
- **Sher Shah**: died at **Kalinjar** (1545), buried at **Sasaram** {1993, 2002, 2015, 2016}
- **Patta and qabuliyat**: **Sher Shah** {2004, 2008, 2009}
- **Kannauj (Bilgram), 17 May 1540**: **Sher Shah beat Humayun** {2019}
- **Purana Qila** in its present form and its **Qila-i-Kuhna** mosque: **Sher Shah** {2009, 2016, 2021}
@end

@section 14.1 | Humayun
- Wrong pair: **Hamida Banu-wife of Alauddin** (she was Humayun's wife) {2023}
- Not Humayun's brother: **Usman** (brothers: Kamran, Askari, Hindal) {2008}
- Kannauj, 17 May 1540: **Sher Shah vs Humayun** {2019}
- Order: **Daurah, Chausa, Kannauj, Samugarh** {2017}

1539 | Chausa: Sher Khan beats Humayun
1540 | Kannauj (Bilgram): Humayun loses; exile

@end

@section 14.2 | Sher Shah
- Highly educated ruler: **Sher Shah** (studied at Jaunpur) {2010}
- **Jaita and Kumpa** of **Marwar** impressed Sher Shah {2022}
- "Lost the empire for a handful of millet": **Sher Shah** {2007}
- Died at **Kalinjar**, his last campaign; buried at **Sasaram** {1993, 2002, 2015, 2016}
- **Purana Qila** in present form: **Sher Shah**; **Qila-i-Kuhna** mosque too {2009, 2016, 2021}
- Brilliant administrator (only 1) {2021}
- **Patta and qabuliyat**: **Sher Shah** {2004, 2008, 2009}
- Rupiya to dam: **1 : 64** (Sher Shah) {2006}

@table
Sher Shah's reform | Detail
Land revenue | Measured land; one-third of produce
Patta, qabuliyat | Title deed and acceptance
Coinage | Silver rupiya, copper dam
Roads | Sarak-i-Azam (Grand Trunk Road), sarais
Dak | Horse post at sarais
@end

@trap
- Died at **Kalinjar** (UP); buried at **Sasaram** (Bihar).
- Chausa (1539) came **before** Kannauj (1540).
- Jaita and Kumpa were of **Marwar**, not Mewar.
- Rupiya:dam = 1:64 (Sher Shah) but 1:40 under Akbar.
@end

@up UP link: **Chausa** lies on the UP-Bihar border near Buxar; **Kannauj (Bilgram)** in UP saw Humayun's final defeat (1540), and **Kalinjar fort** (Banda) is where Sher Shah died in 1545; he had studied at **Jaunpur**.
