@trap
- Jaunpur founder = **Firuz**; Sharqi state founder = **Malik Sarwar** (Khwaja Jahan).
- Zain-ul-Abidin **abolished** jizya; Sikandar Shah (Kashmir) imposed it.
- Yadava = **Devagiri**; Kakatiya = **Warangal** (often swapped).
- Adina Masjid = **Pandua** (Bengal), not Mandu.
@end

@up UP link: the **Sharqi Sultanate of Jaunpur** (1394-1479) was UP's great medieval regional state; its **Atala Masjid** and **Jama Masjid**, and its fame as the "Shiraz of India", come from Ibrahim Shah Sharqi's patronage of learning.
