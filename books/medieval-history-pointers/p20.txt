@section 20.1 | Painting
- Foundation of Mughal painting: **Humayun** {2009, 2010, 2012}
- Statements on Mughal painting: **all** true {1991}
- Jahangir mainly patronised **painting** {2016}
- Jahangir's painters: **Abul Hasan and Aqa Riza** (2 and 3) {2012}
- "**Nadir-uz-Zaman**": **Abul Hasan** {2013}
- "**Nadir-ul-Asr**" and greatest bird painter: **Mansur** {1998, 2014}
- Pahari, Rajput, Mughal, Kangra: styles of **painting** {1994}

@flow Growth of Mughal painting
Humayun | Brought Mir Sayyid Ali and Abdus Samad from Persia
Akbar | Hamzanama; Daswanth, Basawan; tasvir khana
Jahangir | Golden age: portraits, nature; Abul Hasan, Mansur
Shah Jahan | Formal, decorative
Aurangzeb | Court patronage ends; artists move to Rajput and Pahari courts
@end

@section 20.2 | Music
- Haridas' followers set up **5** music centres (per key) {2009}
- Akbar's Dhrupad singers: **Tansen and Haridas** (1 and 2) {2010}
- **Tansen's tomb**: **Gwalior** {1999}
- Tansen's patron before Akbar: **Raja Ramchandra** (Baghela) {2019}
- Not a great name in Hindustani music: **Mal Gurjari** (a raga) {2021}
- Learnt Hindu music from Lala Kalawant: **Akbar** {2016}
- Aurangzeb played the **veena** {2007, 2010}

@trap
- Nadir-uz-Zaman = **Abul Hasan**; Nadir-ul-Asr = **Mansur**.
- Mughal painting began under **Humayun**, not Akbar.
- Tansen's tomb is at **Gwalior**; he sang at Fatehpur Sikri.
- Aurangzeb ended court music but could play the veena.
@end

@up UP link: **Swami Haridas** lived at **Vrindavan** (Mathura), where Tansen is said to have learnt from him; **Fatehpur Sikri** had Akbar's painting workshops and Tansen's performances.
