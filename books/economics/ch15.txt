@chapter 15 | Welfare Schemes II: Rural Development, Employment and Basic Services

@intro
The second schemes chapter covers **rural development** from the Etawah pilot and **Community Development Programme** to the **IRDP-TRYSEM-SGSY-NRLM** line, **wage employment** (NREP, JRY, SGRY, **MGNREGA**), **financial inclusion and clean energy** (Jan Dhan, PAHAL, Ujjwala, Saubhagya), **water and sanitation** (Jal Jeevan Mission, Swachh Bharat, Nirmal Bharat), **housing** (PMAY, Rajiv Awas) and **urban missions** (AMRUT, HRIDAY). It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@table
MGNREGA fact | Detail
Act / start | Passed **2005**; began **2 February 2006** in **200 districts**; **330** in 2007-08; **whole country from 1 April 2008**
Guarantee | **100 days** of wage work a year to **every rural household** (not to every adult member) whose adults volunteer for unskilled work
Extra days | 150 days in drought areas and for forest-rights holders
Outcomes | Higher **purchasing power**, less **distress migration**, **more** bargaining power for labour
Budget | About **Rs. 86,000 crore** a year (2024-25, 2025-26)
**Replaced** | By the **VB-G RAM G Act** (Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin)), **December 2025**: guarantee raised to **125 days**
@end

The Budget **2020-21** cut **Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)** compared with 2019-20; the others rose or stayed level. In Budget **2023-24** MGNREGA was cut by about a third, but the biggest percentage cut and the biggest rise were in other schemes, so both statements are wrong.
