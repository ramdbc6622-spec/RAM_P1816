@chapter 11 | Banking in India

@intro
This chapter covers the **history of Indian banking** (early banks, the Imperial Bank and SBI, the **1969 and 1980 nationalisations**, mergers), **public, private, foreign and cooperative banks**, **development finance institutions** (IFCI, ICICI, IDBI, SIDBI), the **Lead Bank Scheme**, **financial inclusion** and payment banks, **NPAs, Basel norms and deposit insurance**, the rural credit system (**NABARD, RRBs, cooperatives, land development banks, SHGs, KCC**), banking committees and **plastic money**. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2005
Basel II relates to which one of the following?

(b) Measures against cyber crimes
(c) Measures against drug abuse by sportspersons

@ans (d)
@note **Basel norms** come from the **Basel Committee on Banking Supervision** (Bank for International Settlements, Basel, Switzerland). **Basel I** (1988) set minimum capital; **Basel II** (2004) set the **capital adequacy** rules on three pillars; **Basel III** (2010) added more capital, liquidity rules and buffers after the 2008 crisis.
@end
