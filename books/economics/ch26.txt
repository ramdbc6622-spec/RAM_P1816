@chapter 26 | Urbanization II: Cities, Migration and Urban Missions

@intro
The second urbanization chapter covers the **classes of towns**, **million-plus cities** and **megacities** (India and UP), city rankings, **migration** (Lee, Hauser), **urban problems** (slums, crime, poverty), and the **urban missions** (JNNURM, Smart Cities, AMRUT, India Urban Observatory). It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@table
Migration term | Meaning
