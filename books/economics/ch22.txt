@table
Theory | Propounder | Key idea
**Malthusian theory** (1798) | **T.R. Malthus** | Population grows in **geometric**, food in **arithmetic** progression; checks: **preventive** (moral restraint, late marriage) and **positive** (famine, war, disease)
**Optimum population** | **Edwin Cannan** | Size giving the highest per capita income
**Demographic transition** | **W.S. Thompson** (1929), F. Notestein | High birth and death → falling death → falling birth → low both
**Social maladjustment** | **Henry George** | Poverty comes from bad social institutions, not numbers
@end
