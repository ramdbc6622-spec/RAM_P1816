@chapter 18 | Trade Policy, SEZs and Export Promotion

@intro
This chapter covers the **concepts and tools of trade policy** (invisible exports, entrepot trade, tariffs, duty drawback), **globalisation and liberalisation**, the **Foreign Trade Policy** and export houses, **Export Processing Zones and Special Economic Zones**, **export-promotion bodies** (EXIM Bank, ECGC, IBEF, MPEDA, STC, MMTC), **tourism earnings**, and trade disputes (anti-dumping, Super 301). It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.

@pyq U.P.R.O./A.R.O. (Mains) 2017
A tariff :
(a) increases the volume of trade
(b) reduces the volume of trade
(c) has no effect on volume of trade
(d) both (a) and (c)
@ans (b)
@note A **tariff** is a tax on imports (sometimes exports). It raises the price of imported goods and so **reduces the volume of trade**; it protects home industry and earns revenue.
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2011

(b) Refund of import duty to exporters
(c) Obstacles in the matter of exports

@ans (b)
@end

@end


@table
Term | Meaning
**Invisible exports** | Exports of **services** (IT, tourism, shipping, banking) and remittances
**Entrepot trade** | Goods **imported to be re-exported** (Singapore, Dubai)
**Duty drawback** | **Refund of import duty** paid on inputs used in exported goods
**RoDTEP** (2021) | Refund of embedded duties and taxes on exports (replaced MEIS)
**Anti-dumping duty** | Duty on imports sold below their home price; **India** has been one of the **largest users**
**Super 301** | US trade law section to act against countries with **unfair trade barriers**
@end
