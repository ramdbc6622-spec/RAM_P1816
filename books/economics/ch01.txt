@table
Concept | Meaning
**Economic growth** | Quantitative rise in output: **GDP** and per capita income
**Economic development** | Growth **plus structural and qualitative change**: health, education, poverty, equality
**Sustainable development** | Development that protects the **environment** for future generations
**GDP** | Value of final goods and services produced **within** the country in a year
**GNP / GNI** | GDP + net factor income from abroad
**NNP** | GNP - depreciation; **NNP at factor cost = national income**
**Per capita income** | National income ÷ population: best simple measure of the **standard of living**
@end

@trap
**Mixed economy = public + private sectors.** **Hindu rate of growth = 3.5%** (Raj Krishna). **Naoroji** made the first per capita income estimate (Rs. 20, 1867-68); **Mahalanobis** chaired the 1949 committee. National income is now estimated by the **NSO** (CSO + NSSO, 2019). Base year **2011-12** (revision to 2022-23). **Per capita income** measures growth and living standards. **Maharashtra** has the largest economy; **Bihar** the lowest per capita income. **Households** save the most.
