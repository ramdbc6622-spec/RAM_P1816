@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2019
The Environmental Kuznets Curve (EKC) shows the relationship between per capita GDP and environmental loss. What is the shape of Environmental Kuznets Curve?
(a) Inverted ‘U’ shaped
(b) Inverted ‘V’ shaped
(c) Inverted ‘L’ shaped
(d) None of these
@ans (a)
@end


@table
Milestone | Year | Idea
Rachel Carson, *Silent Spring* | 1962 | Pesticide damage; start of the environmental movement
**Club of Rome**, ***The Limits to Growth*** | **1972** | Finite resources limit growth
Stockholm Conference | 1972 | First UN environment conference
**Brundtland Report**, ***Our Common Future*** | **1987** | Defined **sustainable development**: meeting present needs without compromising future generations
Rio Earth Summit (Agenda 21) | 1992 | Action plan
UN SDGs | 2015 | 17 goals for 2030
@end

The **Environmental Kuznets Curve** is an **inverted U**: pollution rises with income at first, then falls after a turning point. The Brundtland Report is not *The Limits to Growth*, so (R) is false.
