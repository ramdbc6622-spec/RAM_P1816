@chapter 23 | Population II: Density and Literacy

@intro
The second demography chapter covers **population density** (national, state and UT patterns, highest and lowest) and **literacy** (effective literacy, decadal rise, leading and lagging states, districts and UTs, male-female gaps, and UP's figures). All data are from **Census 2011** unless another census is named. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end
