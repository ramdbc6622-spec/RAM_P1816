@chapter 24 | Population III: Sex Ratio, Social Groups and Dynamics

@intro
The third demography chapter covers the **sex ratio** (national trend, states, UTs, districts, child sex ratio), **Scheduled Castes and Tribes**, **religious communities**, the **National Population Policy 2000**, **fertility** (TFR), **mortality** (death rate, IMR), **age structure** (dependency ratio, demographic dividend) and population-resource regions. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end
