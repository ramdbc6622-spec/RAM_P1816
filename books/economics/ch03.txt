@chapter 3 | Agriculture I: Structure, Land Reforms and Production

@intro
Agriculture is the most-asked part of the economy section. This first agriculture chapter covers the **structure of Indian farming** (land reforms, tenancy, farm size classes), agriculture's **share in GDP and jobs**, **crops and leading states**, **foodgrain self-sufficiency and food security**, agricultural **trade**, and the early **schemes** (RKVY, horticulture mission, small-farmer programmes). It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@up UP link: UP leads India in **wheat, sugarcane, potato, mango, milk and total foodgrains**, with about 20% of the country's foodgrains from 7% of its geographical area. Western UP was a core **Green Revolution** region along with Punjab and Haryana. The state has **9 agro-climatic zones**.

@trap
**Kisan Diwas = 23 December** (Charan Singh). **Small farmer = 1-2 ha**, marginal = below 1 ha. Tenancy reform = **rent, tenure, ownership**. Agriculture's share is **decreasing** (about 18% of GVA). **2014-15** and the **Third Plan** saw negative farm growth. **MP** leads in soybean; **Australia** has the highest cotton yield; **China** produces the most vegetables. **States** tax agricultural income. **RKVY = 2007-08**, NHM = 2005.
@end

@heatmap

@pattern
Agriculture is the single biggest economy topic. Older papers (1995-2017) asked figures and states; the **2019-2025** papers moved to concepts (tenancy reform, cereal yield, food security dimensions, cash crops) in statement form. Production data change every year, so learn the leading states.
@end

@next
Likely next angles: **record foodgrain output**, the **Agriculture Census 2021-22** results, **PM-Dhan Dhaanya Krishi Yojana** (100 low-productivity districts, 2025), the **Mission for Aatmanirbharta in Pulses** and the **National Mission on Edible Oils**, **digital crop survey** and Agristack, rice export curbs and their removal, and **natural farming**.
@end
