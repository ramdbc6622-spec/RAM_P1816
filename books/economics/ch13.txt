@unit UNIT II · SOCIAL DEVELOPMENT

@chapter 13 | Human Development

@intro
Social development begins with **human development**: the idea that growth must widen people's choices. This chapter covers the **Physical Quality of Life Index**, the **Human Development Index** (who built it, its indicators and goalposts), India's **HDI rank**, the **Human Development Reports**, **Gross National Happiness**, and the HDI of **Indian states**. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@table
HDI fact | Detail
Built by | **Mahbub ul Haq** (Pakistan) with **Amartya Sen**; published by **UNDP**
First Human Development Report | **1990**
Dimensions | **Long and healthy life** (life expectancy at birth); **knowledge** (mean years of schooling for adults 25+, expected years of schooling for children); **decent standard of living** (**GNI per capita**, PPP $)
Method | **Geometric mean** of the three indices (since 2010); arithmetic mean earlier
Goalposts | Life expectancy 20-85 years; income $100-$75,000
Not in HDI | Social inequality, environment, urbanisation, health and nutrition as such
Related indices | **IHDI** (inequality-adjusted, 2010), **GDI**, **GII** (2010), **MPI** (2010), HPI (1997-2009), planetary-pressures adjusted HDI (2020)
@end

@pyq U.P. U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2013
Which of the following States had the highest Human Development Index in 2008?
(a) Uttar Pradesh
(b) Jharkhand
(c) Odisha
(d) Bihar
@ans (a)
@end


**Kerala** has led the states' HDI in every India Human Development Report (2001, 2011); Punjab followed in 2001, with **UP and Bihar** near the bottom. Kerala's high unemployment is real but does not explain its high HDI, which comes from health and education. In the IHDR 2011 (data for 2007-08), **UP ranked above Jharkhand, Odisha and Bihar**.

@up UP link: UP's HDI is among the lowest of the big states (about 0.6), held back by income and health; it does best on **school enrolment**. UP's own **Human Development Report** (2003) ranked **western UP** districts highest and **Bundelkhand** and eastern districts lowest.

@trap
**Three stages: Comte.** **PQLI: Morris D. Morris** (IMR, life expectancy at one, literacy). **HDI: Mahbub ul Haq, UNDP, 1990.** HDI = **health, education, income**; never social inequality, environment or urbanisation. **Goalposts 85-20.** **India 130th (HDR 2025).** **Jobless, ruthless... = UNDP 1996.** **GNH = Bhutan.** **Kerala tops** the states.
@end
