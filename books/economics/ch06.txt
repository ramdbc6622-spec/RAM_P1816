@chapter 6 | Industry II: Public Sector, Major Industries and Services

@intro
The second industry chapter covers **public sector enterprises** (ONGC, OIL, BSNL, HAL, Navratna and Maharatna status), **oil refineries**, **major industries and their centres** (steel plants, sugar, textiles, aluminium, fertilisers), early industrial history, and the **tertiary (service) sector**, now the largest part of the economy. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end
