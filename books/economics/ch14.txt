@chapter 14 | Welfare Schemes I: Health, Education, Women and Social Security

@intro
Government schemes form the largest block of social-development questions, so they are split into two chapters. This one covers **health and nutrition** (NRHM, ICDS, Ayushman Bharat, Poshan), **skills and innovation** (Skill India, AIM, NIDHI), schemes for **women and girls** (Beti Bachao, Swadhar, Janani Suraksha), **social security and insurance** (AABY, RSBY, PMJJBY, PMSBY, APY), the **National Social Assistance Programme**, and **education** (SSA, RTE, Mid-day Meal, Saakshar Bharat, UP's schemes). It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end
