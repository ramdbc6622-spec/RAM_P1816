@pyq U.P.R.O./A.R.O. (Pre) 2023
Who used the term “Megalopolis”?
(a) Jean Gottmann
(b) Taylor
(c) Ogburn
(d) None of these
@ans (a)
@end


**Robert Redfield** built the **folk-urban continuum** from studies in **Mexico** (Yucatán); with **Milton Singer** he described **primary urbanization** (growth of a **great tradition**, orthogenetic cities) and secondary urbanization (heterogenetic cities). **Jean Gottmann** coined **megalopolis** (1961) for the Boston-Washington city chain.
