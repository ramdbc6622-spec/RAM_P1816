@chapter 16 | Poverty and Unemployment

@intro
This chapter covers **poverty**: its types, the **poverty line** and the committees that drew it (Alagh, Lakdawala, Tendulkar, Rangarajan), state-wise poverty, the **Multidimensional Poverty Index**, theories of poverty and inequality (vicious circle, culture of poverty, Lorenz curve), and **unemployment**: its kinds, measurement by the NSSO and PLFS, and its causes. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@table

**Lakdawala** | **1993** | Calorie-based line, state prices (CPI-AL, CPI-IW) | 36% (1993-94)

The earliest study was **Dandekar and Rath** (*Poverty in India*, **1971**), then **Lakdawala (1989-93) → Tendulkar (2005) → Rangarajan (2012)**. Poverty is measured from **household consumption expenditure** (NSSO surveys), using the **head-count ratio**; per capita income is not used. The **Planning Commission** (now **NITI Aayog**) was the nodal agency. The **HCES 2023-24** puts consumption poverty very low (the World Bank estimates extreme poverty at about **5%** in 2022-23).
