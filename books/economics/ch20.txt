@chapter 20 | International Organizations and Global Rankings

@intro
This chapter covers the **Bretton Woods institutions** (IMF, World Bank), the **WTO** and GATT (agreement on agriculture, subsidy boxes, TRIPS), the **G-20**, **World Economic Forum** and its indices, **ILO**, **ADB**, regional groups (**SAARC, BIMSTEC, ASEAN, BRICS, SCO, GCC, OPEC**), the global **reports** and who publishes them, and the size of the world's **largest economies**. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end
