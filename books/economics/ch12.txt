@chapter 12 | Capital Market, Insurance and Market Basics

@intro
The last money-and-banking chapter covers **insurance** (LIC, IRDAI, indemnity, actuaries, provident and pension funds), the **capital and money markets** (SEBI, mutual funds, UTI, NSE, BSE, Sensex, Greenex, bulls and bears, blue chips, gilt-edged securities, commercial paper), **credit rating agencies**, and basic **market and commerce concepts** (buyer's market, MRP, accounting ratios, contract conditions). It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@pyq U.P. R.O./A.R.O. (Pre) 2023
Given below are two statements. One is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other as Reason (R) :
Assertion (A) : Accounting is the language of business.
Reason (R) : Accounting provides all information needed by a businessman.
Select the correct answer using the code given below :
Code :
(a) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).
(b) (A) is false but (R) is true.
(c) (A) is true but (R) is false.
(d) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A).
@ans (a)

A **buyer's market** has **supply above demand**, so prices fall; a seller's market is the reverse. Perishable vegetables need a **very short-period market**. When total product is constant, **marginal product is zero**. **Shadow prices** for public projects came from **Jan Tinbergen**. The **P/V (profit-volume) ratio** improves by raising prices, changing the sales mix or cutting variable cost.
