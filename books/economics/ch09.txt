@chapter 9 | Economic Planning and NITI Aayog

@intro

It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@table

The **Gadgil formula** (1969) shared Central plan assistance among states, giving the most weight to **population** (60%); the **Gadgil-Mukherjee** revision (1991) kept population at 60%. Gandhi favoured a decentralised, village-based economy and **opposed central planning**.

(d) 1990-91

@end


The **rolling plan** idea came from **Gunnar Myrdal** (*Indian Economic Planning in its Broader Setting*). It was used by the **Janata government** from **1978** to 1980, after it ended the Fifth Plan a year early.
