@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2016

(a) Marginal farmers
(b) Landless agricultural labourers
(c) Scheduled Castes/Scheduled Tribes
(d) Persons living in semi-urban areas
@ans (d)
@end


Cities differ from villages in **values, household composition, way of life and economic activity**. Urban growth shows up as a rise in the **urban population, the number of towns**, total population and urban income. A rural community is marked by **we-feeling, territory and self-sufficiency**, not cultural diversity. Causes of urbanization are **rural-urban migration, industrialisation and city facilities** (schools, jobs); a high rural standard of living would slow it.
