@section 26.3 | Migration and Urban Society
- Correct: northern cities' skewed sex ratio, scattered eastern cities, fewer rural migrants in western cities (1, 2, 4) {2010}
- Correct: 1, 2 and 3; urbanisation = rural-to-urban migration; the **Indus Project** was India-**USA**, not Canada {2013}
- Rural-to-urban migration: **population implosion** (Philip M. Hauser) {2003}
- Migration theory: **Lee** (Everett Lee, push-pull) {2017}
- Cities expand as village-city flow rises (A and R true, R explains A) {1998}
- Largest stream is **rural to rural**, not rural to urban; it is mostly women (A false, R true) {2017}
- **Urban corridors**: **expanding urban functions** {2009}

@section 26.4 | Urban Problems
- Crime rose with urbanisation and industry; family breakdown explains it (A and R true, R explains A) {2002}
- Rapid urbanisation brings **slums, crowding, falling services** (1, 2, 3); not fast infrastructure {2001}
- Correct: concrete coasts, a big city between Mumbai and Ahmedabad, slums and pollution (2, 3, 4); Maharashtra is not the most urbanised {2008}
- Urban poverty is rooted in rural areas with low literacy (A and R true, R explains A) {2010}
- Largest slum population among options: **Delhi** {2011}
- Slum population order: **Maharashtra, AP, West Bengal, UP** {2009}
- Most slum-reporting cities: **Tamil Nadu** {2007}
