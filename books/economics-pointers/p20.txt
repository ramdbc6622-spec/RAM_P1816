@flow From GATT to WTO
GATT 1947 | General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade
Uruguay Round 1986-94 | Arthur Dunkel draft; added services, TRIPS, farm trade
WTO 1 January 1995 | Geneva; now 166 members
Doha 2001 | Doha Development Agenda (Qatar)
@end

@table

@end
