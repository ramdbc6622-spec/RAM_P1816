@section 10.1 | Money and Currency
- Broad money (M3): **currency, demand deposits, time deposits** (all) {2008}
- Money multiplier: **broad money ÷ reserve money** {2008, 2009}
- **Black money**: illegal income on which tax is unpaid {2002, 2003}
- Not a demonetisation aim: **raising GDP growth** {2017}
- Paper currency began: **1862** (per key; Act 1861) {2011}
- Decimal coinage: **1957** {1995, 2005, 2010}
- RBI can print notes up to **Rs. 10,000** {2014}
- Minimum gold stock: **Rs. 115 crore** {2008}
- Note issue: **minimum reserve system** {2004, 2007, 2010}
- Coins issued by the **Ministry of Finance** {2010}
- Mints: **Mumbai, Kolkata, Hyderabad** (and Noida) {2008}

@table
Measure | Includes
M0 | Currency + bank deposits with RBI
M1 | Currency with public + demand deposits
M2 | M1 + post office savings
M3 | M1 + time deposits (broad money)
@end

@section 10.2 | Inflation
- Fall in inflation rate: prices rise **more slowly** (II only) {2017}
- Inflation benefits **debtors** {1995, 2016}
- Bottleneck inflation from supply gaps (R explains A) {2021}
- **Core inflation**: headline minus **food and fuel** {2017}
- Common measure: **CPI** (WPI earlier) {2004, 2008, 2012}
- Headline inflation: **CPI (Combined)** {2021}
- CPI: covers goods and services; used by RBI (1 and 3); not compiled by OEA {2019}
- CPI-IW base shifted from 1982 to **2001** {2008}; earlier 1960 to **1982** {1990}
- Central employees' wages compensated by **CPI-IW** {2001}; DA based on **CPI** {2008}
- WPI published by the **Ministry of Commerce and Industry** {2021}
- WPI's largest weight: **manufactured products** {2008}
- WPI 2004-05 series released **14 September 2010** {2009}
- **PPI**: average change in producers' prices {2017}
- Not an anti-inflation step: **cutting interest rates** {1996}
- WPI revision group (2004): **Abhijit Sen** {2004}

@flow Price indices
CPI (Combined) | NSO; headline; RBI target
CPI-IW | Labour Bureau; DA (base 2016)
WPI | Commerce Ministry (OEA); goods only (base 2011-12)
Core | Headline − food − fuel
@end

@section 10.3 | The RBI
- RBI: apex bank, regulates money, oversees NABARD (I, II, IV); not loans to businesses {2001}
- Government's financial agent and adviser: **RBI** {2017}
- Treasury bills sold by the **RBI** {2009}
- J&K banking (then) not done by RBI (*) {2014}
- Not correct: RBI was **established in 1949** (it was **1935**; nationalised 1949) {2011}
- HQ: **Mumbai** {2010}
- Bankers' bank: **RBI** {2010}
- Forex reserves kept by the **RBI** {2012}
- Credit creation controlled by the **RBI** {2010}
- Not correct: RBI issues **all** notes (Re. 1 note by Government) {2007}
- Re. 1 note: **Finance Secretary's** signature {2004}
- Governors in order: **I.G. Patel, Manmohan Singh, C. Rangarajan, D. Subbarao** {2021}
- Never RBI Governor: **Raja J. Chelliah** {2005}
- RBI financial year: **April-March** (from 2020-21; earlier July-June) {2013}

@section 10.4 | Monetary Tools
- Not an RBI function: **credit creation** (commercial banks do it) {2008}
- Monetary policy **complements** fiscal policy {2016}
- Not a monetary aim: **equitable distribution of income** {2011}
- Monetary policy by the **RBI** {2006}; money and credit controlled by the RBI {2010}
- Not quantitative control: **credit rationing** {2017}
- **OMO**: trading in securities {2008, 2010}; a **quantitative** tool {2017}
- **Operation Twist**: sell short-term, buy long-term to boost long-term investment (R explains A) {2016}
- Anti-inflation steps: **raise bank rate, CRR, SLR** (1, 2, 3); not buying securities {2016}
- Cash and liquid assets ratio: **SLR** {2007}
- Not set by the RBI: **PLR** {2008}
- Since reforms **both SLR and CRR reduced** {1999}
- Deposits kept with RBI: **CRR** {2013}
- CRR rise: **less liquidity** {2010}; **less credit creation** {2004}
- Repo rate: **monetary policy** {2017}
- "Repurchase option" (key): **none of the above** {1998}
- RBI's short-term lending rate: **repo** {2008}
- Banks lend to RBI at **reverse repo** {2008}
- **Bank rate**: RBI's rediscount rate {2008}; charged by RBI {2009, 2011}
- Repo-reverse repo gap (2010-11): **1%** {2010}
- 5 Dec 2019: repo **5.15%**, reverse repo **4.9%** {2014, 2019}
- Repo regulated by the **RBI** {2002}

@table
Tool | Meaning
CRR | Cash kept with RBI
SLR | Cash, gold, G-secs kept by banks
Repo | RBI lends to banks
Reverse repo / SDF | Banks park money with RBI
Bank rate | RBI rediscounts bills
OMO | Buy or sell G-secs
@end
