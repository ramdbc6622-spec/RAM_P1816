@section 18.1 | Trade Concepts
- **Invisible export**: export of **services** {2006, 2007}
- **Entrepot trade**: goods **imported for export** {2002}
- A **tariff** **reduces the volume of trade** {2017}
- **Duty drawback**: **refund of import duty** to exporters {2011}
- Slow growth of exports: **all of the above** causes {2017}

@table
Term | Meaning
Invisible exports | Services (IT, tourism, shipping) and remittances
Entrepot trade | Imports for re-export (Singapore, Dubai)
Duty drawback | Refund of import duty on inputs of exports
RoDTEP (2021) | Refund of embedded taxes on exports; replaced MEIS
Anti-dumping duty | On imports sold below home price; India is a top user
Super 301 | US law against countries with unfair trade barriers
@end

- **Free trade policy**: **absence of tariffs** {2005}
- FTP 2004-09 target: **1.5%** of world trade by 2009 {2007}
- High-performing exporters: **Star Trading Houses** {2011}
- Towns of Export Excellence: all four options were named (*) {2013}
