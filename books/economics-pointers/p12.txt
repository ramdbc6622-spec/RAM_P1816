@section 12.5 | Market Basics
- Market tendency leads to **consumerism** {2013}
- **Buyer's market**: supply exceeds demand {2010}
- Vegetables: **very short-term** market {2013}
- Total product constant → marginal product **zero** {2013}
- Fixed demand, more supply → price **falls** {2016}
- Businessmen profit from price rise; buyers cut needs (both true, R not the explanation) {2002, 2003}
- Profit-volume ratio improved by **all** listed means {2017}
- **Shadow prices** for public goods: **J. Tinbergen** {2010}
- Can bargain below MRP (true); MRP must be charged (false) {2025}
- Trade covers **demand and supply** (only 1) {2021}
- **Merchandise turnover-COGS/average stock, current ratio-CA/CL, acid test-liquid assets/CL, capital employed ratio-NPBIT/total assets** {2023}
- A condition is essential (true); breach gives only damages (false, it allows repudiation) {2023}
- Commerce removes exchange hurdles and delivers goods (both) {2021}
- Accounting is the language of business; gives all needed information (R explains A) {2023}
