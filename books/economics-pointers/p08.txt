- GST Council: State finance ministers as members; decides rates and thresholds (1 and 2); States cannot keep VAT {2016}
- GST in October 2020: **over Rs. 1 lakh crore** {2016}
- ITC is input-tax credit (true); available on non-taxable supplies (false) (only 1) {2023}

- **Project Saksham**: **indirect tax IT network** {2017}
