@section 23.1 | Population Density

- Highest density (2011): **Bihar** {2004, 2012, 2014, 2016, 2017, 2023}
- Order: **Bihar, West Bengal, Kerala, Uttar Pradesh** {2009, 2010, 2019, 2023}
- Densest BIMARU state: **Bihar** {2000}
- Lowest density: **Arunachal Pradesh** {2001, 2021}
- Ascending (density): **Odisha, AP, Tripura, Punjab** {2003}
- Lowest-density order (2001): **Arunachal, Mizoram, Sikkim, Nagaland** {2006}
- Sparsest in 1991 options: **Jammu and Kashmir** {1991}

@section 23.2 | Literacy in India
- **Effective literacy**: population aged **7 and above** {2014}
- Biggest rise in literacy: **1991-2001** {2006}
- Literacy 2011 (provisional): **74.04%** {2004, 2012}
- Most literate district (provisional 2011): **Serchhip** (Mizoram) {2010}
- First fully literate district outside Kerala: **Bardhaman** (West Bengal) {2008}
- Biggest literacy rise 2001-11: **Bihar** {2011, 2016}
- Highest literacy among options: **Manipur** {2017}
- Second in female literacy after Kerala: **Mizoram** {2014}
- Second in literacy and in urbanisation: **Mizoram** {2017}
- Highest literacy among options (2001): **Mizoram**; top in the North-East {2004, 2006, 2008}
- 2001 order: **Kerala, Himachal, Tamil Nadu, Manipur** {2006}
- Order: **Kerala, Mizoram, Goa, Maharashtra** {2002}
- Lowest four (ascending, 2011): **Bihar, Arunachal, Rajasthan, Jharkhand** {2010}
- 2001 ascending: **Bihar, UP, Rajasthan, MP** {2002}
- Highest UT literacy (2001): **Lakshadweep** {2013}
- 2011 order: **Tripura, Himachal, Sikkim, Nagaland** {2010}
- Male-female gap (2011): **16.68** points {2010}
- Female literacy (2011): about **65.5%** {2011}
