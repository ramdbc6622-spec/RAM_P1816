@section 13.1 | Ideas of Development
- Three stages of social development: **Auguste Comte** {2021}
- Physical quality of life: **infant mortality and literacy** {2004}
- **PQLI**: **Morris D. Morris** {2019}
- First to stress the human side of development: **UNDP** {2013}

@section 13.2 | The HDI
- Developed by **Mahbub ul Haq**; first by **UNDP** {2006, 2018}
- First HDR 1990 with HDI (true); led by Haq and Priesner (false) (only 1) {2025}
- Life expectancy goalposts: 85 and 25 then; **85 and 20** now (*) {2006}
- Not in HDI (2004): social inequality; adult literacy also dropped since 2010 (*) {2004}
- Not used: **social inequality** {2020}
- HDI indicators: **income, education, life expectancy** (1, 2, 4) {2024}
- Not a constituent: **health and nutrition** {2008, 2009}
- Not in 1990 HDI: **urbanisation** {2013}
- HDI and SDG ranks of States go together; SDGs stress health and education (R explains A) {2019, 2024}

@flow How HDI is built
Health | Life expectancy at birth
Knowledge | Mean and expected years of schooling
Living standard | GNI per capita (PPP)
HDI | Geometric mean of the three (since 2010)
@end
