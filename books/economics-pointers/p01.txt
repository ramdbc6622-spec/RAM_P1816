@section 1.1 | Nature of the Economy
- Mixed economy: **public and private sectors co-exist** {1990, 1999, 2006, 2013}
- India: **mixed economy** {1990, 2006, 2013, 2016}
- India underdeveloped and mixed (true); Keynes "father of economics" (false; Adam Smith) {2024}
- Underdeveloped economy: **low per capita income, low capital formation** {2017}
- Developing signs: **agriculture, disguised unemployment, poor human capital** {2017}
- India: a **developing** economy {2011}
- Features: **agriculture dominant, low per capita income, mass unemployment** {2004}
- Not a feature: **lack of natural resources** {2004}
- Not a second-generation reform: **legal system reforms** {2009}
- Indian model serves **state and person both** {2013}
- Not an economic activity: **voluntary social service** {2023}

@section 1.2 | Growth vs Development
- Prices and money income both double: real income **unchanged** {2004}
- Amartya Sen: **people-oriented**, security for the poorest, safeguards on global integration {1999}
- "Plan for people, not commodities": **Amartya Sen** {2004}
- Hindu rate of growth: **national income** growth {1996, 2004, 2006}
- Labour's GNP share falls because **wages lag prices** {2008}
- **Development-constructive change, growth-GDP, sustainable-environment, quality of life-health** {2010}
- 1991 NEP strategy: **all** (LPG) {2015}
- Not a growth factor: **technocrats and bureaucrats** {2021}
- Best growth measure: **per capita product** {2013}
- Development = better living; per capita income alone not enough (R explains A) {2024}
- Standard of living: **per capita income** {2013}

@table
Term | Meaning
GDP | Output within the country
GNP | GDP + net factor income from abroad
NNP | GNP - depreciation
National income | NNP at factor cost
Per capita income | National income ÷ population
@end

@section 1.5 | Savings
- Capital formation depends on savings and institutions; investment essential (both) {2021}
- Slow per capita growth: **population, prices, slow farm and industry** (1, 2, 3) {2001}
- Highest savings rate of the 2000s: **2007-08** {2009}
- Savings ratio 2006-07: **above 32%** of GDP {2008}
- Biggest saver: **household sector** {2001, 2004}
- Household savings mostly in **physical assets** {2009}
- Savings order: **household, private corporate, public enterprises, government** {1998}
