@section 16.1 | What Poverty Is
- Day for the Eradication of Poverty theme 2022-23: **"Dignity for all in practice"** {2023}
- Types of poverty: **absolute, relative, subjective** (not "functional") {2023}
- UN defined **absolute poverty** in **1995** (Copenhagen Summit) {2017}
- Poverty line differs across time and countries because basic needs vary (A and R true, R explains A) {2025}
- Not a social indicator of poverty: **size of the house** {2025}
- "The Challenge of World Poverty": **Gunnar Myrdal** {2023}

@section 16.2 | The Poverty Line
- Methods used: **head count ratio, calorie intake, household consumption expenditure** (not per capita income) {2020}
- Poverty level is fixed on **household consumption expenditure** {2009, 2013}
- Defined in terms of **calorie intake** {2008}
- Line based only on nutrition: **Alagh** Committee (1979) {2020}
- Calories: **2,100 urban** and **2,400 rural** per person a day {2001, 2008}
- New BPL standards committee: **Suresh Tendulkar** {2013}

- Lines used for estimates (then): **Lakdawala** Committee {2013}
- Urban BPL identification: **S.R. Hashim** Committee {2018}

- Fixes the poverty line: **Planning Commission** (now **NITI Aayog**); NITI is the nodal agency {2014, 2018}
- **Lorenz curve** measures **income inequality** {2018}
- **Cultural poverty**: **Oscar Lewis**; **vicious circle of poverty**: **Nurkse** {2020, 2014}
- BPL people as an identified group helps **targeting** (A and R true, R explains A) {1998}

@section 16.4 | Multidimensional Poverty
- UNDP poverty categories: **both** income poverty and human poverty {2022}
- **Human Poverty Index**: HDR **1997**; replaced by **MPI** in **2010** {2004, 2020}
- Global MPI report: **OPHI and UNDP** (3 and 4) {2024}
- MPI in the HDR every year since **2010** is correct; it does not measure only BPL people (only 1) {2025}
- MPI has **10 indicators** {2010}
- MPI dimensions: **health, education, living standard** {2019}
- Best index for the **intensity** of poverty: **MPI** {2015}
- India's MPI-poor share (HDR 2013): **53.7%** {2014}
- Not a cause of poverty: **rising rate of investment** {2013}
- Not a social indicator of poverty: **fewer means of transport** {2017}
- Indian social structure: **(i), (ii), (iii)**; poverty is not a structural feature {2014}

@flow How the MPI is built
3 dimensions | Health, education, living standard
10 indicators | Nutrition, child mortality, schooling, attendance, 6 living-standard items
Deprivation cut-off | Poor if deprived in one-third or more of weighted indicators
MPI | Headcount (H) x intensity (A)
@end

@section 16.5 | Unemployment
- Educated unemployment rises with fast growth when professional education is lacking (A and R true, R explains A, per key) {2005, 2006}
- Urban poverty is rooted in rural areas; low rural education (A and R true, R explains A) {2013}
- **Disguised unemployment**: more people on a job than needed {2004, 2008, 2010}
- Found mainly in the **primary (agricultural)** sector, in **rural** areas (I and II) {2004, 2006, 2017}
- Unemployed for the major part of the year: **usual status** unemployment {2019}
- Not an NSSO measure: **current monthly status** {2005, 2016}
- Unemployment data collected and published by **NSSO** {2014}
- Not a cause of unemployment: **growth of per capita income** {2014}
- Rate between Jul-Sep 2019 and Jul-Sep 2022: key says **increased**; PLFS urban data show about 8% to about 7%, so treat with care (*) {2024}

@end
