- Order: **fiscal, revenue, primary** {2002, 2003, 2004, 2008}
- Fiscal deficit minus interest: **primary deficit** {2004, 2008, 2010}
- Primary deficit 2010-11: **below 2%** of GDP {2009}
- Revenue deficit minus capital-asset grants: **effective revenue deficit** {2015}
- Fiscal deficit mostly met by **domestic borrowing** {2005}
- Deficit financing causes inflation by raising money supply (R explains A) {2003}
- Failed deficit financing: **inflation**; raises **money supply** {1993, 2016}
- **FRBM Act**: **2003** {2008}

@table
Deficit | Formula
Revenue | Revenue spending − revenue receipts
Effective revenue | Revenue deficit − capital-asset grants
Fiscal | Total spending − (revenue + non-debt capital receipts)
Primary | Fiscal deficit − interest
@end
