@section 17.3 | Balance of Trade and Payments
- Trade deficit peaked 2012-13, then fell as POL and non-POL deficits fell (A and R true, R explains A) {2017}
- Balance of trade includes **goods** {2016}
- Net exports = **exports minus imports** {2017}
- Balance of payments relates to **exports and imports** {2012}
- BoP includes **all of the above** {2009}
- BoP = complete record of all economic transactions of residents with the **rest of the world** {1994}
- CAD definition is true; CAD comes from **importing** more, not exporting more (A true, R false) {2016}

@flow Balance of payments
Balance of trade | Goods exports minus goods imports
+ Invisibles | Services, income, transfers (remittances)
= Current account | Deficit (CAD) when payments exceed receipts
+ Capital account | FDI, FPI, loans, NRI deposits
= Overall BoP | Settled by change in forex reserves
@end
