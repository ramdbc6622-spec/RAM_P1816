@section 22.1 | Population Theories
- Population control is needed for the environment; growth does **not** keep balance (A true, R false) {2023}
- Natural growth = **crude birth rate and crude death rate** (A and B) {2018}
- Gradual long-run change in growth pattern: **demographic transition** {2016}
- Stable population: **constant birth and death rates** {2014}
- India's phase now: **high growth with definite signs of slowing** {2015}
- Malthus's best check: **birth control** (per key; Malthus stressed moral restraint) {2017}
- Malthusian theory: about **population** {2022}
- Population **geometric**, food **arithmetic** {2024}
- **Optimum-Cannan, social maladjustment-Henry George, demographic transition-Thompson, food supply-Malthus** {2023}
- Not a demographic feature: **standard of living** {2015}

@flow Demographic transition
Stage 1 | High birth, high death; slow growth
Stage 2 | Death rate falls; population explodes
Stage 3 | Birth rate falls; growth slows (India now)
Stage 4 | Low birth, low death; stable
@end
