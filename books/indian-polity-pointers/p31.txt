@chapter 31 | The Planning Commission / NITI Aayog
@meta Built from 17 UPPSC questions (1991–2023). Focus: extra-constitutional status, PM as chairman, NDC, NITI Aayog structure.

@hot
- **Planning Commission** is **not** a constitutional body; set up by a **Cabinet resolution** (1950) {1999, 2003, 2004, 2008, 2010, 2013, 2013, 2014}
- **NITI Aayog** = extra-constitutional; set up by Cabinet resolution (1 Jan 2015) {2015, 2016, 2018}
- **NDC** (1952) approved Five-Year Plans; linked Commission and States {2002, 2005, 2010, 2012, 2016}
@end

@section 31.1 | Status
- Planning Commission set up by a **Cabinet resolution** {1999}
- Not constitutional: **Planning Commission** {2003, 2004, 2008, 2010, 2013, 2013, 2014}
- Extra-constitutional: **NITI Aayog** {2015, 2018}

@section 31.2 | Planning Commission
- Chairman: **Prime Minister** {2006}
- Deputy Chairman: rank of **Cabinet Minister** {1991, 1994}
- Never Deputy Chairman: **P. Chidambaram** {2015}
- "Economic cabinet" for Union and States; makes Plans (both true, R not the explanation) {2010}

@section 31.3 | NDC
- Formed **6 August 1952** {2016}
- Approves **Five-Year Plans** {2002}
- Coordinator between the Commission and States: **NDC** {2012}
- Not true: Planning Commission was the **highest** body for plans (NDC was) {2004}
- NDC discusses progress, suggests ways, gives guidelines; **not** money allocation (1, 2, 3) {2005, 2010}

@section 31.4 | NITI Aayog
- Planning Commission abolished by PM **Narendra Modi** {2015}
- NITI set up by a **Union Cabinet resolution** {2016}
- Not correct: NITI has a **full-time chairman** (PM is chairperson) {2015}
- Not in Governing Council: **Governors** (CMs and LGs are) {2023}

@table

@trap
- "Economic and social planning" is a **Concurrent List** subject, but the bodies are **executive**.
- NITI has **full-time members** but no full-time chairman.
- Governing Council: **CMs and LGs**, not Governors.
@end

@up UP's own **State Planning Institute** and the UP CM sits on NITI Aayog's **Governing Council**. UP launched its State-level think tank on NITI lines (State Transformation Commission).
