@section 9.1 | National Flag
- True: adopted **22 July 1947**; false: 21 spokes (it is **24**), ratio 3:4 (it is **2:3**) {2004}
- Adopted by the Constituent Assembly: **22 July 1947** {1990, 2020}

@section 9.2 | National Calendar
- Based on **Saka** era (not Vikram), adopted **22 March 1957** (not 26 January 1950): neither statement true {2019}
- First month: **Chaitra** {2016}

@table

@trap
- Spokes **24**, not 21; ratio **2:3**, not 3:4.
- Calendar: **Saka**, not Vikram; **1957**, not 1950.
- Saka year = Gregorian minus **78** (from 22 March).
@end

@up The National Emblem comes from the **Sarnath** Lion Capital (Varanasi, UP). The **Ganges river dolphin**, the national aquatic animal, is UP's state aquatic animal too.
