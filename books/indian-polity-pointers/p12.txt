@section 12.1 | Origin and Nature
- **Nehru Report (1928)** advocated FRs {2009}
- "A pledge to our people and a pact with the civilised world": **Dr. S. Radhakrishnan** {2021}
- Rights are **claims of citizens against the State** {2017}
- FRs guard against State action, are in Part III, aim at justice (1, 2, 3 correct) {1998}
- Incorrect: right to die is an FR, Constitution wholly federal, FRs can be abrogated (B, C, D) {2023}

- **Arrest and detention-Art 23** is wrong (it is **Art 22**) {2016}
- **Titles-18, religious affairs-26, minority language-29, education-21A** {2019}

@section 12.3 | Article 13 and Basic Structure
- Art 13 secures the Constitution's paramountcy for **FRs** {1999}
- Basic structure doctrine: **Kesavananda Bharati** {2013, 2015}
- **Duties-42nd Amendment, Parliament can amend FRs-Kesavananda, basic structure-Minerva Mills, trafficking-Art 23** {2007}

@flow Amending FRs: the case trail
Shankari Prasad 1951 | Parliament can amend FRs
Golaknath 1967 | Parliament cannot amend FRs
24th Amendment 1971 | Undid Golaknath
Kesavananda 1973 | Can amend, not the basic structure
Minerva Mills 1980 | Reaffirmed; FR-DPSP balance
@end

@section 12.4 | Emergency
- FRs **can be suspended** during Emergency {2002}
- Not suspendable: **right to life and personal liberty** (Arts 20, 21; 44th Amendment) {2008}

@section 12.5 | Right to Equality
- Five Articles: **14 to 18** {2014, 2015}
- Equality from **Arts 14, 15, 16** (not Art 13) {2009}
- Not included: **economic equality** {2008}
- Odd one out: **Art 19** (freedom group) or Art 14 (all persons) (*) {2000}
- State may treat unequals differently; equal law among equals (R explains A) {2016}

@section 12.6 | Article 14 and Equal Pay
- Absolute limit on legislative power: **Art 14** {1999}
- Equality before law: **Art 14** {2015}
- Equal pay for equal work deduced from **Arts 14, 16 + Preamble ("socialist")** {1999, 2001, 2002}

@section 12.7 | Articles 15 and 16
- **Champakam Dorairajan** (1951) led to amending the **right against discrimination** (Art 15(4), 1st Amendment) {1997}
- Equal opportunity in public employment: **Art 16(1) and 16(2)** {2009}
- "Descent, residence" in non-discrimination in State jobs: **Art 16(2)** {2023}

@section 12.8 | Articles 17 and 18
- Untouchability abolished: **Art 17** {1994, 2003, 2013, 2015, 2017}
- Achieved by **laws, education, awareness** (1, 2, 3) {1992}
- Untouchability is structural violence rooted in religious sanction (R explains A) {2019}

@section 12.9 | Article 19
- Right to Freedom: **Arts 19-22** {2016}
- Not under Art 19 now: **right to property** (19(1)(f) deleted) {2024}
- Freedom of the **press**: **Art 19(1)(a)** {2001, 2004}

@section 12.10 | Articles 20 and 22
- No self-incrimination: **Art 20(3)** {2004}
- Double jeopardy: **Art 20** {2010}
- Not in Art 20: **prohibition against torture** (from Art 21) {2023}
- Rights of arrested persons: **Art 22** {2013}
- Preventive detention without Advisory Board: **3 months** {2009, 2013}

@section 12.11 | Articles 21 and 21A
- Due process (as read now): **Art 21** {2014}
- Travel abroad: **Art 21** {1997}
- Recognised FRs: **shelter, travel abroad, equal pay** {1998}
- Under 19(1)(d) with 21: **right to privacy** {2003, 2004}
- Not under Art 21: **capital punishment** {2017}
- Education for 6-14: a **Fundamental Right** (Art 21A, 86th Amendment, 2002) {2006}
- Free education 6-14; education key to development (R explains A) {2016}

@section 12.12 | Against Exploitation
- Covers **trafficking and forced labour, child labour in factories** (2, 3) {2024}
- Child exploitation: **Art 24**; children in factories: **Art 24** {2005, 2009, 2011, 2017, ?}
- **Bonded Labour** abolished by an Act of **1976** {2001, 2010}
- Child labour in hazardous work banned by all listed (Constitution and UN) {2002, 2003}

@section 12.13 | Freedom of Religion
- Includes **propagation, Sikhs' kirpans, social-reform laws**; not conversion by bodies {2001}
- **Art 25**: freedom of religion {1996}
- "Hindu" in Art 25 excludes **Parsis** (includes Sikh, Jain, Buddhist) {2014}

@section 12.14 | Cultural and Educational
- **Art 29-minority educational institutions** is wrong (that is **Art 30**) {2009, 2015}
- Minorities' right to run educational institutions: **Art 30** {1997, 2017}
- "Minorities" not defined; only religious and linguistic recognised (both true, R not the explanation) {2019}

@section 12.15 | Article 32
- Guardian of FRs: **judiciary**; **Supreme Court** {2010, 2012, 2015, 1992, 2004, 2015}
- Not correct: **President** is guardian of FRs {2013}
- Art 32 enforces **FRs** only {1997}
- "Heart and soul": **right to constitutional remedies**; Art 32 gives a remedy (R explains A) {2004, 2010, 2010, 2016}

@section 12.16 | Writs
- Greatest safeguard of liberty: **habeas corpus**; issued by High Court for liberty {2015, 2015}
- **Prohibition-"to be certified"** is wrong (that is certiorari) {2019}
- Order to perform duty: **mandamus** {2001, 2007}

@table
Writ | Meaning
Habeas corpus | "Have the body"; against unlawful detention
Mandamus | "We command"; perform public duty
Prohibition | "To forbid"; stops a lower court midway
Certiorari | "To be certified"; quashes an order made
Quo warranto | "By what authority"; illegal holding of office
@end

@section 12.17 | Right to Property
- Omitted by the **44th Amendment**; removed from FRs {2004, 2004, 2010, 2013, 2013, 2015, 2007, 2014}
- Now a **constitutional legal right** (Art 300A) {1992, 1996}

@section 12.18 | Citizens vs All Persons
- Not for foreigners: **freedom of expression** {2007, 2015}
- For all persons: **equal protection of laws** {2010}
- Not for all persons: **non-discrimination, free movement, contesting elections** (2, 3, 4) {2002}
