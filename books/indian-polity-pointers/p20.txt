@chapter 20 | The Parliament II: Rajya Sabha
@meta Built from 28 UPPSC questions (1990–2025). Focus: permanent House, 6-year term, state seat numbers, Art 249 and Art 312 powers, money-bill limits, Chairman.

@hot
- Rajya Sabha is **permanent**, never dissolved; **one-third retire every 2 years** {2011, 2012, 2014, 2016}
- **Exclusive** RS power: create new **All India Services** (Art 312) {1997, 2018, 2025}
- **Art 249**: Parliament legislates on a State subject after an RS resolution {2011, 2012, 2017}
- State seat matching: Maharashtra 19, TN 18, Bihar 16, Karnataka 12, Gujarat 11, Rajasthan, Odisha 10, Kerala 9, Punjab 7 {2017, 2021, 2025}
- Presiding officer who is **not a member**: VP as RS Chairman {1992, 2010, 2011, 2013}
@end

@section 20.1 | Composition (Art 80)
- Elected by **elected MLAs** of States {1990}
- President nominates **12** {2006}

@section 20.2 | Term and Age
- True: **not dissolved**, **one-third retire every 2 years**; not 5 years, not 25 years (1 and 3) {2014}
- Not dissolved because one-third retire every two years (R explains A) {2016}
- Member's term: **6 years** (RPA 1951) {1992}
- RS is **not subject to dissolution** {2011}; dissolved by **none** {2012}
- Minimum age: **30** {2006}
- First constituted: **1952** (3 April) {2017}

@section 20.3 | State Seats (Fourth Schedule)
- Equal then: undivided AP and TN (18) (*) {1999}
- UP's 34 then = AP 18 + MP 16 (*); UP now **31** {2000, 2002}
- **Maharashtra 19, Karnataka 12, Bihar 16, Tamil Nadu 18** {2025}
- **Rajasthan 10, Gujarat 11, Karnataka 12, Punjab 7** {2021}
- **Gujarat 11, Karnataka 12, Kerala 9, Odisha 10** {2017}
- Fewest elected members among options: **Himachal Pradesh** (3) {2006, 2010}
- UTs represented in **both Houses** (as a class) {2015}

@section 20.4 | Special Powers
- Wrong: **only the RS** makes laws on State List matters (Parliament does) {2011}
- Legislate on a State subject: **Art 249** {2012, 2017}
- Exclusive: **recommend a new All India Service** {1997, 2018}
- RS can resolve for a new AIS (true); needs two-thirds of **total** (false, it is of present and voting) (only 1) {2025}

@flow Rajya Sabha-only powers
Art 249 | Two-thirds present and voting; Parliament may legislate on a State subject (1 year)
Art 312 | Two-thirds present and voting; new All India Service
Art 67(b) | VP removal starts only in RS
Art 352/356 | Keeps Emergency alive when LS is dissolved
@end

@section 20.5 | Limits
- LS need not accept RS recommendations on Money Bills; **RS** has 14 days (A false); Money Bill not introduced in RS (R true) {2017}
- Cannot start in RS: **Money Bill** {2021}
- LS-only: **Money Bill introduction, rejecting or amending it, CoM accountability**; not AIS (I, II, III) {2004}

@section 20.6 | Chairman
- Chairman: **Vice-President**, ex officio {2011, 2013}
- Presiding officer not a member: **Rajya Sabha** {1992, 2010}

@trap
- RS term **6 years** comes from the **RPA 1951**, not the Constitution.
- Art 312 and 249: two-thirds of members **present and voting**.
- Many UTs (Ladakh, Chandigarh, A&N) have **no RS seat**.
@end

@up UP sends **31** members, the most of any State; they are elected by UP's **403** MLAs by single transferable vote. UP's 34 seats fell to 31 when Uttarakhand (3 seats) was formed in 2000.

@last
- Max **250** (238 + 12 nominated); now 245
- Elected by **elected MLAs**; STV
- Permanent; **1/3** retire every 2 years; term **6 years**
- Minimum age **30**; first constituted **1952**
- UP **31**; Maharashtra 19; TN 18
- **Art 249** State subject; **Art 312** new AIS
- Money Bill never starts in RS; 14 days for RS
- Chairman = **VP**, not a member
- Deputy Chairman presides when VP acts as President
- Only some UTs have RS seats
@end
