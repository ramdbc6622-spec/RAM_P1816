@chapter 25 | The Governor
@meta Built from 26 UPPSC questions (1992–2024). Focus: appointment and pleasure tenure, qualifications, oath, discretion, Arts 200 and 213, immunity, first woman Governor.

@hot
- Governor holds office at the **pleasure of the President**; no impeachment {1992, 1997, 2004, 2004, 2004, 2009, 2010}
- Appointed by the **President** (Art 155) {2012, 2015}
- Oath given by the **Chief Justice of the High Court** {2013, 2014}
- Ordinance: **Art 213**; reserve Bill for President: **Art 200** {2004, 2016, 2017}
- One person can be Governor of **two or more States** (7th Amendment) {1996, 2016, 2024}
@end

@section 25.1 | Appointed, Not Elected
- Elected Governor dropped: **another election, rivalry with the CM, parliamentary system** (1, 3, 4) {2002, 2003}

@section 25.2 | Article Map
- **Art 155-removal** is wrong (155 = appointment; tenure is Art 156) {2024}
- Same person can be Governor of two States (A false); Art 153 says a Governor for each State (R true) {2024}
- Two-State Governor's pay shared as the **President** decides {2016}

@section 25.3 | Appointment and Qualifications
- Appointed by the **President**; under **Art 155** {2012, 2015}
- True: **35 years**; can govern two or more States (2 and 4); no "by birth", no LS-eligibility test {1996}
- Oath by the **High Court Chief Justice** {2013, 2014}

@section 25.4 | Term and Removal
- Appointed by President, at his pleasure, head of State executive, normally 5 years (all four) {2004, 2010}
- Not true: removed by the **State Legislature** {1997, 2004}
- Responsible to the **President** {1992}
- In office as long as the President wishes: **Governor** {2004}
- No impeachment provision: **Governor** {2009}
- Rajasthan Governor dismissed (1981): **Raghukul Tilak** {2003}

@section 25.5 | Discretion (Art 163)
- Acts on his own: **testing majority, dismissing CM, reserving a Bill, returning a Bill** (not HC consultation) {2006}

@flow Governor and a State Bill (Art 200)
Assent | Bill becomes law
Withhold assent | Bill falls
Return (not Money Bill) | Assembly may re-pass; then must assent
Reserve for President | Art 201; mandatory if it endangers the HC
@end

@section 25.6 | Bills and Ordinances
- Reserve a Bill for the President: **Art 200** {2004}
- Ordinance: **Art 213**; approved by the **State Legislature** {2016, 2017, 2008}

@section 25.7 | Legislative and Judicial Powers
- True: does **not appoint HC judges**; **nominates MLCs** (1 and 3); he is part of the Legislature and has pardon powers {2017}
- Not true: Governor can **pardon a death sentence** (only commute/suspend) {2005}

@section 25.8 | Immunity (Art 361)
- "Cannot be sued for any act" false; President impeached, Governors dismissed (A false, R true) {2004}
- Not true: courts can inquire into **ministers' advice** to the Governor (barred by Art 163(3)) {2016}

@section 25.9 | J&K and UTs
- Sadar-e-Riyasat renamed Governor (1965): **6th Amendment of the J&K Constitution** {2007}
- UTs administered by the **President** {1995}

@section 25.10 | Firsts
- First woman Governor: **Sarojini Naidu** (UP, 1947) {2013}
- 13 February (her birthday) is **National Women's Day** {2002}

@trap
- President's oath from **CJI**; Governor's from the **HC Chief Justice**.
- Governor can't pardon a **death** sentence; President can (Art 72).
- **Art 200** = Governor reserves; **Art 201** = President decides.
@end

@up **Sarojini Naidu**, India's first woman Governor, governed the **United Provinces** (1947-49) and died in office at Lucknow. The UP Governor is chancellor of State universities and nominates **10** UP MLCs.
