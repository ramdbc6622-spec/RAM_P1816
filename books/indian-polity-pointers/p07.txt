@chapter 7 | The Preamble
@meta Built from 27 UPPSC questions (1991–2025). Focus: 42nd Amendment words, order of words, types of justice and liberty, legal nature, cases, "soul".

@hot
- **42nd Amendment (1976)** added **Socialist, Secular, Integrity**; Sovereign and Republic were original {1991, 2006, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2010, 2013, 2017, 2025}
- Preamble = "**soul**" of the Constitution {2006, 2008, 2008, 2013, 2015}
- **Justice**: social, economic, political (3); **no** religious justice; **no** economic freedom under Liberty {2008, 2016, 2018, 2021, ?}
- **Kesavananda Bharati** (1973): Preamble **is part** of the Constitution; Berubari (1960) said not {2014, 2017}
- **Non-justiciable**, can be amended, cannot override specific provisions {2004, 2009, 2019}
@end

@section 7.1 | Text and Purpose
- Preamble ideals elaborated in the **DPSP and FRs** {2002, 2004}
- Constitution's objectives reflected in the **Preamble** {2013}

@section 7.2 | Original Text and 42nd Amendment
- Status on 26 January 1950: **Sovereign Democratic Republic** {2008, 2009}
- Absent in 1949: **Socialist, Integrity, Secular** (Republic was present) {2009}
- Not in the Preamble in 1975: **Integrity** {2006}
- **Secular and Socialist** added by the **42nd Amendment** {2008, 2017}; Secular by the 42nd {1991, 2010, 2013}
- Inserted by the 42nd: **Socialist and Secular** (not Sovereign, Republic) {2025}
- Added: **Socialist and Secular**; "Gram Swarajya" is not in the Preamble {2010}
- Balram Singh case (Nov 2024): SC upheld the insertion of Socialist and Secular

@section 7.3 | Order and Absent Words
- Order: **Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic (Republic)** {2023}
- Not in the Preamble: **Federal** {2021}

@flow Preamble at a glance
Source | We, the people of India
Nature | Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic
Justice (3) | Social, economic, political
Liberty (5) | Thought, expression, belief, faith, worship
Equality (2) | Status and opportunity
Fraternity (1) | Dignity, unity and integrity
Date | 26 November 1949
@end

@section 7.4 | Secular
- "Secular" in India: **no state religion** {2005}
- India described as Secular in the **Preamble** {2005}

@section 7.5 | Four Objectives
- Justice, liberty, equality, fraternity: **3, 5, 2, 1** {2018}
- Types of justice: **three** {2016}: **social, economic, political** {2021}
- Not mentioned: **religious justice** {?}
- Liberty not mentioned: **economic freedom** {2008}
- Goals: justice, liberty of thought, equality of opportunity, dignity: **all** {1998}
- **Social equality** is guaranteed {2008}

@section 7.6 | Legal Nature
- True: came from the **Objectives Resolution**, **non-justiciable**, **cannot override** provisions; false: cannot be amended {2009}
- Legal nature: **not enforceable** {2019}
- Not enforceable, useful, states aims, helps interpretation: all true (*) {2004}

@section 7.7 | Cases
- Preamble is **part** of the Constitution: **Kesavananda Bharati** {2014, 2017}
- Preamble (secularism) part of basic structure: **S.R. Bommai** (1994) {2012}

@table
Case | Holding
Berubari (1960) | Preamble **not** part of Constitution
Kesavananda Bharati (1973) | Preamble **is** part; can be amended, not basic structure
S.R. Bommai (1994) | Secularism part of basic structure
LIC v. Consumer Education (1995) | Preamble integral part
Balram Singh (2024) | 42nd Amendment words upheld
@end

@section 7.8 | Descriptions
- "**Soul**" of the Constitution: the **Preamble** {2006, 2008, 2008, 2013, 2015}

@trap
- **Economic justice** is in; **economic freedom** is not. **Religious justice** is not; faith and worship are under Liberty.
- **Berubari** = not part; **Kesavananda** = part.
- Ambedkar's "heart and soul" = **Article 32**, not the Preamble.
@end
