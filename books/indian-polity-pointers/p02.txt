@section 2.1 | Idea and First Acceptance
- Idea of a Constituent Assembly: **M.N. Roy** (1934) {2001, 2017}
- Proposal for an elected Assembly: **Cripps Mission** (1942) {2008, 2010}

@section 2.2 | Cabinet Mission and Composition
- Assembly set up under the **Cabinet Mission, 1946** {2009}
- Not a Cabinet Mission member: **William Wood** (members: Pethick-Lawrence, Cripps, A.V. Alexander) {2015}
- One member per **10 lakh** population {2001, 2002, 2003}
- Members elected **indirectly by Provincial Assemblies** {1993}
- Women members: **15** {2014}
- Strength: **389** originally; **299** after Partition

@section 2.3 | Interim Government, 1946
- Announced: **24 August 1946**; took office 2 September 1946 {2022}
- Not a member: **Dr. S. Radhakrishnan** {2010}

@section 2.4 | First Meeting and President
- First meeting chaired by **Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha** (temporary President) {1990, 2013}
- Permanent President: **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** (11 December 1946) {?}
- First meeting: **9 December 1946** {1990, 1995, 2011, 1990}

@section 2.5 | Objectives Resolution
- Moved by **Nehru** on 13 December 1946; adopted **22 January 1947** {2007, 1998}
- True: the Objectives Resolution shaped the Constitution; Preamble states objectives; people gave the Constitution. False: Head of State directly elected {2010}

@section 2.6 | B.N. Rau and Readings
- Constitutional Adviser: **B.N. Rau**; first draft by **B.N. Rau** {1998, 2014, 2009}
- Third reading began **17 November 1949** (some texts 14 November) (*) {2014}

@section 2.7 | Adoption and Commencement
- Completed and adopted: **26 November 1949** {2002, 2006, 2010}
- Came into force: **26 January 1950** {1990}
- Enacted by the **Constituent Assembly**; adopted and enacted 26 November 1949 {2012, 2013, 2015}
- Order: **first meeting, Drafting Committee, adoption, commencement** {2023}

@section 2.8 | Sessions and Time
- Sessions: **11** for framing; key says **12** (counting 24 Jan 1950) {2005}
- Last meeting: **24 January 1950** {2018}
- Time taken: **2 years, 11 months, 18 days** {2007}

@section 2.9 | Nature and Critics
- Constitution provides for all needs; called a "borrowed" Constitution (both true, R not the explanation) {2008}
- Founding Fathers minimised minority interests: **Ivor Jennings** {2004}
- "Assembly was Congress and Congress was India": **Granville Austin** {2013}
- Congress should be disbanded after independence: **Mahatma Gandhi** {2011}
