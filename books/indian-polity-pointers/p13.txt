@section 13.1 | Objectives
- DPSPs aim at economic and social democracy (R explains A) {2025}
- Not an objective: **a religious state** {2013, 2017}
- Welfare state: **DPSP** {2004, 2004, 2013}; in both **Preamble and DPSP** {1997}

@section 13.2 | Source and Types
- Source: **Ireland** {1994, 2005, 2008, 2015}
- Classified as **socialist, Gandhian, liberal-intellectual** (all) {2018}

@section 13.3 | Nature (Art 37)
- **Not enforceable** by courts, yet **fundamental in governance** (both true) {2023}
- DPSPs are **non-justiciable** {2004}
- "A cheque payable at the bank's convenience": **K.T. Shah** {2007}

@section 13.5 | Article 39
- Equal pay for equal work: a **DPSP** {1998, 2004, 2010}; included in DPSP {2012}
- Not an FR: **equal pay for equal work** {2014}
- Protection from exploitation for **children, women, tribals** (1, 2, 3) {2009}

@section 13.6 | Right to Work
- MGNREGA gives 100 days; right to work is **not** in Part III (A true, R false) {2013}
- MGNREGA implements **Art 43** (with 41) {2009, 2010}
- Update: MGNREGA replaced by **VB-G RAM G Act 2025** (125 days), from 1 July 2026

@section 13.7 | Education
- Not a DPSP now: free education up to 14 (moved to **Art 21A**) {2010}
- Not a DPSP: free education up to **16** (never in Constitution) {2013}
- DPSPs silent on **adult education** {2008}

- "State shall endeavour to protect the environment": a **DPSP** {2009}
