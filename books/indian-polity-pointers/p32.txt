@chapter 32 | Lokpal and Important Commissions
@meta Built from 37 UPPSC questions (1991–2024). Focus: Lokpal and UP Lokayukta, NHRC, minorities bodies, CVC, Mandal, Venkatachaliah, UPSC and State PSCs.

@hot
- **Lokpal**: Indian ombudsman; first recommended by the **ARC (1966-67, Morarji Desai)**; first Bill **1968** {1998, 2014, 2017}
- **UPSC/PSC**: constitutional; annual report to the **President**; State PSC member removed only by the **President** after SC inquiry {2010, 2013, 2014, 2020, 2021, 2022}
- **NHRC** is **statutory** (1993), not constitutional {2004, 2007, 2007, 2015}
- **Mandal Commission**: set up by **Morarji Desai** (1979), report **1980** {1991, 2008}
@end

@section 32.1 | Lokpal
- Indian ombudsman: **Lokpal** {2014}
- ARC that first proposed Lokpal and Lokayukta: headed by **Morarji Desai** {2017}
- First Lokpal Bill: **1968** {1998}
- 2011 Bill: introduced 4 Aug 2011, sent to Standing Committee, called "weak" (1, 3, 4) {2010}
- Civil society drafters: **Anna Hazare, Santosh Hegde, Shanti Bhushan** {2010}
- Selection committee: **PM, CJI (or nominee), Speaker, LoP**, eminent jurist; not RS Chairman {2024}

@section 32.2 | Lokayukta
- First Lokayukta: **Maharashtra** (1971) {2015}
- First to pass a Lokayukta Bill in 2011: **Uttarakhand** {2012}
- UP Lokayukta reports to the **Governor** {2007}
- UP Lokayukta tenure: **5 years** (or 70) per current law; 8 years in 2017 {2017}

@section 32.3 | Minorities
- "Minority" undefined; Minority Commission not constitutional (both true, R not the explanation) {1991}
- Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities: one for India (not each State), **Art 350B** by the **7th** Amendment (neither) {2024}

@section 32.4 | Human Rights
- Human rights stress the **dignity of man** {2014}
- Second-generation rights: **right to work** and education {2010}
- NHRC head need not be ex-CJI; term 3 years or 70 since 2019 (both false now) {2015}
- NHRC Chairperson: former **CJI or SC judge** (2019 law) {2007}
- Not constitutional: **NHRC**; status different from the others: **NHRC** (statutory) {2007, 2004}

@section 32.5 | Vigilance and Years
- CVC in no Article (**statutory**, 2003 Act) {2020}
- Wrong pairs: Prevention of Corruption Act (**1988**), Enforcement Directorate (**1956**) {2017}
- Politician-criminal nexus: **Vohra Committee** (1993) {1995}
- Wrong: **NDC-1954** (it is **1952**) {2016}

@section 32.6 | Review and Mandal
- Constitution review commission: recommendatory, chaired by **M.N. Venkatachaliah** (1 and 3) {2002, 2003}
- Chairman (2000): **M.N. Venkatachaliah** {2002}
- Mandal set up by **Morarji Desai**; report **1980** {2008, 1991}

@section 32.7 | UPSC
- UPSC: **constitutional** body {2014}
- First PSC provided by the **GoI Act, 1919** (set up 1926) {2008}
- UPSC functions come from the **Constitution, laws, rules and conventions** (all) {2024}
- Annual report to the **President** {2010, 2013}
- P.C. Hota Committee on civil services: **2004** {2021}

@section 32.8 | PSCs (Arts 315-323)
- State PSC member removed by the **President after SC inquiry** {2021}
- Parliament plays **no** role in removing the **UPSC Chairman** (only 3) {2020}
- Joint PSC: by **Parliament** on States' request {2022}
- UPSC can serve a State on the **President's** approval {2014}
- UPPSC functions extended by the **UP Legislature** (Art 321) {2021}
- UPPSC expenses charged on the **State Consolidated Fund** {2010}

@trap
- RS Chairman is **not** on the Lokpal panel.
- Linguistic minorities officer: **Art 350B** (7th Amendment), one for India.
- Mandal: **set up** by Morarji Desai, **implemented** by V.P. Singh (1990).
- State PSC member: Governor appoints, **President removes**.
@end

@up **UPPSC** (Prayagraj, 1937) is a State PSC: its members are appointed by the UP Governor, removed by the President; its expenses are charged on UP's Consolidated Fund. The **UP Lokayukta** (1975 Act) reports to the Governor.
