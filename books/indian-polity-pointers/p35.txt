@section 35.1 | Recognition
- National party: State party in at least **four** States {2000}
- State party: **8%** vote alone (key's 6% needs seats) {2014}
- Regional party: **Akali Dal** (CPI also now) {2008}
- Not a national party: **TDP** (and CPI now) {2009}

@end
