@section 24.1 | Strength (Art 124)
- Sanctioned strength changed: 26 (2004), 31 (2015), 34 (2019), 38 per the 2026 law cited in the masterbook (*) {2004, 2015}
- Strength fixed by **Parliament by law**, not by amendment

@section 24.2 | Appointment and Oath
- Collegium: CJI + **4** senior-most judges {2010}
- President's reference on appointments: **Art 143(1)** (Third Judges case, 1998) {1998}
- Executive, legislature and CJI in appointments: **2nd ARC** {2014}
- Oath quoted ("true faith and allegiance… uphold the Constitution"): **Chief Justice of India** {2004}

@section 24.3 | Tenure and Removal
- Resignation to the **President** {2014, 2023}
- Retirement age: **65** {1990}
- Salaries determined by **Parliament** {2008}
- Retired SC judges may practise in **no court** {1997}

@flow Removing an SC judge (Art 124(4))
Motion | 100 LS or 50 RS members
Speaker / Chairman | Admits; three-member inquiry committee
Each House | Majority of total + two-thirds present and voting
President | Order of removal (proved misbehaviour or incapacity)
@end

@section 24.4 | Acting CJI, Ad hoc, Seat, Benches
- Acting CJI appointed by the **President** {2000, 2001}
- **Ad hoc judges**: only for the **Supreme Court** (Art 127) {2004, 2010}
- Can sit elsewhere with the **President's approval** (Art 130) {2008}
- Constitutional cases: at least **five** judges {2012, 2012}
- English in SC proceedings: **Art 348** {2013}

@section 24.5 | Court of Record (Art 129)
- Court of Record: can **punish for its contempt** {2008}
- Wrong pair: **Art 124-Court of Record** (it is **Art 129**) {2023}
- Courts of Record: **High Courts and Supreme Court** {2008}

@section 24.6 | Jurisdiction
- Has **original, appellate and advisory** jurisdiction {2010, 2013}
- Not true: **exclusive** power to issue writs for FRs {2017}
- Appeal in constitutional cases: **Art 132 with 134A** {2002, 2004}
- Interpretation cases come under **appellate** jurisdiction {2013}
- TDSAT judgments challenged in **High Court and SC** {2003}

@section 24.7 | Writs (Art 32)
- FRs safeguarded under **Art 32** {2010}
- FR enforcement: **High Court and Supreme Court** {2004, 2004, 2006}
- SC issues five writs; Parliament may empower other courts (Art 32(3)) (both) {2023}

@section 24.8 | Advisory (Art 143)
- Advisory opinion sought by the **President** {2010, 2012}
- Advice to the President on law or fact of public importance {2016}

@section 24.9 | Review and Curative
- Curative petition rests on **Art 142** (per key) {2014}
- Review: **Art 137** {2009}; SC **can review** {2011} and **change** its judgment {2005}

@section 24.10 | Judicial Review
- Declares laws **unconstitutional** {1994}
- Enjoyed by **SC and High Courts** {2017}
- SC has **no exclusive** power over Central laws (Art 131A removed 1977); guardian (A false, R true) {2019}
- Judicial review in **India and USA** {2013}
- Review limited; "bag of borrowings" (both true, R not the explanation) {2017}
- Guardian of the Constitution: **Supreme Court** {2015, 2015}

@section 24.11 | Basic Structure and Benches

- "FRs let a man chalk out his life": **Golaknath** {2012}
- Second largest bench (11): Golaknath, R.C. Cooper, T.M.A. Pai; Minerva was 5 (*) {2004}
