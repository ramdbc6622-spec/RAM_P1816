@chapter 22 | Parliamentary Committees
@meta Built from 18 UPPSC questions (2002–2025). Focus: financial committees, Estimates Committee (LS only, largest), PAC and the CAG, JPC ratio.

@hot
- **Estimates Committee**: largest financial committee, **Lok Sabha only**, 1-year term {2008, 2010, 2010, 2016, 2017, 2020}
- **PAC**: 15 LS + 7 RS; chairman named by the **Speaker**; examines **CAG reports** {2004, 2006, 2013, 2014, 2014, 2021, 2022, 2025}
- Financial committees: **PAC, Estimates, Public Undertakings** {2007}
@end

@section 22.1 | Classification
- Financial committees: **PAC, Estimates, Public Undertakings** (all three) {2007}
- Not a standing committee: none (all four were standing, *) {2015}
- **PAC-financial, Petitions-functional, JPC on stock scam-ad hoc, DRSCs-standing** {2005}
- No parliamentary committee on **welfare of minorities** {2012}

@section 22.2 | Estimates Committee
- Largest (among options): **Estimates Committee** (30 members) {2017, 2020}
- Term: **one year** {2016}
- No RS member: **Estimates Committee** {2008, 2010}; drawn from **Lok Sabha** {2010}

@section 22.3 | PAC
- PAC reports to the **Lok Sabha** (true); 15 RS members (false) (only 1) {2025}
- PAC reports to **Parliament** {2002, 2015}
- Members: **15 LS + 7 RS = 22** {2014}
- Chairman named by the **Speaker** {2004, 2006, 2013, 2014}
- CAG audit report examined by the **PAC** (and COPU for PSUs) {2022}
- PAC scrutinises the **CAG's report** {2021}

@flow Money trail and its watchdogs
Estimates Committee | Before spending: economy in estimates
Parliament votes grants | Appropriation Act
CAG audits | Reports to President
PAC examines | After spending: post-mortem
@end

@section 22.4 | Joint Committees
- Standing committees: LS and RS members in a **2:1** ratio {2013}
- 2G JPC: **20 LS + 10 RS** {2010}; headed by **P.C. Chacko** {2010}

@trap
- Estimates = **LS only**; PAC and COPU have **7 RS** members.
- PAC chairman is **appointed by the Speaker**; Opposition chair is a convention.
- A JPC is **ad hoc**; it ends after reporting.
@end

@up The UP Legislature has its own **PAC, Estimates Committee and Public Undertakings Committee**; CAG's UP reports go to the UP PAC via the Governor.

@last
- Financial committees: **PAC, Estimates, COPU**
- Estimates: **30**, LS only, 1 year, largest
- PAC: **22** (15 + 7); oldest (1921)

- COPU: 22; public sector audit
- DRSCs: 2:1 ratio (21 LS + 10 RS)

@end
