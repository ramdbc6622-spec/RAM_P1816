@chapter 37 | Official Language
@meta Built from 13 UPPSC questions (1990–2025). Focus: English for 15 years, Kher Commission, Art 350A, Eighth Schedule languages, State official languages.

@hot
- **22** languages in the **Eighth Schedule**; Bhojpuri and Rajasthani are **not** {1993, 2010, 2025}
- English allowed for **15 years** after 1950 (Art 343) {2023}
- **Art 350A**: primary education in the **mother tongue** {2002, 2003}
- First Official Language Commission: **1955**, **B.G. Kher** {1998}
@end

@section 37.1 | Union Language
- English permitted for all official purposes for **15 years** {2023}
- Amending language provisions: special majority (Art 368) for Articles; simple majority for laws under Art 343(3) (*) {2005, 2018}

@flow Hindi as official language
14 Sept 1949 | Assembly adopts Hindi in Devanagari (Hindi Day)
Art 343 | Hindi official; English for 15 years
1955 | Kher Commission (Art 344)
1963 | Official Languages Act; English continues
@end

@section 37.2 | Commission (Art 344)
- First Commission: **1955**, chaired by **B.G. Kher** {1998}

@section 37.3 | No National Language
- Correct: **Bodo** is in the Eighth Schedule {2016}
- Mother-tongue instruction at primary level: **Art 350A** {2002, 2003}

@section 37.4 | Eighth Schedule
- Not included: **Bhojpuri** (Nepali, Dogri, Bodo are in) {2025}
- In 1993 none of Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali was in (all added 2003) (*) {2021}
- Added by amendment: **Sindhi** (1967) and **Konkani** (1992) {1990}
- Not included: **Rajasthani** {1993}
- Number: **22** {2010}

@section 37.5 | State Languages (Art 345)
- Sanskrit as second official language: **Uttarakhand** (2010) {2011}

@section 37.6 | Speakers
- Most spoken Scheduled language (after Hindi): **Bengali** {1998}
- Order (2011 Census): **Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil** {2001}

@trap
- India has **no national language**; Hindi is the official language of the **Union**.
- Union numerals are **international** form (1, 2, 3), not Devanagari.
- **Pali and Prakrit** are classical languages but **not** Scheduled.
@end

@up UP's official language is **Hindi** with **Urdu** as the second official language (1989). Bhojpuri and Awadhi, widely spoken in UP, are **not** in the Eighth Schedule.
