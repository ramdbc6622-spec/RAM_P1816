@section 39.5 | Who Decides
- SC list specified by the **President** (Art 341) {2006, 2016}
- Most STs by number: **Madhya Pradesh** {1991}

@section 39.6 | Scheduled Areas
- President notifies Scheduled Areas; States with them need a **Tribes Advisory Council** (both) {2023}

@trap
- **Rajya Sabha** has no SC/ST or women's reservation.
- Art 331 is not deleted; it **lapsed** via Art 334 (104th Amendment).
- TAC = **Fifth** Schedule; District Councils = **Sixth** Schedule.
@end

@up UP has **17 SC-reserved Lok Sabha seats** and the largest SC population of any State. UP's STs are few (Tharu, Buksa, Gond and others); Sonbhadra has the State's highest ST share.
