@chapter 15 | The President
@unit UNIT IV · THE UNION EXECUTIVE
@meta Built from 57 UPPSC questions (1992–2025). Focus: Art 53, electoral college, impeachment, vacancy and acting President, veto and ordinance, the sequence of Presidents.

@hot
- No President and no VP: **Chief Justice of India** acts {1992, 2005, 2014, 2014, 2015, 2021}
- Executive power of the Union in the President: **Article 53** {2008, 2010, 2015, 2021}
- Vacancy filled **within six months** {1998, 2005, 2013, 2016}
- Impeachment: **Art 61**, by **either House**, **14 days'** notice {1994, 2007, 2008, 2014, 2015, 2017}
- **Neelam Sanjiva Reddy**: only unanimous election; ex-Speaker; not VP before {1998, 2002, 2004, 2021, 2022}
- Joint sitting (Art 108) summoned by the **President** {2012, 2015, 2015}
@end

@section 15.1 | Nominal Head
- Head of the Republic: the **President** {2013}
- Indian President differs from the British King; **not** like the US President (A true, R false) {1998}
- "Represents the nation but does not lead it": **President** {2007}
- Authority: **titular, de jure, constitutional, nominal** (2 and 4) {2006}

@section 15.2 | Executive Power
- Vested by **Art 53** {2008, 2010, 2015}; in the **President** only {2021}
- Heads Union Executive; power is **not** unlimited (A true, R false) {2012}
- Executive power in the President, the constitutional head (R explains A per key) {2023}
- Executive power is carrying on government (both true, R not the explanation) {2010}
- Supreme command is his, but **regulated by law** (A true, R false) {2005, 2010}

@section 15.3 | Election (Arts 54-55)
- **Indirect** election by an electoral college (R explains A) {2015}
- Electoral college: **elected MPs, elected MLAs of all States, elected MLAs of Delhi and Puducherry** {2001}
- Delhi and Puducherry added by the **70th Amendment** {2017}
- Not included: **elected MLCs** {2015}
- Wrong: election can be postponed if the electoral college is incomplete {2016}
- Nomination needs **50 proposers and 50 seconders** (no option correct, *) {2004}
- Disputes decided by the **Supreme Court** (Art 71)

@flow Who votes, who removes
Elected MPs | Vote and impeach
Nominated MPs | No vote; take part in impeachment
Elected MLAs (States, Delhi, Puducherry) | Vote; no role in impeachment
MLCs | No role at all
@end

@section 15.4 | Qualifications
- Only true: **35 years**; not "citizen by birth"; qualified for **Lok Sabha** (not "Parliament") {1998}
- Not a qualification: **education** {1992}
- An MP or MLA elected President **vacates the seat** on taking office {2008}

@section 15.5 | Impeachment (Art 61)
- Procedure in **Art 61** {2015, 2017}
- Removed before term only by **impeachment** {1994}
- Initiated by **either House** {2007, 2008}
- Notice: **14 days** {2014}
- Ground: **violation of the Constitution**; two-thirds of total membership of each House

@section 15.6 | Vacancy and Acting President
- Vacancy filled **within six months** {1998, 2005, 2013, 2016}
- No VP: **Chief Justice of India** acts {1992, 2005, 2014, 2014, 2015, 2021}
- CJI who acted as President: **M. Hidayatullah** (1969) {2003, 2004}

@section 15.7 | Legislative Powers
- President is **part of Parliament**; no Bill becomes law without assent (R explains A) {2007, 2023}
- Wrong: President **takes part in debates** {2010}
- Joint sitting summoned by the **President** {2012, 2015, 2015}
- Withhold assent: **Art 111** {2004, 2008, 2011}
- Not his power: **returning a Money (financial) Bill** {2007}

@table
Veto | Use
Absolute | Withhold assent (Private Member's Bills, after cabinet resigns)
Suspensive | Return once (not Money Bill); bound if passed again
Pocket | No time limit; Postal Bill 1986 (Zail Singh)
Money Bill | Cannot be returned; prior recommendation
Amendment Bill | Must assent (24th Amendment)
@end

@section 15.8 | Ordinance (Art 123)
- Ordinance power: **Art 123** {2013, 2016}
- 2002 electoral-reform ordinance signed under **Art 123** {2015}

@section 15.9 | Appointments
- Appoints all listed officials {2002}
- Not appointed by him: the **Vice-President** (elected) {1997}
- Appoints **FC chairman and UT administrators/CMs** (1 and 3) {2005}
- Governors' duties in emergencies: **Art 160** {2007}
- Declares **Scheduled Areas**: President {2006}

@section 15.10 | Art 143 and Art 72
- Consult the Supreme Court: **Art 143** {2012}
- Incorrect: the President **must accept** SC advice {2008}
- Pardon is an **executive power** (Art 72) {2010}

@trap
- Qualified for **Lok Sabha** (President) vs **Rajya Sabha** (VP).
- **MLCs** never vote; nominated MPs impeach but do not elect.
- Acting Presidents (Giri, Hidayatullah, Jatti) are **not counted** in the sequence.
@end

@up UP has the largest share of the presidential vote: its MLA vote value is **208** (highest), and 80 LS + 31 RS MPs. **Ram Nath Kovind** (14th President) is from **Kanpur Dehat**, UP; **Zakir Husain** studied and taught at Aligarh.
