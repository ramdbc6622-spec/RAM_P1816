@chapter 16 | The Vice-President
@meta Built from 12 UPPSC questions (2003–2025). Focus: electoral college of all MPs, Rajya Sabha qualification, removal from the Rajya Sabha, Art 71.

@hot
- VP elected by **all members of both Houses** (elected and nominated); no MLAs {2012, 2012, 2016}
- Removal resolution only in the **Rajya Sabha** {2003, 2004, 2007, 2013}
- VP is ex-officio **Chairman of the Rajya Sabha** but **not its member** {2004, 2010, 2025}
@end

@section 16.1 | Election (Art 66)
- Electoral college: **all members of both Houses** {2012}
- Votes: **Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha members** only (1 and 2) {2012}
- Election indirect like the President; State legislators have **no role** in VP election (only 1) {2016}

@section 16.2 | Qualification and RS Chair
- Must be qualified for the **Upper House** because he chairs it (R explains A) {2010}
- Not correct: qualified for **Lok Sabha**; removed by **impeachment** (1 and 4) {2005}
- Ex-officio Chairman of RS; **not** a member (only 1) {2025}
- Presided by a non-member: **Rajya Sabha** {2004}

@section 16.3 | Acting President (Art 65)
- Second highest dignitary; no formal VP functions; acts in absence and in vacancy (all four) {2013}

@section 16.4 | Removal (Art 67)
- Removed by a resolution of the **Council of States** (effective majority), agreed by the Lok Sabha (simple majority) {2013}
- Proposed only in the **Rajya Sabha** {2003, 2004, 2007}

@flow Removing the Vice-President
Notice | 14 days, in Rajya Sabha only
Rajya Sabha | Effective majority (of then members)
Lok Sabha | Agrees by simple majority
Not impeachment | No grounds stated in the Constitution
@end

@section 16.5 | Disputes (Art 71)
- Election voided by SC: acts done **on or before** the decision stay valid (only 1) {2023}

@section 16.6 | Vice-Presidents
- Wrong: **Zakir Husain** was the first VP (it was **S. Radhakrishnan**, 1952) {2013}

@table
Item | President | Vice-President
Electors | Elected MPs + elected MLAs | All MPs (incl. nominated)
Qualification | Eligible for Lok Sabha | Eligible for Rajya Sabha
Removal | Impeachment, either House | RS resolution + LS agreement
Disputes | Supreme Court (Art 71) | Supreme Court (Art 71)
@end

@trap
- Nominated MPs **vote for VP**, not for President.
- VP chairs RS but casts only a **casting vote**.
- VP removal = **not impeachment**; starts only in RS.
@end
