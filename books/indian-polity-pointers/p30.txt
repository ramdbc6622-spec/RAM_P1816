@chapter 30 | The Finance Commission
@meta Built from 15 UPPSC questions (1993–2023). Focus: tax sharing, Art 280, five-year cycle, chairman plus four members, chairmen of the 13th and 14th, State Finance Commissions.

@hot
- **Finance Commission** shares taxes between Centre and States {1993, 2002, 2006, 2008, 2010, 2013, 2023}
- Constituted **every 5 years** under **Art 280** by the **President** {2008, 2010, 2011, 2021}
- **Chairman + 4 members**; reports to the **President** {2008, 2023}
- **State Finance Commission** is a **constitutional** body {2015, 2015, 2016}
@end

@section 30.1 | Functions
- Distributes taxes between Centre and States {2023}; finances shared on its advice {2008, 2010, 2013}
- Determines the States' share of taxes and grants {1993, 2002}
- Advises the **President** on dividing net tax proceeds {2006}

@section 30.2 | Constitution (Art 280)
- Every **5 years** {2008, 2010, 2011}
- Appointed under **Art 280** {2021}
- Chairman and six members; reports to NITI Aayog: **neither** true {2023}
- Chairman and **four** other members {2008}
- Qualifications set by **Parliament** (1951 Act), not the President (A false, R true) {2021}

@flow How a Finance Commission award works
Art 280 | President appoints chairman + 4
Finance Commission (Misc. Provisions) Act 1951 | Qualifications
Report | To President; laid in Parliament
Vertical + horizontal shares | Union decides on acceptance
@end

@section 30.3 | Classification
- "Statutory" body (per key; strictly **constitutional**) {2023}

@section 30.4 | Chairmen
- 13th FC: **Vijay Kelkar** {2012}
- 14th FC: **Y.V. Reddy** (42% share) {2016}

@section 30.5 | State Finance Commissions
- SFC is constitutional (true); Union FC **can** recommend aid to Panchayats (R false) {2015}
- SFC is a **constitutional** body (Arts 243I, 243Y) {2015, 2016}

@trap
- Report goes to the **President**, not NITI Aayog or Parliament directly.
- President appoints; **Parliament** fixes qualifications.
- FC is **constitutional**; Planning Commission and NITI are **executive** bodies.
@end

@up UP is the largest gainer from FC awards: about **17.6%** of the States' pool under the 15th and 16th FCs. The **UP State Finance Commission** (Art 243I) shares State taxes with Panchayats and urban bodies.
