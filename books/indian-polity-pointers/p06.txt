@section 6.2 | Schedule Matching
- **7th-legislative distribution, 8th-languages, 9th-validation of Acts, 10th-disqualification for defection** {2016, 2016, 2025}
- **3rd-oaths, 4th-RS seats, 7th-lists of subjects, 8th-languages** {2022}
- **4th-Council of States, 6th-tribal areas, 8th-language, 9th-land reforms** {1999}
- **Ninth-RS seats** is wrong {2023}; **RS seats-Second** is wrong {2019}

@section 6.3 | Third Schedule
- In the Third Schedule: oaths of a **Union Minister, SC Chief Justice, MP**; **not** the President (Art 60) {2017}

@section 6.4 | Fifth and Sixth Schedules
- Sixth Schedule: **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram** {2016, 2023, ?}
- Of Sikkim, Tripura, Nagaland: only **Tripura** {?}

@section 6.5 | Seventh Schedule
- Three lists: **VII Schedule** {2021}
- **Union-census, State-police and public order, Concurrent-population control, residuary-space research** {2004}
- **Stock exchange-Concurrent** is wrong (it is **Union**) {2009, 2010}

@section 6.6 | State List
- **Railway police**: State List {1997, 2001}
- **Gas** (and gas-works) is not in the Union List (it is State) {1997}
- **Criminal procedure**: Concurrent {2006, 2011}

@section 6.7 | Concurrent List and 42nd Amendment
- **Criminal matters**: Concurrent {2005}
- **Economic planning**: Concurrent {2006}
- **Marriage, divorce, adoption**: Concurrent {2010}
- Concurrent includes **adulteration and succession** (trade and commerce taken as not) {2024}
- **Education** to Concurrent: **42nd Amendment** {2008, 2017}

@section 6.10 | Eleventh and Twelfth
- **Eleventh**: **Panchayati Raj** {2014, 2015}; it has **29** items {2019}

@trap
- **2nd** = salaries; **4th** = RS seats. **5th** = Scheduled Areas; **6th** = AMTM tribal areas.
- President, VP, Governor oaths are in **Arts 60, 69, 159**, not the 3rd Schedule.
- Railways = Union; **railway police = State**. Stock exchange = Union.
@end

@up UP has no Fifth or Sixth Schedule areas. UP has **31 Rajya Sabha** and **80 Lok Sabha** seats (Fourth Schedule for RS). UP Panchayats take the 29 Eleventh Schedule subjects.
