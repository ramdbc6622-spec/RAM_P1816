@section 26.1 | Creating a Council (Art 169)
- Created or abolished by **Parliament on the Assembly's resolution** {2008}
- Article: **169** {?}
- Abolished but not dissolved: **State Legislative Councils** {2007, 2018}

@section 26.2 | Bicameral States
- No Council: **Rajasthan** {2013}
- Not bicameral: **MP** {2006, 2008, 2008}
- 1956 bicameral States: 7 (per source; none of options) (*) {2004}
- Bicameral now (6): **UP, Bihar, Maharashtra, Karnataka, AP, Telangana**

@section 26.3 | Council Composition (Art 171)
- Not more than **one-third** of Assembly; at least **40** (both) {2023}
- Nominated share: "none of the above" per key (about 1/6 by Art 171) {2017}

@section 26.4 | Assembly (Art 170)
- Minimum and maximum: **60 and 500** {2017}; maximum **500** {2014}
- Exception to 60: **Sikkim** (32) {2015}
- Largest Assembly: **UP** (403) {2006}
- **Manipur-62** is wrong (it has **60**) {2022}

@section 26.5 | Elections, Speaker, Quorum
- Assembly elections conducted by the **Election Commission of India** {2008}
- Final authority on MLA disqualification (Art 192): **Governor** (on EC opinion) {2016}
- Speaker stays until **just before the new Assembly's first sitting** {2004}
- Quorum: **10 members or one-tenth**, whichever is more {2021}

@section 26.6 | Money and Ordinary Bills
- State Money Bill only on the **Governor's** recommendation {2012}
- Council may hold an Assembly Bill **3 months** first time {2017}
- Total detention of ordinary Bills: **4 months** (3 + 1) {2005}

@section 26.7 | State Contingency Fund
- Established by the **State Legislature** {2014}
- Operated by: none listed (at the Governor's disposal) (*) {2004}

@section 26.8 | State Executive and CM
- Art 162 heading: **extent of executive power of State** {2019}
- Not correct: Governor acts on CM's advice in **all** functions {2010}
- Not correct: CM is "normally selected" by the Governor {2010}
- CM's duties: **Art 167** {2013}
- First woman CM: **Sucheta Kripalani** (UP, 1963) {2004}

@section 26.9 | State Ministers
- Minimum CoM: **12** {2020}
- Ministers' pay fixed by the **State Legislature** {2008}
- J&K CM tenure: no fixed term (*) {2008}

@section 26.10 | Reservation and Civil Code
- Muslim quota proposed: **Andhra Pradesh** {2003}
- Only common civil code (2009): **Goa**; Uttarakhand UCC since 2025 {2009}
