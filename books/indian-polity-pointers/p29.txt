@chapter 29 | Emergency Provisions
@meta Built from 16 UPPSC questions (1992–2024). Focus: three emergencies, Art 352 grounds and approval, Arts 358-359, President's Rule, Art 360 never used.

@hot
- **Three** types of emergency: Arts **352, 356, 360** {2007, 2009, 2010, 2010, 2014}
- Art 352 grounds: **war, external aggression, armed rebellion** (not internal disturbance since 44th) {2016, 2016, 2017}
- Parliament approves a National Emergency within **one month** {2003, 2004, 2006}
- **Art 360** (Financial Emergency) **never used** {2006, 2015, 2016}
- **Arts 20 and 21** cannot be suspended (44th Amendment) {2009, 2015}
@end

@section 29.1 | Three Types
- Types: **three** {2009, 2014}
- Duty to protect States from aggression and disturbance: **Art 355** {2001}
- Grounds for an emergency: **external aggression, breakdown in a State, financial crisis** (1, 3, 4) {2007, 2010, 2010}

@section 29.2 | National Emergency (Art 352)
- Art 352 deals with emergency; it can come any time (both true, R not the explanation) {2024}
- Grounds: **war, external aggression, armed rebellion** {2016, 2016, 2017}
- Parliament's approval: **one month** {2003, 2004, 2006}

@flow National Emergency after the 44th Amendment
Cabinet's written advice | President proclaims
Within 1 month | Both Houses approve by special majority
Every 6 months | Fresh approval needed
Revocation | President, or LS resolution (simple majority)
@end

@section 29.3 | FRs in Emergency
- Suspend enforcement of FRs (except 20, 21): **Art 359** {2008}
- What the President can suspend: disputed (*); Art 21 can never be {2015}
- Cannot be suspended: **Arts 20 and 21** {2009, 2015}

@section 29.4 | Lok Sabha Term
- Can be extended **one year at a time** during Emergency (6 months after it ends) {2010}

@section 29.5 | President's Rule (Art 356)
- Maximum: **3 years** {1992}
- First use (among options): **PEPSU** (1953); Punjab 1951 was the very first {2010}
- Governor's Rule (old J&K Section 92): historical only (*) {2015}

@table

Approval | 1 month | 2 months | 2 months

Maximum | Indefinite (6-monthly) | 3 years | Indefinite
Used | 1962, 1971, 1975 | 100+ times | Never
@end

@section 29.6 | Financial Emergency (Art 360)
- Never used: **Art 360**; declared **never** {2015, 2016, 2006}

@section 29.7 | Constituent Assembly
- "Emergency power is a fraud on the Constitution": **K.M. Nambiar** (per key) {2004}

@trap
- "Internal disturbance" is in **Art 355**, not a ground under Art 352.
- **Art 358** suspends Art 19 only in **external** emergency; **Art 359** suspends enforcement.
- Art 352 approval **1 month**; Arts 356 and 360 **2 months**.
@end

@up UP has had President's Rule **10 times** (first in 1968; last in 2002). The 1975 Emergency followed the **Allahabad High Court** verdict in *Indira Gandhi v. Raj Narain*.
