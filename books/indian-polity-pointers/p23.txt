@chapter 23 | Parliamentary Acts
@meta Built from 25 UPPSC questions (1991–2021). Focus: years of colonial Acts, RTI 2005, Code on Wages, Disaster Management Act and lockdown powers, environment Acts.

@hot
- **RTI Act, 2005**: assent **15 June 2005**; a **legal right** (also read into Art 19(1)(a)); MKSS movement {2010, 2013, 2013, 2015, 2019}
- **Disaster Management Act, 2005**: NDMA chaired by the **PM**; with Epidemic Diseases Act 1897 backed the lockdown {2020, 2021, 2021}
- Act-year matching: Arms Act 1878, Royal Titles 1876, High Courts 1861, Divorce 1869, Criminal Tribes 1871 {2016, 2016, 2020}
@end

@section 23.1 | Article 245
- Parliament may legislate for the **Union, territory and Indians and their property abroad** (all) {2021}

@section 23.2 | Colonial Acts
- **Arms Act-1878, Royal Titles Act-1876, Indian High Courts Act-1861, Divorce Act-1869** {2020}
- Criminal Tribes Act: **1871** {2016}
- Wrong pair: Prevention of Seditious Meetings Act-**1908** (it was 1907) {2016}

@section 23.3 | Social Laws
- Not a social Act: **MISA** (security law) {1991}
- 73rd = rural panchayats; 74th = urban bodies (swap is the trap) {2009}
- Domestic Violence Act in force: **26 October 2006** {2015}

@section 23.4 | RTI: Nature
- Assent: **15 June 2005**; passed **2005** {2013, 2013}
- RTI is a **legal right**; both legal and (implied) fundamental {2015, 2010}
- **MKSS** (Aruna Roy): **RTI** movement {2019}

@flow RTI Act timeline
15 June 2005 | Presidential assent
12 October 2005 | Fully in force
2019 Amendment | Centre sets tenure and pay of Information Commissioners
DPDP Act 2023 | Narrowed Sec 8(1)(j) personal information
@end

@section 23.5 | RTI: Provisions and Cases
- Not provided: applicant need **not give reasons** (Sec 6(2)) (*) {2006}
- Applicant must give reasons (overturned view): **Madras High Court** {2014}
- RTI case: **Namit Sharma v. Union of India** {2015}
- RTI raised accountability; still far to go (both true, R not the explanation) {2010}

@section 23.7 | Code on Wages
- Merged in Code on Wages 2019: **Minimum Wages, Payment of Bonus, Equal Remuneration** (and Payment of Wages); not Contract Labour {2021}

@section 23.8 | Disaster Management
- Disaster Management Act: **2005** {2021}
- NDMA chaired by the **Prime Minister** {2021}
- Lockdown legal basis: **Disaster Management Act 2005 and Epidemic Diseases Act 1897** {2020}

@section 23.9 | Environment and Food
- **NGT**: **2010** {2016}
- PFA Act: **1954** {2006}
- Forest Conservation Act: **1980** {2017}
- PPV and FR Act: **2001** {2021}

@trap
- RTI: assent **15 June**; in force **12 October** 2005.
- NDMA = **PM**, though MHA is the nodal ministry; SDMA = **CM**.
- Contract labour goes to the **OSH Code**, not Code on Wages.
@end

@up UP has a **State Disaster Management Authority** chaired by the **Chief Minister**. The **UP Information Commission** (Lucknow) hears RTI appeals in the State.
