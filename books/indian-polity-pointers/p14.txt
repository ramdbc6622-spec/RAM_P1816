@chapter 14 | Fundamental Duties
@meta Built from 18 UPPSC questions (1993–2022). Focus: Swaran Singh Committee, 42nd Amendment, Part IV-A/Art 51A, 11 duties, spot-the-non-duty.

@hot
- **Swaran Singh Committee** recommended; **42nd Amendment** (1976) added 10 duties {1993, 2002, 2008, 2012}
- **Part IV-A, Article 51A** {2006, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2015}
- **11** duties now (11th by the 86th Amendment, 2002) {2014, 2014}
- **Not** duties: voting, paying taxes, respecting parents, abolishing untouchability, protecting monuments, organising panchayats {2007, 2008, 2014, 2015, 2019, 2021, 2022}
@end

@section 14.1 | Origin
- Recommended by the **Swaran Singh Committee** {2008, 2012}
- Added as **Art 51A** by the 42nd Amendment {2015}
- In **Part IV-A** {2006, 2010, 2011, 2012}
- Ten duties added by the **42nd** {1993, 2002}
- **Part V-Fundamental Duties** is wrong {2009}
- Incorrect: Duties are part of FRs {2009}

@section 14.2 | Eleven Duties
- Number: **eleven** {2014, 2014}

@flow Duties timeline
Swaran Singh Committee | 1976; suggested 8 duties
42nd Amendment | 1976; 10 duties, Part IV-A
86th Amendment | 2002; 11th duty: parents educate children 6-14
Verma Committee | 1999; ways to make duties work
@end

@section 14.3 | Nature
- Not true: duties **can be enforced by mandamus** {2000, 2003}

@section 14.4 | Spot the Non-Duty
- Not a duty: **voting** {2021}
- Not a duty: **abolition of untouchability** (Art 17) {2008}
- Not a duty: **organising village panchayats** (Art 40) {2015}
- Duties: **defend the country, composite culture, public property**; not paying income tax {2019}
- Not in Part IV-A: **respect parents and teachers** {2014}

@section 14.5 | Monuments vs Culture
- Not a duty: **protect monuments** (that is Art 49, DPSP) {2007, 2022}

@section 14.6 | Environment
- Protecting **wildlife** is a duty {2008}
- "Duty of every citizen to protect the environment": **Art 51A** {1998}

@section 14.7 | Cross-Part Matching
- **Respect international law-Art 51(c), compassion for living creatures-51A(g), scientific temper-51A(h), separate judiciary-Art 50** {2019}

@trap
- "Duty of every citizen" = **51A(g)**; "State shall endeavour" = **48A**.
- **Composite culture** = duty (51A(f)); **monuments** = DPSP (Art 49).
- Duties are **non-justiciable**; no writ.
@end
