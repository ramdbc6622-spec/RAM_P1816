@up UP follows a 3-tier system under the **UP Panchayat Raj Act 1947** and **Kshettra and Zila Panchayat Act 1961**: Gram Panchayat (Pradhan), Kshetra Panchayat (Pramukh), Zila Panchayat (Adhyaksh). **Ghaziabad** is a Nagar Nigam; UP Mayors are directly elected for 5 years.
