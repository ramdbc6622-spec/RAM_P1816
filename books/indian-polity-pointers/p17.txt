@chapter 17 | The Council of Ministers
@meta Built from 29 UPPSC questions (1992–2021). Focus: PM and CoM, six-month rule, collective responsibility to the Lok Sabha, no-confidence, Deputy PM, PM facts.

@hot
- CoM **collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha** only (Art 75(3)) {2012, 2012, 2016, 2019, 2021}
- Non-member minister: **six months** {1993, 1995, 2015}
- No-confidence motion needs **50** LS members {2009, 2013, 2015}
- Government can move a **confidence motion** {2006, 2010}
@end

@section 17.1 | PM and the Union Executive
- True: PM **appointed by President**, **heads the CoM**, need not be an MP at appointment; executive power is **not** in the PM {2011}
- PM is the **real head** of the CoM {2013}
- **Art 78**: duties of the **PM** (inform the President) {2014}
- Legal name for suits (Art 300): **Union of India** {2021}

@section 17.2 | Cabinet vs Council
- Cabinet = **ministers of Cabinet rank** {1992}
- CoM capped at **15%** of Lok Sabha (91st Amendment, 2003)

@section 17.3 | Appointment (Art 75)
- Appointment and tenure of ministers: **Art 75** {2001}
- Non-member minister: **6 months** {1993, 1995, 2015}
- Not true: Cabinet membership limited to the **Lower House** {2011}

@section 17.4 | Responsibility
- Collectively responsible: **to Lok Sabha, a constitutional duty, Art 75(3)** (1, 2, 3) {2012}
- Responsible to the **Lok Sabha** {2012}
- Ministers hold office at the **President's** pleasure, not the PM's (only 1) {2016}
- Responsible to both Houses: **false**; both Houses can supply ministers: true {2019, 2021}
- Ministers make policy; collective responsibility (both true, R not the explanation) {1993}

@section 17.5 | Confidence and No-Confidence
- CoM can move a **confidence motion** {2006, 2010}
- Resigns on a no-confidence vote in the **Lok Sabha** {2012}
- No-confidence needs **50** members {2009, 2013, 2015}
- Wrong: **President's Rule** follows CoM resignation {2010}

@flow No-confidence motion
Notice | Lok Sabha only (Rule 198)
Support | At least 50 members
Debate and vote | No reason needed
Passed | Whole Council of Ministers resigns
@end

@section 17.6 | Deputy PM
- Calling someone Deputy PM is political; gives no PM power (R explains A) {2004}
- Deputy PM: **not in the Constitution** (K.M. Sharma v. Devi Lal, 1990, upheld it) {2009}

@section 17.7 | Prime Ministers
- 12th PM (counting Nanda): **Deve Gowda** {2005}
- PM more than once with gaps: **Nanda, Indira Gandhi, Vajpayee** {2010}
- Never a CM before PM: **Chandra Shekhar** {2011}
- Was a Lok Sabha member as PM: **Chandra Shekhar** {2015}
- LPG policy: **P.V. Narasimha Rao** (1991) {2013}
- Not true of Manmohan Singh: chaired the **Finance Commission** {2004}
- **Rajiv Gandhi-Karam Bhumi** is wrong (his samadhi is **Veer Bhumi**) {1996}

@section 17.8 | Machinery
- Cabinet reorganisation report: **Gopalaswami Ayyangar** {2010}
- Official document: **White Paper** {2008}

@trap
- Ministers serve at the **President's** pleasure, responsible to **Lok Sabha** only.
- Ministers may come from **either House**.
- **50 members** is a Lok Sabha rule, not an Article.
- Deputy PM is **extra-constitutional**, not unconstitutional.
@end

@up Nine PMs sat for UP seats: Nehru (Phulpur), Shastri (Allahabad), Indira Gandhi (Rae Bareli), Charan Singh (Baghpat), Rajiv Gandhi (Amethi), V.P. Singh (Fatehpur), Chandra Shekhar (Ballia), Vajpayee (Lucknow), Modi (Varanasi). **Charan Singh** and **V.P. Singh** were UP Chief Ministers.
