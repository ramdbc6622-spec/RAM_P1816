@chapter 1 | Constitutional Development in British India
@unit UNIT I · MAKING OF THE CONSTITUTION
@meta Built from 21 UPPSC questions (2002–2023). Focus: which Act did what first; the 1935 Act alone gives 12 questions.

@hot
- **GoI Act, 1935**: **dyarchy at the Centre**, **All-India Federation**, residuary powers with the **Governor-General**, Federal Court, ordinance power, Burma separated {2008, 2008, 2008, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, ?}
- **1935 Act** = main source of the Constitution (about 250 Articles drawn from it) {2010, 2015}
- **Regulating Act, 1773** = Supreme Court at Calcutta (1774); first Chief Justice **Elijah Impey** {2002, 2003, 2010, 2016}
- **1919 Act** = first **bicameral** central legislature; Chamber of Princes {2008, 2021, 2023}
@end

@section 1.1 | Regulating Act and Pitt's India Act
- Supreme Court at Calcutta: **Regulating Act, 1773** {2010}
- First Chief Justice: **Sir Elijah Impey**; Le Maistre was only a puisne judge {2016}
- Match: **Board of Control-Pitt's India Act 1784, Supreme Court-1773, missionaries-Charter Act 1813, Law Member-Charter Act 1833** {2002, 2003}

@section 1.2 | The Charter Acts
- Trade monopoly ended **except tea and China trade**: **Charter Act, 1813** {2017}
- First legal opening of offices to Indians (Section 87): **Charter Act, 1833** {2012}

@flow Constitutional milestones
Regulating Act 1773 | Governor-General of Bengal; Supreme Court
Pitt's India Act 1784 | Board of Control; dual government
Charter Acts 1813, 1833, 1853 | Trade ends; GG of India; open civil service exam
GoI Act 1858 | Crown rule; Secretary of State
Councils Acts 1861, 1892, 1909 | Indians nominated; budget discussion; separate electorate
GoI Act 1919 | Provincial dyarchy; bicameral Centre
GoI Act 1935 | Federation; dyarchy at Centre; provincial autonomy
Independence Act 1947 | Two Dominions; Assembly sovereign
@end

@section 1.3 | Councils Acts, 1858 to 1909
- Power to **discuss the budget** (no vote): **Indian Councils Act, 1892** {2003}
- 1909 (Morley-Minto): separate electorate for Muslims; resolutions on budget; Minto = "Father of Communal Electorate"

@section 1.4 | Government of India Act, 1919
- Legislature made **bicameral** first: **1919 Act** {2008}
- 1919 Act provided an ICS exam in India; by 1941 Indians outnumbered Europeans in the ICS (both true) {2021}
- **Chamber of Princes** (120 members): **1919 Act** {2023}

@section 1.5 | Government of India Act, 1935
- **Dyarchy at the Centre**: **1935 Act** {2008, 2013}
- **Federal system** and dyarchy at Centre: **1935 Act** {?}
- **All-India Federation**: **1935 Act** {2010, 2012}; federal government provided by the 1935 Act {2008}
- **Residuary powers** under the 1935 federation: **Governor-General** {?}
- Federal Court: Act 1935, began **1937** (key: "none of the above" when asked for the Act) {2014, ?}
- **Ordinance** power of the President: relic of the **1935 Act** {2008}
- **Burma** separated from India: **1935 Act** {2011}
- Main source of the Constitution: **1935 Act** {2015}
- **Not** a feature of the 1935 Act: a **written Constitution** for the country {2010}

@trap
- **1919** = dyarchy in the **provinces**; **1935** = dyarchy at the **Centre**.
- 1892 = discuss budget; 1909 = resolutions on budget; 1919 = vote on part of budget.
- Residuary powers: 1935 = **Governor-General**; today = **Parliament**.
@end

@up The **Federal Court** idea, provincial autonomy and dyarchy shaped the United Provinces' first elected ministry (Govind Ballabh Pant, 1937) under the 1935 Act. The Allahabad High Court began at Agra in 1866 (moved to Allahabad in 1869) under the Indian High Courts Act, 1861.
