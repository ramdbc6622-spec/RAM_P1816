@unit UNIT V · PARLIAMENT
@meta Built from 40 UPPSC questions (1995–2025). Focus: Lok Sabha strength, 1971 Census freeze, state seats, reserved seats, quorum, Speaker and pro tem Speaker.

@hot
- Maximum Lok Sabha strength **550** (530 States + 20 UTs; Anglo-Indian seats lapsed 2020) {2006, 2007, 2008, 2018}
- **UP** has the most seats in both Houses (80 LS, 31 RS) {1995, 2012}
- Quorum: **one-tenth** of total members {2004, 2006, 2008, 2017}
- Speaker resigns to the **Deputy Speaker**; removed by LS majority of members {2007, 2008, 2008, 2014}
- First Speaker: **G.V. Mavalankar**; first no-confidence against a Speaker too {2000, 2004, 2011, 2023}
- First woman Speaker: **Meira Kumar** {2007, 2008}
@end

@section 19.1 | Elections
- Not correct: rolls prepared under the **State** Government's control (it is the EC) {2011}

@section 19.2 | Strength (Art 81)
- 525 to 545: **31st Amendment** (1973) {2004}
- Maximum: **550**; UT maximum: **20** {2006, 2008, 2018}
- 2009: **530** constituencies from States {2007}

@section 19.3 | Census Freeze
- State seats based on the **1971 Census** {2003}
- **84th Amendment** froze seats till the first census after **2026** {2005}

@flow Seat freeze timeline
42nd Amendment 1976 | Froze seats on 1971 Census till 2000
84th Amendment 2001 | Extended freeze till census after 2026
87th Amendment 2003 | Boundaries redrawn on 2001 Census
106th Amendment 2023 | One-third women's seats after next delimitation
@end

@section 19.4 | State Seats
- Most seats in **both Houses**: **UP** {1995, 2012}
- After UP: **Maharashtra** (48) {2008}
- Bihar then = Assam + undivided MP (54) (*) {2000}
- Equal seats then: AP and WB (42) (*) {2005}
- Wrong then: Assam seats; AP 42 is wrong now (25) {1997}
- Two Lok Sabha seats: **Tripura** {2016}
- Largest UP seat by electors: **Ghaziabad** (*) {2007}

@section 19.5 | Reserved Seats
- No ST seats in LS: **Kerala and Tamil Nadu** {1998}
- No SC/ST LS seats: **Arunachal Pradesh and J&K** (then) {2000}
- SC seats are **proportional** to SC population (WB 42 seats: 10 SC) (*) {2014}
- Most ST seats: **Madhya Pradesh** (6) {1997, 2005}
- Anglo-Indian nomination: 2 then; **lapsed in 2020** (104th) (*) {2014}

@section 19.6 | Sessions and Quorum
- Sitting ended by adjournment, prorogation or dissolution (all) {2000}
- Prorogues the House: **President** {2015}
- Quorum: **one-tenth** {2004, 2006, 2008, 2017}

@section 19.7 | Speaker: Removal
- Resignation to the **Deputy Speaker** {2008, 2014}
- Removed by an LS resolution passed by a **majority of all members** (effective majority) {2007, 2008}

@section 19.8 | Pro tem, Casting Vote, Deputy
- Pro tem Speaker **administers oath**; usually a **senior-most** member {2010, 2017}
- Casting vote only in a **tie**; under **Art 100** {2008, 2015}
- Deputy Speaker presiding: may **vote in a tie** {2016}

@section 19.9 | Speakers
- First Speaker: **G.V. Mavalankar**; first no-confidence against a Speaker {2011, 2023, 2000, 2004}
- First woman: **Meira Kumar** {2007, 2008}
- First tribal: **P.A. Sangma** {2015}
- **11th-Sangma, 12th-Balayogi, 14th-Somnath Chatterjee, 17th-Om Birla** {2025}

@section 19.10 | Leader of Opposition
- Recognition needs **one-tenth** of House (55) (*) {2006}

@section 19.11 | Secretariat (Art 98)
- LS Secretariat under the **Speaker** {1997}
- Common posts allowed; Parliament regulates staff service (both) {2023}

@trap
- Seat **allocation** = 1971 Census; **boundaries** = 2001 Census.
- Only the **Lok Sabha** is dissolved; RS is permanent.
- SC/ST seats are **proportional**; one-third is for **women** (Art 330A).
- Speaker resigns to the **Deputy Speaker**, not the President.
@end

@up **UP: 80 Lok Sabha seats**, the most of any State, with **17 reserved for SCs**; it is also the largest State in the Rajya Sabha (**31**). Ghaziabad is UP's largest constituency by electors. The UP Vidhan Sabha has **403** elected seats.
