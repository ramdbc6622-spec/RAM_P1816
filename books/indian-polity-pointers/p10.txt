@chapter 10 | States and the Union Territories
@meta Built from 32 UPPSC questions (1990–2025). Focus: Articles 1-4, Parliament's power to redraw states, state-formation order, Sikkim, Delhi, linguistic reorganisation.

@hot
- **Parliament** alone can create states and alter boundaries (Art 2-4), by **simple majority** {1990, 2008, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016}
- **State-formation order** questions every few years {2006, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2013, 2013, 2014, 2021, 2025}
- India = "**Union of States**" (Art 1); no right to secede {2008, 2008, 2019}
- **Sikkim**: 35th Amendment (associate) and **36th (full State, 1975)** {2005, 2013}
- First linguistic state: **Andhra (1953)** {2009, 2018}
@end

@section 10.1 | Article 1
- India is a **Union of States** {2008}
- States **cannot secede**; advocacy of secession is not protected speech (only 1) {2019}

@section 10.2 | Articles 2 to 4
- Power to carve a new state: **Parliament**; alter boundaries: **Parliament** {1990, 2008, 2013, 2014, 2015}
- Not under **Art 3**: **admission of new states** (that is Art 2) {2020}
- Wrong: such a law is **not** deemed an amendment of the Constitution (Art 4) {2011}
- New-state Bill: **simple majority** {2016}
- Not a provision: "a State may include a UT" (*; Parliament can merge UT areas under Art 3) {2013}
- Wrong pair: **altering a State's name-State Legislature** (it is Parliament) {2006, 2024}

@section 10.3 | Article 3 Procedure
- President refers the Bill to the State for views in a set time; views **not binding** (both true, R not the explanation) {2023}
- "India is not a Union" false; Parliament can alter states without consent true {1999}

@flow Article 3 procedure
President's recommendation | Bill introduced only on it
Reference to State Legislature | Views within a set time
Views not binding | Parliament may ignore them
Simple majority | Not an Art 368 amendment
@end

@section 10.4 | Counting States and UTs
- 2002: 28 States and 7 UTs; now **28 and 8** (key "none") {2002}
- After Telangana: 29 (2014); key 28 = after J&K became a UT in 2019 {2013}
- Not a UT: **Goa** {2013}

@section 10.5 | Delhi and Sikkim
- Delhi made **National Capital Territory**: **69th Amendment** (1991) {2014}
- Sikkim: **35th** (associate state, 1974) per key; full state by **36th** (1975) {2005, 2013}

@section 10.8 | Linguistic Reorganisation
- SRC (Fazal Ali) set up **1953**, reported 1955 (*) {1990}
- Fazal Ali members: **K.M. Panikkar, H.N. Kunzru** {2025}
- Union of States (no secession), Dhar favoured administrative convenience, JVP against language basis: all true {2008}
- First linguistic state: **Andhra Pradesh**; created **1953** {2018, 2009}

@trap
- **Art 2** = admit new states; **Art 3** = form and alter existing states.
- Art 3 laws are **not** Art 368 amendments; simple majority.
- 1953 = Andhra State (linguistic); 1956 = Andhra Pradesh, SR Act.
- 2000 order: **Chhattisgarh, Uttarakhand, Jharkhand**.
@end

@up **Uttarakhand** was carved out of UP on **9 November 2000** under Art 3 (UP Reorganisation Act, 2000). Parts of UP (Noida, Ghaziabad, Meerut) are in the **NCR**, a planning region, not the constitutional NCT.
