- Match: **Upper Bari Doab-Ravi, Sirhind-Satluj, Gurgaon-Yamuna, Indira Gandhi-Satluj, Ravi, Beas** {2024}

@up UP leads India in **tube-well** irrigation and has the **largest irrigated area**. Its big canal systems are the **Upper and Lower Ganga**, **Sharda Sahayak** (largest in UP) and the **Saryu Nahar** (completed 2021, nine eastern districts).
