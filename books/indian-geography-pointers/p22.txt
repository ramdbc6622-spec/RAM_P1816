@section 22.1 | The Energy Mix
- Largest source of energy: **coal** {2002, 2008}
- Largest share in power generation: **thermal** {2006, 2011}
- Order in the 2000s: thermal, hydro, nuclear, wind; now wind has overtaken hydro and nuclear (*) {2010, 2014}
- Installed capacity (2019): **hydro, gas, nuclear, diesel** {2016}
- **Biogas** is **not** a commercial source {2013}

@section 22.4 | Hydro, Wind, Solar and Tidal
- Hydro station: **Koyna** (Maharashtra) {1992}
- Correct: **Sabarigiri-hydro, Ghatprabha-irrigation, Ramganga-multipurpose**; Idukki is **hydro**, not thermal {2017}
- **Shivanasamudra** = hydel (1902); **Kalpakkam** = nuclear {2023}
- First in wind power: **Gujarat** (Tamil Nadu earlier) {2013}
- Asia's biggest 150 MW wind project (1999): **Tamil Nadu** (Muppandal) {1999}
- First in installed capacity: **Gujarat** per key (Maharashtra in 2006); Gujarat leads now with renewables {2006}
- **Rampura**, first solar-powered village: **UP** {2010}
- First UT on 100% solar: **Diu** {2017}
- Largest tidal energy area: **Khambhat** coast {2022}; most potential: **Bhavnagar** (Gulf of Khambhat) {1991}
