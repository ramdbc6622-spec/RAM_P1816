@chapter 14 | Multipurpose River Valley Projects and Hydropower
@meta Built from 52 UPPSC questions (1991–2025). Focus: dam-river and dam-state matches, Nagarjuna Sagar, Sardar Sarovar, Indus projects, UP projects.

@hot
- **Nagarjuna Sagar** is on the **Krishna** (not the Godavari or Kaveri), in **Andhra Pradesh-Telangana** {1991, 1993, 2004, 2004, 2008, 2011, 2012}
- **Dam-river matches**: **Hirakud-Mahanadi, Matatila-Betwa, Pochampad-Godavari, Idukki-Periyar, Gandhi Sagar-Chambal, Ukai and Kakrapar-Tapi, Rihand-Rihand** {1995, 2002, 2003, 2014, 2016, 2017, 2019}

@end

@section 14.1 | Hydropower
- Huge hydro potential in the Himalaya; the Nepal border does **not** stop eastern UP and Bihar (only statement 1 true) {2025}
- Oldest hydropower station among the options: **Sivasamudram** {2008}
- Oldest power plant: **Sidrapong**; first station at **Darjeeling** {1998, 2013}
- **Sivasamudram** is in **Karnataka** (not Kerala) {2008, 2009, 2011}
- Bhutan plant opened by PM Modi (2019): **Mangdechhu** {2019}

@section 14.3 | The Narmada Projects
- **Sardar Sarovar** beneficiaries: **Gujarat, Maharashtra, MP, Rajasthan** {1999, 2004}
- Sardar Sarovar is on the **Narmada** {1992}
- Most benefit (by water and power share): **Madhya Pradesh** {1993}
- **Harsud** town was submerged by the **Indira Sagar** reservoir {2003}
- **Tawa project-Krishna** is wrong; the Tawa is a **Narmada** tributary {2015}

@section 14.4 | Chambal, Damodar and Others
- **Gobind Sagar** (Bhakra) and **G.B. Pant Sagar** (Rihand) are **not** Chambal projects {2011}
- **Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar** is in **Uttar Pradesh** (Sonbhadra) {2013}
- Benefits shared by more than one state: **Chambal Valley** (MP and Rajasthan) {2007, 2009}
- **Kaveri dispute**: **Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Puducherry** {2010}
- **Damodar** was Bengal's "River of Sorrow": fast upstream, sluggish downstream (R explains A) {2010}
- **Tilaiya-Konar** is wrong; Tilaiya is on the **Barakar** {2013}
- Match: **Tilaiya-Barakar, Panchet-Damodar, Rana Pratap Sagar-Chambal, Matatila-Betwa** {2014}
- All true: Rihand on a **Son** tributary; Hirakud on the **Mahanadi**; Tungabhadra is **AP-Karnataka**; Maithon on the **Barakar** {2016}
