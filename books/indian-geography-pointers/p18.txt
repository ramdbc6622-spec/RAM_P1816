@chapter 18 | Silk, Plantation Crops and Spices
@meta Built from 34 UPPSC questions (1994–2023). Focus: silk types and states, tea, coffee, rubber, spices, commodity boards.

@hot

@section 18.1 | Silk
- Over three-quarters of raw silk came from **Karnataka and AP** in 2006 (no longer true, *) {2006}
- Leading **mulberry** silk state: **Karnataka** {2017}
- **Eri silk-Assam** is correct {2008}
- True: Karnataka = largest raw silk producer; **Jharkhand** = largest **tasar** producer {2010}
- **Muga** silk is found only in India, in **Assam** {2008}
- Largest silk **fabric** producer: **Karnataka** {2013}; most silk **yarn**: **Karnataka** {2017}

@table

@end

@section 18.2 | Tea
- Largest tea producer: **Assam** {1994}
- Over 200 cm rain on sloping hills: ideal for **tea** {2010}
- India was **first** in tea output and use in 2007-08; **China** leads now, India second {2008}
- India exports much tea, but home consumption is **not** poor (A true, R false) {2007}
- "**Green Gold**" = **tea** {2008}
- Tea is a **stimulant** crop {2017}

@section 18.3 | Coffee and Rubber
- Largest coffee producer: **Karnataka** {2002, 2004, 2006, 2010}; over **70%** of output {2016}
- Largest coffee **area**: **Karnataka** {2002}
- Largest rubber producer: **Kerala** {2004, 2014}; **Kerala-Rubber** correct {2022}
- **Rubber-Himachal** is wrong {2017}

@section 18.4 | Plantations, Tobacco and Coconut
- Main plantation crops: **tea, rubber, coconut, coffee** {2001}
- True: **tea = Assam**, **tobacco = Andhra Pradesh**; coffee is not Tamil Nadu's main crop; jute is not Chhattisgarh's {2001}
- Over a third of tobacco: **Andhra Pradesh** (then); **Gujarat** now {2006}
- Largest tobacco producer per the 2007 key: **Uttar Pradesh** {2007}
- Largest tobacco area (2010): **Andhra Pradesh**; **Gujarat** now {2012}
- Largest coconut producer: **Kerala** (then); **Karnataka** since 2023-24 {2006, 2014}

@section 18.5 | Spices, Cashew and Boards
- India leads in **ginger** (and turmeric, chilli); not in cardamom or cloves now (*) {2010}
- **Odisha** is **not** a cardamom state {2014}
- "**Garden of spices**": **Kerala** {2010, 2011, 2013}
- Largest cardamom and pepper producer: **Kerala** {2018}
- Biggest spice producer (2012 key): **Madhya Pradesh** {2012}
- Cloves grow in Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka (*) {2007}
- Major **cashew** producer among the options: **Maharashtra** {2013}
- **Coffee Board-Hyderabad** is wrong; it is in **Bengaluru** {2023}
- **National Horticulture Board**: **1984** {2014}

@trap
- Leaders that **changed**: coconut (Kerala to **Karnataka**), tobacco (AP to **Gujarat**), world tea (India to **China**).
- Leaders that **did not change**: coffee **Karnataka**, rubber **Kerala**, tea **Assam**, mulberry silk **Karnataka**, muga **Assam**.
@end
