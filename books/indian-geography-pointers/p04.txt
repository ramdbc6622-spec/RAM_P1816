@chapter 4 | States and Union Territories
@meta Built from 18 UPPSC questions (1990–2019). Focus: area ranks, shared borders, capitals, NCR, Union Territories.

@hot
- Area ranks: **Rajasthan > MP > Maharashtra > UP**; UP is **4th** by area {2016, 2019, ?}
- **NCR** includes parts of **Haryana, UP and Rajasthan** only; not Punjab or MP {2011, 2017, ?}

@end

@section 4.1 | Places and their States
- **Palanpur** is in **Gujarat** {2004}
- **Malnad** belongs to **Karnataka**, not Andhra Pradesh {2000}

@section 4.2 | Size, Population and Districts
- UP is **4th** by area {2016}
- About **30%** of India's area lies in **Rajasthan, MP and Maharashtra** {?}
- **Maharashtra**: **2nd** in population, **3rd** in area (2011) {2019}
- Most districts: **Uttar Pradesh** (75) {2006}
- **Thar**: densely populated desert, about 10,000 years old, spreading partly through human misuse; less grazing land as farming spreads (all four true) {2008}

@end

@section 4.3 | Shared Borders
- In 1993 undivided MP touched **7 states**; today MP touches **5** {1993}
- **Chhattisgarh** does **not** border **Bihar** {2013}
- **Sonbhadra** touches the boundaries of **four states** {2015}
- **Telangana** borders **AP, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh** {2015}

@up UP borders **8 states + Delhi**, the most of any state: Uttarakhand, **Himachal** (Saharanpur), Haryana, Rajasthan, MP, **Chhattisgarh** and Jharkhand (both via Sonbhadra), Bihar. It does **not** border Punjab or Gujarat. It has **75 districts**, the most in India.

@section 4.4 | Capitals and the NCR
- **Manipur-Aizawl** is wrong; Manipur's capital is **Imphal** (Aizawl is Mizoram's) {1990}
- **Assam-Dispur, Nagaland-Kohima, Arunachal-Itanagar, Meghalaya-Shillong** {1994}
- NCR towns among the options: **Khurja, Karnal, Rohtak** (not Ambala) {2011}
- NCR does **not** include **Punjab** or **Madhya Pradesh** {?, 2017}

@section 4.5 | Union Territories
- Largest UT among the options in 2014: **Delhi**; now **Ladakh** is the largest UT {2014}
- **Puducherry** is spread over **Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Andhra Pradesh** (Puducherry, Karaikal, Mahe, Yanam) {2006}

@flow Union Territories since 2019 (now 8)
31 October 2019 | J&K state split into UTs J&K and Ladakh (UTs: 9)
26 January 2020 | Dadra and Nagar Haveli merged with Daman and Diu (UTs: 8)
UTs with a legislature | Delhi, Puducherry, Jammu and Kashmir
Largest / smallest UT | Ladakh / Lakshadweep
@end

@trap
- The NCR question repeats with different wrong options: **Punjab**, **Himachal**, **MP**. Only Haryana, UP and Rajasthan are in the NCR.
- Older keys call **Delhi** the largest UT; after 2019 it is **Ladakh**.
@end

@last
- Area: Rajasthan, MP, Maharashtra, **UP (4th)**
- Population: **UP** first; Maharashtra 2nd (and 3rd by area)
- UP: most districts (75); borders 8 states + Delhi
- **Sonbhadra** touches 4 states
- Chhattisgarh does not touch Bihar
- **Imphal** (Manipur), **Aizawl** (Mizoram), **Dispur** (Assam)
- NCR = Haryana, UP, Rajasthan
- **Ladakh** largest UT; 8 UTs today
- Puducherry in TN, Kerala, AP
- **Malnad** = Karnataka; **Palanpur** = Gujarat
@end
