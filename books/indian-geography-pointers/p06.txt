@chapter 6 | Drainage I: The Himalayan Rivers
@meta Built from 36 UPPSC questions (1990–2025). Focus: the Ganga system, Satluj, Bundelkhand rivers, Brahmaputra and Indus.

@hot
- **Satluj** is the most-asked river: rises at **Rakas Tal** (Tibet), enters India at **Shipki La**, cuts across **all Himalayan ranges**, is **trans-Himalayan** and **does not rise in India** {2010, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2020, 2023}
- **Devprayag** = **Alaknanda + Bhagirathi**; the river is called **Ganga** from here {2002, 2016, 2023, 2024}
- **Betwa** (Bundelkhand): joins the **Yamuna**; **Rajghat dam** is on it; **Ken-Betwa** (UP-MP) is India's first river link {2006, 2006, 2014, 2015}
- Himalayan rivers are **perennial** because of **snow and glacier melt**, not the monsoon (Assertion-Reason) {2011, 2025}
@end

@section 6.1 | Nature of Himalayan Rivers
- Himalayan rivers are **perennial**: summer **snow-melt** keeps them flowing; the monsoon explains only the rainy-season flood {2011, 2025}
- **Ganga** is an **antecedent** river: older than the mountains, it kept cutting down as they rose {2013}
- **Chambal** is a **superimposed** river (also Son, Banas, Subarnarekha) {2007}
- **Satluj** cuts across **all ranges** of the Himalaya (Zaskar, Greater, Lesser, Shiwalik) {2012, 2014}
- **Trans-Himalayan** rivers (rise north of the Himalaya): **Satluj**, Indus, Brahmaputra {2020}

@table
Drainage type | Key idea | Example
Antecedent | Older than the mountain | Ganga, Indus, Satluj, Brahmaputra
Consequent | Follows the original slope | Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri
Subsequent | Along weak rock bands | Strike-valley tributaries
Superimposed | Let down onto older rocks | Chambal, Son, Banas
@end

@section 6.2 | The Ganga and its Headwaters
- Ganga rises as the **Bhagirathi** at **Gaumukh** (Gangotri glacier); called **Ganga** from **Devprayag** {2002, 2016, 2023, 2024}
- **Badrinath** is on the **Alaknanda**; **Kedarnath** on the **Mandakini** {2002, 2013}
- **Rudraprayag** = Alaknanda + **Mandakini**; **Karnaprayag** = Alaknanda + **Pindar**; **Tehri** = Bhagirathi + **Bhilangna** {2002, 2024}
- Adi Shankaracharya's four mathas: **Jyotirmath, Sringeri, Dwarka, Puri**; **Kedarnath is not** one of them {2002}
- In Bangladesh the Ganga is the **Padma**; the Bhagirathi-Hooghly branch flows past Kolkata {2016}
- Ganga pollution comes from **sewage and industrial waste** (Kanpur tanneries); "holier = more polluted" is false {2005}

@join Panch Prayag: where the Alaknanda's tributaries join
trunk: Alaknanda (from Satopanth glacier)
L|Dhauliganga|Vishnuprayag
R|Nandakini|Nandprayag
L|Pindar|Karnaprayag
R|Mandakini|Rudraprayag
L|Bhagirathi|Devprayag
end: = GANGA (from Devprayag)
@end

@trap
- **Devprayag** (Bhagirathi) and **Rudraprayag** (Mandakini) are the usual swap. Tehri is **Bhagirathi + Bhilangna**, not an Alaknanda confluence.
@end

@end

@section 6.3 | Tributaries of the Ganga
- **Gomti** is the only Ganga tributary that **rises in the plains**: Phulhar Tal, **Pilibhit**; flows past Lucknow and Jaunpur {2008}
- **Yamuna** is **perennial**; it is "dead" below Delhi because water is diverted and sewage added (A true, R false) {2015}
- **Ramganga** joins the **Ganga** (at Kannauj), **not** the Yamuna {2010}
- Yamuna's right-bank tributaries, **west to east**: **Chambal, Sindh, Betwa, Ken** {2020}
- **Son** has the largest catchment among Son, Gandak, Ramganga and Mahananda (about 70,000 sq km) {2017}
- **Gandak** does **not** flow through UP; it flows through Bihar {2021}
- **Jonk** belongs to the **Mahanadi** basin, not the Ganga {2021}
- **Kosi**, the "Sorrow of Bihar", **shifts its course** the most {2013}
- **Mahatma Gandhi Setu** (Patna-Hajipur) is in **Bihar** {1993}
- **Moribund delta** = western part of the **Bengal (Ganga-Brahmaputra) delta** {2019}
- Tributary match: **Ganga-Son, Godavari-Manjira, Krishna-Bhima, Yamuna-Ken** {2003}

@up Gomti (Pilibhit to Lucknow to Jaunpur to Kaithi), the Yamuna's Bundelkhand tributaries (Chambal near Etawah, Betwa at **Hamirpur**, Ken at Chilla in **Banda**) and the Kanpur-Prayagraj-Varanasi stretch of the Ganga are UP's most-asked river facts.

@section 6.4 | Ken-Betwa and Bundelkhand
- **Ken-Betwa Link**: **UP and MP**, MoU **2005**; India's **first** river-interlinking project; foundation stone **25 Dec 2024** {2006}
- **Betwa** joins the **Yamuna** (at Hamirpur) {2014}
- **Rajghat dam** (Lalitpur, joint UP-MP project) is on the **Betwa** {2006, 2015}

@flow Ken-Betwa Link: direction of water
Ken river | Daudhan dam, Panna (MP)
Link canal (221 km) | Carries surplus Ken water north-west
Betwa river | Feeds Bundelkhand: Jhansi, Lalitpur, Mahoba, Banda (UP)
Yamuna | Betwa joins it at Hamirpur
@end

@trap
- Water goes **from the Ken to the Betwa** (Daudhan dam on the Ken). Both are tributaries of the **Yamuna**, not the Ganga.
@end

@section 6.5 | The Brahmaputra System
- **Indus and Brahmaputra** rise at almost the same point, near **Mansarovar-Kailash** {2006}
- Brahmaputra basin: **Tibet, India, Bangladesh** (and Bhutan) {1990}
- **Teesta** was once a Ganga tributary; after the **1787 flood** it joined the **Brahmaputra**: **river capture** (A and R true, R explains A) {2021}
- **Sankosh** forms the **Assam-West Bengal** boundary {2007}

@flow One river, four names: the Brahmaputra
Yarlung Tsangpo | Tibet; rises at Chemayungdung glacier near Mansarovar
Siang / Dihang | Great bend round Namcha Barwa into Arunachal Pradesh
Brahmaputra | Assam, after the Dibang and Lohit join near Sadiya; Majuli island
Jamuna | Bangladesh; joins the Padma, then the Meghna
@end

@section 6.6 | The Indus System
- **Satluj** rises at **Mansarovar-Rakas Tal** and enters India at **Shipki La** {2023}
- **Satluj does not rise in India**; Beas, Ravi and Chenab do {2010, 2013}
- **Kishanganga** (Neelum) is a tributary of the **Jhelum** {2013}
- **Doodh Ganga** flows in **Jammu and Kashmir** (also a stream of that name in Uttarakhand) {2015}
- World's **highest railway bridge**: on the **Chenab** (Reasi, 359 m; opened June 2025) {2006}

River | Source

Ravi | Near Rohtang
Beas | Beas Kund
Satluj | Rakas Tal (Tibet)
@end
