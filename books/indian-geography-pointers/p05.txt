@chapter 5 | Tribes of India
@meta Built from 35 UPPSC questions (1991–2022). Focus: tribe-state matching, the Tharu and Bhotia, Toda polyandry, Andaman tribes.

@hot
- **Tribe-state matching** is asked almost every year: **Toda = Tamil Nadu (Nilgiris), Lepcha = Sikkim, Birhor/Santhal = Jharkhand, Gaddi = Himachal, Jarawa = Andaman** {2002, 2002, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2019, 2020, 2022}
- **Tharu** live in **Uttarakhand and UP**; largest ST of Uttarakhand; **Diwali is mourning** for them {1991, 2005, 2005, 2015, 2017, ?}
- **Toda** (Nilgiri hills) and **Jaunsari** practise **polyandry** {1993, 2005, 2006, 2009, 2013}
- **Gaddi** = tribe of the **Dhauladhar** range (Himachal) {2001, 2002, 2003}
- **Jarawa** live in the **Andaman and Nicobar Islands** {2013, 2016, ?}
@end

@section 5.1 | Racial Groups and Identity
- North-east hill tribes are **Mongoloid** {2003}
- **Santhal** are **Proto-Australoid** {2002}
- Tribes are identified by **distinct culture and separate habitat** {1992}

@table
Racial group | Example tribes
Negrito | Onge, Jarawa, Sentinelese, Great Andamanese
Proto-Australoid | Santhal, Munda, Ho, Oraon, Bhil
Mongoloid | Naga, Khasi, Garo, Lepcha, Bhutia
@end

@section 5.2 | Tribes of UP and Uttarakhand
- **Tharu**: **Uttarakhand and Uttar Pradesh** {1991, 2017}
- **Diwali** is a festival of **mourning** for the **Tharu** {2005, 2015, ?}
- Largest ST of Uttarakhand: **Tharu** {2005}
- **Transhumance** is practised by the **Bhotia** {1997}
- "Bhotia-UP" pairs have **no wrong option** (Bhotia are notified in UP too), so the source marks them (*) {2004, 2010}

@trap
- **Bhotia** (UP, Uttarakhand) and **Bhutia** (Sikkim) are **different** tribes. A "Bhutia" option in a Sikkim pair is correct; a "Bhotia-UP" pair is also correct.
@end

@section 5.3 | Customs and Festivals
- **Polyandry**: **Toda**; **Jaunsari and Toda** (not Gond or Naga) {2005, 2006, 2009}
- **Jhum** (shifting cultivation) is practised by the **Khasi** {2010}
- **Sarhul** (sal-blossom festival): **Oraon, Munda, Santhal** of Jharkhand {2005}
- **Lohasur** (iron god) is worshipped by the **Agaria** (iron smelters) {2016}
- **Onam-Andhra Pradesh** is wrong; Onam is **Kerala's** {2005}
- **Sikkim-Tibetan** is wrong as the main language; it is **Nepali** {2006}
- **Khasi and Garo** speakers live in **Meghalaya** {2002}

@end

@section 5.5 | Andaman and Nicobar Tribes
- **Jarawa** live in the **Andaman and Nicobar Islands**; called India's **most primitive** tribe {2013, 2016, ?}
- **Rengma** is a **Naga** tribe, not native to the islands {2020}

@flow Island tribes, north to south
Great Andamanese | Strait Island (Negrito)
Jarawa | South and Middle Andaman (Negrito)
Sentinelese | North Sentinel (Negrito; no contact)
Onge | Little Andaman (Negrito)
Nicobarese | Car Nicobar (Mongoloid; largest)
Shompen | Great Nicobar (Mongoloid)
@end

@up UP's main tribes are the **Tharu** (Tarai: Lakhimpur Kheri, Bahraich, Balrampur), **Buksa** and **Bhotia**. The Tharu mourn on **Diwali**. **Sonbhadra** has Gond, Kharwar and Cheru communities.

@last
- **Bhil** = largest tribe
- **Toda** = Nilgiris, polyandry; **Jaunsari** = polyandry
- **Tharu** = UP and Uttarakhand; Diwali mourning
- **Bhotia** = transhumance; not the same as Bhutia (Sikkim)
- **Gaddi** = Dhauladhar (HP)
- **Lepcha** = Sikkim; **Birhor, Santhal** = Jharkhand
- **Khasi, Garo** = Meghalaya; Khasi practise jhum
- **Sarhul** = Oraon, Munda; **Agaria** worship Lohasur
- **Jarawa, Onge, Sentinelese** = Andaman (Negrito)
- **Rengma** = Naga, not Andaman
@end
