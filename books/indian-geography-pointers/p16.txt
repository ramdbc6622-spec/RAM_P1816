@chapter 16 | Major Crops I: Crop Seasons, Wheat and Rice
@meta Built from 44 UPPSC questions (2001–2024). Focus: kharif and rabi crops, wheat varieties and diseases, rice area, yield and production.

@section 16.1 | Crop Seasons
- **Mustard** is a **rabi** crop; not kharif {2016, 2012}
- **Soybean** is a **kharif** crop {2004}
- **Gram** is **not** a kharif crop (it is rabi) {2011}
- **Zaid** crops in irrigated areas: **moong and urad** {2011}
- Rabi crops lead in Punjab-Haryana, but winter rain there comes from **western disturbances**, not cyclones (A true, R false) {2023}
- **Rice** is a **transplanted** crop {2017}

@table
Season | Sown | Harvested | Crops
Kharif | June-July | Sept-Oct | Rice, maize, bajra, arhar, cotton, soybean, groundnut
Rabi | Oct-Nov | March-April | Wheat, barley, gram, mustard, pea, potato
Zaid | March | June | Melons, cucumber, moong, urad
@end

@section 16.2 | Wheat: States and Output
- Top three wheat states: **UP, Punjab, Haryana** (then; MP now second) {2006}
- Largest wheat producer (2015-16): **Uttar Pradesh** {2016}
- UP ranks first in **wheat and sugarcane** (and rice since 2024-25) (*) {2008}
- UP's biggest cropping pattern is **rice-wheat**; wheat's most critical irrigation is **crown root initiation**, not flowering {2013}
- Wheat output **increased** from 2006-07 to 2009-10 {2009}

@section 16.3 | Wheat Varieties and Diseases
- Induced mutation: **Sonora-64** in the key (strictly **Sharbati Sonora**, from Sonora-64) {2015}
- Dwarfing gene in wheat: **Norin-10** {2013}
- **Kalyan Sona**, **Raj 3077**, **Pusa Sindhu Ganga**, **UP-308** are **wheat** {2005, 2015, 2016, 2016}
- **Macaroni (durum)** wheat suits **unirrigated** conditions {2004}
- **Triticale** = **wheat × rye** {2014}
- Wheat disease: **rust**; **Karnal bunt** {2003, 2004, 2015}
- Arhar for double cropping with wheat: **UPAS-120** {2010}

@table
Disease | Crop
Rust, Karnal bunt, loose smut | Wheat
Blast; Khaira (zinc lack) | Rice
Tikka | Groundnut
Red rot | Sugarcane
Wilt | Arhar, gram
@end

@section 16.4 | Rice: Area and Production
- Rice originated in **South-East Asia** {2015}
- Chief food crop of India: **rice** {2004}
- Largest crop area: **rice** {2011}
- Rice needs over **100 cm** of rain {2007}
- Largest rice **area**: **UP**; largest producer (then): UP per the key {2007, 2006}
- Wrong pair: Bihar's share of rice output is **not 15.7%** {2024}
- **Aman** paddy is sown in **June-July** (harvested in winter) {2011}
- More than half of rice output: the leading states named in the key (*) {2004, 2009}
- Four main rice producers (then): **WB, Punjab, UP, AP** (*) {2004}
- Ascending order: **Tamil Nadu, Punjab, Andhra Pradesh, West Bengal** {2003}
- Highest rice **yield**: **Punjab** {2015, 2017}
- Punjab **exports** rice, but it does **not** lead in production (A true, R false) {2001}

@flow Rice: three different "largest" answers
Largest area | Uttar Pradesh
Highest yield per hectare | Punjab
Largest production | West Bengal (most years); UP in 2024-25
Largest exporter (world) | India
@end

@section 16.5 | Rice Varieties
- **Jaya, Padma, Krishna**: **rice** {2006}
- **Pusa Sugandha-5**: aromatic **rice** {2006}
- **Mahi Sugandha**: **rice** {2015}
- **Barani Deep**: **paddy** {2013}
- Hybrid basmati: **Pusa RH-10** {2015}
- Seed rate for transplanted basmati: **15-20 kg/ha** {2014}
- Largest area under **hybrid rice**: **Uttar Pradesh** {2014}

@trap
- **Gram and mustard are rabi**; **soybean is kharif**. **Moong and urad** can be kharif or zaid.
- **Norin-10** = wheat dwarfing gene; **Dee-geo-woo-gen** = rice.
@end

@up UP is India's **largest wheat producer**, has the **largest rice area** and the largest **hybrid rice** area, and became the top **rice producer** in 2024-25. Its main cropping pattern is **rice-wheat**. Wheat variety **UP-308** and arhar **UPAS-120** come from UP-Pantnagar breeding.
