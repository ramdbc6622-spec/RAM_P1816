@chapter 3 | Coastal Regions and Islands
@meta Built from 21 UPPSC questions (2001–2016). Focus: longest coastline, named coasts, island channels, Barren and Lakshadweep.

@hot
- **Gujarat** has the **longest coastline** {2006, 2007, 2016, 2018}
- **Ten Degree Channel** separates the **Andaman** group from the **Nicobar** group {2005, ?}
- **Barren Island** (Andaman Sea, Bay of Bengal) is India's only **active volcano** {2010, ?}
@end

@section 3.1 | Sea Level and Maritime Zones
- India's **mean sea level** is measured at **Chennai** {2013}
- **Territorial waters**: **12 nautical miles** from the coast {2005}
- Longest coastline: **Gujarat** (about 2,340 km) {2006, 2007, 2016, 2018}
- Coastal states: **9** (plus 4 coastal UTs) {?}

@flow Maritime zones, from the coast outward
Territorial sea | 12 nautical miles; full sovereignty
Contiguous zone | 24 nm; customs, immigration
Exclusive Economic Zone | 200 nm; fishing, mining, drilling
Continental shelf | To the margin edge (up to 350 nm)
@end

@trap
- Coastline length changed: old figure **7,516.6 km**, new count **11,098.81 km**. Read the options. **Goa** has the shortest state coastline.
@end

@section 3.2 | Named Coasts and Coastal Towns
- **Coromandel** coast: **Krishna delta to Cape Comorin** {2015}
- Northern part of the western coastal plain: **Konkan** {2016}
- West-coast towns north to south: **Janjira, Sindhudurg, Kannur, Nagercoil** {2001}
- West-coast towns among the options: **Janjira, Udupi** (Auroville and Tuticorin are on the east coast) {2002}
- Beaches: **Digha-WB, Gopalpur-Odisha, Calangute-Goa, Marina-Tamil Nadu** {2008}

@flow West coast, north to south
Kathiawar coast | Gujarat: gulfs of Kutch, Khambhat
Konkan | Daman to Goa; Mumbai
Kannada coast | Goa to Mangaluru
Malabar | Mangaluru to Kanyakumari; backwaters (kayals)
@end

@flow East coast, north to south
Utkal coast | Odisha; Chilika lake
Northern Circars | Mahanadi to Krishna
Coromandel | Krishna delta to Kanyakumari; Pulicat
@end

@section 3.3 | Andaman and Nicobar Islands
- Highest peak: **Saddle Peak** (North Andaman) {2009}
- **Ten Degree Channel**: **Andaman** from **Nicobar** {2005, ?}
- **Barren Island** is in the **Bay of Bengal** (Andaman Sea); India's **volcanic** island {2010, ?}

@table
Channel | Separates
Ten Degree | Andaman group from Nicobar group
Duncan Passage | South Andaman from Little Andaman
Coco Channel | North Andaman from Coco Is. (Myanmar)
Nine Degree | Minicoy from main Lakshadweep
Eight Degree | Minicoy from the Maldives
Palk Strait | Tamil Nadu from Sri Lanka
@end

@section 3.4 | Lakshadweep and Other Islands
- **Lakshadweep** is a **coral** island group {2013}
- **Minicoy** is one of the remotest islands from the Indian coast {2013}
- **Rameswaram** lies between India and Sri Lanka {2006}
- **Adam's Bridge** begins at **Dhanushkodi** {2010}
- Most populous island: **Salsette** (Mumbai) {2016}
- Match: **Viyant Syodhar-Gulf of Kutch, Piram-Gulf of Khambhat, Dwarka-Arabian Sea coast, Diu-Kathiawar coast** {2010}
- **Kori Creek** is in the **Rann of Kutch** {2008, 2011}

@trap
- **Ten Degree** = Andaman-Nicobar. **Nine and Eight Degree** belong to the Lakshadweep side. Barren is **active**; Narcondam is **dormant**.
@end

@up UP is landlocked, but UPPSC asks island and coast facts as standard map knowledge. Remember **Salsette** (Mumbai), **Majuli** (largest river island) and **Sriharikota** (ISRO launch site).
