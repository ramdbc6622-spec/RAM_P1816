@section 8.1 | Waterfalls
- Highest waterfall: **Kunchikal** {2018}; height **455 m** {2015}
- **Jog-Krishna** is wrong; Jog is on the **Sharavati** {2005}
- **Budha Ghagh-Kanchi** is wrong; Budha Ghagh is on the **Burha**; **Dassam** is on the Kanchi {2021}
- Match: **Dudhsagar-Mandovi, Duduma-Machkund, Gokak-Ghataprabha, Jog-Sharavati** {2017}
- Match: **Dudhsagar-Goa, Barkana-Karnataka, Khandadhar-Odisha, Palani-Himachal** {2022}
- Waterfall in **Goa**: **Dudhsagar** {2014}
- **Chitrakote** (the "Niagara of India") is on the **Indravati** {2016}, in **Bastar** {2017}

@trap
- **Palani waterfall** is in **Himachal** (Kullu), not the Palani hills of Tamil Nadu.
- **Nohkalikai** (Meghalaya) is the tallest single plunge in many sources; pick Kunchikal when it is offered.
@end

@section 8.2 | Coastal Lagoons and Southern Lakes
- **Vembanad** lake is in **Kerala** {1997}
- Largest saline (brackish) lake: **Chilika**; largest **inland** salt lake: **Sambhar** {2021}
- **Chilika** lies on the **Northern Circars** coast (Odisha) {2008, 2016}
- Largest lagoon on the east coast: **Chilika** {2021}
- Lake shared by two states: **Pulicat** (Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu) {2008}
- **Loktak-Meghalaya** is wrong; Loktak is in **Manipur** {2005}
- **Kolleru-Andhra Pradesh** is correct {2010, 2012}

@up **Fulhar (Phulhar) Tal** in Pilibhit is UP's only lake asked so far: it is the source of the **Gomti**. UP has Ramsar sites such as **Nawabganj, Sandi, Samaspur, Parvati Arga, Saman, Sarsai Nawar, Sur Sarovar (Keetham)** and **Haiderpur**.
