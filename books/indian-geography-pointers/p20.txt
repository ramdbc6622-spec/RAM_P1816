@section 20.1 | Rock Systems
- Most mineralised rock system: **Dharwar** {1995, 2005, 2017}
- Iron ore occurs in **Dharwar** rocks {1994, 2012, 2014, 2019}
- **Vindhyan** rocks are rich in **limestone** {2004}
- **Gondwana** = about 95% of coal; ferrous and non-ferrous minerals are in Dharwar (both true, R not the explanation) {2017}

@section 20.2 | Critical Minerals
- **Lithium** inferred resources at **Reasi** (J&K); India imports lithium, nickel, cobalt (both true) {2023}

@section 20.3 | Iron Ore
- Iron-ore regions: **Kudremukh and Singhbhum** (*) {1992}
- Match: **Gurumahisani-Odisha, Bailadila-Chhattisgarh, Noamundi-Jharkhand, Kudremukh-Karnataka** {2025}
- Match: **Badam Pahar-Odisha, Dalli-Rajhara-Chhattisgarh, Kudremukh-Karnataka, Noamundi-Jharkhand** {2010}
- **Bailadila-MP** is wrong; it is in **Chhattisgarh** {2004}
- Largest mechanised mine: **Bailadila** {2015}
- Top iron-ore states (2020-21): **Odisha and Chhattisgarh** {2022}
- True: richest deposits in **Karnataka** (per key); **Odisha** is the largest producer {2010}
- **Iron ore-Guntur** is wrong {2017}

@section 20.4 | Manganese, Chromite, Nickel
- Leading **manganese** producer: **Maharashtra** (MP for many years; Odisha has most reserves) {2008, 2012}
- **Srikakulam** is **not** in Karnataka (it is in AP) {2023}
- Monopoly in **chromite**: **Odisha** (Sukinda) {2008, 2019}

@section 20.5 | Copper, Zinc, Bauxite, Tin
- Largest **copper** reserves: **Rajasthan** {2011}
- Match: **Chandrapur-Maharashtra, Hassan-Karnataka, Khammam-AP, Khetri-Rajasthan** {2011}
- Near monopoly in **zinc**: **Rajasthan** {2013}
- **Bauxite** = ore of **aluminium** {1997}
- Top bauxite states: **Odisha and Gujarat** {2021, ?}
- **Tin**: reserves and production in **Chhattisgarh** (Dantewada) {2009, 2015}

@section 20.6 | Mica, Asbestos and Stones
- Largest mica producer: **Rajasthan** now (Andhra Pradesh in 2001-04) {2001, 2004}
- **MP** does **not** produce mica {2003, 2010}
- Largest mica belt: **Hazaribagh, Gaya, Munger** {2009}
- India led the world in **sheet mica** in 2001 (*) {2001}
- Asbestos: no state produces it now (*) {2015}
- Self-sufficiency in 1991: **graphite** (*) {1991}
- Best **marble**: **Makrana** (Rajasthan); marble is a **metamorphic** rock {1992, 1991}
- **Garnet** is linked with metamorphic rock {2010}
- **Granite** slabs and slates: **Lalitpur** (UP) {2002}
- Sonbhadra minerals: **andalusite, pyrite, dolomite** (not uranium) {2001}

@section 20.7 | Atomic Minerals, Gold, Diamonds
- **Monazite** is abundant in **Kerala**; also Tamil Nadu {2013, 2002}
- **Jaduguda** (Jharkhand): **uranium** {2007}
- Match: **Domiasiat-Meghalaya, Lambapur-AP (Telangana), Rohil-Rajasthan, Gogi-Karnataka** {2008}
- Diamond mines: **Madhya Pradesh** (Panna) {2014}
