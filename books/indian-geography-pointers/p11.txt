@chapter 11 | Soils
@meta Built from 29 UPPSC questions (1998–2024). Focus: black (regur) soil, laterite, usar and saline soils, erosion.

@hot
- **Black (regur)** soil: from **Deccan basalt**, best for **cotton**, holds moisture; it is **not** rich in humus {1998, 1999, 2004, 2010, 2010, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2018}
- **Laterite**: heavy rain and leaching; **poor in lime**; Malabar coast, Maharashtra {2000, 2001, 2006, 2023}

@end

@section 11.1 | Black (Regur) Soils
- Formed from weathered **basaltic lava**: **regur** {2015}
- Black cotton soil = **regur** {2016}
- Regur is **black**, but **not** because of humus (A true, R false) {1999}
- Best for cotton: **regur** {2014}; good for cotton, not rich in humus {2004, 2013}
- Lava soils: **Malwa plateau** {1998}; Malwa's main soil: **black** {2010}
- Needs little irrigation because it **retains water**: **black** {2010}
- Wrong statement: "black soil is found in the **Himalaya**" {2018}

@section 11.2 | Red, Laterite and Alluvial Soils
- **Red soil**: second-largest group; it surrounds the black soil (both true, R not the explanation) {2024}
- Red colour comes from **diffused iron**, not high iron content; poor in N, P and humus (both true, R not the explanation) {2023}
- Laterite: only statement 1 correct {2023}
- Laterite is **not rich in lime** {2001}
- Laterite found in **Maharashtra** {2006}; mainly on the **Malabar coast** {2000}
- Most productive soil: **alluvial** {2014}
- Match: **Alluvial-UP, Black-Maharashtra, Red-Andhra Pradesh, Desert-Rajasthan** {2021}
- Tea needs **acidic** soil {2002}
- Most deficient micronutrient: **zinc** {2004}
- Himalayan soils are **not** rich in humus overall (A false, R true) {2004}
- Particle under **0.002 mm**: **clay** {2016}

@table
Soil | Rich in | Poor in | Crop
Alluvial | Potash, lime | N, P | Wheat, rice, sugarcane
Black | Lime, iron, potash | N, P, humus | Cotton, soybean
Red | Iron (spread thin) | N, P, humus | Millets, groundnut
Laterite | Iron, aluminium | **Lime**, N, potash | Tea, coffee, cashew
Desert | Salts, phosphate | N, humus | Bajra
Saline/alkaline | Sodium salts | Calcium, N | Salt-tolerant crops
@end

@flow Soil particle sizes, finest to coarsest
Clay | Below 0.002 mm
Silt | 0.002-0.05 mm
Sand | 0.05-2 mm
Gravel | Above 2 mm
@end

@section 11.3 | Saline and Alkaline (Usar) Soils
- Usar soil: **pH above 7**; **paddy can grow** after reclamation; it is reclaimed with **gypsum**, not lime {2019}
- Largest **alkali** area: **Uttar Pradesh** {2007}
- Largest **saline** area: **Gujarat** {2012, 2017}
- Saline soils are unfit for crops because of **dry climate and poor drainage** (R explains A) {2023}

@trap
- **Saline** = Gujarat (Rann of Kutch). **Alkaline / usar** = Uttar Pradesh. **Gypsum** reclaims usar, not lime.
@end

@section 11.4 | Soil Erosion
- Soil loss, smallest to largest: **splash, sheet, rill, gully** {2023}
- Critical erosion: **Shiwalik foothills** {2004}; worst affected: **Chambal valley** (ravines) {2004}
- Erosion is prevented by **afforestation** {2014}

@flow Erosion stages (least to most soil loss)
Splash | Raindrops loosen soil
Sheet | A thin layer washes off evenly
Rill | Small channels form
Gully | Deep ravines (Chambal, Yamuna)
@end

@up UP has India's largest **usar (alkaline)** area: Raebareli, Unnao, Pratapgarh, Kanpur, Etawah. It is reclaimed with **gypsum**. The **Chambal-Yamuna ravines** (Etawah, Agra, Jalaun) are UP's worst gully-erosion zone. Most of UP is **alluvial** (Bhangar and Khadar); Bundelkhand has red and mixed soils.

@last
- **Regur** = basalt = cotton = holds water; not humus
- Lava soil = **Malwa**
- Red colour = **diffused** iron
- **Laterite** = no lime; Malabar
- **Alluvial** = most productive; UP
- Tea = **acidic** soil; **zinc** most deficient
- **Usar** = UP, pH > 7, gypsum
- **Saline** = Gujarat
- Erosion: splash, sheet, rill, **gully** (Chambal)
- Clay < 0.002 mm
@end
