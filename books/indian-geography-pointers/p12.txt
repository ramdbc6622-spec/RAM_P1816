@section 12.1 | Forest Types and Trees
- **Teak and sal**: **tropical dry deciduous** forests {2007}
- Teak grows in **MP** (largest), Karnataka, Maharashtra, UP; more than one option fits (*) {2012}
- **Deodar-Nilgiri hills** is wrong; deodar grows in the **Himalaya** {2023}
- Match: **Teak-central India, Deodar-high Himalaya, Sundari-Sundarbans, Cinchona-Himalayan Tarai** (per key) {1992}
- **Cinchona** does **not** grow in **Chhattisgarh** {2010}
- Match: **moist deciduous-Tarai, dry deciduous-middle Ganga plain, alpine-Arunachal, evergreen-Sahyadri** {2001}
- Match: **tropical-Silent Valley, conifer-Himachal, mangrove-Sundarbans, deciduous-Rajasthan** (per key) {2017}
- Plant with **no flowers**: **fern** {1991}

@flow Forest belts, wettest to driest
Tropical evergreen | Over 200 cm: Western Ghats, north-east, Andamans
Moist deciduous | 100-200 cm: Tarai, sal belt
Dry deciduous | 70-100 cm: teak; middle Ganga plain, Deccan
Thorn forest | Below 70 cm: khejri, babool; Rajasthan
@end

@section 12.2 | The Himalayan Tree Line
- The tree line is **lower** in the **Western** Himalaya than in the Eastern (cooler and drier in the west) {2005}

@flow Himalayan vegetation with altitude
Foothills | Tropical moist deciduous: sal
1,000-2,000 m | Subtropical pine (chir), oak
1,500-3,000 m | Temperate: deodar, blue pine, fir, spruce
3,000-3,600 m | Birch, rhododendron, juniper (tree line)
Above 3,600 m | Alpine meadows (bugyals)
@end

@section 12.3 | Social Forestry and Mangroves
- Multipurpose tree for **social forestry**: **khejri** {2002}
- **Vembanad-Karnataka** is wrong; Vembanad is in **Kerala** {2002}
- Mangrove in the **Godavari** delta: **Coringa** {2021}

@table

Coringa | Andhra Pradesh | Godavari

@trap
- **Coringa** = Godavari; **Pichavaram** = Kaveri; **Bhitarkanika** = Brahmani. **Vembanad** is a Kerala lake.
@end

@up UP's forests are mostly **tropical dry deciduous** (sal and teak) in the **Tarai** (Dudhwa, Pilibhit) and the **Vindhyan** south (Sonbhadra, Mirzapur). UP has a small forest cover (about 6-7% of its area); **Sonbhadra** has the most forest.

@last
- **Teak, sal** = tropical deciduous
- **Deodar** = Himalaya, not Nilgiris
- Evergreen = **Sahyadri** / Silent Valley
- Moist deciduous = **Tarai**
- Tree line **lower in the west**
- **Khejri** = social forestry
- **Coringa** = Godavari delta
- **Sundarbans** = largest mangrove
- Cinchona not in Chhattisgarh
- Fern = no flowers
@end
