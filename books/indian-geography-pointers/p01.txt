@section 1.1 | Size and Extent
- India is the **7th** largest country (not 5th or 6th) and has about **2.4%** of world land {2006, 2022}
- **Tropic of Cancer** divides India into two nearly equal halves; India is **not wholly** in the torrid zone {2006, 2022}
- India is now the **most populous** country (passed China, April 2023) {2016}
- Mainland: **8°4' N to 37°6' N**, **68°7' E to 97°25' E**; area **32.87 lakh sq km**

@section 1.2 | The Tropic of Cancer
- States crossed: **Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, WB, Tripura, Mizoram** (west to east) {1990, 2017}
- **Manipur** lies wholly **north** of the Tropic {2008}
- Of Tripura, Mizoram, WB and Manipur, the Tropic does **not** cross **Manipur** {?}
- Nearest to the Tropic (23°26' N): **Gandhinagar** {1999, 2002, 2010}; next option set: **Jabalpur** {2017}
- **Mahi** is the river that crosses the Tropic **twice** {2016}

@section 1.3 | Longitude and Indian Standard Time
- IST meridian is **82°30' E** (GMT + 5:30) {2013}
- It does **not** pass through **Maharashtra** {2010}
- IST is taken near **Prayagraj (Mirzapur)** {?}
- Gujarat to Arunachal (Walong): about 29° of longitude = about **2 hours** {1992}
- Noon at IST, local time at 120° E: 37°30' × 4 = 150 min ahead = **14:30** {2001}
- Midnight at IST, 6 a.m. at a place: 6 h = 90° east, so **172°30' E** {2002}
- Sunrise 5 a.m. at **Tirap**: Kandla sees it about **7 a.m.** {2010}

@flow How to solve any time question
Find the gap | Subtract the two longitudes
Convert | 1° = 4 minutes; 15° = 1 hour
Decide direction | East = ahead (add); West = behind (subtract)
Check IST | 82°30' ÷ 15 = 5.5 h, so GMT + 5:30
@end
