@section 25.1 | Roads and National Highways
- Road share of total transport: about **80%** {2014}
- Old **NH-4** (Mumbai-Chennai) did not touch MP, Rajasthan or UP (*) {2010}
- Most NH length (then): **UP**; now **Maharashtra** leads {2000}
- Most **state highway** length: **Maharashtra** {2010, 2013}
- NH length order (per key): Arunachal first, Sikkim last {2004}
- Old **NH-3** (Agra-Mumbai) did not touch **Bhopal** {2001}
- Golden Quadrilateral joins **Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai, Mumbai**; EW corridor ends at **Porbandar** {2008}
- **Golden Quadrilateral** = highways {2004}
- First six-lane expressway: **Mumbai-Pune** {2001, 2003}
- **PMGSY**: roads for unconnected villages {2001}

@section 25.3 | Ports and Harbours

- **Kochi** is on the **Malabar** coast {2013}
- Landlocked harbour: **Visakhapatnam** {2023}
- **Chennai-deepest port** is wrong {1997}; **Kolkata-deepest** is wrong {2023}
- Deepest among options: **Paradip** (Visakhapatnam is the deepest landlocked) {2017}
- Not a natural harbour: **Chennai** {2007}; natural ports: **Kochi and Visakhapatnam** {2013}
- Open seaport: **Chennai** {2012}
- Outer port: **Haldia** (of Kolkata) {2012}
- Not on the east coast: **Kandla** {2008}; Odisha coast: **Paradip** {2013}
- **Kandla** is in **Gujarat** {1993}; on the **Gulf of Kachchh** {2004}
