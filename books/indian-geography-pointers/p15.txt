@section 15.2 | Structure and Problems
- Indian agriculture is mainly **a means of livelihood** {2004}
- True: India has more **cultivated area** than the USA, China, Japan; tropical monsoon allows **year-round cropping** {2024}
- Low productivity has **all the given causes** (small holdings, rain dependence, old methods) {2007}
- **Cooperative farming** is **not** a cause of low productivity {2003}
- Largest average holding among the options: **Punjab** (Nagaland largest overall) {2010}
- Dry zone is agrarian; it has second Green Revolution potential (both true, R not the explanation) {2010}
- High productivity in the south: **Tamil Nadu coast** {2004}
- Richest in replenishable **groundwater**: **Uttar Pradesh** {2006}
- Pioneer in **contract farming**: **Punjab** {2005}

@section 15.3 | Fertilisers and Green Manure
- Largest fertiliser users (at that time): **UP and Andhra Pradesh**; per hectare: **Punjab** {2009}
- **Balanced fertilisers** serve all the stated purposes {2008}
- Green manure with the most nitrogen among the options: **cowpea (lobia)** {2016, 2008}
- **Green agriculture** = integrated pest management, nutrient supply and resource management {2016}

@section 15.4 | The Green Revolution
- New Agricultural Strategy: **1966-67** (key says "seventies", meaning the seventh decade) {2015}
- World "Father of the Green Revolution": **Norman Borlaug** {2015}
- "**Evergreen Revolution**": **M.S. Swaminathan** {2015}
- Green Revolution = **wheat** production {2015}
- Biggest gain in output and yield: **wheat** {1994, 2001}
- Wheat yield reached about **2,000 kg per hectare** {2003}
- It modernised farming; the claim of little social or environmental cost is debatable (key: both true) {2014}

@flow Green Revolution in brief
1966-67 | High-yielding variety (HYV) programme starts
Inputs | HYV seeds + fertiliser + irrigation + credit
Crop | Wheat first, rice later
Regions | Punjab, Haryana, western UP
People | Borlaug (world); M.S. Swaminathan, C. Subramaniam (India)
Costs | Falling water tables, salinity, chemicals, regional gaps
@end
