@section 21.1 | Coal: Reserves and Production
- Main source of coal: **Gondwana** system; over **90%** of coal {2006, 2008, 2016, 2017}
- India is a leading coal producer with vast reserves in **Jharkhand and Chhattisgarh** (both true) {2023}
- Over half of proved reserves in **Jharkhand and Odisha**; key says both true, R not the explanation (resources are now about 361 bt, not 293.5) {2019}
- Reserves order in 2014: Jharkhand first; **Odisha** leads in total resources now (*) {2014}
- Key's order **Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Odisha, Maharashtra** fits **production**; proved reserves (2014) = Jharkhand, Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Maharashtra {2016}
- Two leading producers: **Chhattisgarh and Odisha** {2010}
- Production order 2008: Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, MP; now **Odisha, Chhattisgarh, MP, Jharkhand** (*) {2008}
- Largest coal producer: **Odisha** {2002, 2009, 2014}
- Top three producers: **Odisha, Chhattisgarh, MP** {2009}

@section 21.3 | Lignite and the Use of Coal
- Lignite has about 35-40% carbon (true); **Jharkhand** is **not** the lignite leader (A true, R false) {2002}
- Largest lignite deposit: **Tamil Nadu**; largest lignite field: **Neyveli** {2013, 2019}
- **Jharkhand** does **not** have large lignite reserves {2017}
- Most coal is used for **power (energy) generation** {2008}
- Coal is a major part of inter-state rail traffic; Bengal-Jharkhand coal feeds the north-west (R explains A) {2010}

@section 21.6 | Strategic Reserves and Natural Gas
- Strategic reserves at **Visakhapatnam, Mangaluru, Padur**; **Ankleshwar** not chosen {2008}
- "Hydrocarbon Vision 2025" (key: "Hydrogen") = **storage/warehousing of petroleum** {2004}
- Oil exploration: **Oil India Ltd** (with ONGC) {2004}
- **HBJ** pipeline carries gas from the **South Basin** (Bassein); built by **GAIL** {2006, 2014}
- Reliance's huge gas find: **coastal Andhra** (KG basin) {2002}
- **KG-D6**: **gas** {2011}
- Most natural gas: **Bombay High** {2012, 2013}

@trap
- Coal "first" depends on the word: **resources** = Odisha; **production** = Odisha; **coking coal** = Jharkhand (Jharia); **field with the largest reserves** = Raniganj.
- **Hatia** is not a refinery; **Haldia** is West Bengal, not Odisha; **Singareni** is Telangana, not MP; **Maolong (Mawlong)** is Meghalaya.
- **Ankleshwar** is an oilfield, not a strategic reserve site.
@end

@up UP has the **Mathura** refinery (IOC, 1982), the **Singrauli** coalfield's UP part in **Sonbhadra** (feeding Anpara, Obra, Rihand, Singrauli power plants), and the end of the **HBJ pipeline at Jagdishpur** (Amethi), which feeds gas plants such as **Auraiya** and fertiliser units in UP.
