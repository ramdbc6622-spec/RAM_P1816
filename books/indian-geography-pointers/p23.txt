@chapter 23 | Industries and Industrial Centres
@meta Built from 42 UPPSC questions (1994–2024). Focus: steel plants, aluminium centres, PSU headquarters, textile and agro centres, fertilizer, paper and cement towns.

@hot
- **Place-industry matching** dominates: **Renukoot-aluminium, Korba-BALCO, Muri-aluminium (Jharkhand), Sindri-fertilizer, Phulpur-fertilizer, Titagarh-paper, Bhadohi-carpet, Chunar-cement** {1994, 1998, 2001, 2002, 2003, 2006, 2008, 2008, 2010, 2010, 2013, 2017, 2024}
- **Wrong-pair** traps: **Bhilai-MP, BALCO-Raipur, Muri-Chhattisgarh, Amlai-Chhattisgarh, Dharmapuri-Odisha, Sindri-copper, Pipri-pesticides** {2002, 2003, 2004, 2004, 2009, 2010, 2010, 2024}
- **Gujarat** = largest petrochemical centre (**Jamnagar**) {2006, 2013}

@end

@section 23.4 | Textiles, Carpets, Rubber
- Ancient industry of India: **cotton textile** {2004}
- First cotton mill (1818): **Fort Gloster**, near Kolkata {2013}
- **Ahmedabad** is not the largest cotton centre now (Mumbai historically; A false, R true) {2020}
- **Kakinada-jute, Virudhunagar-cotton textile, Channapatna-silk, Bhadohi-carpet** {2010, 2013}
- **Silk-Mysore, petrochemicals-Jawahar Nagar, fertilizer-Talcher, pharma-Rishikesh** {2010}
- First synthetic rubber plant: **Panipat** per key (many texts say **Bareilly**, 1963) {2016}
- Rubber industry at **Panjim** (per key) {2007}

- **Sivakasi** is **not** in Chota Nagpur; it is in the **Madurai-Coimbatore-Bengaluru** region {2015, 2014}
