@chapter 17 | Major Crops II: Cotton, Sugarcane, Oilseeds and Pulses
@meta Built from 44 UPPSC questions (2001–2023). Focus: UP and sugarcane, the southward shift of the sugar industry, soybean, oilseed and pulse varieties.

@hot
- **UP** leads in sugarcane **area and production**; it is the **"Sugar Bowl"** of India {2008, 2013, 2014, 2015}
- Sugar industry shifted **south**: higher cane yield, more sucrose, longer crushing season (R explains A) {2007, 2017}
- **MP** = largest **soybean** area and (until 2023-24) production {2001, 2003, 2014, 2015}
- **Mustard** varieties: **Varuna, Pusa Bold, Pitambari** {2007, 2013, 2015}
- **Arhar** varieties: **Bahar, Malaviya Chamatkar**; Neelam is **not** arhar (it is a mango) {2008, 2008, 2013, 2014}
@end

@section 17.1 | Cotton
- "**White gold**" of Maharashtra: **cotton** {2002}
- Cotton fibre comes from the **seed** (seed hairs) {2009}

@section 17.2 | Sugarcane and Sugar
- Largest sugarcane area: **UP** {2015}; leading producer: **UP** {2013}
- Production order (2013): **UP, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Haryana** {2014}
- "**Sugar Bowl**" of India: **UP** {2008}
- Crop with the highest **irrigated share** of its area: **sugarcane** {2013}
- First sugar mill (1903): **Pratappur** (Deoria, UP) {2013}
- Sugarcane breeding: **Coimbatore** (Sugarcane Breeding Institute) {2009, 2010}
- **Adsali** cane takes about **18 months** to ripen {2010}
- India: over 15% of world sugar, sugar is the 2nd largest agro-industry, India is the **largest consumer**; not the largest producer (Brazil) {2009}
- Shift to the south: **higher yield, more sucrose, longer season** (not cheap labour) {2007}
- South has more sugar industry because of **higher yield and juice** (R explains A) {2017}
- **Sakkar Nagar** (Bodhan) was in AP, now **Telangana** (*) {2008}

@flow Why sugar moved south
Higher cane yield | 90-100+ t/ha vs about 80 in the north
More sucrose | 11-12% recovery
Longer crushing season | 6-7 months vs 4
Strong cooperatives | Maharashtra
@end

@section 17.3 | Oilseeds
- Leading soybean producer: **Madhya Pradesh** (Maharashtra in 2024-25) {2001, 2003, 2015}
- Largest soybean **area**: **MP** {2014}
- Largest oilseed producer (2006): **MP**; now **Rajasthan** {2006}
- Largest **groundnut** producer: **Gujarat** {2006}
- Low area but very high groundnut yield: **West Bengal** {2014}
- **Pegging** happens in **groundnut** {2010}; groundnut needs much **gypsum** {2007}
- **Kaushal**: groundnut {2002}
- **Varuna** {2007}, **Pusa Bold** {2013}, **Pitambari** {2015}: **mustard**
- Highest oil content: **sesame** {2017}
- **Kusum** (safflower) = *Carthamus tinctorius* {2016}

@end

@section 17.4 | Pulses
- India is the largest **producer and consumer** of pulses {2014}
- India does **not** usually export **pulses** (it imports them) {2013}
- Largest pulse producer: **Madhya Pradesh** {2004}
- India lacks pulses but not protein (R explains A, per key) {2009}
- **Green gram (moong)** is **not** a rabi pulse {2023}
- **Legumes** fix nitrogen from the air {2006}
- Nutrient essential for nodules: **cobalt** {2004}
- Poor nitrogen fixer: **rajma** (*) {2009}
- NPK ratio for legumes: **1:2:2** {2013}
- Pulses complement cereals in intercropping (both true, R not the explanation per key; the real reason is nitrogen fixation) {2023}
- **Red gram (arhar)** originated in **India** {2007}
- **Malaviya Chamatkar**: **arhar** {2014}; **Bahar**: **arhar** {2008, 2013}
- **Arhar-Neelam** is wrong {2008}
- Leafless pea: **Aparna** {2013}

@table

Urad (black gram) | Pea
Lobia (cowpea) | Rajma, khesari
@end

@up UP is the **Sugar Bowl**: top in cane area and output; India's first sugar mill was at **Pratappur** (Deoria, 1903). UP-bred varieties: arhar **Bahar** and **Narendra Arhar** (Ayodhya), **Malaviya Chamatkar** (BHU), pea **Azad** (Kanpur), groundnut **Kaushal, Chandra** (Kanpur).
