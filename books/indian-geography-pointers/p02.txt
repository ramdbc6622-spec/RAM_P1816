@chapter 2 | Physical Divisions: Structure, Mountains, Peaks, Passes and Glaciers
@meta Built from 77 UPPSC questions (1990–2025), the biggest geography chapter. Focus: passes and their states, peaks, hills, Himalayan ranges, glaciers.

@hot
- **Passes and states** are asked almost every year: **Nathu La, Jelep La = Sikkim**; **Bomdi La, Se La, Bum La = Arunachal**; **Lipulekh, Niti, Mana = Uttarakhand**; **Shipki La, Rohtang = HP**; **Zoji La = Ladakh** {1998, 2001, 2004, 2006, 2008, 2010, 2011, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2023}
- **Highest peak of each state** (match): Anaimudi-Kerala, Doddabetta-TN, Saramati-Nagaland, Guru Shikhar-Rajasthan, Dhupgarh-MP, Nanda Devi-Uttarakhand {2018, 2021, 2025}
- **Anaimudi** (2,695 m) = highest peak of **South India / the Peninsula** {2005, 2012, 2013, ?}
- **Aravalli** = **oldest** and a **residual** mountain {1991, 2005, 2010, 2017}
- Largest glacier: **Siachen** (Gangotri if Siachen is not offered) {1995, 2013, 2017}
@end

@section 2.1 | Geology and Rock Systems
- Palaeomagnetism shows the Indian landmass moved **northward** {1998}
- The **western coast** is not depositional; it is a **faulted (emergent-submergent)** coast {2004}
- Latest of the old rock systems: **Gondwana** (98% of India's coal) {2016}
- Deccan Trap intertrappean beds have **freshwater** (not sea) fossils {2024}

@flow Rock systems, oldest to youngest
Archaean | Gneiss, schist; no fossils
Dharwar | Metals: iron, gold (Kolar), manganese
Cuddapah, Vindhyan | Sandstone (Red Fort), Panna diamonds
Gondwana | About 98% of India's coal
Deccan Trap | Basalt; black soil
Tertiary, Quaternary | Himalaya, petroleum; alluvial plains
@end

@section 2.2 | The Himalaya
- Himalaya is **still rising**: the Indian plate is pushing into the Eurasian plate (A and R true, R explains A) {2024}
- Most seismic zones are in the Himalaya because of **longitudinal thrusts** {2019}
- **Lesser Himalaya**: marine fossils; **Shiwalik**: remains of early humans; Greater Himalaya sediments are not fossil-less {2019}
- **Himachal** = the **Lesser Himalaya** {2008}
- Youngest range: **Shiwalik** {2010}; if only Himalayan vs Peninsular ranges are offered: **Himadri** {2020}
- **Kumaon Himalaya** lies between the **Satluj and Kali** {2016}

@flow Himalayan ranges, north to south
Trans-Himalaya | Karakoram, Ladakh, Zaskar; K2
Greater Himalaya (Himadri) | Highest peaks, about 6,100 m
Lesser Himalaya (Himachal) | Pir Panjal, Dhauladhar; hill stations
Shiwalik (Outer) | Youngest; duns such as Dehradun
@end

@flow Regional divisions, west to east (Burrard)
Punjab Himalaya | Indus to Satluj
Kumaon Himalaya | Satluj to Kali
Nepal Himalaya | Kali to Tista
Assam Himalaya | Tista to Dihang (Brahmaputra)
@end

@section 2.3 | Ranges, Valleys and Tunnels
- **Pir Panjal** range is in **Jammu and Kashmir** {2007}
- **Atal Tunnel** (Rohtang): BRO, Manali to Lahaul-Spiti, under the **Pir Panjal** {2016}
- Atal Tunnel is the longest highway tunnel **above 10,000 ft**, not the world's longest (only statement 2 true) {2025}
- **Kashmir valley**: between the **Pir Panjal and Great Himalaya** {2020}
- **Kullu valley**: between the **Dhauladhar and Pir Panjal** {1999, 2002}
- **Nelang valley** is in **Uttarakhand** (Uttarkashi) {2016}
- **Imphal basin** is a **lacustrine plain** {2019}
- **Aksai Chin** is part of the **Ladakh plateau** {1995}
- **Shaksgam valley**: PoK area given by Pakistan to China (1963); India protests China's construction {2024}
- **Tripura** does not lie along the **Patkai** hills {2015}

@section 2.4 | The Northern Plains
- Gravelly plain at the foot of the Shiwalik: **Bhabar** {2007}; it is a **piedmont** region {1994}
- **Kuttanad** (Kerala) is India's **lowest** region, below sea level {2015}

@flow Northern plain belts, from the hills southward
Bhabar | Pebbles; streams vanish underground
Tarai | Streams reappear; marshy forest (Dudhwa)
Bhangar | Older alluvium above flood level; kankar
Khadar | New alluvium renewed by floods
@end

@section 2.5 | Old Mountains and Peninsular Hills
- **Aravalli** = oldest mountains {1991, 2010, 2017}; a **residual** mountain {2005}; mainly in **Rajasthan** {?}
- **Satpura** lies between the **Narmada and Tapti** {2007}
- Central India hills, **west to east**: **Satpura, Mahadeo, Maikal, Chotanagpur** {2019}
- **Nilgiri** hills: junction of **Karnataka, Kerala and Tamil Nadu** {2015}
- **Cardamom hills** (**Yela Mala**): **Kerala-Tamil Nadu** border {2008, 2010, 2023}
- **Mandav hills** are in **Gujarat**, not Maharashtra {2011, 2011}
- Hills match: **Garo-Meghalaya, Miri-Arunachal, Kollimalai-Tamil Nadu, Dalma-Jharkhand** {2017}
- Hills match: **Bateshwar-Bihar, Bilari-Karnataka, Chin-Manipur, Dhosi-Haryana** {2022}
- **Western Ghats**: a **block mountain**, gentle eastern slope, **basalt** in the north {2017}
- **Kodaikanal** is in the **Palani** hills, not the Anaimalai {2013}

@section 2.6 | Peaks
- Highest peak of the **Peninsula**: **Anaimudi** {2005, 2012, 2013, ?}
- Highest peak claimed by India: **K2** (PoK); highest in Indian control: **Kanchenjunga**; wholly in India: **Nanda Devi** {1990, 2009}
- Peaks east to west: **Namcha Barwa, Kanchenjunga, Everest, Nanda Devi** {2008}
- **Cho Oyu, Lhotse** are Himalayan; Annamalai, Sirumalai are not {2022}
- Not in India: **Gosainthan** (Tibet) {2005}; **Gurla Mandhata, Namcha Barwa** {2019}
- **Trishul** peak: Uttarakhand (Garhwal) {2021}
- State peaks: **Kerala-Anaimudi, Nagaland-Saramati, Uttarakhand-Nanda Devi, TN-Doddabetta** {2018}
- State peaks: **TN-Doddabetta, Rajasthan-Guru Shikhar, Nagaland-Saramati, MP-Dhupgarh** {2021, 2025}
- First Indian woman on Everest: **Bachendri Pal** (1984) {1996}

@section 2.7 | Mountain Passes
- **Se La** is in **Arunachal**, not Uttarakhand {2015}
- **Bhor Ghat** is in **Maharashtra**, not HP {1998}
- Western Ghat passes north to south: **Thal Ghat, Bhor Ghat, Pal Ghat** {2023}
- **Khyber** is not a Western Ghats pass (it is in Pakistan-Afghanistan) {2016}
- **Niti** = **Uttarakhand** {2023}; **Lipulekh** = **Uttarakhand** (not UP or Ladakh) {2001, 2008, 2014, ?}
- **Nathu La** = **Sikkim** (not Meghalaya or Arunachal) {2010, 2013, 2017, ?}
- **Zoji La** leads to **Leh** (now in Ladakh, not Arunachal) {2016, 2023}
- **Rohtang** = **Himachal Pradesh** {?}
- Match: **Banihal-J&K, Nathu La-Sikkim, Niti-Uttarakhand, Shipki-HP** {2004, 2011}
- Match: **Bum La-Arunachal, Jelep La-Sikkim, Muling La-Uttarakhand, Shipki La-HP** {2006}
- Match: **Mana-Uttarakhand, Nathu La-Sikkim, Zoji La-J&K (Ladakh), Shipki La-HP** {2016}
- Heights: **Debsa 5,360, Bara-lacha 4,850, Rohtang 3,978, Banihal 2,832 m** {2024}

@flow Himalayan passes, west to east
Zoji La, Banihal | Ladakh; J&K
Shipki La, Rohtang | Himachal Pradesh
Mana, Niti, Lipulekh | Uttarakhand
Nathu La, Jelep La | Sikkim
Bum La, Se La, Bomdi La | Arunachal Pradesh
@end

@trap

- **Bomdila** is the town; the pass is **Bomdi La**. **Jelep La** and Nathu La both lead to the Chumbi valley.
@end

@section 2.9 | The Peninsular Plateau
- **Meghalaya plateau** is part of the **Peninsular plateau** {2013}
- **Dhanbad** is densely populated because of **mining and industry** {2007}
- **Dandakaranya** lies in **Chhattisgarh and Odisha** {2008}

@up **Bundelkhand** plateau (Jhansi, Banda, Chitrakoot) is the Peninsular part of UP. The **Tarai** of Pilibhit, Lakhimpur Kheri and Bahraich holds **Dudhwa National Park**. UP has no high peaks or passes; **Lipulekh** is often wrongly given as UP but is in Uttarakhand.

@last
- **Nathu La, Jelep La** = Sikkim; **Se La, Bum La, Bomdi La** = Arunachal
- **Lipulekh, Niti, Mana** = Uttarakhand; **Shipki La, Rohtang** = HP; **Zoji La** = Ladakh
- **Anaimudi** = highest in the Peninsula; **Doddabetta** = Tamil Nadu
- **Aravalli** oldest; **Shiwalik** youngest
- **Kumaon** Himalaya: Satluj to Kali
- **Kullu**: Dhauladhar-Pir Panjal; **Kashmir**: Pir Panjal-Great Himalaya
- **Bhabar** at the foot of the hills, then Tarai
- **Satpura** between Narmada and Tapti; **Mandav** in Gujarat
- **Siachen** largest glacier; **Chaurabari** above Kedarnath
- **Gondwana** = coal; Kuttanad = lowest land
