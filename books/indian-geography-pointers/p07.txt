@chapter 7 | Drainage II: Peninsular Rivers and River Cities
@meta Built from 39 UPPSC questions (1992–2025). Focus: rift valleys and estuaries, river sources, Godavari, cities on rivers.

@hot
- **Narmada and Tapi** flow west through **rift valleys** and form **estuaries**, not deltas {1999, 2002, 2005, 2006, 2010, 2012, 2013, 2017}
- Western Ghat rivers form **no deltas**: short, fast courses over hard rock {2010, 2013, 2014}
- **Godavari** = longest Peninsular river with the largest basin {2009, 2013, 2014}
- **Bhubaneswar is not on the Mahanadi**; **Hyderabad is on the Musi**, not the Krishna {1996, 1999, 2010, 2016}
@end

@section 7.1 | West-flowing Rivers and Rift Valleys
- Western Ghat rivers make **no delta**: short, swift, hard rock (A and R true, R explains A) {2010, 2013, 2014}
- Narmada and Tapi reach the **Arabian Sea** because they flow in **rift valleys** {1999, 2002}
- The Narmada forms **no delta** (A false, R true) {2005}
- River in a **fault trough**: **Narmada** {2010}; rift valley: **Tapi** {2013, 2017}
- **Narmada** flows **between two ranges** (Vindhya and Satpura) {2012}
- **Tapti** forms an **estuary** {2006}
- **Damodar** is famous for **fault-valley (rift) drainage** on the east side {2019}
- Of Periyar, Pennar and Palar, only the **Periyar** falls into the Arabian Sea {2025}
- **Luni**: fresh in the upper course, **saline** lower down {2019}

@flow Which way do they flow?
West to the Arabian Sea | Narmada, Tapi, Mahi, Sabarmati, Luni, Periyar
Form estuaries | Narmada, Tapi (rift valleys)
East to the Bay of Bengal | Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, Pennar
Form deltas | Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri
@end

@section 7.2 | Sources of the Rivers
- **Amarkantak**: source of the **Narmada** (and Son) {2008}
- **Son**'s actual source: **Son Bachharwar**, Bilaspur district {2007}
- **Tapti** does **not** rise in the Western Ghats (it rises at **Multai**, Satpura) {2018}; it falls into the **Arabian Sea** {1992}
- Sources: **Yamuna-Yamunotri, Krishna-Mahabaleshwar, Godavari-Nashik, Mahanadi-Sihawa** {2023}
- **Kaveri** rises in the **Brahmagiri** hills (Talakaveri) {2011, 2013}

@end

@section 7.3 | Basins and Tributaries
- Longest Peninsular river: **Godavari** {2013, 2014}; largest catchment: **Godavari** {2009}
- Largest basin among the options: **Mahanadi** {2021, 2022}
- "Ganga of the South": **Kaveri** in the UPPSC key (NCERT calls the Godavari Dakshin Ganga) {2015}
- **Pranhita** joins the **Godavari**, not the Mahanadi {2023}
- **Tel** is a Mahanadi tributary, **not** the Krishna's {2010}
- **Hagari** is a tributary of the **Tungabhadra** {2009}
- South Indian rivers are mainly **dendritic** {2014}
- **Mahanadi** forms its delta in **Odisha** {2014}
- **Krishna dispute**: Maharashtra, Karnataka, AP (and Telangana); no option complete (*) {2013}
- Water-deficient basins: options 1 and 2 (per the source key) {2002}

@end

@section 7.4 | The Damodar and Others
- **Indravati** is **not** a Damodar tributary (it joins the Godavari) {2019}
- Damodar joins the **Hooghly** {2006, 2008}
- **Eden Canal** is taken from the **Damodar** {2015}
- Most polluted among the options: **Damodar** ("Sorrow of Bengal", coal belt) {2013}
- Mumbai's **Mithi** river rises from **Vihar Lake** {2014}

@section 7.5 | Cities on River Banks
- **Leh** is on the **right bank of the Indus** {2013}
- **Bhubaneswar-Mahanadi** is wrong (Cuttack is on the Mahanadi) {1999, 2010}
- **Hyderabad-Krishna** is wrong; Hyderabad is on the **Musi** {1996, 2016}
- **Cuttack-Mahanadi, Ludhiana-Satluj, Nashik-Godavari, Ujjain-Kshipra** {1992}
- **Ujjain** is on the **Kshipra** {2008}
- **Govindghat** is **not** on the Mandakini (it is on the Alaknanda) {2013}
