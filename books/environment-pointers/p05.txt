@section 5.1 | Greenhouse Effect
- Concept of greenhouse gases: **Joseph Fourier** {2011}
- Greenhouse effect: **rise in global temperature** {2018}
- Without it Earth would average **-18 °C** {2020}
- Enhanced by **CO2**; main gases **CO2 and methane** {2015, 2004, 2013}
- Largest natural share: **water vapour** {2011}
- Not responsible: **argon** {2016, 2022}
- Not directly warming: **oxides of sulphur and nitrogen** {2013}
- Not a natural GHG (of options): **nitrogen oxide** {2019, 2020}
- Not GHG: **SO2**; **oxygen**; **NO2**; **propane**; **nitrogen**; **hydrogen** {2007, 2016, 2017, 2016, 2017, 2012, 2012}
- Both harmful and useful: **CO2** per key (ozone in most texts) {2013}
