@section 12.3 | Project Tiger
- Aim: **save the Indian tiger** from extinction {1994}
- Started: **1973** {2002, 2018}
- International Tiger Day: **29 July** {2014}
- World Tiger Summit 2010: **St. Petersburg** {2011}
- Tiger census 2018: India has **70%** of world tigers; about **30%** outside reserves (both) {2020}
- Largest tiger habitat: **Andhra Pradesh** (Nagarjunsagar-Srisailam) {2011}
- **Bandipur-Karnataka, Melghat-Maharashtra, Buxa-West Bengal, Pakke-Arunachal** {2008}
- **Bandipur-Karnataka, Manas-Assam, Namdapha-Arunachal, Similipal-Odisha** {2006}
- Mizoram's tiger reserve: **Dampa** {2012}
- Wrong: **Buxa-Bihar** (West Bengal); Buxa = **West Bengal** {2010, 2003}
- Lies in two States: **Pench** (MP, Maharashtra) {2008}
- **Sariska**: **Rajasthan** {2012}
- Not in MP: **Amangarh** (UP) {2021}
- Newly declared (2015): **Rajaji** {2015}
- First to use drones: **Bandipur** {2014}
