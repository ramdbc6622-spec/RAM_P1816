@up **Dudhwa National Park** (Lakhimpur Kheri) is UP's only national park: swamp deer (barasingha), tiger, and **rhino rehabilitation** since 1984. UP's other big reserves are **Pilibhit, Amangarh, Ranipur** (tiger reserves) and **Katarniaghat** (sanctuary).
