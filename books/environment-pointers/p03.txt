@section 3.1 | Meaning and Distribution
- Biodiversity: **range of species in an environment**; diverse plants and animals of an area {2014, 2014}
- Term "biodiversity" first used by **Walter G. Rosen** (1985) {2013}
- Most significant aspect: **maintaining the ecosystem** {2015}
- UN decade 2011-20: **Biodiversity** {2012}
- Most biodiversity: **tropical rainforest** {2012, 2014, 2016, 2018}
- Not true: diversity **increases towards the poles** (it decreases) {2019}
- Most butterflies in the tropics; they can't bear cold (R explains A) {2019}

@section 3.2 | India and Hotspots
- Richest biodiversity (of options): **Silent Valley**; Silent Valley is in **Kerala** {2003, 2008}
- **Valley of Flowers**: **Uttarakhand** {2002}
- Hotspot region (of options): **Andaman and Nicobar** (Sundaland) {2009}
- Most biodiverse area: **Western Ghats** {2014, 2015, 2019}
- Not a hotspot: **Vindhyas** {2016}

@table
India's hotspots | Area
Himalaya | Indian Himalaya
Indo-Burma | North-east India
Western Ghats and Sri Lanka | Western Ghats
Sundaland | Nicobar Islands
@end

@section 3.3 | Causes of Loss
- Destruction due to **all** listed causes {2002}
- Maximum danger: **habitat and vegetation destruction** {2017}
- Main reason: **natural habitat destruction** {2015, 2016, 2023}
- Bird decline: **habitat loss and pesticide use** (3 and 4) {2001}
- Not adding biodiversity: **fewer trophic levels** {2015}
- Coral bleaching: **rise in sea temperature**; **global warming** {2012, 2015}

@section 3.4 | Conservation
- Important strategy: **biosphere reserves** {2013}
- Biosphere reserves conserve **genetic variation** {2004}
- Not in-situ: **botanical garden** {2018}
- Cryo-bank gas: **liquid nitrogen** (-196 °C) {2009}

@flow Conservation methods
In-situ | Biosphere reserves, national parks, sanctuaries, sacred groves, Ramsar sites
Ex-situ | Zoos, botanical gardens, seed banks, cryo banks
Cryo bank | Liquid nitrogen at -196 °C
@end

@trap
- **Habitat destruction** beats pollution and hunting as the main cause.
- **Botanical gardens, zoos** = ex-situ; **sacred groves** = in-situ.
- **Vindhyas** are not a hotspot.
@end

@up UP's biodiversity sits mostly in the **Terai** (Dudhwa, Katarniaghat, Pilibhit) and the **Himalayan foothill** belt it shares with Uttarakhand; UP has no hotspot area of its own.
