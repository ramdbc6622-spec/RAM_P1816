@section 9.3 | Uttar Pradesh
- UP's recorded forest area (2011): about **7%** of its area {2012}

@trap
- **Area** (MP) vs **percentage** (Mizoram) vs **very dense** (Arunachal).
- "Recorded forest area" (legal) differs from "forest cover" (satellite).
- Answers change by report year; check the ISFR edition.
@end

@up UP has only about **6%** forest cover in recent ISFRs, far below the 33% goal; the State's mass plantation drives (crores of saplings each July) aim to raise green cover.
