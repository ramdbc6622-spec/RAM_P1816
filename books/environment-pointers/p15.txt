@section 15.1 | Water as a Resource
- World Water Day: **22 March** {2011}
- Not a biotic resource: **freshwater** {2016}
- CGWA under the **Environment Protection Act**; India has the largest groundwater-irrigated area (2 and 3) {2005}

@section 15.2 | Laws
- Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act: **1974** {2023}
- Water Cess enforced: **1977** {2013}

@section 15.3 | Treatment
- Drinking-water purification gas: **chlorine** {2013}
- Disinfectants: **all** listed (chlorine, ozone, UV) {2016}

@section 15.4 | Lakes and Rivers
- Urban lakes under NLCP: **Bhoj, Sukhna, Pichola** (not Chilika) {2002}
- Added to NLCP (2010): **Bhimtal** {2010}
- Central Ganga Authority: **1985** {2014}
- Ganga Action Plan aim: **pollution-free water only** {2022}
- **NGRBA**: **February 2009** {2010}
- Yamuna Action Plan: **1993** {2016}
