@section 7.1 | Ozone and Location
- UV light forms **O3** {2018}
- Ozone layer: **stratosphere** {2008, 2010, 2012, 2018, 2023}
- Wrong: **ozone layer-troposphere** {2018}
- Natural regulator of stratospheric ozone (per key): **nitrogen dioxide** (NOx) {2016}

@section 7.2 | What Ozone Does
- Absorbs **ultraviolet** radiation; protects from **UV rays** {2013, 2014}
- Shields life from UV {2004, 2006}
- Ozone essential for life; blocks high-energy radiation (R explains A) {2010}
- UV causes **skin cancer** {2014}

@section 7.3 | Depleting Substances
- Main depleter: **CFCs** {2009, 2014, 2016}
- Not true: CFCs are **not** greenhouse gases (they are) {2004}
- Depleters: **CFCs, halons, carbon tetrachloride** (all) {2012}
- Not a depleter (key): **methyl chloroform** (actually it is one) {2002}
- Wrong: **CFC-automobile** (CFCs from refrigerants, sprays) {2017}
- Does not deplete ozone: **carbon monoxide** {2008}

@table

@end

@section 7.4 | The Ozone Hole
- Antarctic ozone hole found by a **British team** (Farman, 1985) {2013}
- Ozone "halo" over Tibet (2005): **G.W. Kent Moore** {2013}
- Ozone Day: **16 September** {1996, 2014, 2015, 2022}

@flow Saving the ozone layer
1974 | Molina and Rowland warn about CFCs
1985 | Vienna Convention; British Antarctic Survey finds the hole
16 Sept 1987 | Montreal Protocol (Ozone Day)
2016 | Kigali Amendment: HFC phase-down
~2066 | Antarctic hole expected to heal
@end

@trap
- Ozone absorbs **UV**, not infrared.
- Ground-level ozone is a **pollutant**; stratospheric ozone protects.
- CFCs **are** greenhouse gases too.
@end

@up UP's refrigeration and foam industries (Noida, Ghaziabad) switched from CFCs under India's **Montreal Protocol** phase-out; HCFCs end by 2030.
