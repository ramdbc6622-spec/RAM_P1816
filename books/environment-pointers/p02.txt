@section 2.1 | Food Chains
- Trophic levels: **organisms linked in a food chain** {2023}
- Order: **grass, grasshopper, frog, snake** {2010}
- Primary consumers: **ants and deer** {2006}
- Man: **primary and secondary** consumer {2016}
- Producers: **green plants** {2013}
- Main marine producers: **phytoplankton** {2005}
- Link between biotic and abiotic: **producers** {2017}
- Primary productivity = autotrophs; secondary = heterotrophs (both) {2017}
- Correct: **green plants-ecosystem** (producers) {2015}

@section 2.2 | Aquatic Ecosystems
- **Lotic**: **rivers** (flowing water) {2023}
- Phytoplankton thrive in the **limnetic** (open-water) zone (R explains A) {2022}

@section 2.3 | Energy Flow
- Primary energy source: **solar**; driving force {2015, 2016, 2019}
- Energy flow is **unidirectional** {2015}
- Energy **decreases** up the levels {2017, 2019}
- **Ten Percent Rule**: **Lindeman** {2023}
- Plants capture about **1%** of sunlight; about **10%** passes up (both) {2025}
- Highest level (key): **omnivores** (top carnivores in most texts) {2002}

@flow 10% law of energy
Sun | Plants capture about 1%
Producers | 10,000 units
Herbivores | 1,000 units
Carnivores | 100 units
Top carnivores | 10 units
@end

@section 2.4 | Pyramids and Productivity
- Inverted biomass pyramid: **pond** {2010}
- Equatorial forest densest biomass; rain and heat all year (R explains A) {2023}
- Least biomass production: **deep sea** {2002}
- Decreasing productivity: **mangroves, grasslands, lakes, oceans** {2021}

@table
Pyramid | Inverted?
Numbers | Yes in a tree ecosystem
Biomass | Yes in pond and ocean
Energy | Never
@end

@section 2.5 | Terms and Thinkers
- Bionomics: synonymous with ecology; values natural systems (2 and 3) {2001}
- Land needed to sustain a person: **ecological footprint**; measured in **global hectares** {2012, 2016}
- Zone between two communities: **ecotone** {2012}
- "Deep ecology": **Arne Naess** {2014}
- Monoclimax theory: **F.E. Clements** {2021}
- Niche concept: **Grinnell** {2005}
- Not eco-friendly: **eucalyptus** {2005, 2010, 2011}
- "Ecology is permanent economy": **Chipko**; Chipko = **plant (tree) conservation** {2007, 2015}
