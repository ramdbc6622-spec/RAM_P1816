@section 1.4 | Ecology and Ecosystem
- Ecology: study of **organisms and environment** {2014}
- True: ecosystem has biotic and abiotic parts; **autotrophs** make food (1 and 2) {2002}
- Not true: an ecosystem is a **closed system** {2014, 2015}
- Biosphere is open (true); no new substances can enter (false) {2017}
- Order: **population, community, ecosystem, landscape** (then biome, biosphere) {2017}
- True: **producers outnumber primary consumers** {2019}
- Components not interdependent (false); humans affect environment (true) {2021}
- Largest ecosystem: **ocean**; most stable: **marine** {2003, 2014, 2013, 2018}
- Artificial ecosystem: **rice field** {2016}
- Cycling of elements: **biogeochemical cycles** {2020, 2021}

@section 1.5 | Ecological Balance
- Does not help balance: **deforestation**; disturbs it {2013, 2014}
- Not related to balance: **industrial management** {2005}
- Vegetation is the index of climate; water-loving plants in moist climate (R explains A) {2009}

@section 1.6 | Succession and Niche
- Phases: **nudation, migration, ecesis, reaction, stabilisation**; from migration if nudation missing {2016, 2024}
- Whole sequence of communities: **sere** {2020}
- "Ecological transition": **Bennett** {2020}
- Niche: tolerance range, resources, role; each species distinct (both) {2022}
- Not true: species become **fewer** in a niche when resources are enough {2024}
