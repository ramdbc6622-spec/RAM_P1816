@section 8.1 | Types and Productivity
- Maximum plant diversity: **tropical evergreen forests** {2017}
- Maximum plant biomass: **tropical rainforest** {2017}
- Lowest net primary productivity: **temperate grasslands** {2015}
- Tropical deciduous forests most extensive in **Asia** {2014}
- Evergreen forest: **Western Ghats** {2015}
- Wrong: **Eastern Deccan Plateau-moist forests** (dry deciduous) {2013}

@section 8.2 | Mangroves
- Famous for mangroves: **Sundarbans** {2004}
- Protected mangrove region (of options): **Goa** {2016}
- Bhitarkanika: not Vansadhara-Subarnarekha delta, not West Bengal (**neither**; it is Brahmani-Baitarani, Odisha) {2016}
- Coastal Odisha cyclone-prone; mangrove loss in Mahanadi delta (key: R explains A) {2006, 2010}

@section 8.3 | Forests as a Resource
- Forests renewable; improve the environment (both true, R not the explanation) {2002, 2004}
- Hydrological cycle controlled by **forests** (per key) {2023}
- Natural resources usable later; development depends on technology (both true, R not the explanation) {2016}
- Aluminium a green metal (true); substitutes wood (false) {2009}

@section 8.4 | Causes of Loss
- Forest loss linked to demographic transition; inverse to population (R explains A) {2016}
- Main cause (key): **industrial development** (FAO: farm expansion) {2011}
