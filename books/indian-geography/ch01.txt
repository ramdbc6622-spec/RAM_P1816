@chapter 1 | India: Location, Extent and Standard Time
@unit UNIT I · INDIA: POSITION, LAND AND COAST

@intro
India lies wholly in the **Northern and Eastern Hemispheres**. Its mainland stretches from **8°4' N to 37°6' N** and from **68°7' E to 97°25' E**; with the islands the southern limit falls to **6°45' N** at Indira Point. With about **32.87 lakh sq km**, India is the **seventh largest** country and holds about **2.4%** of the world's land area. The **Tropic of Cancer** cuts the country almost in half, and the **82°30' E** meridian gives Indian Standard Time. This chapter covers size and extent, the Tropic of Cancer, longitude and time, the extreme points, the land neighbours and the boundary lines. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 1.1 | Size, Area and Latitudinal Extent

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2022
With reference to India, which of the following statement(s) is/are correct?
1. India is the sixth largest country in the world.
2. India occupies about 2.4% of the total area of the world.
3. Tropic of Cancer passes through the middle of the country, dividing it into two latitudinal halves.
4. India lies completely in tropic zone.
Select the correct answer from the code given below:
Code :
(a) 2 and 4
(b) 1 and 2
(c) 2 and 3
(d) 3 and 4
@ans (c)
@end

India is the **seventh** largest country, after Russia, Canada, the USA, China, Brazil and Australia, so statement 1 fails. It holds about **2.4%** of the world's land area. The **Tropic of Cancer (23°30' N)** runs roughly through the middle of the latitudinal span, so statement 3 holds. Statement 4 fails because the part north of the Tropic lies in the **sub-tropical and temperate** belt, not the tropics.

The same two traps return: rank (seventh, not fifth) and the torrid zone. The **torrid zone** lies between the two tropics, so only peninsular India south of 23°30' N is inside it. Statements 2 and 4 are the standard facts.

@pyq U.P. R.O./A.R.O. (Mains) 2016
Which of the following statements about India is incorrect?
(a) India is the 7th largest country in the world
(b) India is the second populous country of the world
(c) Its land boundary extends for around 15,200 km
(d) The earliest sunrise is seen in its Mizoram State
@ans (b & d)
@note the source key accepts both (b) and (d). In 2016, (b) was true and only (d) was wrong. UN population estimates show India passing China in April 2023 (UNFPA *State of World Population 2023*), so today (b) is also incorrect.
@end

India's first sunrise is seen at **Dong** in the Anjaw district of **Arunachal Pradesh**, the easternmost inhabited point. Mizoram is a southern state of the north-east and sees the sun later. The land boundary is about **15,106.7 km**, which rounds to "around 15,200 km".

@trap
India is the **7th** largest by area but the **most populous** since 2023. Do not carry the old "second most populous" fact into a 2026 answer. Earliest sunrise means **Arunachal Pradesh** (Dong), not Mizoram or Assam.
@end

(a) Jharkhand

In the north-east the line crosses only **Tripura and Mizoram**. **Manipur** (about 23°50' N to 25°41' N) lies wholly to its north, as do Assam, Meghalaya, Nagaland and Arunachal Pradesh.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 1999; U.P.P.S.C. (GIC) 2010; U.P. Lower Sub. (Pre) 2002

The Tropic is at about **23°26' N**. Gandhinagar (about 23°13' N) is the closest. Jabalpur and Ujjain are both near 23°10' N, a few minutes further south. Agartala (23°50' N) lies north of the line by a wider margin.

@pyq U.P.R.O./A.R.O. (Re-exam) (Pre.) 2016
Which of the following rivers of India crosses the tropic of cancer twice?
(a) Mahi
(b) Chambal
(c) Narmada
(d) None of the above
@ans (a)
@end

The **Mahi** rises in the Vindhyas near Dhar in Madhya Pradesh, south of the Tropic. It flows north into Rajasthan (Banswara), crossing the line, then turns south-west through Gujarat to the **Gulf of Khambhat**, crossing it again. The Narmada flows west wholly south of the Tropic, and the Chambal flows north-east wholly to its north.

@next
Mahi is the only major Indian river cut twice by the Tropic. A follow-up could ask which river crosses the Tropic of Cancer **and** the Equator twice: the **Congo** in Africa crosses the Equator twice.
@end

@section 1.3 | Longitude, Indian Standard Time and Time Calculations

The Earth turns 360° in 24 hours, so **1° of longitude = 4 minutes** and **15° = 1 hour**. Places to the **east** see the sun earlier and are **ahead** in time. India's extent of about 29° of longitude gives a gap of nearly **2 hours** in local time between Gujarat and Arunachal Pradesh. A single standard meridian, **82°30' E**, is used for the whole country. It is 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of Greenwich (GMT + 5:30).

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2002
When it is midnight at IST meridian, a place observes 6 A.M. The meridian on which the said place is located is-
(a) 7°31' E
(b) 172°30' E
(c) 7°30' E
(d) 127°30' W
@ans (b)
@end

The place is 6 hours ahead, so it lies east of India by 6 × 15° = **90°**. Its meridian is 82°30' + 90° = **172°30' E**. The options 7°30' E and 7°31' E are traps for anyone who subtracts 75° (5 hours) instead of adding.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 1992
How many hours of time difference will be there between the most western village of Gujarat and the most eastern village of Arunachal Pradesh, Walong ?
(a) 1 hour
(b) 2 hour
(c) 3 hour
(d) 1/2 hour
@ans (b)
@end

Guhar Moti in Kutch (about 68°7' E) and the Walong-Dong area (about 97° E) are about **29°** apart. At 4 minutes per degree this is about **116 minutes**, which rounds to **2 hours**.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S.(Pre) 2001
When it is noon at IST meridian, what would be the local time at 120° East longitude –
(a) 09.30
(b) 14.30
(c) 17.30
(d) 20.00
@ans (b)
@end

The gap is 120° − 82°30' = **37°30'**, which is 37.5 × 4 = **150 minutes**, or 2 hours 30 minutes. The place is east of India, so add: 12:00 + 2:30 = **14:30**.

@table
Step | Rule
1. Find the gap | Subtract the two longitudes (add if one is east and one is west)
2. Convert | 1° = 4 minutes, 15° = 1 hour, 1' = 4 seconds
3. Direction | East = ahead (add time); West = behind (subtract time)
4. Check | IST is GMT + 5:30 because 82°30' ÷ 15° = 5.5 hours
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2010
The Indian Standard Time Meridian does not pass through-
(a) Andhra Pradesh
(b) Chhattisgarh

The 82°30' E meridian crosses **five states**: **Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Odisha and Andhra Pradesh**. It leaves the coast near Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh. Maharashtra lies wholly west of it.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S (Pre) 2010
If the Sun rises at TIRAP in Arunachal Pradesh at 5.00 a.m. (IST) then what time (IST) will the Sun rise in Kandla in Gujarat?
(a) About 5.30 a.m.
(b) About 6.00 a.m.
(c) About 7.00 a.m.
(d) About 7.30 a.m.
@ans (c)
@end

Tirap (about 95°30' E) and Kandla (about 70°10' E) are about 25° apart, giving a gap of about 1 hour 40 minutes. On a single clock (IST), Kandla sees the sun almost two hours later. The nearest option is **about 7.00 a.m.**

@trap
The two-hour gap between the north-east and Gujarat is the reason for the long-running demand for a separate time zone (**"Chai Bagan time"** in Assam's tea gardens, about one hour ahead of IST). India still has one official time zone. Do not mark "India has two time zones".
@end

@next
Expect a statement question on the **IST meridian**: five states (UP, MP, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh), Mirzapur, GMT + 5:30. CSIR-NPL, New Delhi, keeps the official time for India.
@end

@section 1.4 | Extreme Points of India

@pyq U.P. Lower Sub. (Pre) 2002
The southern most point of India is-
(a) Kanyakumari
(b) Rameshwaram
(c) Indira Point
(d) Point Calimere
@ans (c)
@end

**Indira Point** (earlier Pygmalion Point and Parsons Point) on **Great Nicobar Island** is the southernmost point of India at about 6°45' N. **Kanyakumari** (Cape Comorin, 8°4' N) is the southern tip of the **mainland**. Rameshwaram is on Pamban Island, and Point Calimere (Kodiakkarai) is a cape on the Coromandel coast.

@one
Where is the southernmost point of India located ?
@ans Great Nicobar
@end

@end

Read the stem for "mainland". Southernmost point of India = Indira Point. Southernmost point of mainland India = Kanyakumari. Both are standard answers, and UPPSC lists both as options.

@section 1.5 | Land Neighbours and Border States

India's land boundary of about **15,106.7 km** touches seven countries. The longest stretch is with **Bangladesh** and the shortest with **Afghanistan**, through the Gilgit-Baltistan area of Pakistan-occupied Ladakh.

Pakistan | 3,323 | Gujarat, Rajasthan, Punjab, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh
Nepal | 1,751 | Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Sikkim
Myanmar | 1,643 | Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram
Bhutan | 699 | Sikkim, West Bengal, Assam, Arunachal Pradesh

@section 1.6 | Boundary Lines

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 1996
The boundary between India and Pakistan was demarcated by:
(a) Durand Line
(b) McMahon Line
(c) Maginot Line
(d) Radcliffe Line
@ans (d)
@end

Sir **Cyril Radcliffe** chaired the two boundary commissions of 1947 that divided **Punjab and Bengal**. The award was announced on 17 August 1947. The Radcliffe Line is therefore the origin of both the India-Pakistan and the India-Bangladesh boundaries. The **Maginot Line** was a French defence line against Germany and is a dummy option.

@pyq U.P. U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2006
Durand Line demarcated the Indian boundary with which of the following countries?
(a) Afghanistan
(b) Burma
(c) Nepal
(d) Tibet
@ans (a)
@end

The **Durand Line** (1893) was agreed between Sir **Mortimer Durand**, Foreign Secretary of British India, and **Amir Abdur Rahman Khan** of Afghanistan. Today it is the Pakistan-Afghanistan border. India's own 106 km boundary with Afghanistan lies in the Gilgit-Baltistan area, which is under Pakistan's occupation.

@pyq U.P. P.C.S. (Pre) 2018
McMahon Line forms the boundary between
(a) India and China
(b) India and Pakistan
(c) India and Myanmar
(d) India and Nepal
@ans (a)
@end

The **McMahon Line** was drawn by Sir **Henry McMahon** at the **Simla Convention of 1914** between British India and Tibet. It marks the eastern sector of the India-China boundary along Arunachal Pradesh. China does not accept it.

@table
Line | Year | Between | Named after
Radcliffe Line | 1947 | India-Pakistan, India-Bangladesh | Sir Cyril Radcliffe
Durand Line | 1893 | British India-Afghanistan (now Pakistan-Afghanistan) | Sir Mortimer Durand
McMahon Line | 1914 | India-China (Tibet), eastern sector | Sir Henry McMahon
Line of Control (LoC) | 1972 (Simla Agreement) | India-Pakistan in Jammu and Kashmir | Ceasefire Line of 1949
Line of Actual Control (LAC) | Post-1962 | India-China | De facto line, not demarcated
Maginot Line | 1930s | France-Germany (defence line) | André Maginot
@end

@trap
Do not swap the two "M" lines. **McMahon** is the India-China line (Arunachal). **Maginot** is French and never an Indian boundary. The **Durand** Line is about Afghanistan, and the **Radcliffe** Line is about Partition.
@end

@heatmap

@pattern
UPPSC builds this chapter on map position, not on theory. Three blocks carry most of the items: the Tropic of Cancer (states, towns nearest to it, the Mahi), time and longitude arithmetic, and border states. The arithmetic items are easy marks if you remember 1° = 4 minutes and "east is ahead". Two answers have changed since the exams were held: India is now the most populous country, and Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh are now Union Territories. Learn the eight Tropic states, the five IST states, the seven neighbours with their border states, and the four boundary lines.
