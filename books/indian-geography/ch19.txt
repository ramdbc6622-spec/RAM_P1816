@chapter 19 | Farming Practices, Horticulture and Livestock

@intro
This chapter collects the "agriculture miscellaneous" items of the UPPSC papers: shifting cultivation, seeds, crop diseases and weeds, fibre and minor crops, horticulture (where India is second only to China), the many statement questions of the 2013 Lower Subordinate paper, and **livestock**. India is the world's **largest milk producer** (about a quarter of world output), and **Uttar Pradesh** is the largest milk-producing state and has the most buffaloes. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

**Shifting cultivation** (slash-and-burn) is practised by hill tribes: a patch of forest is cleared and burnt, cropped for two or three years and then left to recover. The regional names are listed in Chapter 5.
