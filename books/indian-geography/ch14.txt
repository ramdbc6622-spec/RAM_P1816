@chapter 14 | Multipurpose River Valley Projects and Hydropower

@intro
Nehru called the big dams the **"temples of modern India"**. A multipurpose project stores river water for **irrigation, hydropower, flood control, drinking water, navigation and fisheries** together. The first were the **Damodar Valley Corporation** (1948, modelled on the Tennessee Valley Authority), **Bhakra-Nangal** and **Hirakud**. UPPSC asks this chapter mainly as **dam-river-state** matching, and it has done so in almost every paper since 1990. This chapter covers the northern projects, the Narmada, the Chambal and Damodar, the Peninsular projects, the Indus-system projects, Uttar Pradesh's own projects and the history of hydropower. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 14.1 | Hydropower and the Himalaya

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2025
With reference to hydroelectricity in North India, which of the following statements is/are correct ?
1. There are huge possibilities of hydroelectricity in the Himalayan region.
2. Eastern Uttar Pradesh and Bihar cannot utilise this potential because the Himalayas situated to their North lies in boundary of Nepal.
Select the correct answer from the code given below :
Code :
(a) Only 2
(b) Neither 1 nor 2
(c) Both 1 and 2
(d) Only 1
@ans (d)
@end

Perennial, snow-fed rivers falling steeply through narrow gorges give the Himalaya most of India's hydropower potential (about 1.3 lakh MW of the national total of about 1.45 lakh MW). Statement 2 is wrong: eastern UP and Bihar can draw on Nepal's rivers through joint projects (Kosi, Gandak, Mahakali Treaty for **Pancheshwar**), and power is shared through the national grid.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 1993
Maximum benefits occupying state by Sardar Sarovar Dam is :
(a) Gujarat
(b) Odisha
(c) Madhya Pradesh
(d) Rajasthan
@ans (c)
@note the key follows the sharing of water and power. The Narmada Water Disputes Tribunal (1979) gave **Madhya Pradesh** about 65% of the river's usable water and **57%** of the dam's power (Maharashtra 27%, Gujarat 16%). By irrigation and drinking water from the dam itself, **Gujarat** gains most.
@end

The **Sardar Sarovar dam** (163 m above foundation) at Kevadia (Ekta Nagar), Gujarat, was completed in **2017**. Its canal carries water across Gujarat into the dry districts of Rajasthan. The **Narmada Bachao Andolan** led by Medha Patkar opposed it over displacement. The **Statue of Unity** stands just downstream.
