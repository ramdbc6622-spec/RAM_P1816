@chapter 15 | Agriculture and the Green Revolution

@intro
Agriculture and allied sectors give about a sixth of India's gross value added but support close to half of its workforce. Holdings are small (average about 1.08 hectares in 2015-16) and mostly rain-fed outside the north-west. The **Green Revolution** of 1966-67 (high-yielding varieties, fertilisers, irrigation and assured prices) made India self-sufficient in food grains, mainly through **wheat** in Punjab, Haryana and **western Uttar Pradesh**. This chapter covers agricultural regions and institutions, the structure and problems of Indian farming, inputs, the Green Revolution and the "colour revolutions". It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@pyq U.P. U.D.A./L.D.A. (Spl) (Pre) 2010
Zero Till Seed cum-Fertilizer Drill was developed at
(a) P.A.U. Ludhiana
(b) G.B. Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar
(c) IISR, Lucknow
(d) IARI, New Delhi
@ans (b)
@end

A **zero-till drill** sows wheat directly into the stubble of the harvested rice crop without ploughing. It saves time, fuel and water and lets wheat be sown on time in the rice-wheat belt of the Indo-Gangetic plain.

@pyq U.P. P.C.S. (Pre) 2003

(a) Pressure of the population on the land

@ans (c)

**Cooperative farming** pools small plots so that machines, credit and inputs can be used efficiently; it is a remedy, not a cause. **Disguised unemployment** means more people work on a farm than are needed, so their marginal product is close to zero.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Spl) (Mains) 2004
In southern India, the area of high agricultural productivity is found in –
(a) Kerala coast
(b) Tamil Nadu coast
(c) Telangana
(d) Vidarbha
@ans (b)
@end

The **Kaveri delta** of the Tamil Nadu coast, the "rice bowl of the south", combines alluvial soil, canal and tank irrigation and two rains (the south-west and north-east monsoons). **Vidarbha** and **Telangana** are largely rain-fed and drought-prone.

@section 15.3 | Fertilisers, Green Manure and Green Agriculture

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2009
The two largest consumers of chemical fertilizers in India are
(a) Andhra Pradesh and Maharashtra
(b) Punjab and Haryana
(c) Punjab and Uttar Pradesh
(d) Uttar Pradesh and Andhra Pradesh
@ans (d)
@note this follows the data of the time (2013-14: UP about 38 lakh tonnes, undivided Andhra Pradesh about 31 lakh tonnes). In 2023-24 the leading states were **Uttar Pradesh**, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Punjab and Karnataka. **Punjab** leads in consumption **per hectare**.
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2008
Balanced fertilizers are used to-
(a) Increase the production
(b) Improve Food quality
(c) Maintain the productivity of the soil
(d) All of these
@ans (d)
@end

The ideal **N:P:K ratio** for Indian soils is about **4:2:1**. Actual use is tilted heavily towards cheap urea (nitrogen). The **Nutrient Based Subsidy** scheme (2010), **neem-coated urea** (2015) and **nano urea** (2021) aim at balance.

(d) Guar
@ans (c)
@end

@one U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2008
In newly improved arid land the crop suitable for the green manure is
@ans Lobia
@end

**Green manure** crops, mostly legumes, are grown and ploughed in green to add nitrogen and organic matter. Legumes fix air nitrogen through *Rhizobium* bacteria in their root nodules. **Cowpea (lobia)**, **sunhemp** and **dhaincha** are the common ones; dhaincha also helps reclaim alkaline (usar) soil.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2003
As a result of 'Green Revolution' the yield per hectare of wheat touched the record figure of :
(a) 1500 kg
(b) 2000 kg
(c) 2222 kg
(d) 3000 kg
@ans (b)
@end

Mexican dwarf wheats (**Lerma Rojo, Sonora 64**) and Indian selections (**Kalyan Sona, Sonalika**) roughly tripled yields where water and fertiliser were available, from about 800 kg/ha in the early 1960s to about **2,000 kg/ha** by the late 1970s. National wheat yield reached about **3,600 kg/ha** in 2024-25.

@note the key reads (R) as true, but the Green Revolution had well-known costs: falling water tables, soil salinity, over-use of chemicals, loss of crop diversity, and wider gaps between regions and between large and small farmers. Most teachers would mark (c). Learn the costs either way.

@trap

@end
