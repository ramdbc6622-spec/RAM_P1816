@chapter 23 | Industries and Industrial Centres

@intro
UPPSC asks mostly **centre-industry pairs** about industry: steel plants and their foreign partners, aluminium smelters, fertilizer and petrochemical towns, paper mills, and PSU headquarters. The chapter follows that order: iron and steel, aluminium, textiles and agro-based industries, chemicals and fertilizers, paper and cement, then the industrial regions. Many answers point to **Uttar Pradesh** (Renukoot, Pipri, Chunar, Phulpur, Aonla, Panki, Bhadohi, Varanasi, Naini, Rae Bareli), so the UP links matter here. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2005

@pyq U.P.U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2013

(a) Bokaro
(b) Durgapur
(c) Kulti-Asansol

@end

**Bhadravati** is in Karnataka, far from the Damodar coalfields. It first used **charcoal** and later **hydroelectricity** from the Jog (Sharavathi) project. The other three are on the Damodar valley coal belt.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2006
Steel Authority of India was established in the year :
(a) 1965
(b) 1971
(c) 1974
(d) 1956
@ans (*)
@note **SAIL** was formed on **24 January 1973** as a holding company for Bhilai, Rourkela, Durgapur, Bokaro and other plants, which had earlier been under Hindustan Steel Ltd. (1954). 1973 is not among the options, so the source marks the question (*).

@pyq U.P.U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2013
In which of the following area, the first cotton textile factory begin in 1818 –
(a) Fort Gloster in West Bengal
(b) Mumbai in Maharashtra
(c) Ahmadabad in Gujarat
(d) Kanpur in Uttar Pradesh
@ans (a)
@end

The 1818 mill at **Fort Gloster** (near Kolkata) failed. The first **successful** modern cotton mill was set up by **C.N. Davar** in **Bombay in 1854**. The first **jute** mill came up at **Rishra** (Hugli) in **1855**.

@heatmap

@pattern
Industry questions are almost entirely **match-the-following on centres**. The most repeated groups are: aluminium (Korba, Renukoot, Hirakud, Koraput, Muri, Alupuram, Belagavi), fertilizers (Sindri, Phulpur, Aonla, Panki, Talcher, Ramagundam), paper (Titagarh, Amlai, Nepanagar, Ballarpur, Brajrajnagar) and steel plants with their foreign partners. Keep a separate list of **UP centres** (Renukoot, Pipri, Chunar, Phulpur, Aonla, Panki, Bhadohi, Varanasi, Rae Bareli, Mathura). Nearly every year at least one UP town appears in an option.
