@chapter 16 | Major Crops I: Crop Seasons, Wheat and Rice

@intro
India grows crops in three seasons: **kharif** (sown with the monsoon in June-July, harvested in September-October), **rabi** (sown in October-November, harvested in March-April) and the short irrigated **zaid** season between them. **Rice** is the chief food crop and occupies the largest area; **wheat** is the main rabi cereal. **Uttar Pradesh** is India's largest producer of wheat (about 30%) and of food grains overall, has the largest area under rice, and in 2024-25 also became the largest producer of rice. UPPSC asks this chapter through seasons, leading states, varieties and diseases. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 16.1 | Crop Seasons

@table
Season | Sowing | Harvest | Main crops
Kharif | June-July | September-October | Rice, maize, jowar, bajra, ragi, arhar, moong, urad, cotton, groundnut, soybean, jute, sugarcane (planting)
Rabi | October-November | March-April | Wheat, barley, gram, lentil, pea, mustard, linseed, potato
Zaid | March | June | Watermelon, muskmelon, cucumber, vegetables, moong and urad (irrigated)
@end

@pyq U.P. R.O./A.R.O. (Pre) 2023
Given below are two statements, in which one is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other as Reason (R) :

Select the correct answer using the code given below :
Code :

Winter rain in the north-west comes from **western disturbances**, which are **temperate** (extra-tropical) systems from the Mediterranean, not "western tropical cyclones". Rabi crops there depend mainly on **irrigation**, with the winter showers as a bonus.

@pyq U.P. Lower Sub. (Pre) 2013
Consider the following statements :
1. Highest production of wheat in India is obtained from the state of U.P.
2. Cropping pattern occupying the highest area in U.P. is rice-wheat.
3. An extension worker does not need political competency.
4. The most critical stage of irrigation in wheat is flowering stage.
Which of these statements are correct ?
(a) Only 1 and 2
(b) Only 2 and 3
(c) Only 1, 2 and 4
(d) Only 1, 2 and 3
@ans (d)
@end

The most critical irrigation for wheat is at **crown root initiation (CRI)**, about **20-25 days after sowing**. If only one irrigation is possible, it is given then; flowering is the second priority. UP link: the **rice-wheat** sequence covers the largest area in UP and across the Indo-Gangetic plain.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2009
The production of wheat in India during 2006-07 and 2009-10 has –
(a) Increase
(b) Decrease
(c) Experienced fluctuations
(d) Remained stagnant

Production rose each year from about 75.8 million tonnes (2006-07) to about 80.8 million tonnes (2009-10). It reached a record of about **117.5 million tonnes in 2024-25**.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Spl) (Mains) 2004
Macaroni wheat is most suitable under what conditions
(a) Highly irrigated conditions
(b) Late sown conditions
(c) Unirrigated conditions
(d) None of the above
@ans (c)
@end

**Macaroni (durum) wheat**, *Triticum durum*, is hard-grained and drought-tolerant, so it suits **rain-fed** conditions of central and peninsular India. It is used for pasta and semolina. Common bread wheat is *Triticum aestivum*.

**Triticale** (*Triticum* × *Secale*) is the first man-made cereal. It joins the yield of **wheat** with the hardiness of **rye**.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2007
In India, rice is cultivated in the areas having over-
(a) 20 cm annual rainfall
(b) 30 cm annual rainfall
(c) 60 cm annual rainfall
(d) 100 cm annual rainfall
@ans (d)
@end

Rice needs high temperatures (above about **25°C**), rainfall above about **100 cm**, and standing water. The **100 cm isohyet** marks its natural western limit; west of it (Punjab, Haryana) rice grows only with canal and tube-well irrigation.

@heatmap

@pattern
Rice and wheat questions follow three patterns: leading states (with the data changing over time), varieties and genes, and seasons. Many keys in this chapter depend on the year: the order of wheat states, the largest rice producer, and the group of states making up half the rice. Learn the current position (UP first in wheat, rice area and, from 2024-25, rice production; Punjab first in rice yield; MP second in wheat) and the position when the exam was set. Variety names repay little effort; learn the series codes instead (UP = Pantnagar, Pusa = IARI, Narendra = Ayodhya).
@end
