@chapter 25 | Transport: Roads, Railways, Ports and Waterways
@unit UNIT V · TRANSPORT, TOURISM AND MISCELLANY

@intro
India has the world's **second largest road network** (about 63.45 lakh km), the **fourth largest railway network**, 12 major ports and a growing set of national waterways. UPPSC questions here follow three lines: **firsts** (first railway, first expressway, first CNG train, first PPP line), **locations** (railway zones and factories, ports and their states and coasts) and **highway numbering**. The highway numbering questions predate the **2010 renumbering**, so several of their keys are now outdated. UP features as the Jhansi corridor crossing, Raebareli's coach factory, Varanasi's locomotive works, Gorakhpur's NER headquarters, NW-1 from Prayagraj and Kushinagar airport. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 25.1 | Roads and National Highways

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2014
The share of Road Transport in total transport of India is–
(a) 100%
(b) 80%
(c) 60%
(d) 40%
@ans (b)
@end

Roads carry about **87% of passenger** traffic and about **60-65% of freight** traffic in India. At the time of the question the commonly quoted figure was 80% of passengers. India's network is about **63.45 lakh km** (MoRTH Annual Report 2024-25), second only to the USA's.

(c) Rajasthan
(d) Uttar Pradesh
