@chapter 7 | Drainage II: Peninsular Rivers and River Cities

@intro
The Peninsular rivers are **rain-fed and seasonal**, flow in broad, graded valleys and follow the **eastward tilt** of the plateau. The Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri build deltas on the Bay of Bengal. The Narmada and Tapi flow **west** in **rift valleys** and end in estuaries. Short, swift streams fall from the Western Ghats to the Arabian Sea. This chapter covers the east- and west-flowing rivers, sources, tributaries, basin sizes, the Damodar and other rivers, and the cities on river banks. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 7.1 | West-flowing Rivers, Rift Valleys and Estuaries

The **Narmada** (1,312 km) and **Tapi** (724 km) flow west along **fault troughs** (rift valleys) between the Vindhya, Satpura and Ajanta blocks. Their steep, rocky valleys trap little silt, and strong tides at the mouth carry away what arrives, so both end in **estuaries**, not deltas.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2019
In which of the following rivers, the upper course contains freshwater but saline water flowing at the lower part?
(a) Barak river
(b) Luni river
(c) Ghaggar river
(d) None of the above
@ans (b)
@end

The **Luni** (about 495 km) rises in the Aravalli near **Ajmer** (where the Pushkar stream, the Sarasvati, joins it) and flows south-west across the Marwar plain into the **Rann of Kutch**. Its water is fresh near the source, but below **Balotra** salts from the desert soils make it **saline**. It is the main river of the Thar.

@trap
**Estuary rivers**: Narmada, Tapi and the short Western Ghat streams. **Delta rivers**: Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri. Of the east-flowing rivers, the **Damodar** alone flows in a rift.
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2014
The rivers of South India mainly have which of the following drainage patterns ?
(a) Radial
(b) Intermittent
(c) Dendritic
(d) Trellis
@ans (c)
@end

A **dendritic** (tree-like) pattern forms on rock of uniform resistance, which covers most of the plateau. **Radial** patterns occur around domes such as **Amarkantak** and the **Girnar**. **Trellis** patterns occur in folded belts such as the Singhbhum and the Himalayan foothills. **Centripetal** drainage feeds **Loktak Lake** in Manipur.

@section 7.4 | The Damodar and Other Rivers

@pyq U.P. B.E.O. (Pre) 2019
Which of the following rivers of India is NOT a tributary of Damodar River?
(a) Barakar
(b) Indrawati
(c) Jamunia
(d) Barki
@ans (b)
@end

@one U.P.U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2006; U.P. Lower Sub. (Spl) (Pre) 2008; U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2008
Damodar is a tributary of river
@ans Hooghly
@end

The **Damodar** rises on the Chotanagpur plateau and joins the **Hooghly** below Kolkata. Its tributaries are the **Barakar** (the largest), **Konar, Bokaro, Jamunia** and **Barki**. The **Indravati** is a tributary of the Godavari. Floods earned the Damodar the name **"Sorrow of Bengal"** until the **Damodar Valley Corporation** (1948, modelled on the Tennessee Valley Authority) built dams at Tilaiya, Konar, Maithon and Panchet.

@pyq U.P.U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2013

(a) Gomati
(b) Periyar
(c) Damodar

The Damodar valley is India's coal and steel belt (Jharia, Bokaro, Durgapur, Asansol). Coal washeries, steel plants and thermal power stations make it one of the most polluted rivers in India, which is why it is chosen over the Gomti here.

@pyq U.P.R.O./A.R.O. (Pre) 2014
Mithi river of Mumbai originates from which of the following lakes ?
(a) Tulsi Lake
(b) Vihar Lake
(c) Powai Lake

The **Mithi** begins from the overflow of **Vihar Lake** (with Powai Lake's overflow joining it) in Sanjay Gandhi National Park. It flows through Mumbai to **Mahim Creek**. Its choked channel was a key cause of the **Mumbai floods of 26 July 2005**.

@heatmap

@pattern
Peninsular rivers are tested through four fixed ideas: rift rivers (Narmada, Tapi, Damodar) end in estuaries; delta rivers flow east; sources (Amarkantak, Mahabaleshwar, Trimbakeshwar, Sihawa, Talakaveri); and the largest and longest basin is the Godavari. City-river pairs are an old UPPSC favourite, with Bhubaneswar and Hyderabad as the repeated traps. Recent papers (2019 to 2025) ask more about tributaries (Pranhita, Jonk) and river behaviour (Luni salinity, fault valleys, Arabian Sea rivers). Two keys need care: "Ganga of the South" (UPPSC: Kaveri) and the Krishna dispute (three states).
@end
