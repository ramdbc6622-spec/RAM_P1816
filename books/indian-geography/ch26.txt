@chapter 26 | Tourism and Miscellaneous

@intro
The last chapter collects two short source sections. **Tourism** questions are about hill stations and their states, city nicknames, temple hills and ghost towns. The **Miscellaneous** questions range from biogas to locusts, space centres and agricultural universities. They are few, but they are easy marks if the location tables are learnt. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

**Udaipur** is the "City of Lakes". **Jaisalmer** is the "**Golden City**", **Jodhpur** the "**Blue City**" (or Sun City) and **Jaipur** the "Pink City". @pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) (Re- Exam) 2015
Which of the following is not a 'ghost-town'?
(a) Kuldhara
(b) Dhanushkodi
(c) Lakhpat
(d) Charkhari
@ans (d)
@end

**Kuldhara** (Jaisalmer) was abandoned by the Paliwal Brahmins in the 19th century. **Dhanushkodi** (Rameswaram) was destroyed by the **1964 cyclone**. **Lakhpat** (Kutch) declined after the 1819 earthquake changed the course of the Indus. **Charkhari** (Mahoba, **UP**) is a living town known as the "Kashmir of Bundelkhand" for its lakes.

@section 26.2 | Temple Hills

In which of the following hills the world famous temple of Lord Venkateshwar (Tirupati) is located?
(a) Shevaroy
(b) Biligiriranga
(c) Javadhee
(d) Mallmalla
@ans (*)
@note The Tirumala **Venkateswara** temple stands on the **Seshachalam** hills (part of the Eastern Ghats) in Tirupati district, Andhra Pradesh. None of the options is correct, so the source marks the question (*). The **Shevaroy** and **Javadi** hills are in Tamil Nadu and the **Biligiriranga** hills in Karnataka.
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2015
The south Indian temple of Lord Ranganatha (also known as Lord Venkatesha) is located on :
(a) Biligiri Ranga Hills
(b) Thirumala Hills
(c) Nandi Hills
(d) Chamundi Hills
@ans (a)
@end

@trap
Two temples, two hill ranges: **Tirupati (Venkateswara)** is on the **Seshachalam/Tirumala** hills in Andhra Pradesh. **Biligiri Rangaswamy (Ranganatha)** is on the **BR Hills** in Chamarajanagar, Karnataka, which is also a tiger reserve. **Chamundi Hills** is in Mysuru and **Nandi Hills** near Bengaluru.
@end

@pyq U.P. U.D.A./L.D.A. (Mains) 2010
Locusts enter India from –
(a) Bangladesh
(b) Nepal
(c) Pakistan
(d) Sri Lanka
@ans (c)
@end

Desert locusts reach **Rajasthan and Gujarat** from Pakistan, breeding there in the summer monsoon. The **Locust Warning Organisation** is at **Jodhpur** (headquarters at Faridabad). The **2020** swarm reached as far as **UP** (Jhansi, Agra, Lalitpur).

@pyq U.P. Lower Sub. (Spl) (Pre) 2004
Which one is not correctly matched ?
(a) Sriharikota – Andhra Pradesh
(b) Thumba – Kerala
(c) Bhabha Atomic Research Centre – Karnataka
(d) Pokhran – Rajasthan
@ans (c)
@end

**BARC** is at **Trombay, Mumbai** (Maharashtra). **Sriharikota** (Satish Dhawan Space Centre) is in Tirupati district, AP. **Thumba** (TERLS, 1963) is near Thiruvananthapuram. **Pokhran** (Jaisalmer) was the site of the 1974 and 1998 nuclear tests. India's second spaceport is coming up at **Kulasekarapattinam** (Thoothukudi, Tamil Nadu).

@pyq U.P.R.O./A.R.O. (Pre) 2016
Which of the following is not correctly matched?
(a) Jawaharlal Nehru Krishi Vishwavidyalaya - Madhya Pradesh
(b) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel University of Agriculture and Technology - Uttar Pradesh
(c) Indira Gandhi Krishi Vishwavidhyalaya - Rajasthan

**IGKV** is at **Raipur** (Chhattisgarh) and **ANGRAU** at **Guntur** (Andhra Pradesh). Telangana's university is the Professor Jayashankar Telangana Agricultural University, Hyderabad. **JNKVV** is at **Jabalpur** and **SVPUAT** at **Meerut** (UP link). UP's other agricultural universities: **Pantnagar** (GBPUAT, India's first, 1960, now Uttarakhand), **CSAUAT Kanpur**, **ANDUAT Kumarganj (Ayodhya)** and **Banda University of Agriculture and Technology**.

@heatmap

@pattern
Tourism questions are **match-the-following** on hill stations and states, often four states from four corners of India, so one sure pair usually fixes the code. Miscellaneous questions repeat **location** facts (BARC Trombay, HMI Darjeeling, IGKV Raipur). The UP angle shows up through Charkhari (Mahoba), Meerut's agricultural university and the 2020 locust swarm.
@end

@next
Likely next angles are UP tourism circuits (**Ramayana**, **Buddhist**: Sarnath, Kushinagar, Kapilvastu-Piprahwa, Shravasti, Sankisa, Kaushambi; **Krishna**: Mathura-Vrindavan), the **Kashi Vishwanath Corridor** and the **Ram Mandir** at Ayodhya, and **Swadesh Darshan 2.0** sites. Another angle is India's new **UNESCO** sites (e.g. the **Maratha Military Landscapes**, 2025, and **Moidams** of Assam, 2024).
@end
