@chapter 8 | Waterfalls, Lakes and Hot Springs

@intro
Most of India's big waterfalls are on the edges of the Peninsular plateau: where rivers drop off the Western Ghats (Jog, Dudhsagar, Kunchikal), over the Vindhyan and Rewa scarps (Chachai, Keoti), or off the Ranchi and Bastar plateaus (Hundru, Chitrakote). India's lakes are of many kinds: coastal lagoons (Chilika, Pulicat, Vembanad), tectonic and glacial lakes of the Himalaya (Wular, Roopkund), inland salt lakes (Sambhar), a meteorite crater (Lonar) and oxbow lakes of the plains. UPPSC asks this chapter almost entirely by location matching. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 8.1 | Waterfalls and their Rivers

@pyq U.P. P.C.S. (Pre) 2018
Which of the following is the highest waterfall in India?
(a) Jog fall
(b) Kunchikal fall
(c) Rakim Kund fall
(d) Kevti fall
@ans (b)
@note UPPSC has always treated **Kunchikal** as India's highest. The World Waterfall Database now lists lower heights for it, and many sources give **Nohkalikai** (Meghalaya, about 340 m, a single plunge) as the tallest. Choose Kunchikal when it is offered; if not, Nohkalikai.
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2015
Which one of the following is the correct height of Kunchikal Waterfall ?
(a) 335 metre
(b) 337 metre
(c) 399 metre
(d) 455 metre
@ans (d)
@end

**Kunchikal** is a series of cascades on the **Varahi** river in Shivamogga, **Karnataka**. It was listed at **455 m** in total drop when these questions were set. The water is now largely diverted for the Varahi hydroelectric scheme.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2005
Which one of the following pairs is not correctly matched ?
(a) Sivasamudram Waterfall - Cauvery
(b) Chulia Waterfall - Chambal
(c) Jog Waterfall - Krishna
(d) Dhuandhar Waterfall - Narmada
@ans (c)
@end

**Jog Falls** (253 m), also called Gersoppa, is on the **Sharavati** in Karnataka. **Sivasamudram** on the Kaveri powered one of Asia's earliest hydroelectric stations (1902). **Chulia** is on the Chambal near Rawatbhata, Rajasthan, and **Dhuandhar** on the Narmada at Bhedaghat near Jabalpur.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2021

(b) Chachai Waterfall - Bihad River

**Budha Ghagh (Lodh) Falls** in Latehar, Jharkhand's highest waterfall, is on the **Burha (Budha)** river. The **Kanchi** river forms the **Dassam Falls** near Ranchi.

@section 8.2 | Coastal Lagoons and Lakes of the South

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 1997
Vembanad Lake is in:
(a) Andhra Pradesh
(b) Kerala
(c) Orissa
(d) Tamil Nadu
@ans (b)
@end

**Vembanad**, the largest lake of **Kerala** and the longest lake in India (about 96 km), spreads over Alappuzha, Kottayam and Ernakulam. The **Nehru Trophy Boat Race** is held on its Punnamada stretch. It is a **Ramsar site** (2002).

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2021
Largest saline water lake in India is :
(a) Chilika
(b) Sambhar
(c) Lonary
(d) Wullar
@ans (a)
@note **Chilika** (Odisha, about 1,100 sq km) is the largest brackish-water lagoon. **Sambhar** (Rajasthan) is the largest **inland** salt lake. The key takes "lake" in its widest sense and picks Chilika; if the stem says "inland", answer Sambhar.
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2021
Which of the following is the largest lagoon lake situated on the east coast of India?
(a) Pulicat
(b) Chilika
(c) Kulleru
(d) Kilivali
@ans (b)
@end

**Chilika** lies south of the Mahanadi delta in **Odisha**, cut off from the sea by a long sand bar. It is the largest wintering ground for migratory birds in India and home to **Irrawaddy dolphins**. With **Keoladeo** it became one of India's first two **Ramsar sites in 1981**.

(a) Lonar - Madhya Pradesh
(b) Nakki - Gujarat

**Pulicat**, the second largest brackish lagoon, lies on the **Andhra Pradesh-Tamil Nadu** border; the island of **Sriharikota** separates it from the sea. **Kolleru** is a large freshwater lake between the Godavari and Krishna deltas in Andhra Pradesh. **Lonar** (Buldhana, Maharashtra) fills a crater made by a meteorite about 50,000 years ago. **Nakki** is on Mount Abu, Rajasthan.

@section 8.3 | Himalayan and Other Inland Lakes

@pyq U.P. R.O. / A.R.O. (Pre) 2021
Which of the following is the largest Fresh Water Lake of India?
(a) Kolleru
(b) Wular
(c) Nalsarovar
(d) Sambhar
@ans (b)
@note **Wular** (Bandipora, Jammu and Kashmir), fed by the Jhelum, is the standard answer and the largest by volume. By surface area at full flood, **Kolleru** can exceed it. Sambhar is salt, and Nalsarovar (Gujarat) is a shallow wetland.
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S.(Pre) (Re-Exam) 2015
Which of the following lakes of India is located in Jammu and Kashmir?
(a) Phulhar lake
(b) Kolleru lake
(c) Anchar lake
(d) Hamirsar lake
@ans (c)
@end

**Anchar** lake lies on the north-western edge of **Srinagar**, near Dal and Nigeen lakes. **Hamirsar** is the historic tank of **Bhuj** (Gujarat).

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2015
Which one of the following is called the 'Mysterious lake'?
(a) Jor Pokhri Lake
(b) Dodital lake
(c) Roopkund Lake
(d) Red hills lake
@ans (c)
@end

**Roopkund** (about 5,000 m, Chamoli, Uttarakhand) is a small glacial lake on the Nanda Devi Raj Jat route. Hundreds of human skeletons lie around it, which is why it is called the **"Skeleton Lake"** or **"Mysterious Lake"**. DNA studies (2019) show the dead came from different groups and periods.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2016
The snow-covered Ghepan Lake is located in –
(a) Uttarakhand
(b) Himachal Pradesh
(c) Sikkim
(d) Jammu and Kashmir
@ans (b)
@end

**Ghepan Ghat** lake in **Lahaul-Spiti** (Himachal Pradesh) is a growing **glacial lake**, watched for the risk of a **glacial lake outburst flood (GLOF)** like the one from **South Lhonak** lake that hit the Teesta valley in Sikkim in **October 2023**.

@heatmap

@pattern
Every question in this chapter is a location: which river makes which waterfall, which state holds which lake. Karnataka and Jharkhand supply most waterfall options, and Kerala, Odisha and Andhra Pradesh most lake options. Three keys need a note: the highest waterfall (UPPSC keeps Kunchikal), the largest saline lake (Chilika for "lake", Sambhar for "inland lake"), and the largest freshwater lake (Wular). Recent papers (2020 to 2022) added Kaliveli, Sala, Barkana, Khandadhar and hot springs, so expect lesser-known names in the next set.
@end
