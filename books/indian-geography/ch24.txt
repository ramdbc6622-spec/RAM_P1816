@chapter 24 | Research Centres and Institutes

@intro
UPPSC regularly asks where a national institute is located, especially the **ICAR** institutes. Uttar Pradesh hosts a large share of them: **Lucknow** (sugarcane, subtropical horticulture), **Kanpur** (pulses), **Varanasi** (vegetables), **Jhansi** (grassland and agroforestry), **Mathura** (goats), **Meerut** (farming systems), **Ghaziabad** (organic farming) and **Prayagraj** (eco-rehabilitation). Learn this chapter as location tables, with the UP institutes as a group of their own. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 24.1 | Polar Research Stations

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2011
The Third Indian Research Centre had been founded in Antarctica by the name of :
(a) Saraswati
(b) Bharati
(c) Anveshan
(d) Yamnotri
@ans (b)
@end

@table
Station | Region | Year | Note
Dakshin Gangotri | Antarctica | 1983-84 | First station; buried in ice, now a supply base
Maitri | Antarctica (Schirmacher Oasis) | 1989 | Working; to be replaced by Maitri-II
Bharati | Antarctica (Larsemann Hills) | 2012 | Working
Himadri | Arctic (Ny-Alesund, Svalbard, Norway) | 2008 | India's first Arctic station
Himansh | Himalaya (Spiti, HP) | 2016 | High-altitude glacier research station
@end

The **National Centre for Polar and Ocean Research** (NCPOR), Goa, runs India's polar programme.

@heatmap

@pattern
Institute questions are straight **location recall**. Of the 34 PYQs here, nearly a third have an answer in **Uttar Pradesh**, so the UP table in section 24.2 is the highest-value revision list. The rest cluster around **Hyderabad** (ICRISAT, CRIDA, NAARM), **Karnal** (NDRI, CSSRI, wheat), **Nagpur** (IBM, NEERI) and **Mysuru** (CFTRI, CIIL).
@end

@next
Likely new questions: India's Antarctic station **Maitri-II**, the IRRI **South Asia Regional Centre at Varanasi**, the **International Potato Center South Asia Regional Centre** at Agra (UP), the **National Institute of Solar Energy** (Gurugram) and **CIFOR-ICRAF**. Also expect **renamed** institutes, e.g. Directorate → Indian Institute, or NCOF → National Centre for Organic and Natural Farming.
@end
