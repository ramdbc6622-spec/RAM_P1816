@chapter 3 | Coastal Regions and Islands

@intro
India's mainland coast runs from the Rann of Kutch to the Sundarbans. Nine states and four Union Territories lie on the sea. The **western coastal plain** is narrow, broken by estuaries and backwaters. The **eastern coastal plain** is wide and built by deltas. Offshore lie two very different groups of islands: the **Andaman and Nicobar Islands**, the peaks of a submarine mountain chain in the Bay of Bengal, and the **Lakshadweep**, low coral atolls in the Arabian Sea. This chapter covers the sea level and maritime zones, coastline length, the named coasts, beaches, the islands and the channels between them. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 3.1 | Sea Level, Maritime Zones and Coastline Length

@pyq U.P.U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2013
Which of the following coasts the mean sea level of India is measured ?
(a) Mumbai
(b) Chennai
(c) Kochi
(d) Visakhapatnam
@ans (b)
@end

The key follows the common statement that India's **mean sea level** is reckoned from the **Chennai** tide gauge. Heights on Survey of India maps are given above this mean sea level.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2005
The limit of the territorial water of India extends upto
(a) 3 nautical miles from the coast
(b) 6 nautical miles from the coast
(c) 12 nautical miles from the coast
(d) 24 nautical miles from the coast
@ans (c)
@end

@table
Zone (measured from the baseline) | Width | India's rights
Territorial sea | 12 nautical miles | Full sovereignty, including airspace and seabed
Contiguous zone | 24 nautical miles | Customs, immigration, sanitation and security control
Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) | 200 nautical miles | Sole right to fish, mine and drill
Continental shelf | To the edge of the margin (up to 350 nm) | Seabed resources
@end

These limits come from India's **Maritime Zones Act, 1976** and the **UN Convention on the Law of the Sea (1982)**. One nautical mile is 1.852 km, so the territorial sea is about 22.2 km wide.

@pyq U.P. P.C.S. (Pre) 2007, 2018; U.P.U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2006; U.P. R.O./A.R.O. (Pre) (Re-Exam) 2016
Which of the following States of India has the longest coastline?
(a) Maharashtra
(b) Andhra Pradesh
(c) Kerala
(d) Gujarat
@ans (d)
@end

India's coastline was long given as **7,516.6 km**. A fresh measurement on larger-scale charts (released by the Government in 2024-25) gives **11,098.81 km**. Gujarat stays first on both counts.

@table
State | Coastline in the new count (km)
Gujarat | 2,340.62 (longest)
Andhra Pradesh | 1,053.07
Maharashtra | 877.97
Kerala | 600.15
@end

@one
In India, how many states are on coastal lines?
@ans 9
@end

The nine coastal states, west to east, are **Gujarat, Maharashtra, Goa, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Odisha and West Bengal**. The four coastal Union Territories are **Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Puducherry, Lakshadweep, and Andaman and Nicobar Islands**.

@trap
The coastline figure has changed. Older books give 7,516.6 km (mainland 5,422.6 km plus islands); the revised figure is 11,098.81 km. A 2026 question may use either, so read the options. **Goa** has the shortest coastline among the states.
@end

@section 3.3 | Andaman and Nicobar Islands

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2009
Which of the following is the highest peak of Andaman and Nicobar group of islands ?
(a) Saddle peak
(b) Mount Thuillier
(c) Mount Diavolo
(d) Mount Koyale
@ans (a)
@end

**Saddle Peak (732 m)** near Diglipur on **North Andaman** is the highest point of the islands. **Mount Thuillier (642 m)** on Great Nicobar is the highest in the Nicobars.

@pyq U.P.P.S.C. (GIC) 2010
Barren Island is located in-
(a) Bay of Bengal
(b) Arabian sea.
(c) Mediterranean Sea
(d) China sea
@ans (a)
@end

@one
Which one of the following is a volcanic island of India?
@ans Barren Island
@end

**Barren Island**, about 135 km north-east of Port Blair in the Andaman Sea, has **India's only active volcano**. It erupted in 1991 after about two centuries of rest and has been active on and off since. **Narcondam**, further north, is a dormant volcano.

@trap
Ten Degree Channel lies between the **Andaman** and **Nicobar** groups. **Nine** and **Eight** Degree Channels belong to the **Lakshadweep** side. Barren is active; Narcondam is dormant.
@end

@section 3.4 | Lakshadweep and Other Islands

@pyq U.P.U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2013
Which of the following is a coral island ?
(a) New Moore
(b) Car Nicobar
(c) Andaman
(d) Lakshadweep
@ans (d)
@end

**Lakshadweep** (36 islands, about 32 sq km) is built of coral atolls on the Laccadive-Chagos ridge, 220 to 440 km off Kerala. **Kavaratti** is the capital and **Minicoy** the largest and southernmost island. **New Moore (Purbasha / South Talpatti)** was a delta island in the mouth of the Hariabhanga river, disputed with Bangladesh; it has since been submerged.

@pyq U.P.U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2013
Which one of the following is one of the remotest islands from Indian coast ?
(a) Bhatkal
(b) Arnala
(c) Minicoy
(d) Henry
@ans (c)
@end

**Minicoy** lies about 400 km from the Kerala coast, near the Maldives. **Arnala** (off Vasai, Maharashtra), the islet off **Bhatkal** (Karnataka) and **Henry Island** (Sundarbans, West Bengal) are inshore islands close to the mainland.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2006
Which one of the following Indian Island lies between India and Sri Lanka?
(a) Elephanta
(b) Nicobar
(c) Rameshwaram
(d) Salsette
@ans (c)
@end

@pyq U.P. U.D.A./L.D.A. (Spl) (Pre) 2010
The Adam's Bridge begins from –
(a) Dhanushkodi
(b) Mandapam
(c) Pamban
(d) Rameshwaram
@ans (a)
@end

**Rameswaram (Pamban) Island** lies between the Palk Bay and the Gulf of Mannar. Its tip, **Dhanushkodi**, is where **Adam's Bridge (Ram Setu)**, a chain of shoals about 48 km long, begins and runs to **Mannar Island** in Sri Lanka. The new **Pamban vertical-lift railway bridge** joining the island to Mandapam opened in 2025.

@pyq U.P. P.C.S. (Mains) 2016
The most populous island of India is:
(a) Majuli
(b) Andaman
(c) Lakshadweep
(d) Salsette
@ans (d)
@end

Most of **Mumbai** stands on **Salsette Island**, which makes it the most populous island in India. **Majuli** in the Brahmaputra (Assam) is the largest river island and became India's first island district in 2016.

@pyq U.P.P.S.C. (Spl.) (Pre) 2008; U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2011
Kori Creek lies in –
(a) Gulf of Kutch
(b) Gulf of Khambhat
(c) Little Rann of Kutch
(d) Rann of Kutch
@ans (d)
@end

**Kori Creek** is a tidal inlet of the **Rann of Kutch** near the India-Pakistan border, west of Lakhpat. Its neighbour **Sir Creek** is the disputed estuary on the boundary. The area is being developed for marine fisheries and border tourism.
