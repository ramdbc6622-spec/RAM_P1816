@section 4.1 | Elasticity and Surface Tension
- Rubber's Young's modulus is **less** than steel's; coil stretching depends on **shear modulus** (only 2) {2023}
- Raindrops are spherical due to **surface tension** {2005}
- Oil spreads on water: its surface tension is **less** than water's {1994, 2010}
- Pressure inside a soap bubble: **more than atmospheric** {1995, 2014}
- Charging a soap bubble: its **radius increases** {2019}
- Iron needle floats due to **surface tension** {2012}

@section 4.2 | Pressure and Flow
- Pen leaks in a plane: **lower air pressure** {1992}
- Sudden fall in barometer reading: **stormy** weather {1996}
- Streamlined flow in a pipe: **slowest near the walls, fastest along the axis** {2003}

@section 4.3 | Density and Water at 4 C
- More of a substance does not change its **density** {1992}
- Cooling water from 9 C to 3 C: volume **first decreases, then increases** {1997}
- Maximum density of water: **4 C** (277 K) {2008, ?}
- Aquatic life survives freezing: water is **densest at 4 C**, so liquid water stays below the ice {2005, ?}

@flow Anomalous expansion of water
Above 4 C | Cooling makes water contract
At 4 C | Maximum density; sinks to the bottom
0-4 C | Water expands on cooling
0 C | Ice forms on top, less dense; floats
Result | Lake bottom stays liquid; fish survive
@end

@section 4.4 | Archimedes' Principle and Flotation
- Iron nail floats on mercury, sinks in water: iron is **denser than water, lighter than mercury** {1994}
- Needle sinks, iron ship floats: needle's density is **greater than the water it displaces** {2004}
- Bucket feels **heavier above the water surface** {2009}
- 200 g of water in a weightless balloon weighs **zero** in water {2014}
- Iceberg above the sea: about **1/9** {2005}
- Easier to swim in the sea: sea water is **salty** (denser) {2002}
- Ship from river to sea: it **rises a little**; sea water is denser (A and R true, R explains A) {1992, 2004, ?}

@trap
- Water's density peak is **4 C**, not 0 C; ice is **less** dense and floats.
- Soap bubble pressure is **higher** inside; oil spreads because its surface tension is **lower**.
- Steel is **more** elastic (higher Young's modulus) than rubber.
@end
