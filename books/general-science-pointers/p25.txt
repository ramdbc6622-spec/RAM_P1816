@section 25.4 | Medicinal and Drug Plants
- **Quinine**: **cinchona bark** {2016, ?}
- **Arteether** (antimalarial): from a **seed plant** (Artemisia annua) {2005}
- Synthetic drug that replaced quinine: **chloroquine** {2000}
- Hydroxychloroquine is fully **synthetic**; no plant fits (*) {2016}
- **Noscapine**: from **poppy** {2012}
- **Heroin**: from **opium poppy** {2005}
- Opium: **latex of the unripe fruit** {2011, 2014}
- Chief constituent of opium: **morphine** {2016}

@flow From poppy to drugs
Poppy capsule | Unripe fruit is cut
Latex | Dries into raw opium
Morphine | Main alkaloid; painkiller
Heroin | Made from morphine (diacetylmorphine)
Noscapine, codeine | Cough medicines
@end

@trap
- Opium comes from the **fruit**, not the seed or flower.
- Rayon is made from **cellulose**, not starch.
- Hing is a **resin** from roots and stem base, not a seed.
- Litmus = lichen; caffeine = tea **and** coffee.
@end
