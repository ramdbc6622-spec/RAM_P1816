@trap
- **Hygrometer** = humidity; **hydrometer** = liquid density.
- **Pyrometer** = high temperature; **pyrheliometer** = solar radiation.
- **Barometer** = air pressure; **manometer** = gas pressure; **ammeter** = current, not power.
- Kelvin has no negative values; SONAR = sound, RADAR = radio waves.
@end

@up UP link: IMD's **Meteorological Centre, Lucknow** runs the UP network of rain gauges, automatic weather stations and a **Doppler weather radar** (soft detail); the **seismograph** network covers UP because its Terai belt lies in seismic zone IV.
