@section 24.1 | Photosynthesis
- Element in chlorophyll: **magnesium** {2012, 2013}
- Plants make food by **photosynthesis** {2013}
- Sunlight becomes chemical energy; glucose turns into starch (A and R true, R explains A) {?}
- Essential gas: **CO2**; **oxygen** is not needed {2011, ?}
- Oxygen released comes from **water** {2014}
- Most of Earth's oxygen: **algae** (phytoplankton) {2015}
- Most effective light: **red** {2014}
- Artificial light **can** drive photosynthesis {2008}
- Best solar-to-chemical converter: **Chlorella** {2014}
- Does not add CO2 to the carbon cycle: **photosynthesis** {2012, 2013}

@flow Photosynthesis in one line
Inputs | CO2 + water + sunlight
Pigment | Chlorophyll (magnesium); absorbs red and blue
Light reaction | Water split; oxygen released
Dark reaction | CO2 fixed into glucose, then starch
@end

@section 24.2 | Transport in Plants
- **Phloem**: transports **food** (organic nutrients) {2012, 2015}
- **Xylem**: transports **water** {2015, 2023}

@section 24.3 | Mineral Nutrition
- Not a micronutrient: **magnesium** (a macronutrient) {2019}
- Plants take nitrogen as **nitrate** {2016, 2021}
- Yellowing and poor growth: lack of **nitrogen** {2023}
- More oil in mustard: **sulphur** {2021}
- Nodule formation in legumes: **boron** (per key) {2019}
- **Cuscuta** (amarbel) is a parasite; it feeds on the host's **stem** via haustoria, not leaves (A true, R false) {2016}
- **Crop logging**: plant analysis to judge **nutrient needs** {2006}

@table
Nutrient | Role / deficiency
Nitrogen | Leaf growth; lack = yellowing (chlorosis)
Phosphorus | Roots, flowering
Potassium | Disease resistance, quality
Sulphur | Oil content (mustard)
Magnesium | Chlorophyll centre
Zinc | Khaira disease of rice
Boron | Flowering, nodules
@end

@section 24.4 | Plant Hormones
- A plant hormone: **cytokinin** {2015, 2016, 2021}
- Not a plant hormone: **insulin** {2011}
- **Auxin-apical dominance, gibberellin-cell elongation, cytokinin-cell division, abscisic acid-growth inhibition** {2016}
- Drought tolerance: **abscisic acid** {2013}
- Correct (per key): weeping jelly from excess acid; **cytokinin** breaks dormancy (1 and 4) {2013}
- Ripening gas: **ethylene** (the key prints "ethane") {1996, 2007, 2008, ?}
- Hastens ripening: **ethrel** {2023}
- **Ripening-ethylene, baking soda-sodium bicarbonate, soft drink-CO2, quartz-sodium silicate** (per key) {2017}
