- Heaviest natural element: **uranium** {2004}

@section 15.3 | Corrosion
- Galvanised iron does not rust: **zinc** oxidises first (A and R true, R explains A) {2002}
- Rusting needs **oxygen and moisture** {2016}
- Rusting **increases** iron's weight {2008}
- Rust is brown iron oxide, not **tannin** (A true, R false) {1998}
- Silver turns black from **silver sulphide** (both, 2025); the 2008 key is misprinted (*) {2025, 2008}

@section 15.4 | Alloys
- **Amalgam**: alloy with **mercury** (mercury-zinc) {2012}
- **Solder**: **tin and lead** {1996, 2005}
- **Bronze**: **copper and tin** {2015, 2016}
- **German silver**: **0% silver** (copper, zinc, nickel) {2003}
- Common to brass, bronze, German silver: **copper**; brass = **copper and zinc** {?, ?}
- True: brass is copper-zinc; amalgams contain mercury (i and iii); magnetite is an **iron** ore {2001}
- Not used for stainless steel: **silicon** {2004, 2017}
- Stainless steel: iron with **chromium**, nickel, manganese, carbon {?, ?}
