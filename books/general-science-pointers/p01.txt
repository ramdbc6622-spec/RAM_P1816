- **Light year** and **parsec** are units of **distance**, not time {1997, 2010, 2010, 2013, 2023, ?}
- **Celsius and centigrade** are temperature scales, **not units of heat** {2001, 2004, 2017}

@section 1.1 | Work, Power and Energy Units
- Unit of power: **watt** {2010}
- Unit of work: **joule** {1996}
- Megawatt measures power **generated** {1998}

@section 1.2 | Astronomical and Small Lengths
- **Light year**: distance light travels in **one year**; a unit of **distance** {2010, 2013, ?}
- **Parsec**: unit of **distance** {1997}
- Distance at which Earth's orbit radius subtends **1 arc second**: **1 parsec** {2023}
- Multiply by 0.39 to get inches: **centimetre** {2010}
- **1 nanometre = 10^{-7} cm** {2013}
- Correct: **1 metre = 10^{9} nanometre** {2023}
- 5 feet in nanometres: none of the options (about 1.524 x 10^{9} nm) {2023}
- **1 micron = 10^{-4} cm** {2011}
- Smallest unit of length: **fermi** (femtometre, 10^{-15} m) {2005}

@section 1.3 | SI Units and Matching
- **Acceleration-m/s^{2}, force-newton, work-joule, impulse-newton second** {2001, 2005}
- Wrong SI pair: **pressure-dyne** (dyne is a CGS unit of force) {2013}
- **Watt-power, knot-speed of a ship, nautical mile-navigation, calorie-heat** {2002}
- **Joule-work, ampere-current, watt-power, volt-potential, calorie-heat** {1990}
- Wrong pair: **light year-time** (it is distance) {2010}
- **Mach-high speed, angstrom-wavelength, pascal-pressure, joule-energy** {2006}
- **Decibel**: **sound intensity** {2011}
- Wrong pair: **Celsius-unit of heat** {2001, 2004}
- Not a unit of heat: **centigrade** {2017}
- Wrong SI pair: **radioactivity-curie** (SI unit is the **becquerel**) {2022}
- Electric potential is a **scalar** quantity (only 1) {2021}

@section 1.4 | Odd Units
- **1 barrel** of oil = **159 litres** {2009}
- **Cusec-rate of flow, byte-computer, Richter-earthquake, bar-pressure** {2008}
- **Dobson unit**: **ozone** concentration {2021}

@trap
- **Parsec > light year > AU**; all three measure **distance**.
- **1 micron = 10^{-4} cm**, not 10^{-6} cm (that is in metres).
- SI unit of radioactivity = **becquerel**; curie is older. Dyne is force, not pressure.
- **Knot** = speed (nautical mile per hour); **nautical mile** = distance at sea.
@end

@up UP link: **Indian Standard Time** is reckoned from **82.5 E**, which passes near **Mirzapur** (UP); the time signal itself is kept by the **National Physical Laboratory**, New Delhi.

@last

- Fermi = smallest length unit
