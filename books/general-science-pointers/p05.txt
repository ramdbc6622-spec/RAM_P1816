@section 5.1 | Nature and Speed of Light
- Visible light: about **390-780 nm** {2005}
- Eye most sensitive at **5500 Å** (yellow-green) {2008, 2014}
- Not correct: eye is most sensitive to **red** {2021}
- Light seems to go straight: its **wavelength is very small** {2014}
- Highest velocity: **light**; light is faster than sound {2002, 2018}
- Light is fastest in a **vacuum** {2014}
- Sunlight reaches Earth in about **8 minutes** (8 min 20 s) {2006}
- Not luminous: **Moon** (it reflects sunlight) {2023}

@section 5.2 | Refraction and Total Internal Reflection
- Unchanged when light changes medium: **frequency** {2003}
- Pool looks shallower: **refraction** {2023}
- Sun and Moon look oval near the horizon: **refraction** {2008}
- Optical fibre: **total internal reflection** {2008, 2010, ?}
- Optical fibre: high capacity, low power, **no radio interference** (1, 2, 3) {2001, 2002, 2003}
- **Mirage**: **total internal reflection** {1995, 2012, 2013, 2014}
- Diamond shines: **total internal reflection** {1996, 2012, 2015, 2017}
- 3-D pictures: **holography** {1990, 2008}

@section 5.4 | Mirrors and Lenses
- Air bubble in water acts as a **concave lens** {2002, 2008, 2010, 2013}
- Images between two parallel mirrors: **infinite** {1994, 2013, 2015}
- Mirror to see your full image: **half your height**; for 1.50 m, **0.75 m** {2010, 2011, 2014, 2014}
- Radius of curvature of a plane mirror: **infinity** {2015}
- Not correct: doctors use a **convex** mirror for ENT checks (they use **concave**) {2010}
- Searchlight and car headlight: **concave mirror** {2009, 2023}
- Rear-view mirror: **convex mirror** {1991, 2010, 2011, 2015}
- Power of sunglasses: **0 dioptre** {1995}
- Astronomical telescope image: **virtual and magnified** {2013}
- Equal convex and concave lenses together: a **plane glass sheet** {2017}

@section 5.5 | The Human Eye
- Image on the retina: **smaller and inverted** (real) {1995, 2004}
- Retina is like a camera's **film** {2011}
- Eye focuses by **changing the lens's convexity** (accommodation) {2014}
- Least distance of distinct vision: **25 cm** {2008}
- Not true of hypermetropia: corrected by a **concave** lens (it needs convex) {2010}
- Myopia corrected by a **concave lens** {2002, 2008, 2011, 2013}

@trap
- Mirage is **total internal reflection** (per keys), not simple reflection.
- Frequency never changes in refraction; speed and wavelength do.
- **Convex** = rear view; **concave** = headlight; ENT doctors use **concave**.
- Myopia = **concave**; hypermetropia and old-age presbyopia = **convex**.
@end
