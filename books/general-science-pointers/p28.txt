@section 28.1 | Cloning
- First cloned organism: **Dolly**, a **sheep** {2002, ?}
- **Garima II**: a **cloned buffalo** (NDRI Karnal) {2009}
- First all-green glowing transgenic pigs: **Taiwan** {2010}
- **Injaz**: first cloned **camel** {2008}
- Tried with some success: **disease resistance, growth promotion, animal cloning** (1, 2, 3); not human cloning {2001, 2003}
- Genetic engineering vector: **plasmid** {2015}
- **Bacteriophages** infect bacteria and are used in genetic engineering (both) {2017}

@section 28.2 | Genomes and Stem Cells

- Cells that become any cell type: **stem cells** {2006}
- Non-controversial stem cells: **bone marrow** stem cells {2008}
- **Plant Field Gene Bank, Banthara** (Lucknow): looks after **all the above** {2001, 2003}

@section 28.3 | Transgenic Crops
- Bt cotton: gene from a **bacterium** {2010}
- **Terminator technology**: seeds that give **sterile** next-generation seed {1999, ?}
- **Orobanche** weed: **tobacco** fields {2014}
- Transgenic plant: **golden rice** {2012}
- Golden rice helps consumers and farmers; its yellow comes from **beta-carotene** (A and R true, R explains A) {2000, ?, ?}
- **Super rice**: **G.S. Khush** {2007}

@flow How a transgenic crop is made
Pick a gene | e.g. Bt toxin gene from Bacillus thuringiensis
Vector | Plasmid (Agrobacterium) carries the gene
Insert | Gene enters plant cells
Grow | Transgenic plant with new trait
Examples | Bt cotton (2002, India), golden rice, Bt brinjal (Bangladesh)
@end

@section 28.4 | Diagnosis and DNA Fingerprinting
- **Amniocentesis**: used to find **foetal sex** (now banned for that in India) {2011}
- **Genico** technology: advance **information on genetic diseases** {1999}
- DNA samples for crime: **blood, bone, hair, saliva** (all) {2008}
- **Biochip**: **RNA, DNA and protein** {2008}
- Paternity: **DNA fingerprinting** {2002, 2010}
- First crime solved by DNA (England): **1983** per key (the Pitchfork case, solved 1987-88) {2006}
- DNA fingerprinting used in **all** the areas {2004}
- Fingerprints on multi-coloured surfaces: **fluorescent powder** {2013}
