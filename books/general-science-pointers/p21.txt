@section 21.1 | The Cell
- Plant cell differs from animal cell by its **cell wall** {1991}
- Power house of the cell: **mitochondria** {2013}
- Plasma membrane: **lipids and proteins** (both) {2008}
- Not true: the nucleus has **no role** in reproduction (it does) {2024}

@table
Organelle | Job
Nucleus | Controls the cell; holds DNA
Mitochondria | Respiration; ATP (power house)
Chloroplast | Photosynthesis (plants)
Ribosome | Protein synthesis
Lysosome | Digestion ("suicide bags")
Golgi body | Packaging and secretion
Cell wall | Plants only; cellulose
@end

@section 21.2 | Heredity and DNA
- Mendel's laws: worked on the **garden pea** {2002}
- **Genome mapping**: mapping **genes** {2002}
- Change in a gene's base sequence: **mutation** {2020}
- Unit of heredity: **gene** {2010}
- Not a part of DNA among options: **tyrosine** (an amino acid) {1994}
- DNA sugar: **deoxyribose** {2016}
- Double helix: **Watson and Crick** (1953) {2012, 2018, ?}
- Human chromosomes: **46** (23 pairs) {1991, 1992}
- No sex chromosomes: **lizard** (per key) {2004}

@flow From gene to protein
DNA | Double helix; A-T, G-C base pairs; deoxyribose
Transcription | DNA to mRNA (in the nucleus)
Translation | mRNA to protein (on ribosomes)
Mutation | Change in base sequence
@end

@section 21.3 | Biofertilisers
- **Biofertilisers**: **cultures of micro-organisms** {2023}
- Azolla and blue-green algae are biofertilisers; alfalfa is a crop (*) {2001, 2002}
- Not a nitrogen fixer: **mycorrhiza** {2017}
- Nitrogen-fixing bacteria live in roots of **pulses** {2019}
- Wrong pair: **Azotobacter-bioinsecticide** (it fixes nitrogen) {2002, 2003}
- Blue-green algae: biofertiliser for **paddy** {2006, 2010}
- Biological nitrogen fixation: **blue-green algae** {2019}
- Azolla works because of its partner **blue-green alga** (Anabaena) {2011}
- Azolla is an **aquatic fern** {?, ?}
- Biofertiliser microbes: **cyanobacteria** {?}
- Water-logged rice fields: **Azolla** {2023}
- Biofertiliser source: **Azolla** {2008, 2009}
- Nitrogen-use efficiency improved by **all of the above** {2009}
- Phosphate biofertiliser: **VAM** (vesicular arbuscular mycorrhiza) {2019}
- Soybean nitrogen fixer: **Rhizobium japonicum** {2006}

@table
Biofertiliser | Use
Rhizobium | Legume root nodules (pulses, soybean)

@end
