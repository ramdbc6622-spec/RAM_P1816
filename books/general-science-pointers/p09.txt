@flow Fission versus fusion
Fission | Heavy nucleus (U-235, Pu-239) splits; reactors, atom bomb
Chain reaction | Controlled in a reactor, uncontrolled in a bomb
Fusion | Light nuclei (H, deuterium) join; Sun and stars
Hydrogen bomb | Uncontrolled fusion; a fission bomb triggers it
Tokamak | Fusion reactor design (ITER, France)
@end

@table
Part | Material | Job
Fuel | U-235, Pu-239, (Th-232 via U-233) | Fission
Moderator | Heavy water, light water, graphite | Slows neutrons
Control rods | Cadmium, boron | Absorb neutrons
Coolant | Heavy water, water, liquid sodium, CO2 | Removes heat
Shield | Concrete, lead | Stops radiation
@end
