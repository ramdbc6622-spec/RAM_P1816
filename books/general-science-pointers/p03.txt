@section 3.1 | Quantities, Motion and Inertia
- Vector quantity: **displacement** {2014}
- Man inhaling deeply on a sensitive balance: reading **increases** (per key) {2013}
- Acceleration: **a = (v - u)/t** {2003}
- Average speed for two equal halves: **2v_{1}v_{2}/(v_{1} + v_{2})** {2024}

- Head leans back as a train starts: **inertia of rest** {2010}
- Hard to walk on ice: **less friction** than on a road {1994}
- Harder to start a loaded cart than keep it moving: **less friction once moving** (static > kinetic) {2013}

@section 3.3 | Gravity, Weight and Mass
- Leaning Tower of Pisa stands: the vertical through its **centre of gravity falls within the base** {2009}
- 10 kg and 1 kg balls dropped from 20 m: **land together** {1992, 2015}
- Weight of a body: **maximum at the poles** {2006, 2009, 2015}
- Apparent weight highest in **hydrogen** (least buoyancy among the options) {2017}
- Weight feels more in a lift **accelerating upward** {1990}
- Pendulum clocks lose time in summer: **longer pendulum, longer period** {1994, 2012}
- Earth-Sun distance doubled: gravity becomes **one-fourth** {2009}
- We do not feel Earth's orbital speed: our **relative speed** is zero {2013}
- If gravity vanished: **weight zero, mass unchanged** {2012}

@flow Weight versus mass
Mass | Amount of matter; same everywhere (kg)
Weight | Force = mg (newton); changes with g
Poles | g largest; weight maximum
Equator | g smaller (bulge and rotation)
Free fall / orbit | Apparent weight zero
@end

@section 3.4 | Satellites and Escape Velocity
- Astronauts cannot stand erect: key says **no gravity**; the real reason is **weightlessness** in free fall {2007}
- Apple released from an orbiting spaceship: **moves along with it** {2010}
- Geosynchronous satellite: centripetal force from **Earth's gravity** {2006}
- Satellite kept in orbit by **centripetal force** {2013}
- No atmosphere on the Moon: **escape velocity is less than** the gas molecules' rms speed {2012}

@table
Fact | Value
Earth's escape velocity | 11.2 km/s
Moon's escape velocity | About 2.4 km/s
Orbital speed near Earth | About 7.9 km/s
Geostationary height | About 35,786 km; period 24 h
g on the Moon | About one-sixth of Earth's
@end

@trap
- **Displacement, velocity, force, weight** = vectors; distance, speed, work = scalars.
- Average speed over equal distances is the **harmonic**, not simple, mean.
- Gravity follows the **inverse square**: double the distance, one-fourth the force.
- Orbit "weightlessness" is **free fall**, not zero gravity.
@end
