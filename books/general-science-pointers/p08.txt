@section 8.1 | Electrical Energy and the Unit
- **1 kWh = 3.6 x 10^{6} J** {2006}
- 100 W bulb for 4 hours: **0.4 unit** {2009}
- Five 100 W bulbs for 20 hours: **10 units** {2017}
- Fifteen 200 W bulbs for 24 hours (Silkyara tunnel): **72 units** {2023}

@section 8.2 | Resistance, Bulbs and Circuits
- Of two bulbs, the **dim one has higher resistance** {2008, 2009, ?}
- Maximum light from two identical bulbs: connect in **parallel** {2021}
- 15 V across a brown-green-black resistor (15 ohm): **1 ampere** {2023}
- No current between bodies at the **same potential** {2014}
- n equal charged drops merge: potential becomes **n^{2/3} V** {2015}

@section 8.3 | Dynamo, Motor and Transformer
- Dynamo is a **converter of energy** {2009}
- Works on electromagnetic induction: **dynamo** {2012, 2017}
- Dynamo: **mechanical to electrical** {?}
- Electrical to mechanical: **electric motor** {2007}
- Motors burn at low voltage: they **draw more current** {2008}
- Transformer: **steps AC voltage up or down** {2006}
- Mobile charger: a **step-down transformer** {2017}
- AC to DC: **rectifier** {2006}

@table
Device | Does
Dynamo / generator | Mechanical to electrical (induction)
Electric motor | Electrical to mechanical
Transformer | Changes AC voltage (works only on AC)
Rectifier | AC to DC
Inverter | DC to AC
Fuse | Melts on excess current
@end

@section 8.4 | Safety: Fuse, Earthing and Plugs

- Fuse wire: **high resistance, low melting point** {1993}

@section 8.7 | Conductors and Lightning
- Best conductor: **silver** {2015}
- Lightning conductors protect buildings by sending charge to earth (A and R true, R explains A) {1999}
- Copper rods for lightning conductors: better conductor, does not rust (A and R true, R explains A) {2013}
- Closed car is safe in lightning: it acts as a **hollow conductor** (A and R true, R explains A) {2023}

@section 8.8 | Superconductors and Semiconductors
- Superconductors would pay most at **room temperature** {2000}
- Highest superconducting temperature: about **133-138 K** when asked (*) {2013}
- High-temperature superconductors: **ceramic oxides** {2000}
- Semiconductor resistance on heating: **decreases** {2015}
- Transistor component: **germanium** {2016}
- Commonest transistor material: **silicon** {2015}
- Semiconductors: **silicon and germanium** (1 and 4) {2005, 2015, ?}

@trap

- Fuse = **low** melting point, **high** resistance; earth pin is the **longest and thickest**.
- Transformers work only on **AC**; charger = step-down transformer + rectifier.
@end
