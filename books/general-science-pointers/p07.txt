@section 7.1 | The Electromagnetic Spectrum
- Increasing frequency: **radio, infrared, visible, X-rays** {2023}
- Decreasing frequency: **gamma, X-rays, ultraviolet, radio** {2025}
- Long radio waves are reflected by the **ionosphere** {1991, 1998, ?}
- Atmosphere is heated mainly by **longwave terrestrial radiation** {2022}
- Not true of cosmic rays: they are **electromagnetic waves** (they are charged particles) {2005}
- Telecommunication waves: **microwaves** {2013}
- Dish signals fail in rain: rain **absorbs and scatters** radio energy (1, 2, 3 per key) {2017}
- TV signals limited in range: Earth is **curved** {1994, 2010}
- CT scan uses **X-rays** {2011}

@section 7.2 | Propagation and Speed of Sound
- Highest sound velocity: **steel** {2008, 2018}
- Speeds: **air < water < steel** {?}
- Sound can travel in **solids and gases** (and liquids) {2002}
- Sound cannot travel in a **vacuum** {1990, 2014}
- Astronauts cannot hear each other on the Moon: **no atmosphere** (no medium) {2002, ?}
- Men's and women's voices differ: vocal cords differ in **length and thickness** (A and R true, R explains A) {2025}

@table
Medium | Speed of sound (approx.)
Air (20 C) | 343 m/s
Water | 1,480 m/s
Steel | About 5,000-6,000 m/s
Vacuum | Zero (no sound)
@end

@section 7.3 | Echo, Reverberation and Ultrasound
- Reverberation in large halls: **multiple reflections** (A and R true, R explains A) {2002, 2003}
- Concert hall walls should **absorb sound** {2007}
- Clear echo needs at least **16.5 m** (17 m) {2007}
- Ultrasound can kill insects, clean, treat disease, run automatic doors (**all correct**) {2004}
- **Ultrasonic**: above **20,000 Hz** {2012}
- Bats hunt in the dark with **ultrasonic waves** {2005}
- Heart vibrates at infrasonic; **Mach number** for speed; ultrasonic above 20,000 Hz (1, 3, 4); sound is **slower** in gases {2002}

@table

@end

@section 7.4 | Noise Limits
- WHO safe city noise: **45 dB** {2010}
- Day limits: **residential 55, silence zone 50, industrial 75 (key option 70), commercial 65 dB** {2022}
- Hazardous noise: above **80 dB** {2008, 2013}
