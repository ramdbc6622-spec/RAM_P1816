- Third-generation computers: **integrated circuits** {2016}
- Miniaturisation: **IC chips** {2007}
- IC chip material: **silicon** {2002, 2004, 2006, 2010, 2014}
- ICs are classed by the **number of transistors** {2015}
- First Indian supercomputer: **PARAM** {2004}
- Wrong pair: **PARAM 10,000-missile** (it is a supercomputer) {1999, 2006, 2009}

- Fastest computer (2008): none of the options (*); **El Capitan** (USA) leads now {2008}

- Smallest, fastest brain-like computer: **quantum computer** {2000}

@section 10.2 | Hardware and Memory
- Most important part: **CPU** {2012}
- Intel Core i9: a **processor** {2023}
- After a double click, information goes to the **CPU** {2013}
- Only input devices: **mouse, keyboard, scanner** {2024}
- Word document, video or MP3: a **file** {2013}
- CPU = **Central Processing Unit** {?}
- Memory and word length are measured in **bits** {2006, 2010}
- **1 byte = 8 bits** {2006}
- **1 GB = 1,024 MB**; **1 KB = 1,024 bytes** {?, ?}
- **Bit** = **binary digit** {2013}
- MAC address: **48 bits** {2023}
- Self-complementing code: **5211** {2013}
- Permanent, non-volatile memory: **ROM** {2012, 2024}
- **RAM** = Random Access Memory; loses data when power goes {2002, 2003, 2013, ?}
- Fastest memory: **cache** {2013}
- Not true of flash memory: **cheaper than disk drives** {2004}
- Large-storage hardware: **both** options {1999}
- **Index hole**: **floppy disk**; **IRQ 6**: floppy drive controller {2013, 2015}
- Keyboard cable port: **USB** {2015}
- Laser printers use a **semiconductor laser** {2009}
- Daisy wheel printer: **impact** printer {2013}

@table
Memory | Fact

@end
