- Extra glucose stored as **glycogen** in the **liver** {2004}

- **Bile** is made in the **liver** {1999}
- Gallstones affect **fat** digestion {2017}
- Proteins to amino acids: **pepsin** (per key) {2017}
- **Vitamin-carotene, enzyme-pepsin, hormone-testosterone, protein-keratin** {1998, 2021}
