@trap
- Curd = **lactic** acid, not acetic; vinegar = **acetic**.
- Natural rubber = **isoprene**; neoprene = **chloroprene**.
- Bullet-proof vest = **Kevlar**; window = **polycarbonate**.
- **Calcium carbide** ripening is banned in India (it gives acetylene and arsenic traces).
@end

@up UP link: **mango** ripening with banned **calcium carbide** is a recurring FSDA (UP food safety) target in Lucknow, Malihabad and Saharanpur mandis; ethephon and ethylene chambers are the approved methods.
