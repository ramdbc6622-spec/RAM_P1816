@intro
The non-conventional energy questions cover renewable vs non-renewable sources, the **Sun** as the ultimate source, **solar**'s place in India's energy mix and its "greatest potential", firsts (**Cochin** airport, **Diu**, **Sikkim** as the organic state), the **International Solar Alliance**, **biofuels** (jatropha, karanja, ethanol), **hydrogen** as the cleanest fuel, and geothermal sites. India's targets: **500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030**, a goal it is already halfway past. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2025
With reference to International Solar Alliance', which of the following statements is/are correct?
1. It is the first treaty based Inter-governmental organisation headquartered in India with 38 member countries.
2. World Solar Bank and One Sun One World One Grid Initiative were initiated under it.
Select the correct answer using the code given below :
Code :
(a) Only 2
(b) Neither 1 nor 2
(c) Both 1 and 2
(d) Only 1
@ans (a)
@end

The **International Solar Alliance** was launched by India and France at **COP21 (Paris, 2015)** and has its headquarters at **Gurugram**. It is the first treaty-based intergovernmental body based in India, but it has **more than 100 member countries**, not 38, so statement 1 is false. Its initiatives include **One Sun One World One Grid** and the proposed **Global Solar Facility / World Solar Bank**.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2016
In which of the following areas of India have geothermal energy sources not been found?
(a) Godavari Delta
(b) Ganga Delta
(c) Himalayas
(d) West Coast
@ans (b)
@end

India's geothermal provinces include the Himalaya (**Puga** valley, Ladakh, chosen for India's first geothermal plant; Manikaran), the **west coast** (Konkan), **Cambay** (Gujarat), the **Son-Narmada-Tapi** zone and the **Godavari** valley. The **Ganga** delta has none.

@trap
**Coal is a fossil fuel**, not biomass energy in the usual sense, although the source treats it as ancient biomass. **Nuclear** energy is not biomass. **Hydrogen is the least polluting fuel.** **Solar is now second after fossil fuels** in installed capacity, ahead of hydro and wind. **ISA has more than 100 members**, not 38. **Diu** (not Chandigarh) was the first solar UT, and **Cochin** the first solar airport.
@end
