@chapter 7 | The Ozone Layer

@intro
Ozone questions are short and settled: ozone is formed by **UV light**, it lies in the **stratosphere**, it absorbs **ultraviolet** radiation, it is destroyed by **CFCs, halons, carbon tetrachloride and methyl chloroform**, the ozone hole was discovered by a **British** team in 1985, the **Montreal Protocol** (1987) controls the depleting substances, and **16 September** is Ozone Day. UV exposure causes **skin cancer**. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 7.1 | Ozone and Where It Lies

@pyq U.P. P.C.S. (Pre) 2018
Ultraviolet radiation from sunlight causes the reaction that produce which of the following?
(a) CO
(b) SO2
(c) O3
(d) Fluorides
@ans (c)
@end

Ozone forms in two steps. UV light splits an oxygen molecule (O2 into O + O), and each free O atom then joins another O2 to make **O3**. This **Chapman cycle** creates and destroys ozone naturally, keeping its level roughly steady.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2016
In stratosphere, the level of ozone is naturally regulated by :
(a) Nitrous oxide
(b) Nitrogen dioxide
(c) CFC
(d) Water vapours
@ans (b)
@end

About **90%** of all ozone is in the **stratosphere**, mostly between **15 and 35 km**. The thickness of the ozone column is measured in **Dobson Units (DU)**: the world average is about 300 DU, and an "ozone hole" is an area below **220 DU**. Natural **nitrogen oxides** (NO, NO2) take part in the reactions that keep ozone levels balanced.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2014
Harmful ultraviolet radiation coming from the sun can cause –
(a) Liver cancer
(b) Brain cancer
(c) Oral cancer
(d) Skin cancer
@ans (d)
@end

@table
UV band | Wavelength | Fate
UV-A | 315-400 nm | Mostly reaches the ground; ageing of skin
**UV-B** | 280-315 nm | **Mostly absorbed by ozone**; the part that gets through causes sunburn, **skin cancer**, **cataract** and weakened immunity, and harms phytoplankton and crops
UV-C | 100-280 nm | Completely absorbed by ozone and O2
@end

"**Good up high, bad nearby**": stratospheric ozone protects us, while **ground-level (tropospheric) ozone** is a pollutant and a greenhouse gas. It forms in **photochemical smog** from NOx and hydrocarbons in sunlight, and damages lungs and crops.

@ans (b)
@note The key follows the source, but **methyl chloroform** (1,1,1-trichloroethane) **is** an ozone-depleting substance and was phased out under the Montreal Protocol. Today **nitrous oxide (N2O)** is the largest ozone-depleting *emission*, though it is not controlled by the Protocol. On current science, all four options deplete ozone.
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2008

(a) Methane
(b) Carbon monoxide
(c) Nitrogen Oxides (NO)

@ans (b)

@table
Ozone-depleting substance | Use | Status
**CFCs** (CFC-11, CFC-12) | Refrigerators, ACs, aerosol sprays, foam | Phased out (2010 globally)
**Halons** (halon-1211, 1301) | Fire extinguishers | Phased out; very strong bromine depleters
**Carbon tetrachloride** | Solvent, feedstock | Phased out
**Methyl chloroform** | Industrial solvent | Phased out
**Methyl bromide** | Fumigant (soil, grain) | Phased out except for quarantine uses
HCFCs | Replacements for CFCs (weaker) | India's phase-out by 2030
HFCs | Do not deplete ozone, but are strong greenhouse gases | Being cut down under the **Kigali Amendment** (2016)
@end

One **chlorine atom** freed by UV light from a CFC molecule can destroy about **1,00,000 ozone molecules** in a chain reaction. Over **Antarctica** the destruction is fastest in spring (September-October), because **polar stratospheric clouds** form there in the extreme winter cold.

@table
Milestone | Year | Detail
Molina and Rowland warn about CFCs | 1974 | Nobel Prize 1995 (with Crutzen)
Antarctic ozone hole reported | **1985** | **British Antarctic Survey**: Joseph Farman, Brian Gardiner, Jonathan Shanklin

@end

@trap
Ozone absorbs **UV, not infrared**. The **ozone layer is in the stratosphere**; ground-level ozone in the troposphere is a pollutant. The ozone hole was found by a **British** team (Farman, 1985), while the "ozone halo" over Tibet was found by **Kent Moore** (2005). **Ozone Day = 16 September**. **CFCs are also greenhouse gases**, so "not responsible for greenhouse effect" is false.
@end
