@chapter 16 | Organisations, Awards, Days and Miscellaneous

@intro
The closing chapter collects the "where is it" and "when is it" questions: **headquarters** of UN and other bodies (UNEP Nairobi, WMO Geneva, IMO London, Greenpeace Amsterdam), Indian **research institutes** (NEERI Nagpur, CAZRI and AFRI Jodhpur, WII Dehradun, IIFM Bhopal), **environment awards**, **important days**, and a miscellaneous set on Mission LiFE, World Heritage Sites, people's movements and land degradation neutrality. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.

@pyq UPPCS (Pre) 2021

(d) Dakshin Gangotri and Maitri
