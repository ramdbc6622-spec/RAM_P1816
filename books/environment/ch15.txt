@chapter 15 | Water Conservation and River Cleaning

@intro
This short chapter covers water laws and plans: **World Water Day** (22 March), the **Water Act 1974** and the **Water Cess Act 1977**, drinking-water disinfection (chlorine, ozone, chloramine), groundwater stress and the **Central Ground Water Authority**, the **National Lake Conservation Plan**, and the history of Ganga cleaning, from the **Central Ganga Authority** (1985) and **Ganga Action Plan** to the **NGRBA** (2009) and **Namami Gange** (2014). It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 15.1 | Water as a Resource

@pyq U.P.P.C.S (Pre) 2011
The 'World Water Day' is celebrated on:
(a) 28 February
(b) 22 March
(c) 5 June
(d) 11 July
@ans (b)
@end

@pyq U.P.R.O./A.R.O. (Pre) 2016
Which one of the following is not a biotic resource?
(a) Freshwater
(b) Coal
(c) Petroleum
(d) Fish
@ans (a)
@end

**Biotic** resources come from living things or once-living matter: forests, fish, livestock and also the **fossil fuels**, coal and petroleum, which formed from ancient organisms. **Water**, minerals, air and sunlight are **abiotic** resources.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2005
Consider the following statements :
1. 36% of India's districts are classified as "overexploited" or "critical" by the Central Ground Water Authority (CGWA).
2. CGWA was formed under the Environment (Protection) Act.
3. India has the largest area under groundwater irrigation in the world.
Which of the statements given below is/are correct?
(a) 1 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 2 only
(d) 1 and 3 only
@ans (b)
@end

India is the **world's largest user of groundwater**, using more than the USA and China combined, and groundwater supplies about **60%** of its irrigation. The **CGWA** (1997) was set up under **Section 3(3) of the Environment (Protection) Act 1986**. The **2024** national assessment rated about **11%** of assessment units as "**over-exploited**", mostly in Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan and western UP. The **Atal Bhujal Yojana** (2019) promotes community groundwater management in seven states, including **UP**.

@section 15.2 | Water Laws

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2023
When did the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act was enacted in which of the following year?
(a) 1976
(b) 1977
(c) 1974
(d) 1975
@ans (c)
@end

@pyq U.P.R.O/A.R.O. (Mains) 2013
Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Cess was enforced in –
(a) 1973
(b) 1975
(c) 1977
(d) 1979
@ans (c)
@end

The **Water Act 1974** was India's first major pollution law. It created the **Central and State Pollution Control Boards**. The **Water Cess Act 1977** levied a cess on water used by industries and local bodies to fund the boards; it was **repealed in 2017** when GST came in. The **Jan Vishwas Act 2023** replaced jail terms for most minor offences under the Water Act with fines.

(a) Sulphur dioxide
(b) Chlorine
(c) Fluorine
(d) Carbon dioxide
@ans (b)
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2016
Which of the following is/are used as a disinfectant of water?
(a) Ozone
(b) Chlorine dioxide
(c) Chloramine
(d) All of the above
@ans (d)
@end

Water can be disinfected with **chlorine** (gas or bleaching powder), **chloramine**, **chlorine dioxide**, **ozone**, **UV light** or by boiling. Chlorine leaves a **residual** in the pipes that keeps protecting the water, while ozone and UV leave none. **Jal Jeevan Mission** (2019) aims to give every rural home a tap connection and had covered nearly **80%** of rural households by 2025.

@up UP link: UP has the **longest stretch of the Ganga** (about 1,140 km of its 2,525 km), so most Namami Gange projects are here: sewage treatment plants at **Kanpur** (including the **Sisamau** drain tapping), **Prayagraj**, **Varanasi** and **Mathura-Vrindavan** (for the Yamuna). The **Gomti Action Plan** (1993) and the **Gomti riverfront** relate to **Lucknow**. The 2025 **Prayagraj Mahakumbh** raised questions about water-quality monitoring at the Sangam.
