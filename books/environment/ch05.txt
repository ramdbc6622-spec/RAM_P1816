@chapter 5 | Greenhouse Effect and Climate Change
@unit UNIT III · CLIMATE AND ATMOSPHERE

@intro
This is the single largest block of environment questions in UPPSC papers. The questions are about **which gases are greenhouse gases** (CO2, methane, N2O, water vapour, ozone, CFCs) and which are not (N2, O2, Ar, H2, SO2, NO2, propane). Others cover the sources of **methane** (paddy fields, cattle, wetlands), the effects of **global warming** (glaciers, sea level, coral bleaching, disease), basic numbers (-18 °C without the greenhouse effect, about 1 °C of warming since 1900), **net zero**, top emitters and the **Milankovitch** theory. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 5.1 | The Greenhouse Effect

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2011
The concept of Greenhouse Gases was postulated by –
(a) C.C. Park
(b) J.N.N. Jeffers
(c) Joseph Fourier
(d) L. Zobler
@ans (c)
@end

**Joseph Fourier** (1824) first described the atmosphere as trapping heat. **John Tyndall** (1859) showed that CO2 and water vapour absorb infrared radiation. **Svante Arrhenius** (1896) first calculated how much warming more CO2 would cause.

@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2020
Without green house effect, the average temperature of earth surface would be:
(a) 0°C
(b) – 18°C
(c) 5°C
(d) – 20°C
@ans (b)
@end

The **natural** greenhouse effect keeps the Earth at about **+15 °C** instead of about **-18 °C**, so it is essential for life. The problem is the **enhanced** greenhouse effect caused by extra gases from human activity.

@table
Greenhouse gas | Main sources | Global warming potential (100 yr)
Water vapour | Evaporation | Largest natural share; a feedback, not a direct driver
**Carbon dioxide (CO2)** | Fossil fuels, cement, deforestation | 1 (reference); about 2/3 of human-caused warming
**Methane (CH4)** | **Paddy fields**, cattle (enteric), wetlands, **termites**, landfills, coal mines, gas leaks | About 28-30
**Nitrous oxide (N2O)** | Nitrogen fertilisers, manure | About 265-300
Ozone (tropospheric) | Formed from NOx and VOCs in sunlight | Short-lived
**CFCs, HFCs**, PFCs (incl. CF4), **SF6** | Refrigerants, industry | Thousands; SF6 about 23,500
@end

**Not greenhouse gases**: the main gases of the air, **nitrogen (N2), oxygen (O2) and argon (Ar)**, which have two identical atoms or are single atoms and do not absorb infrared, as well as **hydrogen**, **SO2** (it actually *cools*, by forming aerosols) and **NO2/NOx**. Note that **nitrous oxide (N2O)** *is* a greenhouse gas.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2019; U.P. P.C.S. (Mains) 2017
The gas, which is emitted in the paddy fields and increases the earth's temperature is:
(a) Nitrogen
(b) Carbon dioxide
(c) Carbon monoxide
(d) Methane
@ans (d)
@end

Flooded paddy soils are **anaerobic**, so methane-producing bacteria (methanogens) thrive in them. **Alternate wetting and drying** and **direct-seeded rice** cut these emissions. The **Global Methane Pledge** (COP26, 2021) aims to reduce methane emissions by 30% by 2030. **India has not joined it**, citing farm livelihoods.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S (Pre) 2021
Which reference to 'NET-ZERO' GOAL FOR 2050, which of the following statements is/are correct?
1. It means that a country will bring down its emissions to zero by 2050.
2. It means that a country's emissions will be compensated by absorption and removal of greenhouse gases from the atmosphere.

Codes.
(a) Only 1
(b) Only 2
(c) Both 1 and 2

**Net zero** means that whatever greenhouse gases are still emitted are **balanced** by removals (forests, carbon capture), not that emissions fall to zero. **India** announced its net-zero target year, **2070**, at **COP26 (Glasgow)** as part of the "**Panchamrit**". China's target is 2060, and the USA's and EU's 2050.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2024
What does the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) emphasize regarding reductions in greenhouse gas (GHG) emission?
1. Gradual and limited reductions are enough.
2. Deep, rapid and sustained reductions are essential.
Select the correct answer from the codes given below:
(a) Both 1 and 2
(b) Neither 1 nor 2
(c) Only 1
(d) Only 2
@ans (d)
@end

The **IPCC** was set up in **1988** by the **WMO and UNEP** and is based in Geneva. Its Sixth Assessment Report (2021-23) says that to keep warming to 1.5 °C, global emissions must fall about **43% by 2030** and **60% by 2035** (from 2019 levels), reaching **net zero CO2 around 2050**.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2015
Who had suggested that shifting Earth on its axis is one of the factors, responsible for the climatic change?
(a) Robert Hooke
(b) Milutin Milankovich
(c) George Simpson
(d) T. C. Chamberlain
@ans (b)
@end

**Milankovitch cycles** are slow changes in the Earth's orbit and axis that pace the ice ages: **eccentricity** (about 100,000 years), **obliquity** or axial tilt (about 41,000 years) and **precession** (about 26,000 years). **Solar irradiance** changes come from the Sun itself and are not part of this orbital theory.

@trap
**N2O is a greenhouse gas, NO2 is not.** Students mix them up. The **main air gases (N2, O2, Ar) are not greenhouse gases**, and neither are H2, SO2 or propane. **Water vapour** causes the largest *natural* share, but **CO2** is the main human driver. **Net zero is not zero emissions.** Milankovitch is about the **orbit**, not about solar output.
@end

@pattern
**"Which is not a greenhouse gas"** is the most repeated question in the whole environment syllabus (more than ten versions since 2004). Methane from **paddy fields** and the impacts of global warming come next. The 2021-2024 papers add **net zero**, the **IPCC**, coral bleaching and urban heat islands, so expect current climate science, not just definitions.
