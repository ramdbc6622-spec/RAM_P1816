@chapter 1 | Environment, Sustainable Development and Ecology
@unit UNIT I · ECOLOGY AND ECOSYSTEMS

@intro
This chapter lays the foundation for the whole subject. It covers what the **environment** is, the idea of **sustainable development** (Brundtland 1987, Rio 1992, Agenda 21), the classic environmental books (Silent Spring, The Population Bomb, The Limits to Growth), India's **Environment (Protection) Act 1986** and **Ecomark**, and then core **ecology**: ecosystem structure, levels of organisation, biogeochemical cycles, **ecological succession** and the **niche**. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@pyq U.P. R.O./A.R.O. (Mains) 2017
Given below are two statements, one labelled as Assertion (A) and other as the Reason (R).
Assertion (A) : The biosphere is an example of an open system.
Reason (R) : There cannot be reinvestment of new substances in the biosphere but there is no restriction on the investment and exit of energy.
Select the correct answer using the codes below:
(a) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is correct explanation of (A)
(b) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)
(c) (A) is true but (R) is false
(d) (A) is false but (R) is true
@ans (c)
@end

An ecosystem is **open** for **energy**: sunlight comes in and heat goes out, so energy flows **one way**. **Nutrients**, however, are **recycled** again and again. The Reason is wrong because new matter does enter and leave the biosphere, for example through volcanic gases and sediments.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2020
The entire sequence of communities that successively change in a particular area are called:
(a) Ecological succession
(b) Sere
(c) Community dynamics
(d) Pyramid of biomass
@ans (b)
@end

@table
Succession term | Meaning
**Nudation** | A bare area is created (lava, landslide, abandoned field)
**Migration (invasion)** | Seeds and spores arrive
**Ecesis** | The arrivals germinate and establish themselves
Aggregation / competition | Numbers build up and species compete
**Reaction** | The organisms change the site (soil, shade, moisture)
**Stabilisation** | A stable **climax community** is reached
**Sere** | The whole sequence of communities; each stage is a **seral stage**
Pioneer species | The first colonisers, e.g. **lichens** on bare rock
Primary / secondary succession | On a site never vegetated before / on a disturbed site that still has soil
Hydrosere / xerosere | Succession starting in water / on a dry surface
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2024
Which of the following statements is not true about "ecological niche"?
(a) Dominant species occupy extensive and broader ecological niche.
(b) Number of species becomes large in ecological niche of natural ecosystem, if resources are sufficient.
(c) Number of species becomes small in ecological niche, if resources are sufficient.
(d) Dominance of single species becomes minimum if the resource distribution is equitable in a habitat.
@ans (c)
@end

A **niche** is a species' role or "profession": the conditions it tolerates, the food and resources it uses, and when and where it is active. A **habitat** is its "address". By **Gause's competitive exclusion principle**, no two species can occupy exactly the same niche for long.

@trap
**Agenda 21 = 1992**, not 1995. **Brundtland = 1987**, *Our Common Future*. **Ecosystems are open** for energy, so "closed system" is the wrong option. Succession **starts with nudation**; if nudation is not among the options, it starts with migration. **Sere** is the whole sequence of communities, while "ecological succession" is the process.
@end
