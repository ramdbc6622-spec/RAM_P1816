@chapter 11 | National Parks

@intro
National-park questions are almost entirely **park-to-state** and **park-to-animal** matches. Learn Corbett (India's first park), Kaziranga (rhino), Gir (lion), Dachigam (hangul), Keibul Lamjao (the floating park), Eravikulam (Nilgiri tahr), the marine parks, and the state traps (Pin Valley, Bandipur, Similipal). India has about **107 national parks**, with **Madhya Pradesh** having the most. **Dudhwa** is UP's only national park. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2006
The Corbett National Park gets its water from
(a) Alaknanda river
(b) Ramganga river
(c) Dhauliganga river
(d) Sarda River
@ans (b)
@end

**Corbett** began as **Hailey National Park (1936)**, was renamed Ramganga National Park (1954-55) and then Corbett (1956), after the hunter-naturalist **Jim Corbett**. It lies in Nainital and Pauri (then UP, now **Uttarakhand**) along the **Ramganga**, and **Project Tiger** was launched there in **1973**. The world's first national park was **Yellowstone (USA, 1872)**.

@up UP link: **Dudhwa National Park** (Lakhimpur Kheri, 1977), UP's only national park, runs India's first **rhino rehabilitation** programme. One-horned rhinos were brought from Assam and Nepal in **1984** to a fenced area, and the herd has since grown to about 40. Dudhwa, with the **Kishanpur** and **Katarniaghat** sanctuaries, forms the **Dudhwa Tiger Reserve**, home to the **swamp deer (barasingha)**, Bengal florican and gharial (in the Girwa river at Katarniaghat).

@trap
These park-state pairs are the commonest traps: **Pin Valley = Himachal**, not J&K. **Bandipur = Karnataka**, not Tamil Nadu. **Similipal = Odisha**, not MP. **Indravati = Chhattisgarh (Bastar)**. **Keoladeo = birds**, not lions. **Periyar = elephant and tiger**, not hangul. **Rajiv Gandhi NP = Nagarhole (Karnataka).** The first marine park is in the **Gulf of Kutch**.
@end

@heatmap

@pattern
This is one of the heaviest topics in the book (39 PYQs). Most questions are **park-state matches**, and the same few traps recur (Pin Valley, Bandipur, Similipal), including in the 2016, 2018 and 2024 papers. Park-animal pairs (Kaziranga rhino, Gir lion, Dachigam hangul, Eravikulam tahr) come next. Learn the master table above row by row.
@end
