@chapter 10 | Forest and Wildlife Conservation Efforts

@intro
This chapter covers India's **policies and laws** for forests and wildlife: the **National Forest Policy** (1952 and 1988, with the one-third target), the **Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972**, the **Forest (Conservation) Act 1980** and the other pollution Acts in date order. It also covers projects and schemes (**Project Tiger, Project Elephant, MISHTI, Operation Olivia**), institutions (**FRI Dehradun, NBPGR**) and a few wildlife facts (WWF's panda, pugmarks, the whale shark). It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@pyq UP R.O./A.R.O. (Pre) 2023
With reference to Preservation of Wildlife in India, which of the following statement/s is/are correct?
1. The Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972 provides legal framework for the protection of wild animals only.
2. Project Elephant aims to conserve wild Asian elephants only.
(a) Both 1 and 2
(b) Only 1
(c) Only 2
(d) Neither 1 nor 2
@ans (d)
@end

The **Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972** protects **plants as well as animals**, both wild and captive. It created protected areas and Schedules of species. The **2022 amendment** cut the Schedules from six to **four** and added CITES provisions. **Project Elephant** (1992) protects elephants **and their habitats and corridors**, and also deals with human-elephant conflict and captive elephants, so statement 2 is too narrow.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2025
With reference to 'Operation Olivia', which of the following statements is/are correct?
1. It is an initiative of Indian coast Guards to protect Olive Ridley turtles along the coast of Odisha.
2. Operation Olivia is carried out during the month of August which is the nesting time of Olive Ridley turtles.
Select the correct answer from the code given below :
Code :
(a) Only 2
(b) Neither 1 nor 2
(c) Both 1 and 2
(d) Only 1
@ans (d)
@end

**Operation Olivia** is run by the **Indian Coast Guard** every year from about **November to May**, covering the olive ridley **mass nesting** (*arribada*) season of roughly **January to March**. The main nesting sites are **Gahirmatha**, **Rushikulya** and the **Devi** river mouth in Odisha.

@note According to the **Zoological Survey of India**, India has about **6.5-7.5%** of the world's recorded animal species (more than 1 lakh species) on only 2.4% of its land. The figure depends on the source and year, so no option is clearly right and the source marks the question (*).

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Spl) (Pre) 2008
India's largest fish is:
(a) Stonefish
(b) Whale shark
(c) Marlin
(d) Hilsa
@ans (b)
@end

The **whale shark** (up to about 18 m) is the world's largest fish. It is protected under **Schedule I** of the Wildlife Act, and **Gujarat**'s Saurashtra coast is a key area for it.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2008
The pugmark technique is used –
(a) For bird watching in forests
(b) For breeding rare wildlife in captivity
(c) For estimation of population of various wild animals
(d) For tattooing wildlife to distinguish one species from the other
@ans (c)
@end

**Pugmarks** (footprints) were the old way of counting tigers. Since 2006 the **All-India Tiger Estimation** has used **camera traps** with stripe-pattern recognition. The 2022 count found **3,682 tigers** (central estimate; range 3,167-3,925), about **75%** of the world's wild tigers.

**Suriname** has about **93%** forest cover, the highest of any country, followed by Guyana, Gabon and Micronesia. Among large economies, **Japan** (about 68%) is the most forested.

@trap
The **1952 Forest Policy** classes were protected, national and village forests; **national parks** are a Wildlife Act category, not one of them. **Forest Conservation Act = 1980** and **Environment Protection Act = 1986**. **MISHTI = 2023**, not 2019. Olive ridley nesting is **January-March**, not August. The **Wildlife Act protects plants** too.
@end
