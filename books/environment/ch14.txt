@chapter 14 | Pollution: Air, Acid Rain, Water, Sound and Soil

@intro
This is the largest chapter in the book. It covers **air pollution** (primary vs secondary pollutants, CO and haemoglobin, PAN and photochemical smog, fly ash, lichens as indicators, NCAP and PRANA), **acid rain** (SO2 and NOx; Norway and Canada), **water pollution** (arsenic, fluoride, BOD, eutrophication, the Ganga at Kanpur), **noise** (decibels, green mufflers), **soil** pollution, and a miscellany of pollutants and diseases: **Bhopal's MIC**, lead, mercury (Minamata), cadmium (itai-itai), DDT, plastics and Euro/BS norms. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

Normal rain is slightly acidic (pH about **5.6**) because of dissolved CO2. **Acid rain** (pH below about 5.6) forms when **SO2** and **NOx** turn into sulphuric and nitric acids. It kills fish in lakes, damages forests, and corrodes marble (**"stone leprosy"** on the **Taj Mahal** from **Mathura refinery** and Agra industry, which led to the **Taj Trapezium Zone**, 1996). **Scandinavia** (Norway, Sweden) and **Canada** suffer from acid rain blown in from industrial Britain, Germany and the USA.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Spl) (Mains) 2008

**BOD** (Biochemical Oxygen Demand) is the oxygen that microbes need to break down the organic matter in water, so **high BOD means more organic pollution (sewage)**. **DO** (Dissolved Oxygen) is the oxygen actually present, so **low DO means polluted water**. Bathing-quality water needs **BOD of 3 mg/L or less** and **DO of 5 mg/L or more**.

**PM10** (10 micrometres or smaller) reaches the airways, and **PM2.5** (2.5 micrometres or smaller) reaches deep into the lungs and the blood. Indian 24-hour standards are **100 and 60 µg/m³**. The WHO's 2021 guidelines are far stricter: **45 and 15 µg/m³**.

India follows **Bharat Stage (BS)** norms, which are based on the Euro norms. India **leapfrogged from BS-IV to BS-VI in April 2020**, skipping BS-V. BS-VI fuel has only **10 ppm sulphur** (0.001%), compared with 50 ppm for BS-IV and 500 ppm (0.05%) for Euro-II.

@trap
**SO2 is primary**, while ozone, PAN and smog are **secondary** pollutants. **CO binds haemoglobin.** **Lichens disappear** in polluted air. **High BOD and low DO mean polluted water.** **Fluoride causes knock-knee**, **mercury Minamata** and **cadmium itai-itai**. **Bhopal = MIC (1984).** **Eutrophication is water pollution**, not air pollution. India went **BS-IV to BS-VI**, skipping BS-V.
