@section 6.1 | Definition and Location
- Desert: rainfall under **25 cm** a year {2009}
- Hot deserts on **western margins at 15°-30°**; trade winds lose moisture moving west (R explains A) {2019}
- Deserts can supply solar energy; they get a year's human energy use in 6 hours of sun (R explains A) {2013}

@section 6.2 | Deserts of the World
- Biggest desert: **Sahara** (largest hot desert) {1994}
- Smallest to largest: **Great Sandy, Gobi, Arabian, Sahara** {2023}
- Most populated desert: **Thar**; largest in South Asia: **Thar** {2012, 1996}
- **Gobi**: **Mongolia and China** only {1993, 2025}
- **Taklamakan**: **China** {2008}
- Wrong: **Gibson-Brazil** (Gibson is in **Australia**) {2016, 2018}
- **Karakum**: **Turkmenistan** {2022}
- **Dasht-e-Lut**: **Iran** {2015}
- Not true: Atacama lies in **southern** Chile (it is **northern**) {2006}
- Driest place: **Atacama** {2009}
- Temperate desert: **Patagonian** {2011}
- **Kalahari**: **Botswana and Namibia**; in the **Southern Hemisphere** {2008, 2010, 2015}
