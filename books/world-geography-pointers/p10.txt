@section 10.1 | Africa
- Crosses the Equator twice: **Zaire (Congo)** {2015}
- Crosses the Tropic of Capricorn twice: **Limpopo** {2010}
- Not in the Nile basin: **Chad** {2013}
- **Blue Nile** rises in **Lake Tana** {2016}
- Egypt "gift of the Nile", an oasis in the Sahara (R explains A) {2005}

@flow The Nile
White Nile | From Lake Victoria
Blue Nile | From Lake Tana (Ethiopia); most silt
Khartoum | Confluence (Sudan)
Aswan High Dam | Lake Nasser, Egypt
Delta | Arcuate, on the Mediterranean
@end
