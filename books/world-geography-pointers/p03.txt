@section 3.1 | Latitude, Longitude, Time
- Equator meets Prime Meridian in the **Atlantic Ocean** (Gulf of Guinea) {2009}
- Farthest north of the options: **Rome** {2011}
- Noon at Greenwich, 5 pm local: **75° E** {2008}
- GMT 3 am, local 6 am: **45° E** {2006}
- Great circle with Prime Meridian: **180°** {2000}
- First sunrise of the millennium in India: **93°30' E** (Arunachal) {2001}
- Prime Meridian crosses **Algeria and France** {2008, 2010}
- Date Line 12 hours from Greenwich, at 180° (R explains A) {1992}
- Date Line has no minimum distance (per key) {1997}
- Ship moving at Earth's spin speed: still **10:00 Monday** {2000}
- Shortest path: along a **meridian** (great circle) {1992}

@section 3.2 | Equator and Tropics
- Equator does not pass: **Equatorial Guinea and Rwanda** {2020}
- On the Equator: **Colombia and Kenya** {2010}; **Kenya** {2007, 2008}
- Equator and both Tropics cross: **Africa** {2004}
- Tropic of Cancer does not cross **Iran** {2005}
- Tropic of Capricorn does not cross **Bolivia** {1997}
- Capricorn crosses **Australia, Namibia, Brazil, Chile** {2003}

@section 3.3 | Solstices and Seasons
- Equinoxes: **21 March and 23 September** {1994, 2004}
- Longest day: **21 June** {1992}; summer solstice **21 June** {2010}
- Smallest noon shadow: **21 June** {2006}
- 12-hour day at summer solstice: **Equator** {1997}
- Noon Sun at Tropic of Cancer on winter solstice: **43°** {1997}
- Midnight sun: **North Pole** (and Arctic) {1990}

@trap
- **Iran** is not on the Tropic of Cancer; **Bolivia** not on Capricorn.
- **Equatorial Guinea** (mainland) and **Rwanda** are off the Equator.
- Earth is **nearest the Sun in January**, yet it is northern winter.
@end
