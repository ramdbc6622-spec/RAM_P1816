@section 9.4 | Salinity and Tides
- Main salt in sea water: **sodium chloride** {2000}
- Highest salinity (per key): **Lake Van** (Dead Sea is actually saltier) {1995}
- Saltiest sea: Red Sea among seas; Dead Sea overall (*) {1998, 2012}
- Salinity is highest near the **Tropics of Cancer and Capricorn** {2023}
- Salinity gradient: **halocline** {2015}
- Tides: **gravity, centripetal and centrifugal forces** {1991}
- **Spring tide**: Sun, Earth, Moon in a **straight line** {1999}

@flow Spring and neap tides
New or full moon | Sun-Earth-Moon in line
Spring tide | Highest high tides
Quarter moon | Sun and Moon at 90°
Neap tide | Lowest range
@end
