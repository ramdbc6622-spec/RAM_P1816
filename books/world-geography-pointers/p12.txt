@chapter 12 | Straits, Gulfs, Canals and Dams
@meta Built from 24 UPPSC questions (1991–2025). Focus: straits and what they separate, the Suez Canal, Soo and Ludwig canals, dam-river matching.

@hot
- **Suez Canal** joins the **Red Sea and Mediterranean**; ends at **Port Said and Suez**; cut India-Europe route by about **7,000 km** {1992, 1992, 1995, 2005, 2010, 2013, 2018, 2025}
- Straits: **Gibraltar** (Europe-Africa), **Bering** (Asia-N. America), **Bass** (Tasmania), **Hormuz** (Persian Gulf-Gulf of Oman), **Dover** (Channel Tunnel) {1999, 2004, 2005, 2007, 2010, 2023}
- Dams: **Hoover-Colorado, Kariba-Zambezi, Aswan-Nile, Panchet Hill-Damodar** {2013, 2015, 2016}
@end

@section 12.1 | Straits
- Europe from Africa: **Gibraltar** {1999}
- Asia from North America: **Bering Strait** {2004}
- Tasmania from Australia: **Bass Strait** {2023}
- **Hormuz**: between the **Persian Gulf and Gulf of Oman** {2005}
- UK-France tunnel: **Strait of Dover**; Dover joins the **English Channel and North Sea** {2007, 2010}
- **Lombok Strait**: between **Bali and Lombok** {2017}
- **Ten Degree Channel**: **Andaman from Nicobar** {2001}

@section 12.2 | Gulfs
- Persian Gulf does not border **Oman** (main coast on Gulf of Oman) {2007, 2008}
- Correct: **Gulf of Tonkin-Vietnam** {2003}

@section 12.3 | Canals
- Suez joins the **Red Sea and Mediterranean**; (2 and 3) {1992, 2025}
- Ports at the ends: **Port Said and Suez** {2013}
- Suez shortened India-West distance; joins Mediterranean and Red Sea (R explains A) {1992}
- Largest navigation canal: **Suez** {1995, 2005, 2010}
- India-Europe saving: about **7,000 km** {2018}
- Lakes north to south: **Manzala, Timsah, Great Bitter, Little Bitter** {2019}
- **Soo Canal**: **Superior and Huron** {1991}
- Inland waterway in southern Germany: **Ludwig Canal** {2017}
- Longest sea bridge then: **Jiaozhou Bay** (Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau now) {2009}

@end

@section 12.4 | Dams
- **Colorado-Hoover, Damodar-Panchet Hill, Nile-Aswan, Zambezi-Kariba** {2013}

- China's first Brahmaputra dam (2010): **Zangmu** {2013}
