@section 2.1 | The Earth
- Earth's nickname: **Green Planet** (per key) {1999}
- Most abundant element in the crust: **oxygen** {2013}
- Sunlight reaches Earth in about **8 minutes** {2005}
- Axis tilted **23.5°** (66.5° to orbital plane) {2016}
- True: northern end of axis = North Pole; axis stays parallel (1 and 3) {2000}
- A ball thrown from a satellite keeps **orbiting in the same path** {1995}

@table
Crust element | Share
Oxygen | 46.6%
Silicon | 27.7%
Aluminium | 8.1% (most common metal)
Iron | 5.0% (most in the whole Earth)
@end

@section 2.2 | The Moon
- One face seen because rotation = revolution (R explains A) {2006}
- Moon's core: **viscous liquid** per key (now thought solid inner + fluid outer) {2004}
- No sound on the Moon: **no atmosphere** {2003}
- Half moon: Sun-Earth-Moon angle **90°** {2005}
- Match: **planet-Uranus, satellite-Moon, comet-Halley, spacecraft-Mariner** {1992, 1999}
- Blue moon: **two full moons in a month** {2007, 2009}
- On the Moon an object's **weight decreases** (mass same) {2006}
- Earth-Moon distance: about **3,84,400 km** (key 364 thousand, perigee) {2008}
- SELENE: **Japan**, first Asian lunar orbiter of its kind {2009}
