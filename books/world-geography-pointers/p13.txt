@section 13.1 | Composition and Layers
- Largest share: **nitrogen**; permanent gas: **nitrogen** {1993, 2017}
- Ascending order: **troposphere, stratosphere, mesosphere, ionosphere** (key gave descending, a) {2005}
- Lowest layer: **troposphere** {2016}
- Ozone layer: **stratosphere** {1997, 2003, 2005}
- Deflects radio waves: **ionosphere**; reflects wireless signals {2005, 2013}
- Northern Lights: **ionosphere** (thermosphere) {?}
- Correct: **troposphere-weather, stratosphere-ozone, ionosphere-radio waves**; not mesosphere-aurora (1, 2, 3) {2002}

@flow Layers, ground up
Troposphere | 0-18 km; weather; temperature falls
Stratosphere | Up to 50 km; ozone; jets fly low in it
Mesosphere | 50-80 km; coldest; meteors burn
Thermosphere / Ionosphere | 80-400+ km; radio reflection; aurora
Exosphere | Outer fringe
@end

@section 13.2 | Heat and Pressure
- Air heated mainly **from the Earth's surface** (long-wave); conversion happens at the surface (R explains A) {1997}
- **Albedo**: **reflecting power** {2019}
- Not true: maximum-temperature zone lies **on the Equator** (it is around 10-20° N, the thermal equator) {2002}
- Humid summer heat: **muggy** weather {2002}
- **Horse latitudes**: about **30°** {2008}
- Air pressure lowest in **summer** {2014}

@end

@section 13.3 | Winds, Jets, Clouds
- Leftward deflection in the Southern Hemisphere: **Coriolis force** {2023}
- Jet streams are **westerly** (A false); speed 300-500 km/h (R true) {2024}
- Highest clouds (of options): **cirrostratus** {2009}

@trap

- Aurora and radio reflection = **ionosphere**, not mesosphere.
@end
