@chapter 8 | Capital Cities, Towns and Geographical Sobriquets
@meta Built from 50 UPPSC questions (1990–2024). Focus: capital matching by region, largest city vs capital, Central Asian and African capitals, nicknames.

@hot
- **Capital matching** is the format in almost every question here {1992, 1995, 1997, 2003, 2009, 2009, 2011, 2015, 2016, 2019, 2023}
- **Central Asia**: **Tashkent, Dushanbe, Bishkek, Ashgabat, Astana**; Bishkek is **not** Tajikistan's {2007, 2009, 2011, 2019}
- **Africa** wrong pairs: **Nigeria-Rabat, Cape Verde-Bamako, Khartoum-Ethiopia** {2007, 2020, 2024}
- **Finland**: Helsinki; "land of a thousand lakes"; Oslo is **Norway's** {1993, 2001, 2012, 2015}
@end

@section 8.1 | Europe
- Not capitals: **Rotterdam, Zurich** (Riga, Zagreb are) {2023}
- All pairs correct; key took **Berlin-Germany** (*) {2008}
- **Bulgaria-Sofia, Georgia-Tbilisi, Iceland-Reykjavik, Norway-Oslo** {2003}
- **Helsinki**: **Finland** {1993}; **Finland-Oslo** is wrong {2001}
- Wrong: **Christchurch-New Zealand** (capital **Wellington**) {2010}
- Not a national capital: **Bonn** {2006}
