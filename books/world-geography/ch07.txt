@chapter 7 | Countries and their Borders
@unit UNIT III · THE POLITICAL WORLD

@intro
This is a **map-work** chapter. UPPSC asks about country size and order, coastlines, who borders whom (Israel, Gaza, Afghanistan, Zambia, Indonesia), regional groupings (Arabian Peninsula, Middle East, Horn of Africa, Scandinavia, Central America), **landlocked** countries, dependent territories and famous **boundary lines** (Radcliffe, Durand, McMahon, Maginot, 38th parallel). Several keys here are marked (*) because more than one option, or none, is correct. Check the notes. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 7.1 | Size and Coastline

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 1996
The largest country in the world is :
(a) Brazil
(b) Australia
(c) China
(d) U.S.A.
@ans (*)
@note By area the largest country is **Russia** (about 17.1 million sq km), which is not among the options. Of the options, the **USA** and **China** are roughly the same size (about 9.5-9.8 million sq km, depending on how water and disputed areas are counted). So the source marks the question (*). By population, **India** overtook China in **2023**.

@pyq U.P. R.O./A.R.O. (Mains) 2021
With reference to 'Indonesia', which of the following statements is / are correct?
(1) It comprises of over 15,000 islands.
(2) It has over 150 active volcanoes.
Select the correct answer using the code given below:
Code:
(a) Only 1
(b) Only 2
(c) Both 1 and 2
(d) Neither 1 nor 2
@ans (d)
@note Indonesia has about **17,000** islands, so statement 1 ("over 15,000") is literally true and (a) is the factually better answer. It has about **127-130** active volcanoes, so statement 2 is false. The Commission's key is (d), and it is kept here. Indonesia is moving its capital from Jakarta to **Nusantara** in East Kalimantan (Borneo).
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2022
With reference to Borneo Island, located in South East Asia, which of the following statements is / are true?
1. It is divided among three countries.
2. It has been formed due to volcano deposition.
Select the correct answer from the code given below.
Code :
(a) Both 1 and 2
(b) Only 1
(c) Neither 1 nor 2
(d) Only 2
@ans (a)
@note The Commission's key is (a). **Borneo**, the world's third-largest island, is shared by **Indonesia** (Kalimantan), **Malaysia** (Sabah, Sarawak) and **Brunei**, so statement 1 is true. The island is mostly **folded and uplifted** rock, not volcanic deposits, so on the facts the answer would be **(b) Only 1**, as the source's own explanation also says.
@end

@trap
**Durand = Afghanistan-Pakistan**, not India-Bangladesh. **Guyana is in South America**, while **Guinea** and **Guinea-Bissau** are in Africa. **Finland is Nordic but not Scandinavian.** **Norway has no Baltic coast.** **North America has no landlocked country**, and in South America only **Bolivia and Paraguay** are landlocked.
@end

@heatmap

@pattern
This is one of the most heavily asked political chapters, and the focus has shifted to **2021-2023 R.O./A.R.O. papers** with current-affairs angles (Gaza, Israel's neighbours, Indonesia, Borneo, the Middle East). Expect **border-pair** and **landlocked** questions every year. Several keys are disputed, so read the notes. When a "which is not" question looks too easy, check for a tiny border such as Zambia-Botswana.
@end
