@chapter 10 | Rivers of the World

@intro
World-river questions take three forms: river **records and quirks** (Congo crosses the Equator twice, Limpopo crosses the Tropic of Capricorn twice, Mississippi has a bird's-foot delta), river **direction and outlet** (which Siberian rivers flow north, where the Volga ends), and above all **city-on-river** matches. The city-river tables are the highest-value part of this chapter. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 10.1 | Rivers of Africa

@pyq U.P. Lower Sub. (Pre) 2015
Which of the following rivers crosses the Equator twice?
(a) Zaire
(b) Niger
(c) Nil
(d) Amazon
@ans (a)
@end

@pyq U.P.P.S.C. (GIC) 2010
Which of the following river of Africa crosses Tropic of Capricorn twice?
(a) Zambezi
(b) Limpopo
(c) Congo
(d) Niger
@ans (b)
@end

The **Congo (Zaire)** is the world's **deepest river** (over 220 m) and second only to the Amazon in discharge. Its loop across the Equator gives it rain from both hemispheres, so it flows steadily all year. The **Limpopo** forms the South Africa-Botswana-Zimbabwe borders and curves across the Tropic of Capricorn twice.

@table
Nile fact | Detail
Length | About 6,650 km; usually counted as the world's longest river
White Nile | From **Lake Victoria** (Uganda, Tanzania, Kenya); headstreams in Rwanda and Burundi
Blue Nile | From **Lake Tana** (Ethiopia); brings about 85% of the flood water and silt
Confluence | **Khartoum** (Sudan)

Mouth | Arcuate (fan-shaped) delta on the Mediterranean, near Alexandria and Port Said
"Gift of the Nile" | Herodotus' description of Egypt
@end

@section 10.2 | Rivers of Asia and Europe

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2016
The largest river of Asia is –
(a) Indus
(b) Brahmaputra
(c) Yangtze
(d) Hwang Ho
@ans (c)
@end

The **Yangtze** (Chang Jiang, about 6,300 km) is Asia's longest river and the world's third longest. It hosts the **Three Gorges Dam**, the world's largest power station. The **Hwang Ho** (Yellow River) is "**China's Sorrow**" because of its floods.

The **Ob, Yenisei and Lena** of Siberia flow **north** into the Arctic Ocean. Their mouths freeze while their upper courses thaw, which causes huge spring floods. The **Amur** forms the Russia-China border and reaches the Pacific through the Strait of Tartary. The **Tigris** and **Euphrates** join to form the **Shatt al-Arab** before the Persian Gulf.

The **Mekong** (about 4,900 km) runs from Tibet through **China, Myanmar, Laos, Thailand, Cambodia and Vietnam**, with its delta in **southern Vietnam**. India works with the region through the **Mekong-Ganga Cooperation** (2000).

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 1992
River Volga falls into :
(a) Red Sea
(b) Caspian Sea
(c) Black Sea
(d) Mediterranean Sea
@ans (b)
@end

The **Volga** (about 3,530 km) is **Europe's longest river** and flows entirely within Russia. The **Danube** (about 2,850 km), second longest, flows through or along **10 countries**, more than any other river, and reaches the **Black Sea**.

@trap
Capital pairs on the same river are classic traps: **Kinshasa and Brazzaville** face each other across the **Congo**, the closest pair of national capitals. **Vienna, Bratislava, Budapest and Belgrade** all lie on the **Danube**. **Vienna is not on the Volga**, **Lisbon is not on the Tiber**, and **Kyiv is on the Dnieper, not the Don**.
@end

@heatmap

@pattern
More than a third of these PYQs are **city-river** matches, usually European capitals with one African or American decoy. Learn the table above in pairs. The 2021-2025 papers add **statement-type** questions on the Rhine, Mekong and Nile, and on river **direction** (Amur, Angara, Mekong).
@end
