@chapter 8 | Capital Cities, Towns and Geographical Sobriquets

@intro
This is the most "pure memory" chapter in the book. Nearly every paper has a **capital-country** match, and the decoys are usually capitals from the same region (Central Asia's "-stans", Africa's Accra, Nairobi and Lusaka, Scandinavia's Oslo and Helsinki). The trap is a **former** or **largest** city given as the capital: Yangon, Shanghai, Christchurch, Bonn, New York, Zurich, Rotterdam. The chapter ends with **sobriquets**, the nicknames of places. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 8.1 | Capitals of Europe

@pyq U.P. R.O./A.R.O. (Pre) 2023
Which of the following is/are not the capital of any European country?
1. Riga
2. Rotterdam
3. Zagreb
4. Zurich
Select the correct answer from the code given below:
Code:
(a) 2 and 4
(b) 2 and 3
(c) Only 4
(d) Only 1
@ans (a)
@end

**Riga** is the capital of Latvia and **Zagreb** of Croatia. **Rotterdam** is Europe's largest port, but the Netherlands' capital is **Amsterdam** (the government sits at The Hague). **Zurich** is Switzerland's largest city, but the federal city is **Bern**.

@heatmap

@pattern
Capitals are asked **every year**, often twice. The newer papers (2019-2024) favour **Central Asia**, **Africa** and **disputed or partly recognised** capitals (Ramallah, Pristina, Taipei, North Nicosia). Learn capitals region by region, and keep a list of **changed capitals** (Nay Pyi Taw, Gitega, Astana, Abuja, the Egyptian New Administrative Capital, Indonesia's Nusantara). Sobriquet questions are easy marks if the table above is revised.
@end

@next
Likely next angles: **Nusantara** (Indonesia's new capital), Egypt's **New Administrative Capital**, **Astana**'s name changes, capitals of **BRICS+** members (Abu Dhabi, Tehran, Addis Ababa, Cairo, Jakarta), capitals in the news (Kyiv, Tel Aviv vs Jerusalem, Damascus, Kabul, Port-au-Prince, Khartoum), and "**Garden City**" type nicknames (Singapore, Bengaluru).
@end
