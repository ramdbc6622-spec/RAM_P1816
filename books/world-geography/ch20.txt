@intro
The last chapter brings together **world transport** (the North Atlantic route, the Trans-Siberian railway, the longest tunnels, the busiest ports, shipbuilding) and a **miscellany** of geography: thinkers (Sauer, Engler, Griffith Taylor, Lee), map terms (isogonic lines, umland), famous places (Grand Banks, Bermuda Triangle, Golden Triangle), the headquarters of international bodies, and a few current-affairs facts. @section 20.1 | Sea Routes, Railways and Tunnels

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2003; U.P.U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2002
Assertion (A) : North Atlantic sea route is the busiest sea route in the world.
Reason (R) : It connects two major industrial zones of the world.
In the context of the above statements which of the following is correct?
Code :
(a) (A) and (R) both are true, and (R) is correct explanation of (A).
(b) (A) and (R) both are true, and (R) is not correct explanation of (A).
(c) (A) is true, but (R) is false.
(d) (R) is true, but (A) is false.
@ans (a)
@end

@one U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 1998, 2004
Which one is the busiest sea route of the world?
@ans North Atlantic Ocean
@end

The **North Atlantic route** links the two oldest industrial regions, **north-eastern USA/Canada** and **western Europe**, and is called the "**Big Trunk Route**". By container volume, the **Asia-Europe** (via Suez) and **trans-Pacific** routes are now as important.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2019
Which one of the following cities is not located on the Trans-Siberian rail route?
(a) Kazan
(b) Omsk
(c) Sochi
(d) Chita
@ans (c)
@end

The **Trans-Siberian Railway** (about **9,300 km**, the world's longest line, completed 1916) runs from **Moscow** through Kazan (on some routings), Yekaterinburg, Omsk, Novosibirsk, Irkutsk (Lake Baikal) and Chita to **Vladivostok**. **Sochi** is a Black Sea resort in the far south-west of Russia.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2014; U.P.P.C.S. (Spl) (Mains) 2008

@pyq U.P. Lower Sub. (Pre) 2013
The longest rock tunnel of the world is located in
(a) Finland
(b) Greenland
(c) Japan
(d) Sweden
@ans (*)
@note The world's longest rock tunnel is the **Delaware Aqueduct** (about 137 km, USA), a water-supply tunnel. Finland's **Paijanne** water tunnel (about 120 km) is second, so (a) is the closest of the options. Because the longest is in none of the listed countries, the source marks the question (*).
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2012
The world's longest Railroad tunnel Seikan is in :
(a) China
(b) South Korea
(c) Japan
(d) Malaysia
@ans (c)
@end

The **Seikan Tunnel** (53.9 km, linking Honshu and Hokkaido) was the longest rail tunnel until Switzerland's **Gotthard Base Tunnel** (**57.1 km**, 2016) overtook it. The **Channel Tunnel** (50.5 km) has the longest **undersea** section. In India, the **Sela** tunnel (Arunachal) and the rail tunnels on the **Udhampur-Srinagar-Baramulla** line are recent landmarks.

@section 20.4 | Places and Facts

@pyq U.P.U.D.A./L.D.A. (Spl) (Mains) 2010
Grand Bank is located at :
(a) Western coast of North America
(b) Western Europe coast
(c) Eastern coast of North America
(d) Eastern coast of Africa
@ans (c)
@end

The **Grand Banks** off **Newfoundland** are among the world's richest fishing grounds. They are shallow, and there the warm **Gulf Stream** meets the cold **Labrador Current**. The famous **cod** fishery there collapsed in 1992.
