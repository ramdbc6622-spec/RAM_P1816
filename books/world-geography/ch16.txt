@chapter 16 | Major Tribes of the World
@unit UNIT V · HUMAN AND ECONOMIC WORLD

@intro
Tribe questions are **tribe-to-habitat** matches, and the same dozen groups keep returning: **Eskimo/Inuit** (Arctic), **Lapps/Sami** (Scandinavia), **Kirghiz** (Central Asia), **Bedouin** (Arabia), **Bushmen/San** (Kalahari), **Pygmies** (Congo), **Maasai** (East Africa), **Semang** (Malaysia), **Ainu** (Japan), **Maori** (New Zealand) and **Veddas** (Sri Lanka). The classic trap is the **Maasai** placed in West Africa or Zambia. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2015
To which of the following racial groups the natives of North America belong ?
(a) Austric
(b) Caucasoid
(c) Mongoloid
(d) Negroid
@ans (c)
@end

The native peoples of the Americas are descended from Asian groups who crossed **Beringia**, the land bridge where the Bering Strait is now, during the last Ice Age (about 15,000-20,000 years ago). That is why older textbooks group them as "Mongoloid".

@trap
**Maasai = Kenya and Tanzania (East Africa)**. They are never in West Africa, Zambia or Sudan. **Semang = Malaysia**, not Indonesia. **Koryak = Kamchatka (Russia)**, not Alaska. **Kirghiz = Central Asia**, not Sudan. **Inuit live in Alaska, Canada and Greenland**; the people of Sweden and Finland are the **Sami (Lapps)**. **Pygmies are hunter-gatherers**, not pastoral nomads.
@end

@heatmap

@pattern
Each tribe question is an **odd-one-out** or **match** on habitat, and UPPSC keeps returning to the same twelve groups. The newer papers (2019-2025) add less-known names (Teda, Beja, Lur, Koryak, Punan, Ruwala). Learn the master table by continent, and remember the Maasai trap: it appeared four times.
@end
