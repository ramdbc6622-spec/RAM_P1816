@section 4.1 | Geological History

@pyq U.P.U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2006
Which of the following Method is used to determine the age of the Earth ?
(a) Carbon dating for the age of the fossils.
(b) Germanium dating
(c) Uranium dating
(d) All of the above
@ans (c)
@end

**Carbon-14** has a half-life of only about 5,730 years, so it can date things up to about 50,000 years old. The Earth's age, about **4.54 billion years**, comes from **uranium-lead** dating of meteorites and the oldest minerals (zircons).

@section 4.3 | Earthquakes

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2008
The 'Ring of Fire' is associated with
1. Earthquake
2. Volcanoes
3. Pacific Ocean
4. Forest Fires
Choose your answer with the help of codes given below :
Code :
(a) 1, 2 and 3
(b) 2 and 3
(c) 2 and 4
(d) 1, 2, 3 and 4
@ans (a)
@end

The **Pacific Ring of Fire** (about 40,000 km) traces converging plate edges around the Pacific. It has about **75%** of the world's active volcanoes and about **90%** of its earthquakes, in Chile, the Andes, Mexico, California, Alaska, Japan, the Philippines, Indonesia and New Zealand.

@pyq U.P. Lower Sub. (Pre) 2015
The intensity of earthquakes is measured
(a) On the Richter scale
(b) On the Kelvin scale
(c) In decibel
(d) In Pascal
@ans (a)
@end

Strictly, the **Richter scale** (1935) measures **magnitude**, the energy released, and the **Modified Mercalli scale** measures **intensity**, the damage felt. Seismologists now prefer the **moment magnitude scale** (Mw). Each whole step on these scales means about **32 times** more energy.

@table
Earthquake term | Meaning
Focus (hypocentre) | Point **inside** the Earth where the rupture starts
Epicentre | Point on the **surface** directly above the focus; first to feel the shaking
P-waves | Primary, fastest; pass through solids and liquids
S-waves | Secondary; only through solids, so the outer core is liquid
L (surface) waves | Slowest but most destructive
Shadow zone | About 105°-145° from the epicentre, where direct P- and S-waves do not arrive
Seismograph / seismogram | Instrument / the record it makes
@end
