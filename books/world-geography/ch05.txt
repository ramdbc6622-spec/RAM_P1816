@chapter 5 | Continents, Mountains, Plateaus and Valleys

@intro
UPPSC tests landforms with **"which country / which continent"** questions. You need the size order of the continents, the longest ranges (Andes, Rockies, Himalaya, Great Dividing Range), the classic fold, block, volcanic and residual examples, Europe's and Africa's ranges, the Tibetan and Colorado plateaus, and famous valleys and basins (Death Valley, Panjshir, Silicon Valley, the Great Artesian Basin). It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

Which one amongst the following is the largest plateau?
(a) Coconino
(b) Aquarius
(c) Colorado
(d) Columbia
@ans (c)
@end

The **Tibetan (Qinghai-Tibet) Plateau**, averaging about **4,500 m** and covering about 2.5 million sq km, is the world's highest and largest plateau, the "**Roof of the World**". The **Colorado Plateau** (USA) contains the **Grand Canyon**, and the **Columbia Plateau** is a lava plateau.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2019
Telegraphic Plateau is a part of :
(a) North Atlantic Ridge
(b) South Atlantic Ridge
(c) Indian Ocean Ridge
(d) None of these
@ans (a)
@end

The "**Telegraph Plateau**" was the name **Matthew Maury** (1850s) gave to a seemingly shallow stretch of the North Atlantic floor between Ireland and Newfoundland, where the first **transatlantic telegraph cable** (1858) was laid. It is part of the **Mid-Atlantic Ridge** region.

**Death Valley** (Mojave Desert, California) holds the record for the **highest air temperature ever recorded**: **56.7 °C** at Furnace Creek (1913). Its **Badwater Basin** (-86 m) is the lowest point in North America. The "**Devil's Golf Course**" is a field of jagged salt crystals there.

@trap
**Great Basin (USA)** and **Great Artesian Basin (Australia)** are paired as decoys. So are **Darling Range** (WA coast) and **Darling River** (NSW), and **White Mountains** (USA) and **Black Mountains** (USA), both Appalachian. **Death Valley** is a **rift (graben)** valley known for **heat**, even though it is also very low and salty.

@pattern
About two-thirds of these PYQs are **range-to-country** or **range-to-continent** matches, and several are from 2020-2024. That shows UPPSC is now asking about less familiar ranges (Pontic, Zagros, Hoggar, Toubkal, Darling, Balkan). Learn the European and African range tables together. Valley questions are about famous US places.
