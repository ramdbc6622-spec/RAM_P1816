@chapter 11 | Islands, Lakes and Waterfalls

@intro
This chapter covers island records (Greenland, New Guinea, Borneo), island **groups** (Japan's four islands, Indonesia's sequence, Melanesia vs Polynesia, the Caribbean), the **Great Lakes** of North America and Africa, lake **records** (Caspian, Baikal, Tanganyika, Superior, Titicaca) and the great **waterfalls** (Angel, Tugela, Victoria). Recent papers (2021-2023) favour statement-type questions on a single island or lake. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@pyq U.P. R.O./A.R.O. (Mains) 2021
With reference to 'Lake Victoria in Africa', which of the following statements is/are correct?
(1) The lake is surrounded by four countries.
(2) It is situated in the African rift valleye.
Select the correct answer using the code given below:
Code :
(a) Only 1
(b) Only 2
(c) Both 1 and 2
(d) Neither 1 nor 2
@ans (d)
@end

**Lake Victoria** is shared by **three** countries (Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania). It lies in a shallow basin **between** the eastern and western arms of the Rift Valley, not inside the rift, which is why it is shallow (about 80 m). The **rift lakes** (Tanganyika, Malawi, Albert, Turkana) are long, narrow and deep.
