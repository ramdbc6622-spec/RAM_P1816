@chapter 12 | Straits, Gulfs, Canals and Dams

@intro
These are the world's **choke points and engineering works**. Straits questions ask which strait separates which landmasses (Gibraltar, Bering, Bass, Hormuz, Dover, Lombok, the Ten Degree Channel). Canal questions focus on **Suez**: what it joins, its ports, its lakes and the distance it saves. Dam questions match dam, river and country (Hoover, Aswan, Kariba, Grand Coulee, Zangmu). Shipping crises (Red Sea attacks, Hormuz tensions, the 2021 Suez blockage) keep this chapter in the news. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 12.2 | Gulfs and Bays

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2007, 2008
The Persian Gulf does not form the border with –
(a) Bahrain
(b) Iraq
(c) Kuwait
(d) Oman
@ans (d)
@note **Oman**'s main coast faces the **Gulf of Oman** and the Arabian Sea, which is why it is the key. Strictly, Oman's **Musandam** exclave does touch the Persian Gulf side of the Strait of Hormuz.
@end

@table
Suez Canal fact | Detail
Opened | **1869** (Ferdinand de Lesseps); nationalised by **Nasser** in **1956**
Length | About 193 km; **no locks** (sea level)
Ends | **Port Said** (Mediterranean) and **Suez / Port Tewfik** (Red Sea)
Lakes, north to south | **Manzala - Timsah (Ismailia) - Great Bitter - Little Bitter**
India-Europe saving | About **7,000 km** compared with the route round the Cape of Good Hope
In the news | **Ever Given** blockage (March 2021); traffic falls after the **Red Sea / Houthi** attacks (2023-25)
@end

@table
Canal | Joins | Country
Panama (1914) | Atlantic (Caribbean) and Pacific | Panama; uses **locks** and Gatun Lake; drought cut traffic in 2023-24
Kiel | North Sea and Baltic | Germany; busiest artificial waterway by ships
Soo (Sault Ste. Marie) | **Lake Superior and Lake Huron** | USA-Canada
Welland | Lake Erie and Lake Ontario (bypasses Niagara) | Canada
St. Lawrence Seaway | Great Lakes and the Atlantic | USA-Canada
Ludwig / Rhine-Main-Danube | Main (Rhine) and Danube | **Southern Germany** (Bavaria)
Volga-Don | Volga (Caspian) and Don (Black Sea) | Russia
White Sea-Baltic | White Sea and Baltic | Russia
Grand Canal | Beijing to Hangzhou | China; oldest and longest artificial canal
Corinth | Gulf of Corinth and Saronic Gulf | Greece
@end

@pyq U.P. Lower Sub.(Pre) 2009
World's longest sea bridge has been built on
(a) East China Sea
(b) Gulf of Tonkin
(c) Jiaozhou Bay
(d) South China Sea
@ans (c)
@note The **Jiaozhou Bay Bridge** (Qingdao, about 41.6 km, 2011) held the record when the question was set. Today the longest sea crossing is the **Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge** (about 55 km including tunnels, 2018). India's longest sea bridge is the **Atal Setu** (Mumbai Trans Harbour Link, about 21.8 km, 2024).
@end

@trap
**Hoover is on the Colorado**, not the Tennessee. The **TVA** dams are on the Tennessee. **Kariba and Cahora Bassa are both on the Zambezi** (Kariba upstream, on the Zambia-Zimbabwe border). The **Soo Canal** joins **Superior and Huron**, not Superior and Michigan. **Oman's main coast is not on the Persian Gulf**.
@end
