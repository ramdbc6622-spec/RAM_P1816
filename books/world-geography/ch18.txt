@chapter 18 | World Minerals and Energy Resources

@intro
This is the biggest single section of the World Geography source (65 PYQs). Questions come in two kinds. **Region-mineral** pairs are stable and safe to learn: Ruhr, Donbas, Kuzbas, Appalachian coal; Lorraine, Kiruna, Krivoi Rog, Mesabi, Pilbara iron; Chuquicamata, Katanga, Bingham copper; Witwatersrand and Kalgoorlie gold; Kimberley diamonds; Kinta tin. **"Largest producer"** questions change with every data release, so many older keys carry notes with the **2023** position (BGS World Mineral Production / USGS). It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

Other pipelines to remember: **TAPI** (Turkmenistan-Afghanistan-Pakistan-India), **Nord Stream** (Russia-Germany, damaged in 2022), **BTC** (Baku-Tbilisi-Ceyhan) and **Power of Siberia** (Russia-China).
