@chapter 6 | Deserts and Grasslands

@intro
This chapter covers dry lands and grass lands. **Desert** questions ask why hot deserts lie on the **western margins** of continents, size order, which desert is in which country (Gobi, Taklamakan, Karakum, Dasht-e-Lut, Atacama, Kalahari, Gibson, Patagonian) and records (largest, driest, most populated). **Grassland** questions are about regional names: Prairies, Pampas, Steppes, Veld, Downs, Llanos, Campos, Puszta and Savanna. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 6.1 | Deserts: Definition and Location

@pyq U.P. B.E.O. (Pre) 2019
Given below are two statements, one is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other as Reason (R). Choose the correct answer from the codes given below:
Assertion (A) : Most of the hot deserts of the world are located on the Western margins of continents between 15º-30º North and South latitudes.
Reason (R) : Trade winds lose their moisture and humidity as it move westward.
Codes :
(a) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
(b) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)
(c) (A) is true but (R) is false
(d) (A) is false but (R) is true
@ans (a)
@end

Three things make the **west-coast hot deserts** (Sahara, Kalahari-Namib, Atacama, Sonoran, Great Australian):
- **Trade winds** blow from the east and have lost their moisture by the time they reach the western coasts.
- The **subtropical high-pressure belt** (about 30°) brings sinking, drying air.
- **Cold ocean currents** offshore (Canary, Benguela, Humboldt, California, West Australian) chill the air and stop rain.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2013
Statement (A) : Deserts can be effective sources for the production of perennial energy.
Reason (R) : As much energy is consumed by mankind in one year, Desert can receive the energy is more than that in only 6 hours from the Sun.
Choose the correct answer from the following code –
(a) (A) and (R), both are correct and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).
(b) (A) and (R), both are correct, but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A).
(c) (A) is correct, but (R) is false.
(d) (A) is false but (R) is correct.
@ans (a)
@end

This is the **Desertec** idea of German physicist **Gerhard Knies**. India's desert solar parks follow it: **Bhadla** (Rajasthan), one of the world's largest, and the **Khavda** renewable park (Kutch), planned at 30 GW.

@one U.P. Lower Sub. (Pre) 2009
The Driest place on earth is
@ans Atacama Desert
@end

The **Atacama** lies in **northern** Chile, between the Andes and the cold **Humboldt (Peru) current**. Some weather stations there have never recorded rain. It is rich in **nitrates**, **copper** (Chuquicamata) and **lithium**, which makes it part of the "Lithium Triangle" with Bolivia and Argentina.

@trap
**Gibson is in Australia**, not Brazil. **Atacama is in northern Chile**, not southern. **Patagonia** is a **temperate rain-shadow** desert (east of the Andes), not a trade-wind one. The **Gobi** does not enter Russia or Kazakhstan. The **Karakum** is in Turkmenistan and the **Kyzylkum** in Uzbekistan-Kazakhstan. **Largest desert overall = Antarctica**, but the **largest hot desert = Sahara**.
@end

@heatmap

@pattern
Deserts and grasslands are a **"name to place"** topic. Expect one desert question and one grassland question in most years. Recent papers (2022-2025) moved to **Central Asian and Iranian** deserts (Karakum, Dasht-e-Lut, Gobi's extent) and to **size order**. Grassland questions keep testing **tropical vs temperate** (Savanna, Llanos and Campos are tropical; Downs are temperate) and the Puszta-Hungary and Downs-Australia pairs.
@end

@next
Likely next angles: **desertification** (UNCCD, COP16 at Riyadh, 2024), the **Great Green Wall** of the Sahel, the **Aral Sea** turning into the Aralkum desert, **Rub al Khali** (Empty Quarter), **Namib** (oldest desert, fog-fed), and **lithium in the Atacama**.
@end
