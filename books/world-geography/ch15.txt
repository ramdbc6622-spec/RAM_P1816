@chapter 15 | World Climate and Forests

@intro
This chapter links **climate types** with the **forests** they support. UPPSC tests the Amazon **Selva**, the **Taiga**, monsoon, Mediterranean and coniferous trees, the Mediterranean shrublands (maquis, chaparral, fynbos, matorral), and the climate regions themselves. The **Mediterranean** climate alone accounts for five PYQs, with its winter rain explained by the shifting westerlies. The chapter closes with records (wettest, coldest), **El Nino** and **Koppen**. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@pyq U.P. P.C.S. (Pre) 1998
Soil erosion is a major problem in
(a) In tropical rain forest
(b) In African Savana
(c) In the Mediterranean regins
(d) In Australian Downs
@ans (a)
@end

Under rainforests the problem is really **leaching**. Heavy rain washes nutrients out of the thin soil, so the rich forest grows on poor ground. Once the trees are cleared, the soil is quickly eroded and exhausted, which is why shifting cultivation moves on every few years.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2024
Consider the following statements with reference to Mediterranean climate:
1. It receives rainfall in winter season.
2. Due to winter solstice, air pressure belts shift to the South.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?
(a) Only 2
(b) Neither 1 nor 2
(c) Only 1
(d) Both 1 and 2
@ans (d)
@end

**Why the Mediterranean has winter rain**: around the **winter solstice** the Sun, and with it all the pressure and wind belts, shifts **south**. The 30-40° latitudes then come under the rain-bearing **westerlies**. In summer the belts shift north and these coasts fall under the dry **subtropical high and offshore trade winds**. Because winter rain is caused by the westerlies, not by summer dryness, the 2004 A-R pair is (a) and the 2022 pair is (d).

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 1999; U.P. Lower Sub. (Pre) 2004; U.P. Lower Sub. (Spl) (Pre) 2004
The coldest place on the earth is :
(a) Halifax
(b) Chicago
(c) Siachin
(d) Verkhoyansk
@ans (d)
@end

**Mawsynram** (Meghalaya) averages about **11,870 mm** of rain a year. **Verkhoyansk** and **Oymyakon** (Siberia) are the coldest inhabited places, at about -68 °C. The coldest temperature ever measured was **-89.2 °C** at **Vostok** station, Antarctica (1983). The hottest air temperature recorded is **56.7 °C** in Death Valley, USA.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2023
With reference to the "El-Nino", which of the following statement/s is/are correct?
(1) El Nino involves appearance of warm current of the coast of Peru in eastern pacific.
(2) This warm current increase the temperature of water on the Peruvian coast by 10°C there by increasing the amount of Plankton in the sea.
Select the correct answer using the code given below
Code :
(a) Only 1
(b) Both 1 and 2
(c) Only 2
(d) Neither 1 nor 2
@ans (a)
@end

In an **El Nino** the cold Humboldt upwelling off Peru is replaced by warm water. **Plankton and fish (anchovy) collapse**, so statement 2 is wrong. El Nino usually **weakens the Indian monsoon**, while **La Nina** strengthens it. The Indian Ocean has its own see-saw, the **Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD)**.

@pyq U.P. P.C.S. (Pre) 2018
'Vegetation is the true index of climate'. This statement is associated with :
(a) Thornthwaite
(b) Koppen
(c) Trewartha
(d) Stamp
@ans (b)
@end

**Wladimir Koppen** (1884, revised 1918-36) based his climate classes on **vegetation zones**, with letter codes: **A** tropical, **B** dry, **C** warm temperate, **D** cold (snow), **E** polar, plus **H** highland. India's monsoon climate is **Amw/Aw/Cwg**.
