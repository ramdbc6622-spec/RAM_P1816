@chapter 26 | Viral, Bacterial and Parasitic Diseases
@unit UNIT V · HEALTH, DISEASES AND BIOTECHNOLOGY

@intro
This chapter sorts infectious diseases by their cause. **Viral**: COVID-19 and its vaccines, dengue, Japanese encephalitis, rabies, polio, hepatitis, AIDS, Nipah, bird flu, Ebola and livestock diseases. **Bacterial**: tuberculosis, cholera, typhoid, tetanus, diphtheria, plague, leprosy, botulism, and the drugs and vaccines against them (DPT, BCG, antibiotics, sulpha drugs). **Protozoan, worm and fungal**: malaria and its vaccines, filaria, sleeping sickness and others. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

**Malaria** is caused by the protozoan ***Plasmodium*** and spread by **female Anopheles** mosquitoes. **Ronald Ross** proved the mosquito link in **Secunderabad (1897)** and won the Nobel Prize (1902). In India *P. vivax* causes most cases and *P. falciparum* the dangerous **malignant** (cerebral) malaria. WHO approved **RTS,S** (2021) and **R21/Matrix-M** (2023; Oxford, made by the **Serum Institute of India**) as malaria vaccines. Dengue and malaria share fever and mosquito bites but are spread by **different mosquito species**.

@trap
**Pandemic = worldwide.** **Aedes = dengue, chikungunya, Zika, yellow fever**; **Culex = JE, filaria**; **Anopheles = malaria**. Dengue lowers **platelets**. **Hepatitis B is a DNA virus**, **HIV an RNA retrovirus**. India's HIV subtype = **1C**. **Rinderpest** was eradicated in 2011. **TB, cholera, typhoid, tetanus, plague, leprosy = bacteria.** **DPT** excludes polio; diphtheria vaccine is a **toxoid**. **R21 = Serum Institute malaria vaccine.** **Peptic ulcer = bacteria.**
