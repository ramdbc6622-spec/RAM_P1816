@chapter 12 | Defence Technology

@intro
Defence questions are about **India's missiles** (Prithvi, Agni, Akash, Trishul, Nag, BrahMos, Astra, Nirbhay, Shaurya, Rudram), **aircraft and drones** (Tejas, Hansa, Netra, FWD-200B), **ships and submarines** (Arihant, Vikramaditya, Scorpene class, Coast Guard vessels), **foreign partners** (Israel's Phalcon, Barak-8 and Iron Dome; Russia's BrahMos) and **joint exercises**. Recent papers ask about tests and exercises from the past year. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@table
Missile | Type | Note
**Prithvi** | Surface-to-surface, short range (150-350 km) | First IGMDP missile (1988); **Dhanush** is its naval version (350 km)
**Agni** series | Surface-to-surface **ballistic**: Agni-I 700-900 km, II 2,000+ km, III 3,000+ km, IV 4,000 km, **V over 5,000 km** (ICBM class), **Agni-Prime** 1,000-2,000 km | Agni-V tested with **MIRV** (several warheads) in **Mission Divyastra**, March 2024
**Akash** | Surface-to-air, medium range (25-45 km) | Exported to Armenia
**Trishul** | Surface-to-air, short range | Programme closed (2008)
**Nag** | **Anti-tank**, fire-and-forget | Helina / Dhruvastra helicopter versions
**Astra** | **Air-to-air**, beyond visual range | First flight test May 2003; on Su-30MKI and Tejas
**BrahMos** | **Supersonic cruise** (Mach 2.8-3) | **India-Russia** joint venture (Brahmaputra + Moskva)
**Nirbhay** | **Subsonic** long-range cruise (1,000 km) | Land-attack
**Shaurya** | Hypersonic surface-to-surface (about 700-750 km) | Canister-launched, land version of K-15
**Rudram** | **Air-to-surface anti-radiation** (destroys enemy radars) | Rudram-II tested from a Su-30MKI in May 2024
**Pralay** | Quasi-ballistic surface-to-surface (150-500 km) | Conventional warhead
**K-4 / K-15 (Sagarika)** | Submarine-launched ballistic missiles | For the Arihant class
@end

Given below are two statements, one is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other as Reason (R).
Assertion (A): In April 2024, India delivered the BrahMos Supersonic Cruise Missile to Philippines.

(a) Both (A) and (R) are true, but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A).
(b) (A) is false, but (R) is true.

The **Philippines** became BrahMos's first export customer (a USD 375 million deal, 2022; first batteries delivered **19 April 2024**); the export version has a range of about **290 km**. BrahMos was used in **Operation Sindoor** (May 2025), India's strikes on terror bases in Pakistan and Pakistan-occupied Kashmir after the Pahalgam attack.

@up UP link: A **BrahMos integration and testing facility** at **Lucknow** (opened 2025) is part of the **UP Defence Industrial Corridor**, which has six nodes: **Agra, Aligarh, Chitrakoot, Jhansi, Kanpur and Lucknow**. **AK-203** rifles are made at **Korwa, Amethi** with Russia, and **HAL Kanpur** builds the Dornier-228.

@trap
**Prithvi = surface-to-surface, Trishul and Akash = surface-to-air, Nag = anti-tank, Astra = air-to-air, Rudram = air-to-surface.** **BrahMos = India + Russia**, supersonic; **Nirbhay is subsonic**. **Iron Dome, Phalcon and Barak = Israel.** **Tejas** is a fighter, **Hansa** a trainer. **INS Vikramaditya = ex-Admiral Gorshkov.** **Khanjar = Kyrgyzstan.** Agni-II is **not** an ICBM.
