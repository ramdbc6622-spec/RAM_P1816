@chapter 2 | Measuring Instruments and Scales

@intro
This chapter is a catalogue of "-meters" and "-graphs": which instrument measures **wind speed, humidity, pressure, high temperature, solar radiation, light, smoke density, blood pressure, earthquakes and radioactivity**. The questions come as single "used for" items or as four-pair match grids, and the same dozen instruments return again and again. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@section 2.1 | Navigation and Detection

@pyq U.P. R.O./A.R.O. (Pre) (Re. Exam) 2016; U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2000
Which one of the following instruments is used for locating submerged objects in an ocean ?
(a) Audiometer
(b) Galvanometer
(c) Sextant
(d) SONAR
@ans (d)
@end

@one
In SONAR, we use
@ans ultrasonic waves
@end

**SONAR** (Sound Navigation And Ranging) sends **ultrasonic** pulses through water and times the echo; it finds submarines, shoals of fish and the sea-floor depth. **RADAR** (Radio Detection And Ranging) does the same in air with **radio waves** (microwaves). An **audiometer** tests hearing, a **galvanometer** detects small currents, and a **sextant** measures the angle of the Sun or a star above the horizon for navigation.

@pyq U.P.U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2001; U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 1996, 2008; U.P. Lower Sub. (Pre) 2004

(a) Detecting objects by using light waves
(b) Reflecting sound waves to detect objects
(c) Determining the presence and location of objects with radio waves
(d) Tracking rain-bearing clouds
@ans (c)
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) (Re. Exam) 2015
'Pyrheliometer' is used for measuring :
(a) Sun spots
(b) Solar radiation
(c) Air temperature
(d) Temperature of plants
@ans (b)
@end

A **pyrometer** reads the heat radiation an object gives off, so it can measure furnaces and even the Sun's surface from a distance; below about 800°C bodies do not radiate enough for it. A **pyrheliometer** points straight at the Sun and measures *direct* solar radiation.

@section 2.3 | Temperature and Light

@pyq U.P. R.O./A.R.O. (Pre) 2017
Which one of the following scales of temperature does not have a negative value?
(a) Celsius
(b) Fahrenheit
(c) Kelvin
(d) Reaumur
@ans (c)
@end


The **Kelvin** scale starts at **absolute zero** (0 K = -273.15°C), the lowest possible temperature, so it has no negative values. **-40° is the same on the Celsius and Fahrenheit scales.** Conversion: C/5 = (F - 32)/9 = (K - 273)/5.

@pyq U.P. R.O./A.R.O. (Pre) 2016
Which of the following is correctly matched?
(a) Thermoresistor - Electronic Thermometer
(b) Capacitor - Thermometer
(c) Bipolar Junction Transistor - Rectifier
(d) Junction Diode - Amplifier
@ans (a)
@end

A **thermistor** (thermal resistor) changes resistance sharply with temperature, which makes it the sensor in digital thermometers. A **junction diode** is a **rectifier** (AC to DC), and a **transistor** is an **amplifier** or switch, so (c) and (d) are swapped.

The **Richter scale** (1935) is logarithmic: each whole number is about **10 times** the ground amplitude and about **32 times** the energy. Seismologists now prefer the **moment magnitude** scale. *Intensity*, the damage felt at a place, is measured on the **Modified Mercalli** scale (I-XII).

@trap
**Hygrometer = humidity**, **hydrometer = liquid density**. **Pyrometer = high temperature**, **pyrheliometer = solar radiation**. **Barometer = atmospheric pressure**, **manometer = gas pressure**. An **ammeter measures current, not power**. The **Kelvin** scale has no negative values. A **diode rectifies**, a **transistor amplifies**. **Richter = magnitude**, **Mercalli = intensity**.
@end

@heatmap

@pattern
Instrument questions are **pure recall** and among the most repeated in the science section: the pyrometer, hygrometer, anemometer, barometer and seismograph each appear in several years. Match grids with four instruments are the commonest format, so learn the whole table rather than single pairs.
@end

Likely next angles: **LIDAR** (laser ranging, used by Chandrayaan and self-driving cars), **Doppler weather radar** (IMD network), **pulse oximeter**, **spectrometer**, the **Mercalli** scale, **dosimeter** for radiation workers, and **AQI monitors** (PM2.5 sensors).
