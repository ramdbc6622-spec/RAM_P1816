@section 16.1 | Carbon and Its Allotropes

@pyq U.P. P.C.S. (Mains) 2016
Which one of the following elements forms the maximum number of compounds?
(a) Hydrogen
(b) Carbon
(c) Nitrogen
(d) Oxygen
@ans (b)
@end


Carbon forms more compounds than any other element (millions) because of **catenation**: its atoms bond to each other in long chains and rings, and it has a valency of four. This is the basis of organic chemistry.

@table
Form of carbon | Structure | Properties and uses
**Diamond** | Each atom bonded to 4 others in 3-D | **Hardest natural substance**; poor conductor; cutting, jewellery; weight in **carats (1 carat = 200 mg)**
**Graphite** | Flat layers of hexagons | **Thermodynamically most stable**; soft, slippery, **conducts electricity**; **pencil "lead"**, lubricant, electrodes, moderator
**Fullerene** (C_{60}, buckyball) | Football-like cage of 60 atoms (pentagons and hexagons) | Discovered **1985** by **Kroto, Curl and Smalley** (Nobel Chemistry **1996**)
**Graphene** | **Single layer** of graphite, one atom thick | **Strongest material** known, excellent conductor; isolated in **2004** (Geim and Novoselov, Nobel Physics 2010)
Carbon nanotubes | Rolled graphene sheets | Very strong, light
Amorphous forms | Coal, charcoal, coke, lamp black | Fuels, filters, pigments
@end

Diamond is the pure element carbon; sand (SiO_{2}), marble (CaCO_{3}) and sugar are compounds. **Oxocarbons** are compounds of carbon and oxygen (CO, CO_{2}), not allotropes.

@trap
**Graphite**, not diamond, is the most stable form of carbon. **Graphene = single carbon layer.** **Fullerene has no fluorine.** **Anthracite** has the most carbon; **pig iron** has the most carbon among irons. Heavy water's molecular mass is **20**. **Vanaspati = hydrogenation** with a nickel catalyst. **Laughing gas = nitrous oxide (N_{2}O)**, not nitric oxide. **Divers: helium + oxygen.** Acids have pH **below** 7. **Aqua regia = HCl + HNO_{3} (3 : 1).** **Baking soda = NaHCO_{3}**, washing soda = Na_{2}CO_{3}. **Bleaching powder = calcium oxychloride.** **Hypo = sodium thiosulphate.**
@end
