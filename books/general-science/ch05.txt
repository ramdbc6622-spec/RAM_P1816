@chapter 5 | Light and Optics

@intro
Light is the single largest physics topic in UPPSC. The questions cover the **visible range** and the eye's peak sensitivity, the **speed of light**, **refraction** (apparent depth, flattened Sun), **total internal reflection** (optical fibre, mirage, diamond), **scattering** (blue sky, red danger signals, black sky in space), **dispersion and the rainbow**, **mirrors and lenses** in daily use, and the **human eye** and its defects. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.

The eye sees roughly **390 (violet) to 780 nm (red)**, that is 3,900-7,800 Å. The ear is most sensitive at about **1,000-4,000 Hz**. **Persistence of vision** is about **1/16 second**, which lets 24 frames per second look like continuous motion in cinema; persistence of hearing is about 1/10 second.

@pyq U.P. R.O./A.R.O. (Pre) 2023
The reason for a swimming pool to appear less deep than the actual depth is :
(a) Reflection
(b) Light scattering
(c) Refraction
(d) None of the above
@ans (c)
@end


When light passes from one medium to another, its **speed and wavelength change but frequency stays the same**; the change in speed bends the ray (**refraction**). Refraction makes a pool look **shallower** (apparent depth = real depth / refractive index), a stick look bent in water, stars **twinkle**, and the Sun appear **flattened (elliptical)** near the horizon and visible about 2 minutes before actual sunrise and after sunset.

Consider the following statements :
1. Fibre optics is based on the principle of total internal reflection.

4. In India, Reliance Group of Industries is engaged in the manufacture of the optical fibre.

(b) 1, 2 and 4
(c) 1, 3 and 4

**Total internal reflection (TIR)** happens when light going from a denser to a rarer medium meets the boundary at more than the **critical angle**; all of it reflects back. **Optical fibres** use TIR to carry light signals through thin glass strands with very little loss, high bandwidth and no electrical (radio-frequency) interference. The key (a) treats statement 4 (about Reliance) as incorrect.

**Rayleigh scattering** is inversely proportional to the **fourth power of wavelength** (1/λ^{4}). Blue light is scattered about 5.5 times more than red, so the **sky looks blue**. At sunrise and sunset sunlight crosses a long path of air, most blue is scattered away and the Sun looks **red-orange**. Red, scattered least, travels farthest through haze and fog, so it is used for **danger signals**, brake lights and aviation warning lamps.

@pyq U.P.U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2001
Assertion (A) : Black hole is such a celestial existence which cannot be seen by telescope.
Reason (R) : The gravitational field of black hole is much strong that even light does not escape from it.
Code :
(a) Both (A) and (R) are true, and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).
(b) Both (A) and (R) are true, but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A).

@end


The **Doppler effect** shifts the light of a source moving away towards longer wavelength (**red shift**). Edwin Hubble (1929) found almost all galaxies are red-shifted, so the **universe is expanding**. A **black hole**'s gravity is so strong that even light cannot escape from inside its *event horizon*; the first direct image (of M87*) came from the Event Horizon Telescope in 2019.

A plane mirror is a sphere of **infinite** radius of curvature, so its power is zero.

@trap
Eye is most sensitive to **yellow-green (555 nm)**, not red. Frequency **does not change** on refraction. **Mirage, diamond sparkle and optical fibre = total internal reflection.** **Violet deviates most, red least.** Red is used for danger because it is **scattered least**. An **air bubble acts as a concave lens**. Rear-view mirrors are **convex**, headlights **concave**. **Myopia → concave lens, hypermetropia → convex lens.** Doctors' ENT mirror is **concave**.
@end
