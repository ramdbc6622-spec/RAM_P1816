(c) Mustard oil – Lubricant
(d) Animal fats - Cholesterol
@ans (c)
@end


**Cholesterol** is a **steroid** (sterol) made mainly by the liver and found only in **animal** fats; plants have **phytosterols**. **LDL** is "bad" cholesterol and **HDL** "good"; **trans fats** (partially hydrogenated oils, vanaspati) raise LDL and **lower HDL**. Mustard oil is food, not a lubricant.
