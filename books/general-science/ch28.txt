@chapter 28 | Genetic Engineering and Biotechnology

**Genetic engineering** (recombinant DNA technology) cuts DNA with **restriction enzymes** ("molecular scissors"), joins it with **ligase** and carries it into cells with **vectors**: bacterial **plasmids** (small circular DNA) and **bacteriophages** (viruses that infect bacteria). Disease resistance, faster growth and animal cloning have all been achieved; **human reproductive cloning** has not, and is banned.

The **Human Genome Project** (1990-2003) read the full sequence of human DNA (about 3 billion base pairs, about 20,000 genes). **Stem cells** can divide and become any cell type; **embryonic** stem cells raise ethical debate, while **adult stem cells** from **bone marrow**, umbilical cord blood and fat are uncontroversial. Bone-marrow transplants for blood cancers are stem-cell therapy.

@up UP link: The **Plant Field Gene Bank** at **Banthra** (Lucknow), run by the **National Botanical Research Institute**, conserves endangered and economically useful plants and guards against bio-piracy.

@one

@ans Transgenic fertile seeds that become sterile in the second generation.
@end

**Bt cotton** carries a *cry* gene from the soil **bacterium** *Bacillus thuringiensis*, whose protein kills bollworms; it is India's only approved GM crop (2002). **Terminator technology** (GURT) makes a crop's seeds **sterile** so farmers must buy new seed each season; it has never been commercialised and India bans it. **GM mustard** (DMH-11) got environmental clearance in 2022.

**Golden Rice** (Ingo Potrykus and Peter Beyer, 2000) carries genes that make **beta-carotene** in the grain, giving it a yellow colour; the body turns it into **vitamin A**. The Philippines approved it in 2021. Buckwheat is a pseudo-cereal, macaroni wheat is *Triticum durum*, and **triticale** is a man-made hybrid, but none of these is transgenic.

**DNA fingerprinting** was developed by **Alec Jeffreys** (Leicester, UK, **1984**); it was first used to convict a criminal in the Colin Pitchfork case (1986-88). The source keys **1983**, close to the technique's invention. It uses repeating DNA sequences (**VNTRs**) unique to each person, from any cells with nuclei: blood, hair roots, saliva, bone, semen. Uses: crime, **paternity disputes**, identifying disaster victims and tracking **endangered species**. In India, **Lalji Singh** (CCMB, Hyderabad) pioneered it; the **Centre for DNA Fingerprinting and Diagnostics** is in Hyderabad.

@section 28.5 | Transgenics and Gene Therapy

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2000
Which one of the following is not achieved by transgenics?
(a) Production of biodegradable plastic
(b) Production of edible vaccines
(c) Production of cloned animals
(d) Production of transgenic crops
@ans (c)
@end


**Transgenic** organisms carry a foreign gene: Bt crops, edible vaccines, bacteria making insulin or biodegradable plastic. **Cloning** makes a genetically identical copy and does not need a foreign gene, so it is not a transgenic achievement.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Spl.) (Mains) 2004
In gene therapy, to correct the functioning of a faulty gene :
(a) Bad gene is eliminated
(b) Another good gene is inserted
(c) Defective gene is stopped from working
(d) The whole bad gene is replaced by a good gene
@ans (*)
@note Gene therapy can work in all of these ways: adding a working copy of the gene (the usual method), silencing a faulty gene, or (with **CRISPR** editing) correcting or replacing it. With several right options, the source marks the question (*); (b) is the classic textbook answer.
@end

**CRISPR-Cas9** gene editing (Doudna and Charpentier, Nobel 2020) lets scientists rewrite DNA precisely. India approved its first indigenous gene therapy trials for **haemophilia** (CMC Vellore, 2024) and released **genome-edited rice** varieties (2025).

@trap
**Dolly** was the first mammal cloned from an adult cell (Roslin, 1996); **Garima** is a cloned **buffalo**; **Injaz** a cloned **camel**. **Plasmids and bacteriophages** are vectors. **Bt** comes from a **bacterium**. **Terminator seeds are sterile.** **Golden rice = beta-carotene → vitamin A.** **Super rice = G.S. Khush.** **Amniocentesis** for sex selection is illegal. DNA fingerprinting works with **all** body cells with DNA. **Cloning is not transgenic.**
@end
