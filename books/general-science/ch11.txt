@chapter 11 | Space Technology

@intro
Space is the most current-affairs-driven topic in the science paper. Older questions ask about **India's first satellites** (Aryabhata, IRS-1A, INSAT, EDUSAT, Kalpana-1), launch vehicles (**SLV → ASLV → PSLV → GSLV**) and ISRO's centres. Newer ones follow the missions: **Chandrayaan-3, Aditya-L1, Mangalyaan, NISAR, LUPEX, INSAT-3DS, RLV-LEX** and astronauts such as **Sunita Williams**. Physics of satellites, rockets and cryogenic engines completes the chapter. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end
