@table
Particle | Charge | Discovered by | Year
**Electron** | -1 | **J.J. Thomson** (cathode rays) | 1897
**Proton** | +1 | **Rutherford** (also Goldstein's canal rays, 1886) | 1917-19
Nucleus | + | **Rutherford** (gold-foil experiment) | 1911

The nucleus holds **protons and neutrons** (together called nucleons); electrons move around it. Chronological order of discovery: **electron → proton → neutron → pi meson**.

A **deuteron** is the nucleus of deuterium (one proton and one neutron), a combination rather than a basic sub-atomic particle. @pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Mains) 2009
The atoms, in which the number of protons is same but the number of neutrons is different, are known as –
(a) Isobars
(b) Isomers
(c) Isotones
(d) Isotopes
@ans (d)
@end


@one
Isotopes are atoms of the same element whose
@ans atomic weights are different, but atomic numbers are the same.
@end

@table
Term | Same | Different | Example
**Isotopes** | **Atomic number** (protons) | Mass number (neutrons) | **Protium, deuterium, tritium** (hydrogen); C-12, C-14
**Isobars** | **Mass number** | Atomic number | Argon-40 and calcium-40
**Isotones** | **Number of neutrons** | Protons and mass number | C-14 and O-16 (8 neutrons each)
**Isomers** | Molecular formula (compounds) | Structure | Ethanol and dimethyl ether
@end

Protium, deuterium and tritium are isotopes of **hydrogen**, not carbon, so statement 2 of the 2025 question is false. Isotopes have the same chemical properties because chemistry depends on electrons, which equal the protons.

@section 14.3 | Physical and Chemical Changes

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 1992
Change of water into the vapour is called –
(a) Natural
(b) Physical
(c) Chemical
(d) Biological
@ans (b)
@end


A **physical change** alters only the state or form and is usually reversible: melting, boiling, freezing, dissolving sugar, breaking glass, magnetising iron. A **chemical change** makes new substances and is hard to reverse: rusting, burning, curdling of milk, cooking, digestion, photosynthesis.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2019
Which of the following is NOT correctly matched?
(a) Desalination – Sea water
(b) Reverse osmosis – Potable water
(c) Denaturation – Proteins
(d) Pasteurization – Tea
@ans (d)
@end

**Pasteurisation** (Louis Pasteur) heats **milk** briefly (about 72°C for 15 seconds, or 63°C for 30 minutes) and cools it quickly to kill disease germs without changing its taste. **Denaturation** is the unfolding of proteins by heat or acid (boiling an egg).

@section 14.4 | Mixtures, Colloids and Solutions

@pyq U.P. P.C.S. (Pre) 2022
Given below are two statements, one is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other as Reason (R).
Assertion (A) : A solution of table salt in a glass of water is homogeneous.
Reason (R) : A solution having different compositions throughout is homogeneous.
Select the correct answer from the code given below :
Code :
(a) (A) is true but (R) is false
(b) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
(c) (A) is false but (R) is true
(d) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)
@ans (a)
@end

A **homogeneous** mixture has the **same composition throughout** (salt water, air, alloys); a mixture that varies from place to place is **heterogeneous**, so the Reason has the definition backwards.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2002
Which of these is not a colloid :
(a) Milk
(b) Blood
(c) Icecream
(d) Honey
@ans (d)
@end


@table
Colloid type | Dispersed in | Example
**Emulsion** | Liquid in liquid | **Milk** (fat in water), butter, cold cream, mayonnaise
**Sol** | Solid in liquid | Paint, ink, starch solution, **blood**
**Gel** | Liquid in solid | Jelly, cheese, curd
**Foam** | Gas in liquid | Shaving foam, whipped cream, soap lather
**Aerosol** | Liquid or solid in gas | Fog, mist, clouds (liquid); smoke, dust (solid)
Solid foam | Gas in solid | Pumice, foam rubber
@end

Colloids scatter a beam of light (**Tyndall effect**) and their particles (1-1,000 nm) do not settle. **Honey** is a concentrated **true solution** of sugars in water, so it is not a colloid; ice cream is a colloid of fat, air and ice.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2017
If an unsaturated sugar solution is prepared by mixing 100 grams of sugar with half litre of water, which of the following physical quantities will not change?
(a) Mass
(b) Density
(c) Concentration
(d) Volume
@ans (d)
@end


Sugar dissolves into the spaces between water molecules, so the **volume** of half a litre of water hardly changes, while mass, density and concentration all go up.

@pyq U.P. R.O./A.R.O. (Pre) 2023
Consider the following statements with reference to osmosis : (1) If the semipermeable membrane is placed between the solvent and solution, the solvent molecules will flow through the membrane from pure solvent to the solution. (2) The process of flow of solvent is called as reverse osmosis.
Select the correct answer using the code given below :
(a) Neither (1) nor (2)
(b) Only (2)
(c) Only (1)
(d) Both (1) and (2)
@ans (c)
@end


**Osmosis**: solvent flows through a semipermeable membrane from **pure solvent (or dilute solution) into the concentrated solution**. **Reverse osmosis** pushes it the other way by applying pressure greater than the osmotic pressure; it is used in **RO water purifiers** and **desalination** plants. So statement 2, which calls ordinary osmosis "reverse osmosis", is wrong.

@trap
The nucleus has **protons and neutrons** only. **Chadwick** found the neutron (1932). Electron's antiparticle = **positron**. **Neutrino** has zero charge. Protium, deuterium and tritium are isotopes of **hydrogen**. **Isotopes** = same atomic number; **isobars** = same mass number. **Milk is an emulsion**; **honey is not a colloid**. A salt-ice mixture works because salt **lowers** the freezing point. Pasteurisation is for **milk**, not tea.
@end
