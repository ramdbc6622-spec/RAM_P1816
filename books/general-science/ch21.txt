@pyq U.P.R.O./A.R.O. (Mains) 2013
Which of the following organelle is called ‘Power House’ of the cell?
(a) Ribosome
(b) Mitochondria
(c) Cell membrane
(d) Centrosome
@ans (b)
@end


@table
Organelle | Job
**Cell wall** (cellulose) | Present in **plant** cells, absent in animal cells
**Plasma membrane** | Lipid bilayer with proteins (fluid mosaic model, Singer and Nicolson, 1972); selectively permeable
**Mitochondria** | **"Power house"**: aerobic respiration makes ATP; have their own DNA
Chloroplast | Photosynthesis (plants only)
**Ribosome** | Protein synthesis
**Nucleus** | Holds DNA as chromosomes; controls the cell and **cell division**
Lysosome | "Suicide bags": digestive enzymes
Golgi body | Packaging and secretion
Centrosome | Spindle formation in **animal** cells
Vacuole | Large in plant cells
@end

@pyq U.P.P.S.C. (GIC) 2010
The unit of heredity in our body is called :
(a) Chromosome
(b) DNA
(c) Gene
(d) Nucleus
@ans (c)
@end


**Gregor Mendel** (father of genetics) crossed **garden peas** (*Pisum sativum*) and found the laws of **dominance, segregation and independent assortment** (1866). A **gene** is a functional segment of DNA and the unit of heredity; a change in its base sequence is a **mutation**. **Genome mapping** locates genes on chromosomes; the **Human Genome Project** was completed in 2003 (about 20,000 genes), and India's **Genome India** project sequenced 10,000 Indians (data released 2025).

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Spl) (Mains) 2004
Which of the following does not have sex-chromosome?
(a) Monkey
(b) Tiger
(c) Butterfly
(d) Lizard
@ans (d)
@end


Humans have **46 chromosomes** (23 pairs: 22 pairs of autosomes + XX or XY). Sex is fixed by the father's sperm (X or Y). Many **reptiles** (most turtles, crocodiles and some lizards) have no sex chromosomes; the **incubation temperature** of the eggs decides their sex.

@section 21.3 | Biofertilizers

@pyq U.P.R.O./A.R.O. (Pre) (Re-Exam) 2023
Biofertilizers are :
(a) Organic manures
(b) Cultures of micro-organisms
(c) Green manures
(d) None of the above
@ans (b)
@end


**Biofertilizers** are cultures of living micro-organisms that add nutrients to soil by fixing nitrogen or dissolving phosphate. They are cheap and eco-friendly.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2006
Which bacteria is responsible for nitrogen fixation in soyabean?
(a) Rhizobium leguminosarum
(b) Rhizobium japonicum
(c) Rhizobium fajioleai
(d) Rhizobium traitolai
@ans (b)
@end


@table
Biofertilizer | Partner / crop | Nutrient
**Rhizobium** | Root nodules of **legumes** (pulses, soybean: *R. japonicum*) | Nitrogen (symbiotic)
**Azotobacter**, Azospirillum | Free-living in soil (cereals) | Nitrogen
**Blue-green algae** (Anabaena, Nostoc) | **Paddy** fields | Nitrogen
**Azolla** + *Anabaena azollae* | Floating fern in **waterlogged paddy** | Nitrogen (up to 40-60 kg N/ha)
**VAM** (vesicular-arbuscular mycorrhiza) | Fungus with roots | **Phosphorus**
PSB (phosphate-solubilising bacteria) | Soil | Phosphorus
@end

**Mycorrhiza** does not fix nitrogen. **Chrysopa** (lacewing) is a biological control insect, and **Azotobacter** is a biofertilizer, not a bio-insecticide. Nitrogen-use efficiency improves with **split doses**, **nitrification inhibitors** (neem coating) and **slow-release** fertilisers.

@trap
**Cell wall** marks a plant cell. **Mitochondria = power house.** Membrane = **lipid + protein**. **Mendel used garden peas.** **Gene** is the unit of heredity. DNA sugar = **deoxyribose**; **tyrosine** is not a DNA base. Humans have **46** chromosomes. **Azolla is a fern** that hosts blue-green algae; **mycorrhiza** supplies phosphorus, not nitrogen. Soybean's partner is *Rhizobium japonicum*.
@end
