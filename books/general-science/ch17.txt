@chapter 17 | Organic Chemistry, Polymers, Acids and Explosives

@intro
Organic chemistry in UPPSC is practical: **methane** from paddy fields, **ethylene** and **calcium carbide** for ripening fruit, methyl alcohol ("wood spirit") and beer, **polymers** natural and synthetic (rubber, silk, nylon, polythene, Teflon, Kevlar, Lexan, Bakelite, PVC), **organic acids** in food (lactic, acetic, tartaric, citric, formic, oxalic), and **explosives** (TNT, RDX, nitroglycerine). It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

Methane gas producing field is –
(a) Wheatfield

All organic compounds contain **carbon** (usually with hydrogen). **Methane** (CH_{4}), the simplest hydrocarbon, comes from waterlogged **paddy fields**, cattle (enteric fermentation), wetlands, landfills and coal mines; it is the main part of natural gas, biogas and CNG.

@table
Explosive | Chemical / maker
**TNT** | Trinitrotoluene
**Nitroglycerine** (TNG, "Nobel's oil") | Glyceryl trinitrate, made by nitrating **glycerol** with nitric and sulphuric acids; also a heart medicine (angina)
**Dynamite** | Nitroglycerine absorbed in kieselguhr (**Alfred Nobel**, 1867)
**Blasting gelatine** | Nitroglycerine + nitrocellulose
**Cordite** | Nitroglycerine + nitrocellulose + petroleum jelly (smokeless propellant)
**Amatol** | **TNT + ammonium nitrate** (no nitroglycerine)
**RDX** | **Cyclotrimethylene trinitramine** (cyclonite), patented by **Georg Friedrich Henning** (1898)
Gunpowder | Charcoal + sulphur + potassium nitrate (saltpetre)
@end

**Chloropicrin** (nitrochloroform) is a fumigant, insecticide and tear gas, not an explosive.

@trap
**Calcium carbide gives acetylene** and is banned for ripening; **ethylene / ethephon** is the proper ripener. **Wood spirit = methanol.** **Nylon is synthetic**; silk, wool and leather are natural. **Kevlar → vests, Lexan → bullet-proof windows.** **Bakelite = phenol + formaldehyde.** **PVC is not biodegradable.** **Vinegar = acetic acid**, tamarind = tartaric, curd = lactic, ant = formic, apple = malic. **Amatol has no nitroglycerine.** **RDX = Henning.**
@end
