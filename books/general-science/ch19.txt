@pyq U.P. P.C.S. (Pre) 2020
Given below are two statements, one is labelled as Assertion (A) and other as Reason (R) :
Assertion (A) : Invert sugar is more sweet than sucrose.
Reason (R) : Invert sugar is obtained by the hydrolysis of sucrose.
Select the correct answer from the code given below:
Code :
(a) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).
(b) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A).
(c) (A) is true but (R) is false.
(d) (A) is false but (R) is true.
@ans (b)
@end

**Honey** is mostly **fructose (about 38%)** and glucose (about 31%), with about 17% water. **Invert sugar** is a 1:1 mixture of glucose and fructose made by splitting (hydrolysing) sucrose; it is sweeter because **fructose is the sweetest natural sugar**. Its sweetness comes from the fructose, not from the way it is made, so (R) does not explain (A).
