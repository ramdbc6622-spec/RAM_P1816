@pyq U.P.R.O./A.R.O. (Mains) 2013
The process by which plants synthesize their food is called:
(a) Osmosis
(b) Respiration
(c) Photosynthesis
(d) Plasmolysis

**Chlorophyll** has a **magnesium** atom at the centre of its porphyrin ring (haemoglobin has iron at the same position). The overall reaction is 6CO_{2} + 12H_{2}O + light → C_{6}H_{12}O_{6} + 6O_{2} + 6H_{2}O.

@pyq U.P. Lower Sub (Spl) (Pre) 2008

@ans (c)

Chlorophyll absorbs mainly **red and blue** light and reflects green. Photosynthesis is **highest in red light**, then blue, and least in green. Bright **artificial light** (LED grow lights in vertical farms) works too. **Algae and phytoplankton** in the oceans produce about half or more of the world's oxygen, and fast-growing **Chlorella** converts sunlight to food very efficiently. *Cuscuta* is a parasite without chlorophyll.

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) (Re. Exam) 2015; U.P. P.C.S. (Pre) 2023
The 'xylem' in plants is responsible for transporting :
(a) Water
(b) Oxygen
(c) Amino acid
(d) Food
@ans (a)
@end


@table
Tissue | Carries | Direction | Cells
**Xylem** | **Water and minerals** | Upward from roots | Mostly **dead** (vessels, tracheids)
**Phloem** | **Food** (sugars, amino acids) made in leaves | Both ways (translocation) | **Living** (sieve tubes, companion cells)
@end

Water rises in tall trees mainly by the **transpiration pull** (cohesion-tension theory), helped by root pressure and capillarity.

@table
Nutrient group | Elements
**Macronutrients** (primary) | **Nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium**
Secondary macronutrients | **Calcium, magnesium, sulphur**
**Micronutrients** | **Iron, manganese, zinc, copper, boron, molybdenum, chlorine, nickel**

Plants take nitrogen mostly as **nitrate (NO_{3}^{-})** and some as ammonium; they cannot use N_{2} gas directly. **Nitrogen deficiency** causes yellowing (chlorosis) of older leaves and stunted growth. **Sulphur** raises the **oil content** of mustard and other oilseeds. **Boron** is needed for nodule formation in legumes, pollen growth and sugar movement; **molybdenum** is part of the nitrogen-fixing enzyme.

@table
Plant hormone | Main effects
**Auxin** (IAA) | **Apical dominance**, cell elongation, rooting of cuttings, phototropism (first plant hormone, Went 1928)

@end

@table
Practice | Meaning
**Gootee** (air layering) | Rooting a branch while still on the parent (litchi, guava, pomegranate) for **vegetative propagation**
Grafting | Joining a scion onto a rootstock (mango, apple)
**Hybrid seed** | F1 seed; vigour falls in the next generation, so **farmers must buy new seed every year**
Breeder → foundation → certified seed | Stages of seed multiplication
**Clonal selection** | Choosing the best clones of **vegetatively propagated** crops (potato, sugarcane, banana)

Tasselling | Male flowers appearing in **maize**
**Pegging** | Groundnut flower stalks pushing into the soil
**Topping** | Removing the flower head of **tobacco**
Tillering | Side shoots in cereals (wheat, rice), not chickpea
@end

Poppy capsules scatter seeds through pores like a **censer** (incense burner) swinging in the wind.

@trap
Chlorophyll has **magnesium**. Oxygen from photosynthesis comes from **water**. **Red light** is most effective. **Xylem = water, phloem = food.** Plants take nitrogen as **nitrate**. **Magnesium is not a micronutrient.** **Sulphur** raises mustard oil content; **boron** helps nodules. **Auxin = apical dominance, cytokinin = cell division, ABA = drought.** Ripening gas = **ethylene**. **Hybrid seed** must be changed every year. **Gootee** = air layering.
@end
