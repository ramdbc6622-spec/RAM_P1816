@chapter 22 | Human Body Systems

@intro
This chapter goes through the human body system by system: **composition and skeleton** (water, oxygen, 206 bones, stapes, joints), **digestion** (enzymes, liver, bile, gallstones), **respiration** (alveoli, ATP), **blood and circulation** (four-chambered heart, haemoglobin, blood groups, blood pressure, pH), **excretion** (kidneys, urea, dialysis, stones), the **nervous system** (cerebellum, hypothalamus) and the **endocrine glands** and their hormones. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

(a) Faster than the heart
(b) Slower than the heart
(c) At the same rate as the heart
(d) Independently of the heart
@ans (c)

The human heart has **four chambers**: two atria and two ventricles. **Pulmonary veins** are the only veins with **oxygenated** blood (lungs → left atrium), and the **pulmonary artery** is the only artery with deoxygenated blood. The heart rests only **between beats** (diastole), about 0.4 s of each 0.8 s cycle at 72 beats a minute. The **pulse** is the same as the heart rate.
