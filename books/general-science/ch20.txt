@pyq U.P.U.D.A./L.D.A. (Pre) 2001

(a) Cenozoic reptiles
(b) Mesozoic birds
(c) Paleozoic amphibians
(d) Mesozoic reptiles
@ans (d)
@end

@one U.P. Lower Sub. (Pre) 1998
Dinosaurs lived in—
@ans Mesozoic era
@end


The early Earth's atmosphere had **methane, ammonia, hydrogen, water vapour and CO_{2}** but **no free oxygen** (Miller-Urey experiment, 1953, made amino acids from such a mix). **Cyanobacteria (blue-green algae)** were among the earliest organisms (fossil stromatolites over 3.5 billion years old) and their photosynthesis filled the air with oxygen.

@table
Theory | Scientist
Inheritance of acquired characters | **Lamarck** (1809)
**Natural selection** ("survival of the fittest" was Spencer's phrase) | **Charles Darwin** (*On the Origin of Species*, 1859), with A.R. Wallace
**Mutation theory** | **Hugo de Vries** (evening primrose)
Laws of inheritance | Gregor Mendel (1866)
@end

**Dinosaurs** were reptiles of the **Mesozoic era** (about 245-66 million years ago: Triassic, Jurassic, Cretaceous); they died out after an asteroid struck Chicxulub, Mexico. Birds evolved from small dinosaurs.

(a) Embryonic zone
(b) Growing point
(c) Zone of elongation

@ans (d)

Desert plants (xerophytes) have **long roots** to reach deep water, **succulent** stems or leaves to store water (cactus, aloe), and small or spiny leaves to cut water loss; the high soil temperature is not the reason. Most water is taken up by **root hairs**.
