@chapter 27 | Genetic, Deficiency and Other Diseases; Plant Diseases

@intro
This chapter covers diseases that are not infections: **genetic disorders** (haemophilia, colour blindness, SCID), **deficiency** and lifestyle diseases (goitre, beriberi, gout, osteoporosis, diabetes, cirrhosis, kwashiorkor), **pollution diseases** (blue baby, knock-knee, Minamata, itai-itai), **radioisotopes in medicine**, cancer and brain disorders, **eye defects**, and medical terms (MRI, BMD, EEG, CPR, cath lab). The last part covers **plant diseases** of crops. It contains **{pyqs} PYQs and {ones} one-liners**.
@end

@pyq U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2009
If a colour blind man marries with normal women, then the symptoms of colour blindness is generated in :
(a) Sons
(b) Daughters
(c) Sons of sons
(d) Sons of daughters
@ans (d)
@end


An X-linked recessive trait passes from an affected **man** to all his **daughters as carriers** (sons get his Y and are normal); a carrier daughter then passes it to half her **sons**. So colour blindness skips a generation and shows in the **daughter's sons** (the man's grandsons).
