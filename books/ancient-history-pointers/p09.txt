@trap
- Junagadh inscription is **Rudradaman's** (Shaka, 150 CE), not Mauryan.
- **Megasthenes** = Chandragupta; **Deimachus** = Bindusara.
- Prinsep read Brahmi (1837); the name Ashoka was found at **Maski** in 1915.
- Mauryas issued **silver** punch-marked coins, not gold.
