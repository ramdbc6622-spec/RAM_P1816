@section 8.1 | Alexander in India
- Women took up arms against Alexander at **Massaga** (Assakenoi) {2013}
- Defeated on the Jhelum: **Porus** {2013}
- Success: **no central power, superior army, help from traitors** (1, 2, 3) {2000, 2002, 2003}
