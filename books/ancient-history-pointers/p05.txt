@section 5.1 | Tirthankaras
- Originator of Jainism: **Rishabhadeva** {2010}
- "Tirthankara": **Jainism** {1993}
- Not a Tirthankara: **Nathamuni** (a Vaishnava acharya) {2004}
- Order: **Abhinandan, Padmaprabhu, Vimalnath, Munisuvrata** {2016}
- **Parshvanatha**: **Varanasi** {2016}
- Parshvanatha's pilgrimage: **Sammed Shikhar** (Jharkhand) {2002, 2002}
- **Adinatha-bull, Mallinatha-water jar, Parshvanatha-snake, Sambhavnath-horse** {2017}
- Wrong pair: **Vasupujya-Sammed Shikhar** (Vasupujya attained nirvana at **Champa**) {2021}

@section 5.2 | UP in Jain Geography
- **Shravasti-Sambhavnath, Kakandi-Suvidhinath, Ayodhya-Rishabhanath, Pabhosa-Padmaprabhu** {2021}
- **Prabhasgiri** (Pabhosa): a **Jain** pilgrimage {2008}
- Pilgrimage of both Buddhists and Jains in UP: **Kaushambi** {2010}

@section 5.3 | Mahavira and Doctrine
- **Kundalpur**: birthplace of **Mahavira** {2004}
- Enlightenment on the **Rijupalika** river {2017}
- First follower (son-in-law): **Jamali** {2008}
- **Triratna** (right faith, action, knowledge): **Jainism** {2004}
- Basic point: **non-violence** {1993}
- No "final annihilation of the world": **Jainism** {2014}

@flow Mahavira's life
Birth | Kundagrama (Vaishali), 540 BCE (traditional 599)
Renunciation | Age 30
Kaivalya | Age 42, Jrimbhikagrama, Rijupalika river
Mission | 30 years; added brahmacharya as the fifth vow
Nirvana | Pavapuri, age 72
@end

@section 5.4 | Literature, Councils and Sects
- Earliest Jain texts: **Ardha-Magadhi** {2006}
- Shvetambara Agamas finally edited at **Valabhi** (key: Pataliputra) {2008}
- **Yapaniya**: a **Jain** sect {2010}
- **Ajivika** founder: **Makkhali Gosala** {1996}
- "Fate assigns everything": **Ajivikas** {2006}
- Ajivikas respected in the Maurya era (only II); they were not forest dwellers {2023}
- **Lakulisha-Pashupata, Nagarjuna-Shunyavada, Bhadrabahu-Jainism, Gosala-Ajivika** {2017}
- **Gommateshwara** (Shravanabelagola) built by **Chamundaraya** {2009}

@table
Council | Place | Head | Result
First | Pataliputra (c. 300 BCE) | Sthulabhadra | 12 Angas compiled; split begins
Second | Valabhi (5th century CE) | Devardhi Kshamashramana | Agamas finally written
@end

@trap
- Mahavira is the **24th**, not the first; Rishabhadeva founded Jainism.
- Jain Triratna = right faith, knowledge, conduct; Buddhist = Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha.
- Parshvanatha gave **four** vows; Mahavira added **brahmacharya**.
- Ajivika = Gosala; Pashupata = Lakulisha; Yapaniya = Jain.
@end

@up UP link: UP holds the birthplaces of many Tirthankaras: **Ayodhya** (Rishabhanatha and four others), **Shravasti** (Sambhavnath), **Kaushambi/Pabhosa** (Padmaprabhu), **Varanasi** (Parshvanatha, Suparshvanatha), **Kakandi** (Suvidhinath) and **Hastinapur**.
