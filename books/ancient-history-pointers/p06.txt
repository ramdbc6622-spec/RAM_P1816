@section 6.3 | Bhagavatism and Vishnu
- First worshippers of Vasudeva Krishna: **Bhagavatas** {1997}
- Earliest record of Bhagavatism: **Heliodorus' Besnagar inscription** {2008}
- Heliodorus was from **Taxila** {2010}
- Deity holding a plough: **Balarama** {2007}
- Avatar that saved the Earth from the sea: **Varaha** {2010}
- Forms of bhakti (Bhagavata): **9** {2010}

@flow Ten avatars of Vishnu
Matsya, Kurma | Fish (flood), tortoise (churning)
Varaha, Narasimha | Boar (raises Earth), man-lion
Vamana, Parashurama | Dwarf, axe-bearer
Rama, Krishna | Epic heroes
Buddha, Kalki | Buddha (some lists Balarama); future avatar
@end

@section 6.4 | Philosophy, Epics and Sacred Places
- Astika vs nastika: belief in the **Vedas' authority** {2005}
- Equal place to jnana, karma, bhakti: **Bhagavad Gita** {2005}
- Not in the Prasthanatrayi: **Bhagavata** (it is Upanishads, Gita, Brahma Sutra) {1997}
- Mahabharata told to 60,000 hermits at **Naimisharanya** {2006}
- Rama meets Hanuman in the **Kishkindha Kanda** {2004}
- Nashik Kumbh: on the **Godavari** {2003}

@trap
- Alvars = Vishnu; Nayanars = Shiva.
- Heliodorus was an Indo-Greek envoy from **Taxila** (of Antialkidas), not Megasthenes.
- Gita is in the Prasthanatrayi; the Bhagavata Purana is not.
- Varaha raises the Earth; Matsya saves Manu in the flood.
@end

@up UP link: **Naimisharanya** (Sitapur) is where the Mahabharata and Puranas were recited; **Mathura** is the centre of Bhagavata worship of Vasudeva Krishna, and **Prayagraj** hosts the Kumbh at the Sangam.
