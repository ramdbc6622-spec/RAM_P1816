@section 13.3 | Pallavas, Chalukyas and Rashtrakutas

- Two naval expeditions to Ceylon (642): **Narasimhavarman I** {2016}
- Chalukya capital: **Vatapi**; greatest king: **Pulakeshin II** {1991, 1991}

- Gave women high office: **Chalukyas** {2007}

- **Kadamba** capital: **Vanavasi** {2005}
- **Takkolam** (949): **Cholas vs Rashtrakutas** {2001}

@section 13.4 | The Imperial Cholas
- Chola empire: **Coromandel coast and part of the Deccan** {1991}
- Capital: **Thanjavur** {2009}
- More known about Cholas: their rule left many records (A and R true, R explains A) {2020}
- Naval power: **Cholas** {1992, 2004}
- Northern Sri Lanka conquered by **Rajaraja I**; he also beat the Chera navy at Kandalur {2014, 2023}
- "Chola lake", **Gangaikondacholapuram**, **Cholagangam**: **Rajendra I** {2008, 2008, 2016}
- Freed Sri Lanka and married his daughter to a Sinhala prince: **Kulottunga I** {2012}
- Heir named in the king's lifetime: unclear (*) {2001}
- Who ended the Cholas: unclear (*); the Pandyas and Hoysalas {2004}
- Special feature: **village self-government** {1991, 1995}
- Garden committee: **Thotta Variyam** {2013}
- Overseas guilds: **Nanadesis, Manigramam** {2018}
- 72 merchants to China: **Kulottunga I** {1992}
- **Nataraja** bronze: **Chola art** {2006}
- Nataraja: four hands; a man's earring right, a woman's left (both) {2021}
- **Dakshinamurti**: Shiva as **teacher** {2013}
- **Rudramadevi** (Kakatiya): fourth ruler; beat Pandyas, Gangas and Yadavas (both) {2023}
