@section 1.1 | Discovery of Indian Prehistory
- **Robert Bruce Foote** (first Palaeolithic tool, Pallavaram 1863): a **geologist** and archaeologist (both accepted) {2015}
- Three-age system (stone, bronze, iron), Copenhagen Museum: **Thomsen** ("Thomson") {2010}

@section 1.2 | Palaeolithic Sites and Rock Art
- **Nevasa-Maharashtra, Isampur-Karnataka, Didwana-Rajasthan, Gudiyam cave-Tamil Nadu** {2023}
- Prehistoric paintings: **Bhimbetka** {2011}
- Most rock paintings: **Bhimbetka**; first noticed by **V.S. Wakankar** {?, ?}

@section 1.3 | Mesolithic: Microliths and Domestication
- Domestication of animals began in the **Mesolithic** {2006}
- Mesolithic animal husbandry: **Bagor and Adamgarh** {2008}
- West to east: **Mahadaha, Lekhahia, Paisra, Birbhanpur** {2021}
- Bone implements: **Mahadaha** (and Sarai Nahar Rai) {2010, 2013}
- Three skeletons in one grave: **Damdama** {2016}
- Most human skeletons in a Vindhyan rock shelter: **Lekhahia** {2016}
- Vindhyan people moved to the Ganga valley in a **dry phase** at the end of the Pleistocene (A and R true, R explains A) {2016}

@flow Stone Age to metal
Palaeolithic | Hunting, handaxes; Pallavaram, Nevasa, Isampur, Didwana
Mesolithic | Microliths, first domestication; Bagor, Adamgarh, Mahadaha
Neolithic | Polished tools, farming; Mehrgarh, Burzahom, Koldihwa
Chalcolithic | Copper and stone; Ahar, Kayatha, Malwa, Jorwe
@end

@section 1.4 | Neolithic: Farming and Settled Life
- Cereals first cultivated in the **Neolithic Age** {2005}
- First cereal used by man: **barley** {1997}
- Earliest agriculture (key): **Lahuradewa** (rice; date disputed) {2004, 2007, 2008, 2010}
- Earliest settled life: **Mehrgarh** {2008}
- Stone Age to Harappan remains at one site: **Mehrgarh** {2008}
- Dog buried with human: **Burzahom** {2008}
- Pit dwellings: **Burzahom** {?}
- Ash mounds: **Sanganakallu** (Karnataka; Budihal also) {2009}

@section 1.5 | Chalcolithic, OCP and Megaliths
- **Navdatoli** excavated by **H.D. Sankalia**; it is in **Madhya Pradesh** {2009, ?}
- Chalcolithic Maharashtra burials: **north-south** under house floors {1997}
- **Ochre-Coloured Pottery** named at **Hastinapur** {2006}
- **Megaliths**: **burial sites** {2005}

@trap

- Earliest rice in UPPSC keys = **Lahuradewa**; earliest settled village = **Mehrgarh**.
- **Mahadaha** = double burials; **Damdama** = triple burial.
- Megaliths are **burials**, not temples.
@end

@up UP link: UP is rich in Mesolithic sites: **Sarai Nahar Rai, Mahadaha, Damdama** (Pratapgarh), **Lekhahia** and **Baghor** in the Vindhyas (Mirzapur-Sonbhadra), **Koldihwa** (Prayagraj, Neolithic rice), **Lahuradewa** (Sant Kabir Nagar) and **Hastinapur** (Meerut, OCP).
