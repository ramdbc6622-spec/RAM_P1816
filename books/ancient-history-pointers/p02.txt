@section 2.1 | Nature and Sources
- Indus civilisation: **proto-historic** age (script not read) {1996}
- Source of knowledge: **archaeological excavations** {1993, 1994, 1996}
- Mohenjo-daro and Harappa are dead cities; being excavated is not the reason (A and R true, R does not explain A) {2009}
- Human society depends mainly on **economy** (per key) {2004}
- Non-Aryan because it was **urban** {2010}
- Placed before the Aryans because of its **pottery** {1990}
- Different from the Vedic culture: **all of the above** {2004}
