@trap
- Mihira Bhoja (Pratihara, Adivaraha) is **not** Bhoja Paramara (Dhar, Udaipur Prashasti).
- Vikramashila and Somapura = Dharmapala; Nalanda = **Kumaragupta I**.
- Jejakabhukti is a **region** (Bundelkhand), not a city.
- The Rashtrakutas raided Kannauj but never held it.
@end
