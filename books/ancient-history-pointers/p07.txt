@section 7.1 | The Sixteen Mahajanapadas
- Listed in the **Anguttara Nikaya** {2004, 2008, 2008}
- Mahajanapadas in present UP: **8** {2021}
- **Kuru-Indraprastha, Panchala-Ahichhatra, Kosala-Saketa, Vatsa-Kaushambi** {2008}
- Wrong pair: **Kosala-Ahichhatra** {2017}
- Capital of North Panchala: **Ahichhatra** {2006}
- Wrong pair: **Surasena-Kaushambi** (Surasena = **Mathura**) {2016}
- **Suktimati**: capital of **Chedi** {2011}
- Ancient Shravasti: **crescent-shaped** town {2010}
- **Kalpi** stands on the **Yamuna** {2015}
- **Champa**: capital of **Anga** {2014}
- **Matsya**: **Rajasthan** {2017}
- **Matsya-Viratnagar, Kuru-Indraprastha, Surasena-Mathura, Asmaka-Potana** {2020}
- Ujjain's old name: **Avantika** {2009}

@section 7.2 | Republics and Kings

@section 7.3 | Rise of Magadha
- Killed his father and was killed by his son: **Ajatashatru** {2007, 2011}
- Magadha took Malwa (Avanti) under **Shishunaga** {2008}
- Varanasi as second capital: unclear (*); UPPSC accepted **Shishunaga** {2007}
- **Aparo Parashurama**: **Mahapadma Nanda** {2010}
- Nanda evidence: **Kharavela's Hathigumpha** inscription {2010}
- A Nanda king dug a canal in **Kalinga** {1999}
- Earliest extant coins: **silver** (punch-marked) {2010}
