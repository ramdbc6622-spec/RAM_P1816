@section 19.1 | The Durbar and the Capital

- Capital transfer under **Lord Hardinge** {1990, 2008}
- Year of transfer: **1912** {2008}
- Bomb at his state entry into Delhi: **Lord Hardinge** (1912) {2008}

@trap
- "Announced" = 1911; "transferred" = 1912; New Delhi inaugurated = 1931.
- The **1903** Durbar was Curzon's; the **1911** Durbar was George V's under Hardinge.
- **Mayo** was killed at **Port Blair** (1872), not Delhi.
@end

@up UP link: The Lucknow Pact (1916) is **not** a 1911 event; it is the usual distractor in 1911 questions.
