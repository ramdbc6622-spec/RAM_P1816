@section 22.1 | Early Life and South Africa
- Karamchand Gandhi Diwan of "all of these" (per key; really Porbandar, Rajkot, Wankaner) {2001}
- True: schooled at **Rajkot**, married at 13, law at **Inner Temple**, influenced by **Unto This Last**: **all four** {2010}
- Thrown off the train at **Pietermaritzburg** {2015}
- Adopted "Seva Dharma" in **South Africa** {2015}
- First Congress session attended: **Calcutta, 1901** {2003, 2004}
- South Africa prepared him for leadership (A true); "Indians would be ready for militant struggle" (R false) {2023}
- Paper in South Africa: **Indian Opinion** {2002}
- Influences: **Ruskin, Thoreau, Tolstoy** (all) {2013}
