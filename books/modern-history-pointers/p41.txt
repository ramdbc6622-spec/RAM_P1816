@chapter 41 | Constitutional Development of India
@unit UNIT IX · CONSTITUTIONAL DEVELOPMENT AND MISCELLANY
@meta Built from 28 UPPSC questions (1990–2023). Focus: the Regulating and Pitt's Acts, Charter Acts, the Agra Presidency, 1858 to 1909, dyarchy in 1919, simultaneous ICS examination, the 1935 Act and Indian verdicts.

@hot
- **Regulating Act (1773)**: first Governor-General of Bengal and the first Supreme Court (Calcutta) {1998, 2013}
- **Indian Councils Act (1861)**: ordinance power, portfolio system; High Courts in **1861** {1997, 2001, 2010, 2013, 2021}
- **Dyarchy** in the provinces: **Act of 1919** {1998, 2004, 2005}
- **Simon Commission** report was the base of the **1935 Act**; Nehru called it a "**charter of slavery**" and "a car with brakes but no engine" {2007, 2010, 2010, 2015}
@end

@section 41.1 | Company Rule
- First Governor-General of Bengal: **Regulating Act, 1773** {2013}
- First Supreme Court: **Regulating Act, 1773** {1998}
- Board of Control: **Pitt's India Act, 1784** {2015}
- Governor-General may overrule his council: **Act of 1786** {1990}
- Charter Act 1813 important: **money set aside for Indian education** {2016}
- Company lost its tea monopoly: **Charter Act, 1833** {2015}
- First functioning Legislative Council: **Charter Act, 1853** {2016}
- Most of present UP put under the **Agra Presidency**: **1834** {2021}

@section 41.2 | Crown Rule to 1919
- Rule passed from the Company to the Crown: **Government of India Act, 1858** {2007, 2010}
- Ordinance power to the Governor-General: **Indian Councils Act, 1861** {1997, 2001}
- Portfolio system replacing collective working: **Indian Councils Act, 1861** {2021}
- High Courts at Bombay, Madras, Calcutta: **1861** (Act; opened 1862) {2010, 2013}
- First indirect elections: **1892** {2016}
- Dyarchy divided administration (A); aimed at responsible government in provinces (R): **both true, R explains A** {1998}
- Dyarchy introduced in the provinces by the **1919** Act {2004, 2005}
- 1919 kept the structure unitary (A); much authority went to provinces (R): both true, R not the explanation (keys differ) {1998}
- Chamber of Princes (120 members): **Act of 1919** {2023}
- Simultaneous ICS examination recommended by the **Montagu-Chelmsford Report** {2010}
- Simultaneous ICS examination finally agreed in **1922** {2014}

@section 41.3 | The 1935 Act and After
- Main elements: **federation and provincial autonomy** (not provincial dyarchy) {1998}
- Council of India abolished by the **1935 Act** {2016}
- Basis of the 1935 Act: **Simon Commission** {2010, 2015}
- 1909 **Morley-Minto**, 1919 **Montagu-Chelmsford**, 1935 **Simon Report and JSC**, 1947 **Mountbatten Plan** {2009}
- Congress rejected the 1935 Act at **Lucknow, 1936** {2013}
- "Charter of slavery": **Nehru** {2010}
- "A car with brakes but no engine": **Nehru** {2007}
- "No need to beg pardon" for borrowing from the 1935 Act: **Ambedkar** {2015}
- "One free country with separate electorates?": **Sardar Patel** {2021}

@trap
- First **indirect** election 1892; first **separate** electorate 1909; first **direct** election 1919.
- 1935 brought dyarchy to the **centre** (never worked) and **ended** it in the provinces.
- Aitchison Commission **opposed** simultaneous ICS examinations.
@end
