@chapter 26 | Khilafat Movement
@meta Built from 10 UPPSC questions (1993–2016). Focus: the Ali brothers, Hakim Ajmal Khan, Gandhi as Khilafat president, Congress motives, Jinnah and Malaviya, Hindu-Muslim unity.

@hot
- Khilafat started and led by the **Ali brothers** (Mohammad Ali, Shaukat Ali) {2008, 2016}
- Warned Gandhi against it and denounced his part: **Jinnah**; did not support it: **Malaviya** {2002, 2008, 2010, 2010, 2011}
@end

@section 26.1 | Leaders
- Started the movement: **Shaukat Ali and Mohammad Ali** {2008}
- Prominent leaders: **Maulana Mohammad Ali and Shaukat Ali** {2016}
- Renounced "Haziq-ul-Mulk": **Hakim Ajmal Khan** {2014}
- President of the All India Khilafat Conference (1919): **Gandhi** {1993}
- Congress backed it for **reinstating the Caliph** and **Muslim sympathy** {2000}

@section 26.2 | Support and Opposition
- Warned Gandhi against fanaticism: **Jinnah** {2002, 2010}
- Denounced Gandhi's role: **M.A. Jinnah** {2011}
- Did not support Khilafat: **Madan Mohan Malaviya** {2008, 2010}
- Speech on unity from the Jama Masjid pulpit (4 April 1919): **Swami Shraddhanand** {2004}
- Result: **Hindu-Muslim differences narrowed** {1996}

@trap
- **Shariatullah** was a Faraizi reformer (1818), not a Khilafat leader.
- The aim was to **restore** the Caliph; Turkey **abolished** the Caliphate in 1924 and the movement ended.
- Jinnah opposed it; Azad backed it.
@end
