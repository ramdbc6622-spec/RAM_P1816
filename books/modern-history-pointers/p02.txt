@section 2.1 | Bengal
- Last Governor of Bengal appointed by the Mughal emperor: **Murshid Quli Khan** {2013, 2021}
- Siraj-ud-Daula lost at **Plassey** {2005}
- "**Heaven-born general**": **Robert Clive** {2009}
- Capital moved from Murshidabad to **Munger**: **Mir Qasim** {2003}
- Most decisive battle: **Buxar** {1990, 2002, 2003, 2012, 2016}
- Nawab of Bengal during Buxar: **Mir Jafar** (restored) {2016}
- Diwani granted by **Shah Alam II** (Mughal emperor), under **Clive** {2003, 2004, 2006, 2010}
