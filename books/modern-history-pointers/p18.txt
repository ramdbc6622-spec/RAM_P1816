@section 18.2 | Separate Electorates
- Morley-Minto Reform passed in **1909** {1994}
- 1909 Act provided **communal representation** {1996}
- Seeds of discord: **reservation of seats and separate electorates for Muslims** {2001, 2004}
- Justice Party: called the Congress Brahmin-dominated, and claimed communal representation for non-Brahmins; **both correct** {2024}
