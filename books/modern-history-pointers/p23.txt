@section 23.1 | UP and the Kisan Sabhas
- UP Kisan Sabha (1918) formed by **Indra Narayan Dwivedi** (with Gauri Shankar Mishra) {2023}
- Baba Ramchandra organised farmers in **Awadh** {2008, 2010}
- General secretary of the All India Kisan Congress at Lucknow: **N.G. Ranga** {2016}
- First All India Kisan Sabha formed at **Lucknow** {2008, 2010}
- Founder president: **Swami Sahajanand Saraswati** {2015}
- Congress leader at the first meeting: **Jawaharlal Nehru** {2017}

@section 23.2 | Leaders and Regions
- Sahajanand **Bihar**, Khudai Khidmatgars **NWFP**, Swami Ramanand Tirtha **Hyderabad**, Abdul Hamid Khan **Southern Assam** {1998}
- Bardoli **Patel**, Bhartiya Kisan Vidyalaya **N.G. Ranga**, Bengal Praja Party **Fazlul Haq**, Bakasht **Shraddhanand** (per key; really Sahajanand) {2006}
- Patel's key role: **Bardoli Satyagraha** {2002, 2010}
- Workers and Peasants Party: "formed in 1927 as an all-India body" wrong; "work inside the Congress" right: **only 2** {2024}
- Bhoodan first started in **Andhra Pradesh** (Pochampalli, now Telangana, 1951) {2013, 2014}
