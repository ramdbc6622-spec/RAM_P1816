@trap
- Home Rule **Society** (Shyamji, London 1905) is not the Home Rule **League** (Tilak and Besant, 1916).
- Udham Singh shot **O'Dwyer** (Punjab Lt. Governor), not General Dyer.
- Kabul 1915 = Mahendra Pratap (President), Barkatullah (PM); Singapore 1943 = Subhas Bose.
@end
