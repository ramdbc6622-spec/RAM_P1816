@section 12.1 | Founding and British Attitude
- Congress founded **1885**, first session at **Bombay** {2010, 2023}
- First president: **W.C. Bonnerjee**; general secretary **A.O. Hume** {2004, 2010, 2014, 2015, 2015}
- Absent at the founding: **Surendranath Banerjee** {1997}
- Viceroy then: **Dufferin**; "microscopic minority" was his phrase {2006, 2011, 2012, 2012, 2017, 2018, 2023}
- "Assist it to a peaceful demise": **Curzon** {2002, 2002}
- Attended a Congress session: **Willingdon**, Bombay 1915 (as Governor) {2008}
- Early nationalists saw British rule as providential {2024}

@section 12.2 | Presidents and Firsts
- First Muslim president: **Badruddin Tyabji** (1887) {1995, 2003, 2004, 2005}
- Tyabji first Muslim; Sarojini Naidu first Indian woman president {2023}
- First woman president: **Annie Besant**, **Calcutta 1917** {2010, 2012, 2015}
- First European president: **George Yule** {2013}
- Never president: **Tilak** {2006, 2008, 2021}
- Of Lal-Bal-Pal, president: **Lala Lajpat Rai** (1920 special) {1997}
- "Swaraj is my birthright": **Tilak** (Lucknow 1916) {2004, 2005, 2015}
- Tilak's last session: **Amritsar 1919** {2014}
- Drafting the new constitution with Gandhi (Amritsar resolution) {2014}
- **C. Vijayaraghavachariar**: **Nagpur 1920** {2016}
- First policy on princely states: **Nagpur** session {2015}
- Gandhi presided only at **Belgaum (1924)** {2011}
- Presidents in order and matches (Azad, Naidu, Motilal and others) {2002, 2004, 2012, 2014}
- Nehru spoke of socialism at **Lucknow (1936)** {2015}
- **Haripura (1938)**: **Subhas Bose** {2008}
- **Jana Gana Mana** first sung at the **1911 Calcutta** session {2010}
- Six years in a row: **Abul Kalam Azad** (1940-46) {2016}
- Suggested winding up the Congress: **Gandhi** {2004}
