@section 39.1 | The Government
- Direct Action Day: **16 August 1946** {2007, 2008, 2010}
- Formed under Nehru: **September 1946** (2 September) {2006, 2015}
- Not ministers on 2 September 1946: **Liaquat Ali Khan and Maulana Azad** {2013}
- Not in the interim cabinet: **B.R. Ambedkar** {2004}
- Railways: **Asaf Ali** {2008}
- Finance Minister: **Liaquat Ali Khan** (League joined on 26 October 1946) {2014}
